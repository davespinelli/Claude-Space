## Proposal

Intent platforms usually fail at the same point: sales stops trusting the score because nobody can explain it. A lead comes up hot, the rep calls, there is nothing there, and after three of those the number gets ignored and you are paying to compute something no one reads.

Plan:
- Write down the intent signals you actually have rights to, with the source and the freshness of each.
- Build the ingest and normalisation layer so every signal lands with a timestamp attached.
- Score so it decomposes: each lead shows which signals moved it, by how much, and when.
- Expose it as an API your existing tools can call, with keys, rate limits and a stable schema.
- Use the LLM where it earns its place, classifying free text such as job posts or notes, not inventing the score itself.

You get the ingest layer, the scoring service, the API and the source.

Pipeline I run in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which signal sources are already first-party or licensed to you, and does this feed an existing CRM or stand on its own?
