## Proposal

Day-to-day entry that runs for months is mostly the same few record shapes repeated, which means the accuracy problem is really a consistency problem. Dates typed three ways, a trailing space on a customer name, a category spelled slightly differently, and the sheet stops summing correctly long before anyone notices the entry was wrong. Someone keeping up with volume by hand cannot also keep those invariants, so the sheet needs to enforce them.

Plan:
- Agree the record shapes and the rules each field has to satisfy.
- Build the Google Sheet with validation on entry: dropdowns for categories, date and number formats enforced, duplicates flagged on arrival.
- Script the parts that repeat, so recurring source files load themselves instead of being typed.
- Add a checks tab: blanks, out-of-range values, duplicates, anything that needs a human look.
- Handle the current backlog through the same path, with a reconciliation against the sources.

You get the sheet, the loader script, the checks tab and a short runbook.
