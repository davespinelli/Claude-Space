## Proposal

The reason accounting exports resist becoming an MIS is that the export changes shape. A new account code appears, a column shifts, and last month's formulas quietly point at the wrong thing. So the first thing to build is not the dashboard, it is a load step that fails loudly when the export does not look like it did last month.

Plan:
- Land the raw export on its own sheet, untouched, and check its columns and row count on every refresh.
- Map account codes to your reporting lines in a table you can edit, not in formulas buried in cells.
- Build the numbers layer once, with period, actual, prior period and variance, and drive every chart off it.
- Put one dashboard sheet on top: headline figures, trend, and the variance table that people actually argue about.
- Make refresh a paste and one click, with a check that totals tie back to the export.

You get the workbook, the mapping table, the dashboard and a refresh note.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
