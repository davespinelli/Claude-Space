## Proposal

Raw sales figures are rarely chart ready. Dates arrive as text, the same product is spelled three ways, and subtotal rows sit in the middle of the detail. A chart built straight on top of that inherits every one of those problems, and nobody notices until somebody questions a number in a meeting and the total will not reconcile.

Plan:
- Clean first: parse the dates, settle on one name per product and region, separate detail rows from totals.
- Agree what each chart is meant to answer, because that decides the chart type rather than the other way round.
- Build on a pivot layer so next month's figures drop in and everything refreshes without rework.
- Keep the formatting consistent across charts and readable when printed in black and white.
- Leave the cleaning steps visible in the workbook so any number can be traced back to the raw row.

You get one workbook with raw, cleaned, pivot and chart sheets, plus a short page on refreshing it next month.
