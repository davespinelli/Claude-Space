## Proposal

Pulling records out of several online forms by hand has one failure that costs more than slow typing: the same response gets copied twice on a later pass, or one that arrived mid pass gets missed, and nobody can tell which. Once duplicates are in, no total from the sheet can be trusted.

Plan:
- Map the fields in each form to one common set of columns, so responses from different forms land in the same shape.
- Check first whether the forms have an export or a sheet link, since if they do the transfer runs itself and you stop paying for it.
- Transfer each response with its form name and submitted date, so every row can be traced back.
- Match on a stable key so a rerun updates rather than duplicates, and flag rows the forms left blank.
- Reconcile the counts per form against the sheet before handover.

You receive the populated sheet, the field mapping, and the repeatable process.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which platform are the forms on, and is this a one time transfer or an ongoing daily one?
