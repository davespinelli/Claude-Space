## Proposal

Accuracy in a keying job comes from the sheet, not from care alone. If the date column accepts free text, dates arrive in three formats. If status is typed rather than chosen, you end up with Active, active and ACTIVE as three values. Setting the workbook up before entry starts is what keeps the result clean, and it takes an hour.

Plan:
- Agree the columns, what each means, which are required and what a blank is allowed to mean.
- Set up the workbook first: data types, dropdown lists for fixed values, and date and number formats.
- Enter the records in batches, then re-check a sample of each batch against the source.
- Flag anything ambiguous or unreadable in the source rather than guessing at it.
- Leave the validation in place so later entries cannot break the format.

You get the completed workbook, a list of anything I could not read or resolve, and the sheet set up for whoever adds to it next.

Proof of how I work, public repo and live pipeline: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
