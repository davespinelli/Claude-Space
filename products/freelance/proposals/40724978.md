## Proposal

Unmatched invoices and partial payments are usually the same problem seen twice. One payment settles three invoices, or one invoice is paid across two payments with a deduction taken, so nothing agrees one to one on amount and a plain lookup leaves both sides open. Matching on amount alone then creates false pairs, because two invoices for the same figure will happily match the wrong payment.

Plan:
- Agree the matching rules first: reference, date window, tolerance for rounding and deductions.
- Match in passes, exact reference first, then amount and date, then many to one groupings.
- Leave the genuinely unresolved items on one exceptions sheet with what is known about each.
- Reconcile the totals per vendor: opening balance, invoices, payments and closing balance have to tie.
- Build the matching as a repeatable sheet, so next month is a refresh rather than a rebuild.

Deliverables: the reconciled ledger, the exceptions sheet and a per vendor summary.
