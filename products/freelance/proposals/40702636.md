## Proposal

Moving expense figures out of one spreadsheet and into another is where category totals quietly stop matching. The line items all transfer, but a category gets spelled two ways, a credit note keeps its minus sign in one sheet and loses it in the other, and the destination total is off by an amount nobody can trace back to a row.

Plan:
- Agree the destination layout and the category list first, so every expense line has exactly one place to go.
- Transfer the lines with the date, category, amount and reference kept together on one row.
- Keep signs and currency as they are in the source, so credits and refunds stay negative.
- Reconcile each category total and the grand total against the source before handover.
- List anything whose category is unclear rather than filing it under the nearest match.

You get the completed destination sheet, a reconciliation tab and a queries list.

Proof of how I work: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
