## Proposal

A steady flow of notes is not a batch job, it is a routine, and routines fail at the handover rather than at the typing. A slip that arrives twice, or that was photographed and then never marked as done, is how the same expense lands in the sheet twice or not at all. Handwriting accuracy is the visible risk. Intake tracking is the one that costs money.

Plan:
- Set up a simple intake: every item gets a reference number and a status before anything is typed.
- Agree the column list per note type, since a receipt and a jotting need different fields.
- Enter each batch against that structure, keeping the original image or scan linked to its row.
- Flag anything genuinely unreadable as a query rather than guessing at it.
- Reconcile weekly: items received against rows entered, so nothing sits unprocessed.

One condition up front: for handwritten items I want to see a sample before committing to the rate, because legibility decides the effort.
