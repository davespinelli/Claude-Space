## Proposal

Scans and native PDFs look like one job but they are two. Text based PDFs copy across exactly and can be automated with no reading errors at all. Scans go through OCR, where the accuracy depends on the image quality, so they need a checking pass that the native files do not. Pricing and planning them the same way is where these projects usually slip.

Plan:
- Split the batch into native PDFs and scans on day one and tell you how many fall in each group.
- Convert the native files by script, preserving headings, tables and numeric types.
- OCR the scans, then check every page against the image and correct rather than accept the first output.
- Keep one consistent template so every Word document and every sheet comes back in the same shape.
- Flag anything genuinely illegible instead of guessing at it.

You receive the Word and Excel files, a list of any flagged items with page references, and a summary of what came from OCR.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many documents and pages in total, and how many are scans rather than native PDFs?
