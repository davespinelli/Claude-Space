## Proposal

Text records that have to land in both Excel and Word normally get typed twice, and from the first correction onward the two copies disagree. The safer shape is to key once into the spreadsheet, which is the structured copy, and generate the Word document from those same rows, so one fix lands in both and the two never drift.

Plan:
- Agree the Excel columns first, since that decides how each record gets split into fields.
- Key once into the spreadsheet, with consistent handling of blanks, dates and name order.
- Produce the Word document from those rows in your formatting and heading style.
- Check a sample twice, independently, and report the measured error rate rather than claiming zero.
- Send a list of anything ambiguous or illegible instead of guessing at it.

Deliverables: the Excel workbook, the formatted Word document built from it, and the query list.

Two questions: roughly how many records, and what form is the source in, typed text, PDF or scans? And does the Word document need to follow a specific template?
