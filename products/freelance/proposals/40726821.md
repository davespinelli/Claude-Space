## Proposal

Recruitment and study abroad are two audiences with different questions, different seasons and different compliance exposure, so one agentic posting system serving both needs the two streams separated at the content layer rather than merged and filtered later. The other thing that decides whether it survives is the approval step: fully autonomous posting on an education brand is how a wrong intake date or a misquoted fee goes out under your name.

Plan:
- Separate recruitment and study abroad into their own content streams, with their own prompts and calendars.
- Generate drafts against a brief you control, with every claim about dates, fees or requirements drawn from a source file, not the model.
- Put a human approval queue between generation and publishing, with one click to approve, edit or reject.
- Publish through each platform's official API, from your own business accounts only.
- Add a dashboard for what went out, what is queued, and what each post earned in reach and replies.

Deliverables: the system, the approval dashboard, a runbook, and two weeks of support after handover.
