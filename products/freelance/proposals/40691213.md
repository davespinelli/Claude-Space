## Proposal

You need mixed numeric and text records moved out of an online database into a structured spreadsheet. The trap in that job is the mixed columns: an ID that is sometimes numeric and sometimes has a letter prefix, a quantity field holding "n/a", and dates in two formats. Excel will silently coerce those, and the errors only show up later when someone sorts or sums the column.

Plan:
- Agree the target column layout and the type rule for every field before anything is entered.
- Pull the records in batches, keeping one row per record and the source identifier intact.
- Validate each column against its rule: type, allowed values, required fields, duplicates.
- Return anything that fails validation as a separate exceptions sheet rather than guessing.
- Deliver the workbook with the rules documented on one page.

You receive the structured workbook, the exceptions sheet, and one revision round.
