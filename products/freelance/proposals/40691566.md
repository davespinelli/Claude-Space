## Proposal

You want confirmation that your read only charting app matches TradingView on candlesticks, moving averages, volume and the oscillators. The mismatches in this kind of app are almost never in the drawing code. They are in the inputs: an indicator warm up period off by one bar, an EMA seeded differently from the first close, a session boundary that shifts the newest candle, or volume aggregated on a different bar edge. Eyeballing two screens will not tell you which.

Plan:
- Agree a test matrix with you: symbols, timeframes, indicator settings and the tolerance you accept.
- Compare side by side at matching crosshair positions, with screenshots for each combination.
- Recompute the indicators independently in Python from the same OHLCV, so a difference points at the data or the formula rather than at my eyes.
- Work the interaction checklist: zoom, pan, crosshair readout, timeframe switch, resize, stale and empty data.
- Log each issue with steps to reproduce, expected versus actual values, and a severity.

You receive the completed checklist, a ranked issue list, and the comparison notebook.

Proof: I run a public market dashboard and backtest engine, https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how do I get access to the app, and which symbols and timeframes matter most to you?
