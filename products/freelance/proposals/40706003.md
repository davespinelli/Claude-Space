## Proposal

Pine Script runs inside a TradingView chart. It can paint signals and it can fill in the strategy tester, but it cannot place an order, hold a position across a restart, or reconcile against what the broker thinks you own. That gap is where automated funds lose money, and it is normally found live rather than in testing.

Plan:
- Restate the Pine rules as a Python backtest with realistic costs, weights set at close and applied next bar, so there is no look ahead.
- Compare that backtest against your Pine results and explain any difference before anything is built on top.
- Build the execution layer on the broker API: order manager, position and exposure caps, kill switch, daily reconciliation against broker state.
- Run it on paper for two weeks with a daily report and a written runbook.
- Go live on your key only, with the risk limits you set.

You get the backtest and report, the bot source, the runbook, and the paper trading log.

Public engine, execution bot and live pipeline: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
