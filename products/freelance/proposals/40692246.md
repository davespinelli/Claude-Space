## Proposal

Your brief covers a full stack build, and the piece that decides whether the rest works is the OCR. If a challan number or an amount is read wrong once in fifty documents, the billing on top of it is wrong too, and nobody notices until a customer disputes an invoice. At this budget I would rather do that layer properly than spread thin.

Plan:
- Agree the challan fields to capture and what a correct extraction looks like on ten real samples.
- Build the extraction pipeline: deskew, OCR, then field parsing with validation per field.
- Score every document for confidence and send anything doubtful to a review queue instead of into billing.
- Expose the output as a clean API or CSV your billing layer consumes, with duplicate detection on challan number.
- Report accuracy measured against the sample set, so you know the real error rate before going live.

You receive the extraction service, the review queue, the test set with measured accuracy, and a README.

Public example of my pipeline work: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space

Two questions: how many challans arrive per day, and is the billing side an existing system I integrate with or part of the build?
