## Proposal

Raw text documents are quick to type up and hard to clean consistently. The word "lightly" usually hides a set of small decisions: whether to keep a trailing note inside a name field, how to write a date that appears three different ways, what to do with a record missing one value. Taken row by row those decisions drift, and the sheet ends up inconsistent in a way nobody notices until it gets sorted or filtered.

Plan:
- Read a sample of your documents first and write the cleaning rules out as a short list you approve.
- Fix the column list and the type each field holds, so dates stay dates and codes stay text.
- Enter the records against those rules, keeping the source document noted per row.
- Check for duplicates, blank required fields and values that break their column type.

You get the finished workbook, the rules list and a queries list.

Proof of how I work: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
