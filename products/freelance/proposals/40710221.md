## Proposal

Customer workbooks go wrong in a predictable order. The same customer appears three times under slightly different spellings, phone numbers carry a mix of formats, dates are text in half the rows, and then a lookup silently returns nothing because of a trailing space. Tidying the visible mess without fixing the shape means you are back here in a month.

Plan:
- Standardise the fields first: names, addresses, phone formats, dates as real dates, consistent casing.
- Find duplicate customers by normalised match rather than by exact string, and give you the merge list to approve before anything is merged.
- Put the workbook on one clean table with validation on the entry columns, so new rows cannot reintroduce the same errors.
- Add the summary view you actually use: counts, status breakdown, and recent activity.
- Hand over a short sheet of the rules applied, so the next person maintaining it knows what is enforced.

This stays inside your own records. I do not add contact details sourced from anywhere else.

Work I run in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many customer rows, and should suspected duplicates be merged by me or only flagged for you?

David
