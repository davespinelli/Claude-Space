## Proposal

Your brief lists five tasks, and four of them are steady work. The one that decides the job is PDF to Excel. A PDF has no columns, only text at positions on a page, so a table continuing onto a second page, or a cell whose text wraps, comes out as split or merged rows. Every value looks right on its own, which is why proofreading does not catch it.

Plan:
- Extract PDF tables by script and flag every row whose column count differs from the header, instead of accepting it silently.
- Format the Word documents through named styles, so a later change to a heading applies everywhere at once.
- Agree the cleaning rules first: date format, name order, and whether a blank means zero or unknown.
- Reconcile each batch on row counts and column totals against the source.
- Return an exception list for anything ambiguous rather than entering a guess.

Deliverables: the formatted documents, the workbook, the exception list, and the extraction script.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many pages of PDF, and are they text or scans? And is there a template the Word documents must match?
