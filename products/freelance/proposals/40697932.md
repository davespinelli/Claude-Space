## Proposal

With investment transactions the date on the document is not always the date that governs. Trade date and settlement date differ by a few days, and mixing them moves transactions across month and year boundaries, so the period totals disagree with the statement even though every row was typed correctly.

Plan:
- Agree which date governs, trade or settlement, and the category list, before any entry starts.
- Enter each transaction with an explicit type: buy, sell, dividend, interest, fee, transfer, since the type drives every total downstream.
- Keep amounts as numbers with one currency column, and keep fees in their own column rather than netted into the trade amount.
- Reconcile per month and per account against the source, and show you the lines that disagree instead of forcing them to balance.
- Put anything ambiguous on a queries list rather than assigning a category by guess.

You receive the completed workbook, the monthly and per account reconciliation, and the queries list.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many transactions and what period do they cover, and do you already have a category list I should follow?
