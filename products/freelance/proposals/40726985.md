## Proposal

Bullet lists are the hardest thing to OCR into a table, because a bullet has no columns. Where the label ends and the number begins has to be inferred line by line, and the errors that do the damage are digit substitutions inside the numbers. A 1 read as a 7, a 0 as an O, a 5 as an S. Those pass every eye check, because the cell reads like a number.

Plan:
- Tune the scan handling to your actual pages rather than to defaults, since contrast decides most of the accuracy.
- Split each bullet into label and value by rule, so the same split applies on every page.
- Flag every figure the reader was not confident about and every line that did not parse, rather than writing a guess into the sheet.
- Check the digits against any printed subtotal the report already carries.
- Deliver the clean sheet with the flagged lines beside it.

Deliverables: the Excel file, the flagged exception list, and the conversion script.
