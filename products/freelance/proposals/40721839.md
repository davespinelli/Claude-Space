## Proposal

Customer information is the data where small errors get expensive on a delay. A mistyped email or a second record for a customer you already have costs nothing today, and costs you a lost customer six months from now when the invoice or the renewal notice goes nowhere.

Plan:
- Agree the field rules first: what is required, the format for phone and email, and what happens to an incomplete record.
- Enter the records against those rules daily, checking for an existing match before creating anything new.
- Validate what a machine can validate: email format, phone length, postcode against city, obvious duplicates.
- Keep a short query list for records I will not guess at, instead of filling the gap and moving on.
- Send a daily note: entered, queried, duplicates found and merged or left for you.

Where the work turns out to be repetitive, I script it, and the per record time drops rather than staying flat forever.

Proof of my automation work: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
