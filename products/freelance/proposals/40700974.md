## Proposal

Since you already have the store list, this is a matching job before it is a collection job. The hard part is deciding when a found record is the same store as the one on your sheet: a branch that trades under a slightly different name, two locations on the same street, a chain outlet. Get that wrong and you fill gaps with the wrong store's details, which is worse than leaving them blank.

Plan:
- Agree which fields you need filled, and the confidence bar for accepting a match.
- Match each of your rows against publicly listed business information, keeping the source URL for every value.
- Fill only high-confidence matches, and mark the rest for your review rather than guessing.
- Flag conflicts where your existing detail disagrees with what is published.
- Return your original sheet with new columns added, so nothing you already hold is overwritten.

To be clear on scope: I collect business-level store details, not named individuals or personal contact data.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many stores are on the list, and which fields are the incomplete ones?
