## Proposal

Taking a prototype live and adding a chatbot are two jobs, and the chatbot is the one that usually disappoints. A model with no grounding answers confidently and wrongly about your own product, and every wrong answer reaches a real visitor. The fix is not a better prompt, it is deciding up front what the bot is allowed to answer from and what it must hand off.

Plan:
- Review the prototype and list what has to change to run as a live site rather than a demo.
- Agree the bot's source material: your pages, a FAQ document, or a product list, and keep answers grounded in it.
- Build the chat endpoint with the API key server side, never in the browser, with rate limiting on the endpoint.
- Add a fallback path so an out of scope question gets a handoff, not an invented answer.
- Deploy, then log real conversations for a few days and tune the gaps that show up.

You receive the live site, the chat integration, the source config and a README.
