## Proposal

The two things you want pull in opposite directions. Instant answers need the bot held tightly to your own content, while capturing enquiries needs it to ask questions back. Builds that try to do both in one flow end up doing neither well, so I would keep them as separate behaviours triggered by what the visitor is actually doing.

Plan:
- Index your existing pages and documents so every answer is grounded in your own wording, with nothing invented about pricing or availability.
- Trigger enquiry capture on intent, not on every message, and pass the details straight to wherever you already work.
- Hand off to email or a form whenever the question falls outside the indexed content.
- Log every conversation so you can see which questions your site is not answering yet.
- Match the widget to your branding and test it on mobile.

You receive the embedded widget, the indexing script, the conversation log with a weekly summary, and a guide for updating answers yourself.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: where should captured enquiries land, email, a sheet or a CRM, and which pages should the bot answer from?
