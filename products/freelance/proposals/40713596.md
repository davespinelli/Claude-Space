## Proposal

Two fields is a small scrape. What breaks it is the day their page changes and the job still finishes green with an empty file, so your price history has a hole in it that you notice a week later.

Plan:
- Pull title and price daily from the pages you name, one row per product per day.
- Parse the price properly: currency, decimal format, discounted versus list, out of stock stored as empty and not as zero.
- Append to a dated CSV or a Google Sheet, so you keep history instead of overwriting yesterday.
- Fail loudly: if a run returns fewer rows than expected or a selector stops matching, you get an email and the old data stays untouched.
- Run it on a schedule in the cloud, so nothing depends on your machine being on.

You get the scraper, the scheduled job, the history file and the source.

Pipeline I run in public on GitHub Actions: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
