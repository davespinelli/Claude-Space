## Proposal

Ranking a score sheet is simple until the ties arrive, and with a class of any size they always do. Two students on the same mark can share rank 2 and push the next to 4, or share rank 2 and let the next take 3. Both are defensible, they give different answers, and parents notice. The same goes for a missing score, which is either a zero or an exclusion, and for whether you rank inside each subject or on the total.

Plan:
- Agree the three rules first: tie handling, missing scores, and rank within subject or overall.
- Build the ranking so it recalculates when you edit a mark, rather than as pasted values.
- Add both views, a per-subject table and an overall table with totals and averages.
- Highlight ties and missing entries so they are visible rather than buried.
- Check the output by hand against a sample of rows before handover.

You get the working file, the rules written on a notes tab, and a short how-to for next term.
