## Proposal

For a brand-new site, the risk is not the conversation, it is what the agent says when it does not know. An agent with no grounded source will answer confidently about pricing, availability or policy you never wrote, and on a launch site that is the first impression. So the build starts with your content as a retrieval source and an explicit refusal path, then the voice layer on top of the same brain.

Plan:
- Ingest your pages, PDFs and FAQ into a small vector index, rebuilt whenever content changes.
- One answering service both channels call, so text and voice never drift apart.
- Voice with streaming speech-to-text and text-to-speech, barge-in supported, with a handoff to a human or a booking link.
- Answer only from your sources, say so plainly when it cannot, and log every unanswered question.
- Widget embedded on your site, plus a dashboard of conversations and gaps.

You receive the service, the widget, the ingest script, deployment and a README.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
