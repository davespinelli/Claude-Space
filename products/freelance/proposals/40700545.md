## Proposal

Copying financial figures out of a portal by eye goes wrong in one specific place: a figure that is correct on its own line but does not add to the subtotal shown on the same screen. If nobody checks totals as they go, the error surfaces weeks later at reconciliation.

Plan:
- Agree the exact screens and fields first, so one portal record maps to one spreadsheet row with the same date and reference.
- Check every batch against the totals the portal itself displays, and report any gap rather than adjusting it.
- Keep a queries list for anything ambiguous, so nothing is guessed.
- Flag duplicates and missing periods by reference, not by scanning.
- If the portal offers an export or a report download, use that instead and reconcile it, which is faster and cheaper for you.

You get the completed spreadsheet, the totals check, the queries list and a note on the mapping used.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: does the portal have any export or report download, and how many records and periods are in scope?
