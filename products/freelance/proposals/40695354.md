## Proposal

Portfolio workbooks usually stay correct until the first corporate action or the first partial sale. Then the cost basis stops matching the units held, dividends get counted as income in one sheet and as return in another, and the yearly figure is quietly wrong in a way no amount of re-typing finds.

Plan:
- Read your existing workbooks as they are and write down the rules you have actually been using for cost basis, dividends and realised gains.
- Set one transaction sheet as the single record: date, security, units, price, fees, type of event.
- Derive holdings, cost basis, realised and unrealised gains and income from that sheet with formulas, so nothing is typed twice.
- Compute returns properly, money weighted for your contributions and time weighted for comparison against an index.
- Reconcile the output against the totals you already trust and show you any line that disagrees.

You receive the rebuilt workbook, the reconciliation sheet, and a short note on how to enter each event type.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many workbooks and roughly how many transactions in total, and do you need currency conversion for any holdings?
