## Proposal

One PDF per patient turning into one row per patient sounds mechanical until a field is missing. A blank cell then means two different things: the form did not ask, or the form asked and nobody answered. In patient records those are not the same, and once they are both blank in the sheet there is no way back to which it was without opening the file again.

Plan:
- Agree the column list from a few sample files before touching the batch.
- Extract with a distinct marker for not present on the form and not filled in, rather than one blank.
- Carry a file reference column on every row so any value can be traced to its source PDF.
- Check the batch for duplicate patients, dates outside plausible ranges and rows short of required fields.
- Delete the source files from my side once you confirm the sheet, and confirm in writing.

You get the workbook, the query list for anything unreadable, and the column rules.
