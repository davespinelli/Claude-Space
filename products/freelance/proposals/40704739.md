## Proposal

Moving the text is the quick half. The time goes on everything attached to it: internal links still pointing at the old addresses, formatting that survives in one system and collapses in the other, and pages whose URL changes so every existing link to them breaks. A migration that copies the words and ignores those three leaves you finding the damage weeks later.

Plan:
- Inventory every page first, and agree what moves as is, what merges, and what is dropped.
- Extract the text with its structure kept, headings and lists intact, not as one flat block.
- Rewrite internal links to the new addresses and list any that point at something no longer there.
- Load into a staging area first so you can compare a sample side by side before anything goes live.
- Hand over the old address to new address map, which is what you need to set up redirects.

You get the migrated content, the address map, and a list of pages that need a decision from you.

A pipeline I run publicly: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: what is the content sitting in now and where is it going, and roughly how many pages are there?
