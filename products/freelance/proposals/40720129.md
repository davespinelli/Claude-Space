## Proposal

Qualification bots rarely fail at the conversation. They fail at the handoff. The agent holds a decent chat, decides the lead is good, then drops it into a form nobody checks that day, so a warm lead goes cold while the bot reports success. The second failure is qualifying on questions the sales side never agreed to, which produces a full pipeline of leads nobody wants to call.

Plan:
- Write the qualification rules down first: the three or four facts that decide whether a lead is worth a call.
- Build the agent to collect those facts conversationally and to stop early when an answer disqualifies.
- Log every conversation as a row: answers, score, transcript and timestamp, so you can check its judgement.
- Route qualified leads into wherever your sales side already works, with an email fallback if that write fails.
- Allow a handover to a person at any point, because people ask questions a bot should not answer.

Deliverables: the agent, the rules file, the lead log, and a short page on changing the questions yourself.
