## Proposal

Entering figures into a template you already built has one real risk: the template has formulas and totals in it, and a value pasted into the wrong row or as text instead of a number breaks a total quietly. The sheet still looks finished, and the error shows up later in a number you rely on.

Plan:
- Read your template first and note which cells are inputs and which are calculated, so nothing gets typed over a formula.
- Enter income and expense lines in the categories your template already uses, keeping amounts as numbers and dates as dates.
- Flag anything in the source that does not map cleanly to one of your categories rather than filing it somewhere convenient.
- Check the result: totals recompute, no blank required rows, no duplicates, signs consistent for income against expense.
- Return a short note listing the items I flagged and where I put them.

You get your own template filled and checked, returned in xlsx.

Proof of my working style: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
