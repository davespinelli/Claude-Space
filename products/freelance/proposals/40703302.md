## Proposal

Entering expenses exactly as they appear in the source is the right instruction, and it is also the one most people quietly break. A vendor name gets tidied, a date gets reformatted, an amount with a footnote gets rounded, and then the sheet no longer ties back to the document it came from.

Plan:
- Transcribe each line as written, including vendor spelling and reference numbers, so every row can be traced back to its source.
- Keep amounts as numbers and dates as real dates, since that is formatting rather than changing the value.
- Where the source is genuinely unclear, a smudged figure or a missing category, list it for you instead of guessing.
- Reconcile the entered total against the source total for each month and report any gap.
- Deliver a clean monthly layout that filters and pivots without further cleanup.

You get the workbook, a per-month total check, and the short list of anything ambiguous.
