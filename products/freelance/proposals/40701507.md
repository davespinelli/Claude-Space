## Proposal

Long only swing systems on Indian equities break in two places unrelated to the signal itself. First, price history: if splits and bonuses are not adjusted, the backtest shows a jump that never happened and the rule looks better than it is. Second, Zerodha itself, where the access token expires daily, so a bot that ran fine on Monday is quietly logged out on Tuesday.

Plan:
- Write your entry, exit and sizing rules as a testable Python module, decided at close and acted on next session.
- Backtest on adjusted daily bars with brokerage, STT and slippage, holding the universe and liquidity filter to what was knowable on the signal date.
- Build the Kite Connect order layer with position caps, exposure limits and a daily token refresh that fails loudly.
- Reconcile broker positions against expected holdings every morning and log every decision to CSV.
- Run it at small size before it carries full capital.

You get the backtest report, the live system and the source.

Proof: my public engine and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
