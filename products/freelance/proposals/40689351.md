## Proposal

Most support chatbots fail in the same way. They answer confidently from a document that was superseded six months ago, or they answer from nothing at all and invent the policy. Retrieval quality, not the model, is what decides this. So I build the retrieval and its evaluation first, and wire the chat interface on top once the right passages are actually coming back.

Plan:
- Ingest your documents with chunking that respects headings, and keep the source and section on every chunk.
- Build the vector index, then test retrieval against a set of real questions you supply and report the hit rate.
- Ground every answer in retrieved passages, cite the source document, and say plainly when the answer is not in the material.
- Add a refresh script so updated documents can be reindexed without rebuilding anything.
- Log unanswered questions, so you can see what the knowledge base is missing.

You receive the app, the ingestion and refresh scripts, the retrieval test set and a README.
