## Proposal

There are two very different jobs hiding behind this title. If your PDFs still carry live form fields, the values come out exactly as typed and the work is mapping fields to columns. If they were flattened or scanned, it is OCR, accuracy depends on scan quality, and the honest deliverable includes a confidence flag rather than a promise of perfection.

Plan:
- Check a sample first and tell you which of the two you have before anything else is agreed.
- Extract field by field into one row per form, keeping the source filename on every row.
- Handle checkboxes, dates and multi page forms as explicit rules rather than guesses.
- Flag low confidence and missing fields, so a person reviews only the rows that need it.
- Deliver as Excel with a summary sheet, plus the script so you can run the next batch yourself.

You get the extractor, the Excel output and the source.

Pipeline I run in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many PDFs are in the batch, and can you send two sample files so I can confirm whether they have live form fields?
