## Proposal

A dashboard built straight off raw production logs tends to look right and total wrong, and the cause sits in the log rather than in the charts. Shifts that cross midnight put output in the wrong day. A downtime row overlapping another gets counted twice. The same line or part is written three ways, so a filter silently drops real output. Those three have to be settled before any tile is drawn.

Plan:
- Agree the definitions first: when a shift starts, how downtime is counted, what one output unit is.
- Put a cleaning step between the raw log sheet and the dashboard, so refreshing is a single paste.
- Standardise line, shift and part labels through a lookup table you can edit yourself.
- Drive the tiles from PivotTables over the cleaned data so they move with the new log.
- Show a reconciliation figure on the dashboard, total logged units against total charted.

Deliverables: the workbook with raw, cleaned and dashboard sheets, the lookup table, and refresh instructions.

Two questions: which four or five numbers have to be on the front page? And do your shifts cross midnight?
