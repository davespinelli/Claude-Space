## Proposal

Daily entry and reconciliation goes wrong in a predictable place. The bank feed and the entered transactions drift apart by a few items, nobody notices, and by month end the difference is thirty lines old and expensive to unpick. Daily only helps if the reconciliation happens daily too.

Plan:
- Agree a daily cut off and a fixed checklist, so the same work happens in the same order every day.
- Enter each day's transactions in Reckon One against your existing chart of accounts. I will not invent a new code without asking.
- Reconcile the bank daily and keep a running list of anything unmatched, with the reason it did not match.
- Send a one page daily note: entered, reconciled, unmatched, and what is waiting on a decision from you.
- At week end, reconciliation status by account, so nothing carries quietly into the next month.

You get the daily work, the daily note, and an unmatched register that stays short.

Proof that I run scheduled processes reliably: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many transactions a day, and is your Reckon One bank feed already connected or are statements uploaded?

David
