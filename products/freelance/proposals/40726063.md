## Proposal

A dashboard built over Xero plus spreadsheets usually disagrees with Xero itself within a month, and the cause is almost never the charts. It is that the two sides count different things. Xero reports accrual by invoice date, the spreadsheet tracks cash as it landed, so the same month has two defensible answers and the dashboard quietly picks one. Once a director sees a tile that contradicts a report they trust, they stop opening the dashboard.

Plan:
- Pin the basis before anything is built: which figures are accrual, which are cash, and label every tile with it.
- Pull from the Xero API on a schedule instead of manual exports, so a refresh is one action.
- Map the spreadsheet accounts to your Xero chart of accounts once, in a file you can edit.
- Reconcile to a named Xero report on every refresh and show the difference rather than hiding it.
- Keep the data pull separate from the presentation layer.

Deliverables: the dashboard, the scheduled refresh, the account mapping, and the reconciliation view.
