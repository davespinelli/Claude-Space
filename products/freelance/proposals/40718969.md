## Proposal

Inventory and supply chain work rarely fails for want of an agent. It fails because reorder points are guesses, lead times are remembered rather than measured, and nobody sees a stockout coming until it happens. An agent on top of that just automates the guess. So the first job is to get your own history into one place and measure demand and lead time from it.

Plan:
- Pull your stock movements and purchase history out of whatever system holds them today.
- Measure demand variability and actual supplier lead times per item, not per category.
- Compute reorder points and order quantities from those numbers, with a service level you choose.
- Put an agent on top that drafts purchase suggestions and explains each one in a line you can check.
- Send a daily exception report: items about to run out, overdue orders, and suppliers slipping.

You receive the pipeline, the reorder model, the agent and a README, running on a schedule.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which system holds your stock and purchase data, and roughly how many active items are there?
