## Proposal

Two fields per receipt sounds like the easy version of this job, and the date is why it is not. A receipt often carries several dates, the transaction, the card authorisation and the print time, and the total sits next to subtotal, tax and tendered amount. Picking the wrong one across a few hundred receipts stays invisible until someone reconciles against a bank statement.

Plan:
- Take a sample across your receipt formats and agree which line counts as the date and which as the total.
- Extract with OCR for images, and read the text layer directly where a PDF has one, which is more accurate.
- Pull the date and total with explicit rules, normalising currency and date format to one standard.
- Attach the source filename to every row so any figure can be traced back.
- Flag low-confidence reads into a separate review list rather than writing an uncertain number as fact.

You receive the CSV, the review list, and the extraction script so future batches run at no further cost.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
