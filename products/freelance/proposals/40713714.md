## Proposal

A decision assistant is only as good as the range model behind it. Most builds reach for a neural net and end up with advice nobody can check, when the thing that makes a tool worth opening is being able to ask why it folded and get an answer in ranges, equity and pot odds rather than a confidence number.

Plan:
- Hand state entry: position, stack depths, board, action history.
- Equity engine: Monte Carlo against a weighted opponent range, exact enumeration on the river.
- Preflop ranges by position and stack depth, held in files you can edit rather than hard coded.
- Decision output: fold, call or raise with a size, shown next to the equity and the pot odds it came from.
- Windows build with an installer, plus a hand log you can review afterwards.

You get the engine, the desktop app, the range files and the source.

Public repo and pipeline: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Straight on scope: I build this as a standalone tool you enter hands into. I do not build screen reading or auto play against a live poker client.

Two questions: cash game or tournament ranges first, and manual entry only?
