## Proposal

Mixed text and numeric rows fail in a quiet way. The numbers arrive as text, dates land in two or three different formats, and the sheet looks finished until someone sorts a column or sums it and gets nonsense. Under 1,000 rows is small enough that the typing is not the work: getting every column to hold one consistent type is the work.

Plan:
- Agree the column list first, and for each column its type, format and whether it can be blank.
- Enter the rows, keeping the original wording for text and typed values for numbers and dates.
- Run a check pass over the result: type per column, out-of-range numbers, duplicate keys, blanks that should not be blank.
- Send you the flagged rows as a short list rather than guessing what they should say.
- Deliver the workbook with the raw entry on one tab and the clean typed table on another.

You receive the workbook, the check report, and any queries listed row by row.

Proof: my public repo and live data pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: what form is the source in, and what column layout do you want on the output?
