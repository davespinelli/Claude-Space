## Proposal

Daily entry into a sales workbook goes wrong in a predictable way. It is not the typing. It is the day someone enters a date as text, or repeats yesterday's row, or the workbook grows to the point where nobody can tell whether last week's numbers are complete. By the time a monthly total looks odd, the mistake is weeks back.

Plan:
- Look at your workbook as it stands and agree the exact columns and what one row means.
- Add validation so bad dates, duplicate rows and impossible amounts are refused at entry.
- Set up a short intake sheet so a day's sales are pasted or typed once and land formatted.
- Add a running check tab: rows per day, daily and monthly totals, gaps flagged.
- Do the daily entry myself for the first week so the process is proven before you rely on it.

You receive the upgraded workbook, the validation and check tabs, and a one page routine for whoever runs it.
