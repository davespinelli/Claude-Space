## Proposal

Tables inside PDFs break in predictable ways: a row that spills onto the next page loses its header, merged cells collapse into one column, and a negative number in brackets becomes text. Generic converters produce something that looks right and totals wrong, so I check the totals.

Plan:
- Extract with pdfplumber, tuned per document layout rather than one setting for everything.
- Rebuild multi page tables so continuation rows keep their headers.
- Convert numbers properly, including bracketed negatives, thousands separators and trailing symbols.
- Reconcile each extracted table against any total printed in the PDF and flag mismatches.
- Output one clean Excel workbook, one sheet per table or one stacked sheet, whichever you prefer.

You receive the workbook, the extraction script so future PDFs cost you nothing, and a short note on anything that needed a judgement call.

Example of my Python data pipeline work, public repo: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space

Two questions: how many PDFs and are they text based or scanned images, and do you want one sheet per file or everything stacked?
