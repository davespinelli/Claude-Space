## Proposal

A steady flow of five page documents is a different job from a one off batch. What matters is the cost per document after the first week, and what happens to the ones that do not fit the usual layout. On accuracy the risk is narrow and specific: OCR substitutes characters inside numbers, 0 for O, 1 for l, 8 for B. A wrong digit in an amount still reads as a real value, so nothing downstream flags it.

Plan:
- Sort the incoming documents into the two or three layouts they actually fall into.
- Set the Word output up as a template, so headings and tables land in the same structure every time.
- Extract the Excel fields by position and label, not by reading order alone.
- Validate what can be checked: totals, dates in range, reference formats, and flag the rest.
- Return anything unreadable as a short query list rather than a guess.

Deliverables: the converted Word and Excel files, the templates, and a per document rate for the ongoing flow.
