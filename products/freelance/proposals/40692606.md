## Proposal

Your brief lists the views you want: revenue, orders, category, occasion, top cities, monthly trend, top products and order time patterns. That is a lot of cuts of one order table, and in Excel the risk is not the charts, it is that each view is built off a slightly different filtered range. Six months later the revenue tile and the monthly trend disagree and no one can say which is right.

Plan:
- Take your raw order export and build one clean fact table with the fields every view needs.
- Derive the cuts from that single table with pivots, so every number traces to the same source rows.
- Build the dashboard sheet: headline tiles, monthly trend, category and occasion splits, top cities and products, and an order time by hour view.
- Add slicers for date range and category so you explore without editing formulas.
- Add a refresh routine: drop in a new export, refresh, and every view updates.

You receive the workbook, the dashboard and a one page refresh note.

Live example of a dashboard I run: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: can you send a sample of the raw order data with its column names, and do you want this rebuilt on your own sales data or a template you fill in?
