## Proposal

A balance sheet transcribed by hand fails differently from ordinary data entry. The sheet still looks finished, but a subtotal no longer foots and assets no longer equal liabilities plus equity, and nothing on the page says so. Speed is not the thing worth buying here. The check that catches one transposed digit is.

Plan:
- Agree the workbook layout first: one column per period, consistent line item labels down the left.
- Transcribe the files into that layout, keeping your own account names rather than renaming them.
- Recompute every subtotal and total from the line items instead of copying the printed figure across.
- Test assets against liabilities plus equity for each period and flag anything that does not tie rather than forcing it.
- Return a short query list for anything illegible or inconsistent, instead of guessing at it.

You get the workbook, a check sheet showing each total recomputed beside the figure on the source, and the query list.
