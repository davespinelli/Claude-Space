## Proposal

Copying the text is the easy half. What decides whether the spreadsheet is usable is the categories, because a page rarely announces which one it belongs to and two people reading it will file it differently. Categories agreed after the fact means re-reading everything, so the list gets fixed first, on a real sample, with a rule for the cases that could go either way.

Plan:
- Take twenty pages across the sites, propose the category list, and agree it with you before the bulk run.
- Capture only text that is publicly visible on the page. No logins, and no personal contact details.
- Produce one row per item with the source URL and capture date, so anything can be checked back.
- Script the capture where a site's pages follow a pattern, and do the rest by hand.
- Spot-check a fixed share of rows against the live pages and report the error rate.

You get the spreadsheet, the category rules in writing, and the list of pages that did not fit.

A pipeline I run publicly: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many sites and roughly how many pages each, and do you already have a category list?

## Delivery plan

3. Day 3 to 4: run the capture and the manual pages, fill the categories.
4. Day 5 morning: spot-check against live pages, report the error rate.
5. Day 5: hand over the sheet with the rules and the unfitted pages listed.
