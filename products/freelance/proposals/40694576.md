## Proposal

120 years of match history in Postgres is the hard part, and you already have it. The risk here is the queries rather than the pages. Season tables, head to head records and career appearance counts across that span get slow the moment the site computes them on every page load, and that lookup is the first thing visitors do.

Plan:
- Agree the page list before any code: club overview, season pages, match pages, opponent head to head, player pages.
- Build a read only API over your database with no schema changes, so your data stays the single source.
- Precompute the heavy aggregates into summary tables refreshed on a schedule, so pages stay fast at any depth of history.
- Build the pages with search and filters by season, competition and opponent, plus charts for the long series such as goals and win rate per season.
- Deploy with a repeatable build and a written handover so you can load a new season yourself.

You receive the site, the API, the refresh job and the full source.
