## Proposal

Entering raw information into a series of spreadsheets by hand is slow, and the errors it produces are the expensive kind: a transposed digit that nobody notices until it is in a total. I do the same job with a script and then verify it, which is faster and checkable.

Plan:
- Agree the target sheet layout and the rules for each field before anything is entered.
- Extract and enter the data programmatically where the source allows it, by hand where it does not.
- Run a verification pass: totals, row counts, and value ranges compared back against the source.
- Put anything ambiguous on a queries tab instead of guessing at it.
- Return the workbook with consistent formatting and correct data types throughout.

You receive the completed spreadsheets, a short accuracy report showing what was checked, and the queries list.

Example of my Python data work, public and tested: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space

Two questions: what format is the raw information in, and roughly how many records are we talking about?
