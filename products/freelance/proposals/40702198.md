## Proposal

Copy and paste work is straightforward until the source and destination disagree on shape. One field arrives combined where the sheet wants it split, a date pastes as text, a trailing space makes two identical values sort apart. That is where accuracy is actually lost, not in the typing.

Plan:
- Do a small first batch, around fifty rows, and send it for your sign-off before the rest.
- Match the destination exactly: column order, splits, date and number formats, trimmed whitespace.
- Work through the full set in batches so you can see progress rather than waiting for one delivery.
- Run a check pass for duplicates, blank required fields and values that do not match their column type.
- Report anything the source leaves ambiguous instead of picking for you.

You get the completed sheet in Google Sheets or Excel, plus a short note of anything I flagged. If the source turns out to be structured enough to script, I will say so and it gets faster and cheaper, not more expensive.

Proof of my working style: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many rows, and where is the text being moved from?
