## Proposal

A workbook that takes a weekly CSV drop breaks on the week the export changes, not on the week the maths is wrong. The time system adds a column or lists the crew in a different order, and a sheet wired to cell positions keeps calculating and returns figures that are quietly wrong. Matching the import on header names, and refusing to run when an expected header is missing, is what makes it safe to use every Friday.

Plan:
- Import by header name, with a mapping you can edit when the export changes.
- Validate on load: missing headers, blank rates, hours outside a plausible range, duplicate entries.
- Put overtime and rounding rules in one visible settings block rather than buried inside formulas.
- Produce the per person pay summary and the per job breakdown from the same source rows.
- Leave the raw import sheet untouched so every pay figure traces back to its CSV row.

Deliverables: the workbook, the header mapping, the validation report and a one page guide for the weekly run.

Two questions: is overtime counted daily or weekly, and over what threshold? And can you send one real CSV export?
