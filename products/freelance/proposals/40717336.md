## Proposal

Duplicates are the part of this that goes wrong quietly. Exact duplicates are easy. The ones that cost you are near matches, the same customer with a trailing space, a different capitalisation, or a name entered two ways, and a blind dedupe either leaves them in or merges two records that were never the same thing.

Plan:
- Agree with you what makes two rows the same record before removing anything.
- Normalise text fields: trim, case, stray punctuation, and consistent date and number formats.
- Group the near duplicates and give you the groups to confirm, rather than deciding on your behalf.
- Validate each column against its type, and list the values that do not fit instead of coercing them.
- Deliver the cleaned file alongside the original, with a removed-rows sheet so every deletion is reversible.

You receive the cleaned workbook, the audit sheet, and the cleaning script so the next export runs through the same rules.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many rows and sheets in total, and which column or combination identifies a unique record?
