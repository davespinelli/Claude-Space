## Proposal

With 50 or more columns of mixed text, numbers and dates, the risk is not the mess you can see. It is the silent damage: a date column that is half day-first and half month-first, so 03/04 changes meaning halfway down the file, numbers stored as text with a trailing space that quietly drop out of every sum, and near-duplicate rows that differ only by spelling. A blanket clean tidies the visible problems and changes values on the way past.

Plan:
- Profile all 50 columns first, types, missing rates, duplicates and format conflicts, and send you that before changing a single cell.
- Agree a rule per column rather than one rule for the file.
- Apply the rules in a script, so the clean is repeatable on your next export.
- Handle near-duplicates as a review list for you, not as automatic deletions.
- Deliver a before-and-after reconciliation so every change is accounted for.

You get the cleaned workbook, the profile report, the reconciliation, and the script.
