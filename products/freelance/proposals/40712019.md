## Proposal

Courier booking assistants fail on the addresses, not on the conversation. A quote needs pickup and drop postcodes, parcel size and weight, and a service level, and if the assistant collects three of the four cleanly your ops team ends up redoing the job by phone anyway. The measure worth building against is the share of chats that become a real booking without a human touching them.

Plan:
- Map the booking flow end to end and list every field a job needs before it can be priced.
- Connect the assistant to your existing booking system through its API, so a chat writes a real job rather than an email.
- Handle the failure paths explicitly: unrecognised postcode, out of area, oversize parcel, and handover to a human with the transcript attached.
- Replay real past bookings as conversations and measure the completion rate before go-live.
- Ship with logging and a weekly review of every handover.

You get the integration, the test set, the numbers and a runbook.
