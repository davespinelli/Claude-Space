## Proposal

Sheets that hold only numbers still break in ways the grid does not show. Figures stored as text sort before every real number, separators and currency symbols make a column unsummable, minus signs arrive as trailing characters or brackets, and a few cells carry a decimal comma where the rest carry a point. Excel shows a tidy column and then refuses to average it.

Plan:
- Profile each sheet and report which columns are genuinely numeric and which are text wearing a number's clothes.
- Convert them properly: strip separators and symbols, normalise decimal marks, resolve negative formats, keep precision.
- Handle blanks, zeros and placeholder values as separate cases, and confirm with you which is which before deciding.
- Flag outliers and impossible values for your review rather than quietly editing them.
- Deliver the cleaning as a script, so next month's sheets go through the same rules rather than being redone by hand.

You get the cleaned workbook, a change log of what was altered per column, and the script.
