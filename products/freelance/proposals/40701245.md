## Proposal

WRF bias correction is easy to make look good and hard to make honest. A model trained and scored on the same period will show a large error reduction that vanishes in operations, because forecast bias drifts with season and lead time. So I hold out whole time blocks rather than random rows, and report skill separately by lead time against the raw WRF baseline.

Plan:
- Align WRF output with your observation series on time and station, and document every dropped or interpolated record.
- Build the baseline first: raw WRF bias, MAE and RMSE by lead time, so improvement has something to be measured against.
- Fit the correction model on earlier periods only, with lagged and seasonal features, and score on unseen later periods.
- Report skill by lead time and by season, not one headline number.
- Deliver as a script that takes a new WRF run and returns corrected values.

You receive the code, the evaluation report and the trained model.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
