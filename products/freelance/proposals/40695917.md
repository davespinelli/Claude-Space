## Proposal

With income and expenses in one sheet the thing that matters is that the signs and categories are consistent, because that is what the totals depend on. A refund entered as an expense, or the same supplier written three ways, will not look wrong in any single row but will make your monthly figures disagree with your bank.

Plan:
- Agree the structure first: date, description, category, income or expense, amount, and how refunds and transfers are recorded.
- Enter every line, keeping amounts as numbers and dates in one consistent format.
- Normalise the descriptions so a repeat supplier or client is spelled the same way each time, which is what makes categorising possible.
- Reconcile the totals: income, expenses and net per month, checked against the source so the sheet ties out.
- Flag anything I cannot categorise confidently on a separate list rather than guessing.

You receive the completed workbook with a monthly summary, the reconciliation, and the list of items needing your call.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
