## Proposal

A workbook someone types into every day is harder to build than it looks. The risk is not the formulas. It is the first time a supplier name is pasted with a trailing space, or a date is typed as text, and the totals quietly stop counting that row.

Plan:
- One entry sheet with validated inputs: supplier picked from a list, dates that must be real dates, quantity fields that reject text.
- Keep every entry in a flat table that is never overwritten, so any figure can be traced back to the day it was typed.
- Put calculations and the stock position on separate sheets that read from that table, so entry and reporting never fight each other.
- A daily view showing current stock by item, what arrived today, and what has fallen below its reorder point.
- Protect the formula sheets so a stray paste cannot break the file, and document how to add a supplier.

You get the workbook, sample data, and a one page guide.
