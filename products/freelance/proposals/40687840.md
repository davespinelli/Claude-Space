## Proposal

A support FAQ bot is judged on the questions it gets wrong, not the ones it gets right. Customers do not phrase things the way an FAQ does, and a bot that guesses confidently at a question it has no answer for costs more trust than it saves time. Two things make it work: grounding every reply in your own documented answers, and handing off to a person the moment it is unsure.

Plan:
- Take your existing FAQ or support answers as the source, so replies quote what you already stand behind.
- Build the bot on the WhatsApp Business API with opt in handling, running against your approved templates.
- Add a confidence threshold: below it, the bot says so and routes the chat to a human with the transcript.
- Log every conversation, and flag unanswered questions weekly so the FAQ improves from real traffic.
- Deliver the source with a README covering configuration and how to add or edit an answer.

You receive the running bot, the handover logic, the logs and the setup documentation.
