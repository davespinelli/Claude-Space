## Proposal

Marketing automation on a CRM that has been in use for a while fails on the data, not on the workflows. Enrolment is decided by properties people filled in by hand over months, so lifecycle stage, lead source and country are inconsistent, and a workflow that reads correctly still sends the wrong sequence to the wrong contacts. The audit of the properties your automation branches on is worth more than the automation itself.

Plan:
- Audit the properties the workflows will use, and fix or backfill the ones that drive enrolment.
- Set lifecycle stage transitions explicitly, so a contact cannot move backwards or enrol twice.
- Define suppression lists and exit criteria before writing any email, not after the first bad send.
- Test each workflow end to end on a seed list before a real contact is ever enrolled.
- Report from HubSpot's own reporting so the numbers match what you already look at.

Deliverables: the property audit, the workflows and lists, the seed-list test evidence, and a written runbook.

Two questions: which HubSpot tier are you on, since workflows need Marketing Hub Professional? And are the existing contacts all opted in for marketing email?
