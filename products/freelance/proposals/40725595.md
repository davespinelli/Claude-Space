## Proposal

A long term entry seat gets bought by the hour, but what it costs is per record, and in most recurring workloads the same three steps repeat every day: pull the file, reshape it, paste it into the sheet. That part does not need a person, and paying for it every week is where the money goes.

Plan:
- Start by doing the work manually for the first week, exactly the way you do it now.
- Write down each recurring task: where the data comes from, what shape it has to land in, who checks it.
- Script the repeated parts, highest volume first, keeping your sheet as the output so nothing changes for you.
- Keep the judgement work manual: exceptions, anything ambiguous, anything a person has to decide.
- Report weekly on what was done, what is now automated and what is still open.

Deliverables: the ongoing entry work, the scripts with a short README you own, and a weekly summary.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: what does a typical week's volume look like, and are the sources files you send me or systems I would need a login for?

David

## Delivery plan
1. Week 1: do the work manually as it stands and document every recurring task.
2. Week 1: agree with you which tasks are worth scripting and which stay manual.
3. Week 2: script the highest volume task, run it alongside the manual process to check it agrees.
4. Week 3: script the next tasks, keep the weekly report running.
5. Week 4: hand over the scripts and the README, and agree the ongoing monthly scope.
