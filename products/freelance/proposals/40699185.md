## Proposal

Diamond price pages change layout more often than most trade sites, because the tables are regenerated per listing batch. A scraper written against today's HTML will fail silently in a few weeks and hand you a report full of blanks. The fix is a collector that checks its own output and tells you when the page changed, rather than one that just keeps running.

Plan:
- Daily scheduled fetch of the pages you name, at a polite rate, with the raw response saved.
- Parse into a dated table of the fields you track, so history builds up rather than being overwritten.
- Validation on every run: expected row count, price ranges, and missing fields, with an alert if a check fails.
- Report out to Excel or a simple dashboard, showing today's figures and the change since last run.
- Runs on a schedule without your machine being on.

You get the collector, the stored history, the report, and a note on adding a field.

Proof: my daily scheduled pipeline runs publicly at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: is the site's data publicly viewable or does it sit behind an account, and which figures does the report need?
