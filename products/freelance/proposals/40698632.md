## Proposal

Handing campaign mail to an agent goes wrong in one specific way: it answers a message it should have escalated, in your name, and you find out from the client. The fix is not a better prompt, it is a stage where drafts wait for you until the agent has earned the right to send on its own.

Plan:
- Start in draft mode. The agent classifies and writes, you approve, and every decision is logged.
- Classify replies into the handful of buckets that actually occur, with anything unusual routed to you rather than answered.
- Draft responses from your own past replies, so the tone is yours and not generic assistant English.
- Track each thread state, so nothing is answered twice and follow ups stop when someone replies.
- Move categories to auto-send one at a time, once the log shows the drafts were right.

You receive the agent, the approval queue, the decision log and a runbook.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
