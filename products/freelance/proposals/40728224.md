## Proposal

A system that learns from images usually succeeds or fails on one question, and it is not which model. It is what counts as similar. Two items can look almost identical and still be a bad recommendation, same colour and different category, while the recommendation a customer wants is often a different shape entirely. Visual distance alone gets you close and then sends you sideways.

Plan:
- Embed the catalogue with a pretrained vision model, so nothing has to be trained before you see results.
- Index the vectors so a lookup stays fast as the catalogue grows.
- Blend visual distance with your catalogue fields, category and availability, so the output is usable rather than just similar.
- Measure it: hold out a sample, score the top results against what you consider correct, and report that number before anything ships.
- Serve it behind a small endpoint that takes an image or an item id and returns ranked results.

Deliverables: the indexing script, the endpoint, the accuracy report, and the source.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
