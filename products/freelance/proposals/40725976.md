## Proposal

Daily sheets from field techs feed two outputs, and the same hour means different things in each. On the invoice it is billable time at a client rate, with travel and breaks treated as the contract says. On payroll it is paid time at the tech rate, with overtime counted against a week rather than a job. One workbook can do both, but only if each entry carries the job, the tech and the hour type from the moment it lands.

Plan:
- One entry sheet that mirrors the daily sheet, with job, tech, date, hour type and material.
- Rate tables kept separate: client rates and pay rates, edited in one place.
- Invoice build per job, with materials and any markup shown as their own lines.
- Payroll build per tech per week, with overtime counted on the week and not the job.
- A checks sheet that flags hours with no job, a job with no rate, and any day that looks double entered.

Deliverables: the workbook, a worked example from your real sheets, and a note on adding techs, jobs or rates.
