## Proposal

A customer database kept in shape by hand drifts back within a month, and it is always the same three things: the same customer entered twice under two spellings, phone numbers and emails formatted differently by whoever typed them, and stale rows nobody is sure they can delete. Hands fix today's copy. What keeps it clean is the rules written down once and run on every new batch.

Plan:
- Full clean pass: duplicate detection across name, email and phone, format standardisation, missing-field report.
- Duplicates come to you as a merge review list, never deleted automatically.
- Turn the rules into a validated entry sheet plus a checking script for new data.
- Weekly run over the first month, with a short report of what it caught.
- Written handover, so it survives without me.

You get the cleaned database, the merge log, the entry template, the script, and a month of weekly checks.

Work I run in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

To be straight about scope: this price covers the clean, the tooling and a month of upkeep, not an open-ended seat.

Two questions: how many records, and does the data live in Sheets, Excel or a CRM?

David
