## Proposal

Because the text in your PDFs is selectable, pulling the characters out is the easy half. What decides whether the Excel file is usable is structure: where one record ends and the next begins, headers repeating on every page, hyphenated line breaks splitting a word in two, and what counts as a keyword rather than a common word that appears a lot.

Plan:
- Agree on two sample PDFs what one Excel row represents and which columns you need.
- Extract the full text per document, stripping repeated headers, footers and page numbers.
- Rejoin words broken across line ends so the keyword counts are not silently wrong.
- Pull keywords with a rule you can see and change: your own term list, plus frequency ranking with stopwords removed.
- Deliver the workbook with a sheet per view, and the script so the next batch is a rerun.

You receive the Excel file, the extraction script with a README, and a short note on any document that needed a judgement call.

Public example of my pipeline work: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space
