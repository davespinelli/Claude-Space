## Proposal

With text-only PDFs the hard part is not reading the words, it is not losing the structure around them. Hyphenated line endings split single words in two. Headers and page numbers repeat on every page and land in the middle of your data. Layout line breaks turn one sentence into four rows. All fixable, but only on purpose rather than by pasting.

Plan:
- Confirm every file has a real text layer, and OCR only the ones that do not, telling you which those are.
- Extract with pdfplumber in reading order, rejoining words broken across line endings.
- Remove repeated headers, footers and page numbers, after showing you the exact list of what will be removed.
- Write one row per paragraph with columns for file, page and sequence, so no text is orphaned.
- Spot check a sample of pages against the source and report the character level match rate.

You receive the workbook, the extraction script for future batches, and the verification sample.

Public example of my automation running on a schedule: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space

Two questions: how many files and roughly how many pages in total? And do you want one row per paragraph or one row per line as it appears on the page?
