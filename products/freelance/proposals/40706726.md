## Proposal

Printed tables of numbers are the case where the second pass matters more than the first. A scanned page has no text layer to fall back on, and the digit pairs that get read wrong are always the same ones: 3 and 8, 1 and 7, 5 and 6 on a page that has been photocopied a few times. One person reading back their own typing will not catch those, because they read what they meant to type.

Plan:
- Set the sheet up before entering anything: one column per field, numbers as numbers, a page and row reference on every line.
- Enter the tables, then verify by a second independent pass on the columns that carry the money, not by proofreading the first pass.
- Tie each column to the totals printed on the page and report any page that does not balance.
- Flag illegible or ambiguous figures in a queries tab rather than picking the likelier digit.
- Send a per-page count so you can see what was entered, what tied out and what is queried.

You get the XLSX workbook, the tie-out report and the queries tab.
