## Proposal

A risk module that sits beside a live trading setup is only useful if it is faster than the position it is judging, and the hard part is rarely the model. It is agreeing what risky means in numbers you already trust, so the score does not become one more reading you learn to ignore.

Plan:
- Write down the risk definition first: exposure caps, drawdown from peak, correlation to what you already hold, and how stale a quote may be before it is dropped.
- Read your live positions and market data through your existing feed rather than a second source that can disagree with it.
- Score continuously on a fixed interval, with the inputs behind each score logged so any alert can be explained after the fact.
- Replay the scorer over your own history to see which past days it would have flagged.
- Alert on threshold breaches only, to whatever you already watch.

You receive the scoring service, the replay report, the config file holding every threshold, and a runbook.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which broker or data feed holds your positions, and do you want alerts only, or the ability to block new orders?
