## Proposal

First line automation rarely fails by refusing to answer. It fails by answering confidently on the questions it should have passed to a person: a refund outside policy, an order that is actually lost, a complaint. Those are the messages that cost you the customer, so the handoff rules matter more than the phrasing of the replies.

Plan:
- Sort a few hundred of your real past messages into three buckets: answer, collect details and pass on, straight to a human.
- Ground the answering bucket in your own policy and product text, so replies come from your material rather than invention.
- Put WhatsApp and the website chat through one handler, with a single conversation history per customer.
- Make the escape hatch explicit: uncertainty, anger or anything money related goes to a person with the full transcript attached.
- Log every conversation and review week one together so the buckets get tuned on real traffic.

You get the running bot, the handoff rules and the source.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

## Delivery plan
1. Days 1 to 2: review message history, agree the three buckets and the handoff rules.
2. Days 3 to 4: content grounding and the answering layer, tested against past messages.
3. Days 5 to 7: WhatsApp and web chat channels into one handler with shared history.
4. Days 8 to 9: handoff into your agent tool, logging and a live pilot on limited traffic.
5. Day 10: review the pilot log, tune the buckets, handover and runbook.
