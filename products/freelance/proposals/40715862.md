## Proposal

Under a thousand rows is the size where a customer list looks tidy and is not. The same company sits in it three times with different spacing, and phone numbers are half text and half number, so any sort or count quietly disagrees with itself.

Plan:
- Take the file as it is and give you a one page read of what is actually wrong, with counts per issue, before changing anything.
- Normalise the mechanics: trim spacing, consistent capitalisation, one date format, phone numbers and postcodes kept as text so leading zeros survive.
- Find likely duplicates by fuzzy match on name plus one other field, and list them for your decision rather than merging them silently.
- Apply the formatting you want: column widths, header row, filters, frozen header.
- Return two tabs, the clean data and a change log showing every row that was touched.

Nothing is deleted without your sign off, and your original file is kept untouched alongside the clean one.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which fields decide that two rows are the same customer, and should the layout match an existing template?
