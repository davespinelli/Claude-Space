## Proposal

An outreach system is decided by deliverability, not copy. Volume from a domain with no sending history goes to spam inside a few days, and once that domain carries a complaint rate, better subject lines change nothing and your ordinary business mail suffers too. It is also the part that cannot be fixed afterwards.

Plan:
- Send from your own domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC in place and the domain warmed before any volume, to contacts you already hold and are allowed to mail.
- Generate drafts from a brief and your own facts, with a human approval step before anything leaves.
- Honour one-click unsubscribe, behind a suppression list nothing can write around.
- Rate limit per recipient domain and per day, and route replies to a person rather than letting the agent answer.
- Report sends, replies, bounces and complaints, so you can stop early.

I do not build list scraping or address harvesting into this. The contacts need to be yours.

Deliverables: the agent, the approval queue, the reporting and a runbook.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
