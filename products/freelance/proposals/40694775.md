## Proposal

Getting retained as an expert witness is usually a response time problem before it is a volume problem. An attorney working a deadline contacts several candidates the same afternoon. The one who comes back quickly, with a CV extract that matches the case type and a straight answer on availability and conflicts, gets the call. A day later is too late no matter how good the credentials are.

Plan:
- Map how a request reaches you today, from first contact to signed engagement, and find where it stalls.
- Build the n8n flow: intake from your form and inbox into one deduplicated record, with case type, jurisdiction and deadline captured.
- Draft a reply within minutes from your own CV and rate material, held for your approval before it sends.
- Run a conflicts check against your prior engagements before anything goes out.
- Track each enquiry through stages so you can see conversion and where it drops.

You receive the exported n8n workflows, the tracking board, the reply templates and a runbook for changing them yourself.

Proof: my public repo and the live pipeline it runs at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: where do enquiries arrive now, and is this inbound only, or do you also want outreach to a list you already hold?
