## Proposal

Calling this the first milestone of a larger automation is the right instinct, because the enquiry step is where the data model gets decided. If the fields you pull out now do not match what the later stages need, every downstream stage inherits the gap. So I would spend the first day on the field list rather than on the flow.

Plan:
- Sample a real week of enquiries and agree the fields to extract, including the ones that are often missing.
- Build the extraction so it handles the messy cases: forwarded chains, replies to old threads, attachments carrying the detail.
- Write each enquiry to a structured store, with the source message linked so anything can be traced back.
- Route anything the extractor is not confident about to a review queue rather than guessing.
- Log volumes and failures daily so you can see what the next stage will need to handle.

You receive the working flow, the field mapping and a short guide for changing it yourself.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many enquiries a day, and where should the extracted records land?
