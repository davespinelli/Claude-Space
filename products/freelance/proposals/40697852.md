## Proposal

When receipts, invoices and bank statements all arrive in the same daily batch, the quiet error is double counting. The card receipt and the bank line are the same transaction, so entering both inflates the day's expenses, and nobody notices until the month refuses to reconcile against the bank.

Plan:
- Agree the category list and the matching rule that ties a receipt to its bank line, so one transaction produces one row.
- Fix a single daily intake: you send the day's documents, I return the updated sheet the same day.
- Enter each item with its document reference, amounts as numbers, dates in one format, so any row can be traced back.
- Reconcile daily against the bank closing balance, which surfaces a mistake on the day it happens rather than at month end.
- Put anything I cannot categorise confidently on a queries list instead of guessing.

You receive the running workbook, a monthly income, expense and net summary, the daily reconciliation and the open queries list.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
