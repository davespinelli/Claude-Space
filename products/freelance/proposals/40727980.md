## Proposal

The report is rarely the hard part of this job. The raw export is. Sales files almost always arrive with the same few problems: one customer under three spellings, returns sitting as positive rows, a campaign label that changed halfway through the year. A chart built before those are settled tells a story confidently, and the story is wrong.

Plan:
- Profile the file first: row counts, date range, duplicates, blanks, and anything that cannot be a real sale.
- Agree the definitions that decide every number: what counts as revenue, how returns are treated, which date is the sale date.
- Analyse what actually moved: trend over the period, what grew and what shrank, which segments and channels carry the result.
- Separate what the data shows from what it suggests, and say plainly where a sample is too thin to support a claim.
- Write it for a reader who will act on it, not for a dashboard.

Deliverables: the written report with charts inline, and the cleaned dataset so the numbers can be checked.
