## Proposal

Mixed source material is the part that costs time. When the same field arrives as text in one source and as a number or a date in another, Excel stores them inconsistently, and the sheet looks correct while sorting, filtering and any later formula quietly misbehave.

Plan:
- Agree the column list first: what each field is, and what type it should end up as.
- Enter the records, keeping dates as real dates and numbers as numbers rather than text.
- Apply your formatting once as a consistent set, so headers, widths, alignment and number formats match across the sheet.
- Run a check pass for duplicates, blank required fields and values that do not fit their column type.
- Deliver a short list of anything the source left genuinely ambiguous rather than guessing at it.

You get the finished workbook, formatted and checked, in xlsx or Google Sheets.
