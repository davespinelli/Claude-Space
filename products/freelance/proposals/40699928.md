## Proposal

An Excel form that emails on a button click fails in two ways. It fires on a half filled row, or it sends twice because someone clicked again while Outlook was slow. Both are cheap to prevent if validation and a sent log go in first.

Plan:
- Lock the form first: required fields, dropdowns where the value is fixed, dates and numbers typed as such, so an incomplete row cannot be submitted.
- Wire the send through Outlook on the user's PC, or over SMTP if the workbook runs unattended.
- Log every send with timestamp, recipient and row reference, so a repeat click is caught rather than delivered.
- Build the message from a template you can edit yourself, with the entry attached as CSV or PDF.
- Test against your macro security settings and leave a one page setup note.

You get the macro enabled workbook, the sent log, the editable template and the note.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: does the mail go out through Outlook on each user's machine or from a server, and does every submission go to one fixed address or does the recipient depend on the entry?
