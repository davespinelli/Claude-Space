## Proposal

The hard part here is not the voice layer, it is that your Excel activity tracker already encodes how your team thinks about work, and a voice system that guesses at that will quietly file updates against the wrong task. Speech to text is close to solved. Mapping a spoken sentence onto the right row and the right owner is where these projects fail.

Plan:
- Read your existing tracker and turn its columns into an explicit schema: tasks, owners, dates, statuses, and which fields a voice update may change.
- Build the intent layer so a spoken update resolves to one record, and read the match back to the speaker for confirmation before it writes.
- Keep a full audit log of every voice input, the transcript, and the change it made, so a bad parse is traceable and reversible.
- Add a text fallback and a web view, because voice is poor for reviewing a backlog.
- Handle the unclear cases by asking a follow up question rather than writing a guess.

You receive the running system, the source, the schema mapping, and a runbook.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
