## Proposal

A treasury backlog is not really a typing job. The records have to tie back to something: statement balances, transfer pairs that appear twice from two sides, value dates against posting dates, and amounts in more than one currency. Keyed quickly without those checks, a backlog gets cleared and then has to be cleared again when a balance does not agree.

Plan:
- Agree the workbook layout and what each field means, including sign convention and date basis.
- Enter the backlog in batches by period or account, so progress is visible and reviewable.
- Reconcile each batch to the closing balance on the source document before moving on.
- Match internal transfers so the same movement is not counted twice.
- Add validation and a lookup list for accounts and counterparties, so future entry cannot break the format.

You get the completed workbook, a reconciliation tab per account, and a short list of items I could not tie out with the reason.

Proof of how I work, public repo and live pipeline: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
