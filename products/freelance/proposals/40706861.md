## Proposal

Advice about AI in Airtable is cheap to give and hard to use, because the useful answer depends on what your base already stores and where the manual hours actually go. Most bases have two or three places worth automating and a dozen that look automatable but would save ten minutes a month. So I would rather map that first and then build, than hand you a strategy document.

Plan:
- Walk your base: tables, links, views, and the steps a record goes through before someone calls it done.
- Time the manual work honestly and rank it, so we spend the budget where the hours are.
- Build the top two workflows end to end, using Airtable automations and scripting where they fit and an external service only where they do not.
- Add a check on the AI-written fields, since a model filling a field silently and wrongly is worse than an empty field.
- Hand over a runbook: what runs when, what it costs, and how to turn it off.

You get the two workflows live in your base, the field checks, and the written ranking of everything we did not build.

How I work in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which Airtable plan are you on, since scripting and webhooks need a paid tier, and which manual step eats the most time today?

David
