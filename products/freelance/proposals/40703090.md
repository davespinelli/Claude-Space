## Proposal

The hard part of a Shopify to accounting sync is not the first push, it is the second one. Orders get edited, refunded and partly fulfilled after they land, so a sync that only creates records leaves you reconciling duplicates and stale totals by hand every month.

Plan:
- Map Shopify fields to your accounting platform first: order, line items, tax, shipping, discounts, refunds, customer.
- Sync on webhooks for orders and customers, so records move within seconds rather than on a nightly batch.
- Make every write idempotent by storing the Shopify id against the accounting record, so a retry updates instead of duplicating.
- Add a retry queue and a daily reconciliation that reports any order not matched on the accounting side.
- Backfill a chosen date range once, then leave it running.

You get the sync service, the field mapping document, the reconciliation report and a short runbook.
