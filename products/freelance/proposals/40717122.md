## Proposal

The drawing part of a paint by numbers script is straightforward. What decides whether the output is usable is region handling. After colour reduction you get thousands of tiny islands, and if every one of them is outlined and numbered the result is unpaintable and the file is slow to even open. Merging regions below a size threshold into their neighbours, then placing each number at a point that is genuinely inside its own region, is where the work sits.

Plan:
- Agree inputs and outputs: source image type, palette size, artboard size.
- Reduce to N colours, then merge any region under a minimum area into its nearest neighbour.
- Trace region boundaries to Illustrator paths on separate layers for outlines, numbers and the key.
- Place each number at an interior point, scaled to fit, skipping regions too small to label.
- Add a palette legend and a batch mode for a folder of images.

You receive the JSX script with a settings block at the top for every threshold, plus two sample outputs.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
