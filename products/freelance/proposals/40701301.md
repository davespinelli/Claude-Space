## Proposal

The phrase that matters in your brief is "exactly as it appears". Most extraction jobs go wrong not on the fetching but on the silent edits: a leading zero dropped, a date reformatted, a footnote merged into the cell above. So I treat the page text as the source of truth and keep values as text unless you tell me a column should be numeric.

Plan:
- Confirm the page list and which fields on each page belong in which column.
- Build a Python extractor that reads only publicly viewable pages, no logins or paywalls.
- Preserve values verbatim, with a flag column where a page is missing a field rather than a blank guess.
- Spot-check a sample of rows against the live pages and send you that check alongside the file.
- Rerun on request if the pages change.

You receive the Excel workbook, the extraction script so you can rerun it yourself, and the verification sample.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many pages, and are they all publicly viewable without a login?

David
