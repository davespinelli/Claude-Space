## Proposal

Tally to Odoo migrations rarely break on the transactions. They break on the master data underneath, because the two systems do not model it the same way. Tally ledgers under groups are not an Odoo chart of accounts, and a party is one ledger in Tally but a partner plus a receivable account in Odoo. Load the vouchers before those maps are agreed and the entries post against accounts nobody chose.

Plan:
- Export the Tally masters first and produce mapping tables for accounts, partners, products and tax, for you to sign off.
- Load masters into Odoo through the API with the Tally reference kept as an external id, so the load is repeatable and never duplicates.
- Post opening balances as of an agreed cutover date rather than migrating history no one will use.
- Migrate the transaction set for the agreed period in dated batches.
- Reconcile after each batch: trial balance, partner balances and stock quantities, Tally against Odoo.

You get the migration scripts, the mapping tables and the reconciliation reports.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
