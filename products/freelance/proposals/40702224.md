## Proposal

PDF to Word and PDF to Excel are two different jobs, and the second one is where hand typing goes wrong. Text reflows fine, but a table retyped by hand loses column alignment and turns numbers into text that will not sum later, and nobody notices until a total is off.

Plan:
- Take a sample of your PDFs first and tell you which ones extract cleanly and which are scans that genuinely need typing.
- Extract the clean ones programmatically, so the text and tables land with structure intact rather than as retyped guesses.
- Type the scanned pages by hand, following your formatting exactly.
- Check numeric columns against the source totals before delivery, not after.
- Return one consistent Word and Excel set, same headers and layout across every file.

You get the finished Word and Excel files plus a short note on anything the source made ambiguous.

Proof of my working style: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many pages, and are the PDFs digital or scanned?
