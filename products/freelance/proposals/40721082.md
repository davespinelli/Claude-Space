## Proposal

Automating options on NSE and BSE differs from automating equities at the order, not the signal. Your rule says buy a strike. The code still has to decide which contract that is on the day, what to do when the strike is thin and the spread wide, and how the position rolls at expiry. If those three are not written down, the bot picks contracts you would not have picked.

Plan:
- Write entry, exit, strike selection, sizing and the expiry roll as testable rules before any code.
- Backtest them on your instruments with real costs and next bar fills, for a measured baseline.
- Build execution on your broker's API with one client order id per signal, position and loss caps, and a kill switch.
- Reconcile against the broker's own positions on every start, so a restart cannot double a position.
- Run one week on paper, checking every fill against the log.

You get the strategy code, the backtest report, the bot and a runbook.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
