## Proposal

Pulling records from several online databases into one sheet usually breaks on the join, not the typing. Each source labels the same record differently, so the combined sheet ends up with the same entity listed twice under two spellings. That is the part worth getting right before any volume goes in.

Plan:
- Agree one target schema first, with the column list and the field that identifies a record.
- Capture each source into its own staging sheet, keeping the original wording untouched.
- Normalise into the target sheet: trimmed whitespace, consistent casing, dates in one format.
- Flag likely duplicates across sources for your decision rather than merging them silently.
- Where a source allows automated export, script it, so a refresh later is a rerun and not a retype.

You get the combined workbook with staging and final sheets, a duplicate report, and a short note on refreshing it.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many records in total, and are the source databases open to view or do they need your login?
