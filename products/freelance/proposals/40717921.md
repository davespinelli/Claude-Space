## Proposal

A jewellery counter has one automation that pays for itself before any of the others: the enquiry that arrives after closing and gets a price only the next morning. The gold rate moves daily, so a quote has to be computed from today's rate rather than looked up in a stored price list, and that computation is exactly what an agent can hold reliably.

Plan:
- One call to list the enquiries you actually receive, ranked by how often they come in.
- Build the first agent on the top two, most likely price or making charge questions and stock availability.
- Wire in the daily metal rate from one source you name, so a quote is rate times weight plus making charge, computed at the moment of asking.
- Keep a human handover. Anything outside the agent's list goes to your phone with the customer's message attached.
- Log every conversation to a sheet so you can see what was asked and what was answered.

You receive the running agent, the rate feed, the log sheet and a README.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which channel do most enquiries arrive on, and do you already have a product list with weights in a file?
