## Proposal

Synthetic accounting data is easy to produce and hard to make usable. Random invoices will not tie to customers or service dates, receipts will not reconcile to the ledger, and anything trained or demoed on it learns the generator rather than the business. For a hauling operation the structure is the point: customers on recurring pickup schedules, containers by size, tonnage and disposal fees, fuel surcharges, missed pickups, and payments that arrive late on a believable spread.

Plan:
- Agree the entity model first: customers, sites, containers, routes, service events, invoices, payments and their links.
- Fix the rules that must always hold, so invoice lines sum to headers and the ledger balances.
- Build the generator in Python with a seed, so any dataset is reproducible exactly.
- Add realistic behaviour: seasonality, price changes, credits, disputes and a controllable rate of messy records.
- Ship a validation pass that checks the rules and reports output that breaks them.

You get the generator, a configuration file, a dataset in CSV or QuickBooks-ready format, and a README.
