## Proposal

Balance sheet style tables have one property that makes them easy to verify: they add up. Subtotals and totals are printed right there in the PDF, so any extraction can be checked against them rather than eyeballed. Most conversions skip that step, which is why bracketed negatives and dropped rows survive into the spreadsheet.

Plan:
- Extract each table with pdfplumber, tuned to the layout of your statements.
- Parse figures properly: bracketed negatives, thousands separators, currency symbols, dashes for nil.
- Preserve the line item hierarchy so subtotals stay distinguishable from their components.
- Reconcile every extracted subtotal and total against the printed figure and flag any difference.
- Deliver one Excel workbook, consistent column layout across all five files, ready for analysis.

You receive the workbook, the reconciliation summary, and the script for future statements.
