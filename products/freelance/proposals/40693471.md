## Proposal

Daily status entry stays accurate for about a week and then drifts, because nothing checks that the status typed into the sheet matches the update you sent. By the time a customer asks, the workbook and the shop floor disagree and nobody knows which is right.

Plan:
- Record each day as new rows rather than overwriting yesterday, so the workbook keeps the full history of every order.
- Take your daily update in whatever form it arrives and load it into the sheet, with a manual path kept for exceptions.
- Check order ids and status values against a fixed list, so a typo shows up the same day instead of a month later.
- Flag orders that have not changed status in a set number of days.
- Add a short daily summary of counts by status at the top of the sheet.

You receive the updated workbook, the import step, the validation rules and a one page instruction sheet.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how does the daily update reach me, as a file, email or message, and what is the full list of valid statuses?
