## Proposal

Swiss company data is not one list. The federal index carries the registry record, the cantonal registers carry the detail, and the same firm turns up under a legal name in one and a trade name in the other. The UID is the only key that holds across them, so a tool that matches on company name starts producing duplicates within a week.

Plan:
- Pull from the public registry sources by canton, activity code and legal form, whichever filters define a lead for you.
- Key every record on the UID, so a rerun updates a company rather than adding it again.
- Normalise name, address, legal form and status into one table with consistent fields.
- Export to CSV or Excel with the pull date on every row.
- Make it rerunnable on a schedule, reporting counts of new, changed and unchanged.

You get the tool, the exports and the source.

Pipeline I run in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

To be straight: I work from public company registry records. I do not scrape personal contact details or anything behind a login.

Two questions: which filters define a lead for you, and do you want CSV, Excel or a direct CRM import?
