## Proposal

With PDF and image sources the risk is not typing speed, it is the digits nobody checks. A transposed figure in a scanned table looks exactly like a correct one in the finished sheet, and it is found weeks later when a total does not reconcile. The part worth paying for is the verification, not the keystrokes.

Plan:
- Take five sample pages first and confirm the column layout and how you want merged cells, footnotes and blanks handled.
- Read the text out of the documents where they allow it, and type only what genuinely has to be typed.
- Check every numeric column against the source: totals reconciled page by page, and unclear figures flagged rather than guessed.
- Keep the output in your format, one sheet per document or one combined sheet.
- Report the pages that were poor scans so you know where the uncertainty sits.

You receive the finished Excel or Word files, a flagged-items list, and a page count reconciliation.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
