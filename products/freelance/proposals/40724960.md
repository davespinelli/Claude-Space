## Proposal

A raw sales export usually carries one row per order line rather than one per order, and that decides what the dashboard can honestly show. Sum revenue by customer and it is right. Add a shipping or discount field that repeats on every line of the same order and that total is now counted several times. It looks plausible on the screen and it is wrong.

Plan:
- Check the grain first: what one row means, and which fields repeat within an order.
- Clean the types: real dates, numbers as numbers, one spelling per customer and product.
- Build a table and pivot cache layer, so next month extends the source instead of breaking the range.
- Put the measures on that layer: revenue, units, average order value, period on period.
- Lay out one screen: filters at the top, trend and mix below, no chart that repeats another.

Deliverables: the workbook with the raw sheet kept intact, the dashboard, and a note on refreshing it next month.
