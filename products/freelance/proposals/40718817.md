## Proposal

Daily inventory upkeep in Excel fails in a specific way: the sheet says what the stock is now, but not what changed or when, so once a count is wrong there is no way to find the day it went wrong. A dated movement log underneath the sheet fixes that, and it also lets a script do the daily update.

Plan:
- Review your current workbooks and write down the rules you apply each day when you update them.
- Add a movements table that records every receipt, issue and adjustment with a date and a source.
- Build the daily update as a script that reads your inbound files and posts movements, leaving stock levels as a computed result.
- Add checks that flag negative stock, duplicate references and any line that does not reconcile.
- Keep the sheet layout you already use, so nothing downstream breaks.

You receive the updated workbook, the script, a check report per run, and a short README.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
