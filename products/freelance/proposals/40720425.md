## Proposal

Daily revenue, units and regional breakdowns in one workbook is a good dataset and an awkward chart. Plotted raw, a daily revenue line is mostly noise, and the regional split turns into a legend nobody can read. The more common failure is quieter: a dashboard wired to a fixed cell range stops updating the moment next month's rows are added, and it keeps showing a chart, just an old one.

Plan:
- Put the raw figures on their own sheet as a proper table, so new rows extend every range automatically.
- Flag the data problems first: missing days, duplicated rows, and regions spelled two ways.
- Build the views from pivots rather than fixed ranges: revenue by month with a rolling average, units against revenue, and region ranking with share of total.
- One dashboard sheet with a date and region filter, instead of a pile of separate charts.
- Keep everything in Excel so nobody needs a new tool to open it.

Deliverables: the workbook with raw sheet, pivots and dashboard, plus a short page on refreshing it.
