## Proposal

You already have the feeds, the simulator and a paper environment, so what you are buying is the strategy logic, and that is the part where a good backtest and a good idea are easiest to confuse. The usual failure is not a bug in the engine. It is a rule tuned until the curve looks right on the same sample it was measured on, which then pays nothing forward.

Plan:
- Agree each rule in writing first: signal, universe, rebalance point, sizing, exit.
- Implement as a vectorized daily bar backtest, weights decided at the close and applied the next day, with explicit costs.
- Report CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown and calendar years against buy and hold.
- Split the sample in halves and walk forward, and report the rules where the edge does not survive.
- Hand the code over in a layout that plugs into your simulator.

Deliverables: strategy code, the report, and the parameter ranges tried.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many strategies do you want implemented, and what universe and history do your feeds cover?
