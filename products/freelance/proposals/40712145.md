## Proposal

PDF and Word are two different jobs here. Word files, and PDFs that still carry a text layer, can be extracted directly, so the characters in your spreadsheet are the characters in the document and no typing error can creep in. Scanned pages have no text layer, and those need OCR plus a human verification pass. Which of the two you have decides the accuracy you get, so I check that first rather than quoting one number for both.

Plan:
- Sort your files into text-layer and scanned before anything else, and tell you the split.
- Extract the text-layer files programmatically into your column layout.
- OCR the scanned ones, then verify every field against the page by eye.
- Flag anything ambiguous in a separate column instead of guessing at it.
- Deliver one clean workbook, consistent dates, numbers and spellings throughout.

You get the workbook, the flagged-items list, and the extraction script so the next batch takes minutes.
