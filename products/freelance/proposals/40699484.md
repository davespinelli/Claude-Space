## Proposal

The expensive failure on research work is not slow typing, it is rows filled to the wrong standard. Two sources disagree on a figure, the researcher picks one each time without recording which, and by the time you spot it the sheet has to be redone.

Plan:
- Agree a source rule per field first: which site counts as authoritative, and what to record when two sources disagree.
- Put a source URL and a date beside every researched field, so any value can be rechecked without repeating the search.
- Run a 20 row pilot for your sign off before the full batch, while the standard is still cheap to change.
- Hold one format for names, dates and numbers, so the sheet sorts and filters cleanly.
- Where a field comes from a published listing, script the pull and keep the manual work for judgement calls.

You get the sheet with its sources column, the pilot batch, a queries list and a note on the method.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many rows and which fields, and is the research limited to published company details? I do not collect personal contact information.
