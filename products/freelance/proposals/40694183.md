## Proposal

The chat flow is the easy half of a marketplace bot. The part that decides whether it works is what happens between payment and delivery: who holds the money, what the buyer gets if the file never arrives, and whether you can reconstruct an order a week later when someone disputes it.

Plan:
- Seller flow: list an item with price, description and the file or key, held by the bot until it sells.
- Buyer flow: browse or search the catalogue, pay, and receive the item automatically once payment confirms.
- One payment provider to start, with every order written to a database so refunds and disputes have a record.
- Admin commands for you: approve or remove listings, view orders, issue a refund, ban a seller.
- Deployed on a small server with logging, so it runs without your machine being on.

You receive the bot source, the deployment and a short admin guide.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which payment method do you want first, and how should sellers confirm they hold the rights to what they list, since that is what keeps the bot from being reported?
