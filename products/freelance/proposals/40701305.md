## Proposal

You already have the strategy, so the risk here is not the signal logic. It is the gap between what your rules do on a chart and what they do against a live order book: partial fills, a missed reconnect, a position left open through a daily loss limit. Prop account rules are unforgiving about exactly those cases, so I build the risk layer first and hang the signals off it.

Plan:
- Write your entry and exit rules as a testable Python module and replay them on historical bars, so you see the trades before any order goes out.
- Build the order manager against the TopstepX API with position caps, a daily loss stop and a flat-by-time rule.
- Add reconnect and reconciliation so a dropped session cannot leave an unknown position.
- Log every decision and fill to CSV so you can audit any day.
- Run it on a simulated account for a week before we go near a funded one.

You receive the bot, the runbook and the source.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which instrument and timeframe, and can you share your account's daily loss and trailing drawdown limits?
