## Proposal

Every word lifted out is clear about completeness but not about shape, and that is the decision that sets the price. One cell per line, one per paragraph, or fields pulled into named columns are three different jobs on the same PDFs. Agreeing that first is what stops the second delivery being a rebuild of the first.

Plan:
- Take one representative PDF and produce three short sample layouts, so you pick the shape by looking at it rather than describing it.
- Extract the full text by script in reading order, keeping section headings so nothing detaches from its context.
- Preserve the page and position of every entry, so any cell can be traced back to where it came from.
- Run the whole batch through the same script, so a layout change later is a rerun rather than redoing the work.
- Check totals per document, words in against rows out, and report any page that yields nothing.

You receive the Excel workbook, the per document check report, and the extraction script.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many PDFs and roughly how many pages each, and are they all text based rather than scanned?
