## Proposal

Capturing every piece of information out of mixed files is not the hard part. The hard part is the columns that hold both kinds at once. A field carrying 1,250 on one record, a blank on the next and "not provided" on the one after either lands as text, and silently drops out of every total below it, or gets forced to a number, and a missing value becomes indistinguishable from a genuine zero.

Plan:
- Agree the target schema first: what one row represents, and which columns are numeric, text or date.
- Extract per file type, scripted wherever the files are digital, so re-running a file gives the same rows.
- Keep missing and zero distinct, each with a stated marker rather than a blank that means two things.
- Reconcile every file: records in, records out, and anything skipped.
- Deliver the clean table with the exception list beside it, not merged into it.

Deliverables: the completed workbook, the exception list, and the extraction scripts.
