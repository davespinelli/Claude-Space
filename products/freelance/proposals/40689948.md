## Proposal

Approval flows in Zoho Books tend to fail in a predictable place. The record can still be edited after it has been approved, or a user whose role is broader than intended bypasses the flow entirely. So the real work is as much in roles and field permissions as in the approval rule itself, and the two have to be designed together or the restriction is cosmetic.

Plan:
- Map which record types need approval (estimates, invoices, bills, expenses) and the amount threshold for each.
- Configure the built in approval workflows where they cover the case, and tell you plainly where they do not.
- Use custom roles and field level permissions to block the operations you want restricted outright.
- Add Deluge custom functions or workflow rules for anything the standard settings cannot express.
- Test each role with a separate user login and hand you the results, including what a user can still do.

You receive the configured organisation, a one page map of roles to permissions, the Deluge code, and the test results.
