## Proposal

You put generating financial reports in Excel next to bank reconciliation, and in practice those are the same problem. The report gets rebuilt by hand every month because the Xero export never lands in the same shape twice, and it is built on a ledger that has not been reconciled yet.

Plan:
- Reconcile the bank in Xero first. A report built on an unreconciled ledger is a tidy guess.
- Build the Excel report once, driven by the export, so the next month is a refresh rather than a rebuild.
- Keep the mapping from Xero accounts to report lines on one visible tab you can change without calling me.
- Add the checks that catch the usual errors: unreconciled items, suspense balances, movements posted to a prior period.
- Run it each month and send the report with a short list of anything that needs your decision.

You get the workbook, the mapping tab, the checks, and a one page instruction sheet.

Proof of my reporting and pipeline work: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which reports do you need, profit and loss, balance sheet, cash summary or something custom? And how many bank accounts are being reconciled?

David
