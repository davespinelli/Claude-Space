## Proposal

Practical AI advice is only worth anything once it lands on a process you actually run. Most consultations end in a deck of possibilities, and nothing in the business works differently the following month. So I would rather structure this as a short audit followed by one workflow built and running in your hands, which also tells you more about what AI can do for you than any deck will.

Plan:
- Two working sessions to map what happens today: the steps, who touches them, how long they take, how often they break.
- Score each candidate on hours saved against build effort, ranked honestly, including the ones AI should be kept out of.
- Build the top one end to end and hand it over running.
- Write the second one up as a spec detailed enough to give to anyone.
- Close with a short memo on what I would and would not automate next, with reasons.

Work I run in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which tools do you already run day to day, and is there one process you already suspect is the worst offender?

David
