## Proposal

At ten pages the risk is not volume, it is the small structural things that a quick conversion gets wrong: a table that breaks across a page and loses its header, merged cells, footnote markers that end up inside a number, and columns that shift when a row wraps. Those are exactly the errors that survive a visual check.

Plan:
- Extract with a script rather than by hand, so the same PDF always gives the same sheet and a corrected version can be rerun in minutes.
- Rebuild tables that span pages into one continuous block with the header carried through.
- Keep numbers as numbers and dates as dates, with the original text preserved where a value is unclear.
- Verify every page against the source, and give you a per page count of rows extracted.
- Match the column layout you want to work in rather than whatever the PDF happened to use.

You receive the Excel file, a page by page verification note, and the extraction script.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: is the PDF text based or a scan, and do you want one sheet for everything or one sheet per table?
