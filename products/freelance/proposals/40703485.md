## Proposal

In a jewelry trading business the same figures usually live in three places at once: a stock sheet, an order or invoice file, and whatever the sales side keeps. Nothing is wrong with any one of them, but reconciling them by hand is where the hours go, and it is the reason stock counts and margins drift apart.

Plan:
- Walk your current process end to end and write it down as it actually runs, not as it is supposed to.
- Rank each step by hours spent against how automatable it is, so we start where the payback is clearest.
- Automate the top two or three inside the budget: usually stock and order reconciliation, a daily figures report, or document and invoice generation.
- Leave the rest as a costed roadmap you can hand to anyone.

You get the written process map, the ranked automation list with estimates, and two or three working automations in Python running on a schedule.

Proof of my working style: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: what tools do you use today for stock and invoicing, and which single task eats the most time?
