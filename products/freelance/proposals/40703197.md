## Proposal

A client list that grows over time collects a specific kind of mess: the same client entered twice with slightly different spelling, fields that were optional at first and are now needed, and records nobody is sure are still current. Typing new rows into it does not fix that.

Plan:
- Agree the column set and what each field means, including which are required.
- Deduplicate on a stable key rather than on exact name matching, and show you every merge before it happens.
- Standardise the recurring fields: dates, phone and address formats, status values from a fixed list.
- Enter and update the records, keeping one row per client and one workbook as the single copy.
- Add validation on the sheet so the next entry cannot break the format.

You get the compiled workbook, a merge log of what was combined, and a short note on records I could not resolve. This is your own client data, kept in your workbook; I do not source or add contact details from elsewhere.

Proof of my working style: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many records, and is this a one-off cleanup or ongoing upkeep?
