## Proposal

Shift schedules go wrong in one specific place. Times typed as text look identical to real time values on screen, and any shift that crosses midnight computes as a negative span, so the hours column quietly under-counts and nobody sees it until payroll. Precise start and end times are only worth having if the sheet can add them up correctly afterwards.

Plan:
- Enter the start and end times into your existing master sheet, keeping your layout and formatting as they are.
- Store them as real Excel time values, not text, so sorting and arithmetic behave.
- Handle overnight shifts explicitly so a 22:00 to 06:00 shift reads as eight hours, not minus sixteen.
- Add a checked hours-per-shift and hours-per-person total you can verify against your own figures.
- Return a short list of any entries that were unclear or conflicting, rather than guessing at them.

You receive the updated workbook, the totals, and the exceptions list.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many shifts and over what date range, and where do the source times come from, a roster, emails or handwritten notes?
