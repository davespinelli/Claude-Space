## Proposal

Clear scans of printed text are the good case for OCR, so the risk is not whether the characters come out. It is the few that come out almost right: a 0 read as an O, a column that drifts when a scan is slightly rotated. Those survive a quick eyeball and show up later, when a total does not add up.

Plan:
- Agree from two sample scans what one Excel row represents and which columns you need.
- Run OCR with deskewing, then parse the text into columns rather than dumping it into one.
- Apply type rules per column so dates, numbers and codes are validated as they land.
- Flag every low confidence character and every value that fails its rule, in a review sheet, and correct them by hand.
- Deliver the workbook with a QA sheet showing counts per document and what was corrected.

You receive the finished Excel file and a short note on any page that was unclear in the scan.
