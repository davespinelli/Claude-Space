## Proposal

Word is the awkward part here, not the typing. Across a batch of product documents some values sit in tables and others in paragraphs, and the same field is labelled two or three ways. A copy and paste pass through that puts values in the wrong columns, and those mistakes are hard to see once they are in a grid.

Plan:
- Agree the target columns and which field is the unique key, usually a SKU or model code.
- Read the documents programmatically, handling table and paragraph layouts, so nothing depends on how one was typed.
- Normalise units, sizes and prices to one format, keeping numbers as numbers and codes as text.
- List duplicate keys and products missing a required field rather than filling the gap with a guess.
- Spot check a sample against the source documents and report the measured error rate.

You receive the clean workbook, an exceptions sheet and the extraction script, so the next batch is a re-run not a re-order.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
