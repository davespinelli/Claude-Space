## Proposal

Tracing who owns a Montreal property usually stops at the same wall: the registre foncier gives you a name, and the name is a numbered company. The chain only closes when the registry record is joined to the enterprise register, and that join fails on address and lot formatting far more often than on the lookup itself.

Plan:
- Pull from published public sources only: Registre foncier du Quebec, the Registraire des entreprises, and the city assessment roll.
- Normalise lot numbers and civic addresses first, because that is where the records refuse to join.
- Resolve corporate owners through the enterprise register to named principals where the record supports it.
- Score each chain by confidence instead of guessing, so you know which ones need a human to finish.
- Ship it as a rerunnable pipeline with a cached data layer, not a one-time extract.

Deliverables: the dataset, the pipeline source, and a short note on what each confidence level means. Public records only, no logged-in sources and no personal contact details.
