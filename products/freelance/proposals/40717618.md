## Proposal

With scanned pages the risk is not the text, it is the numbers. OCR rarely refuses outright. It quietly turns a 5 into an S or drops a decimal, and the sheet still looks finished. So the job is worth doing only if every numeric field is checked rather than trusted.

Plan:
- Run a sample of your scans first and tell you the read rate before quoting any volume beyond this batch.
- Extract text and numbers page by page, keeping page and line references against every row.
- Validate the numbers: expected ranges, decimal places, and column or row totals where the page has them.
- Flag every low-confidence field in its own column instead of silently guessing.
- Hand back the rows I could not read clearly, with the page reference, rather than filling them in.

You receive the spreadsheet, the flagged list, and the extraction script so later batches cost you less.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many pages, and are these printed scans or handwritten? Handwriting changes the accuracy I can promise, so I would want samples first.
