## Proposal

The words "without losing" are the whole job here. Email body text pasted into a cell loses structure in three specific ways: line breaks collapse so paragraphs run together, a reply chain repeats the earlier message underneath, and a line starting with an equals or plus sign is read as a formula. A long message can also exceed what one cell holds and gets cut without warning.

Plan:
- Read the folder directly from .eml, .msg or mbox rather than opening messages by hand.
- Keep the body as text: line breaks preserved inside the cell, leading symbols escaped, nothing silently truncated.
- Strip the quoted reply chain and signature into their own columns, so the new content is separable from the repeat.
- Add subject, sender and date columns plus a source reference, so any row can be traced back to its message.
- Run a count check, emails in against rows out, and list what did not parse.

Deliverables: the workbook, the exception list, and the script.
