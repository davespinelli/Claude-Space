## Proposal

Mixed text and numeric values in the same column is the specific problem here. Numbers stored as text with stray currency symbols, thousands separators and trailing spaces will silently break every sum and every join until they are fixed at the source.

Plan:
- Profile every file and column first, and send you a short report on what is actually wrong before I change anything.
- Normalise types: numerics parsed properly, dates to one format, text trimmed and case standardised.
- Handle duplicates and near duplicates with a rule you approve, never a silent drop.
- Put rows I cannot confidently clean into a separate exceptions file instead of guessing at them.
- Deliver clean CSV or Excel plus the Python script, so your next batch of files takes minutes rather than days.

You receive the cleaned files, the exceptions file, a before and after summary of row and column counts, and the script.

Example of my Python data work with tests: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space

Two questions: how many files and roughly how many rows in total? Should duplicate rows be removed or only flagged for you?
