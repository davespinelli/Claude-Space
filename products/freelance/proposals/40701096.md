## Proposal

A patchwork of manual steps usually hides one real problem: nobody has written down what makes a prospect a record. Your team resolves that by hand every time, which is why the steps never automate cleanly. So the first thing I build is not the agent, it is the matching and deduplication rule, because an agent writing duplicates into a CRM costs more time than the manual process it replaced.

Plan:
- Map the current path from form and inbox to CRM, including the judgement calls people make along the way.
- Define the matching rule for an existing contact or company and confirm it against real recent records.
- Build the agent to extract, match and write, with anything ambiguous held for a one-click human decision.
- Write back to your CRM through its own API so nothing bypasses your field validation.
- Log every write with its source, so a wrong record can be found and reversed.

You receive the running automation, the mapping document and the decision log.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
