## Proposal

An RSI bot lives or dies on when the threshold cross is evaluated. If it is read on a bar that is still forming, the backtest fills at prices that were never available and the live account comes in well under the curve. The fix is to decide at the bar close and act on the next bar, in the test and the live loop, from one shared signal module.

Plan:
- Implement RSI with your period and your entry and exit thresholds, decided at close, orders placed next bar.
- Backtest several years with commission and slippage and no look-ahead.
- Report CAGR, Sharpe, max drawdown, trade count and the worst stretch, plus both halves of the sample separately.
- Wire the same signal module to the broker API in paper mode, with position and exposure caps.
- Schedule it so it runs without your machine, and leave a runbook.

Deliverables: the source, the backtest report, and the paper bot.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
