## Proposal

"Easy to update" is the requirement that decides how this gets built. Most Excel sales dashboards look right on delivery and are abandoned by month three, because adding the new month means re-pointing chart ranges and rewriting formulas, and whoever inherits the file will not do that. The fix is structural: one append-only data sheet, structured tables underneath everything, and charts driven off pivots so a new month is a paste rather than an edit.

Plan:
- One raw sales sheet you append to, with the column set fixed and validated.
- Structured tables and pivot caches so every chart and total expands on its own.
- Dashboard tab: revenue trend, by rep, by product or region, target versus actual, period selector.
- Data checks tab flagging blanks, bad dates and unmapped categories before they reach a chart.
- Short update guide showing exactly what to paste where each month.

You get the workbook, the checks tab and the update guide. Native Excel only, no add-ins to install.
