## Proposal

You state plainly why the bot is currently shut down, which saves the first two days of any review. With an options bot the failure is nearly always in the gap between the backtest and the fill: quotes taken at mid, contracts assumed liquid at size, expiry and assignment handled on paper but not in the account.

Plan:
- Re-run your logic against real historical option data with bid and ask fills rather than mid, plus commission and slippage.
- Rebuild the entry and exit rules exactly as coded, so the test matches the bot and not the intent behind it.
- Check for look-ahead: any value the bot uses that it could not have known at decision time.
- Separate the part of the edge that survives real fills from the part that was an artefact.
- Fix what is fixable in the code and say plainly what is not.

You receive the validation report with charts, the corrected bot code, and the pipeline that produced the numbers.

Proof: my backtest engine and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
