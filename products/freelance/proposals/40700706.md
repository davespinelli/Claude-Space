## Proposal

A sales entry form is easy. The failure is on the other side: two people enter the same day, one of them edits an old figure, and the consolidated sheet no longer agrees with what the team thinks it submitted. That is a keys and logging problem, not a form problem.

Plan:
- Build the entry form with real validation, so a period, a region or a product code cannot be typed free hand.
- Give every submission a key of date plus whoever entered it, so a resubmission updates the existing row rather than adding a second.
- Write submissions to one clean data sheet that nobody edits by hand, keeping the form and the store separate.
- Add a consolidation view with totals by period and by whatever you group on, refreshing from the data sheet.
- Log who submitted what and when, so a disputed number can be traced back.

You get the workbook, the form, the data sheet, the consolidated view and a one page note.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
