## Proposal

Most lists that look like manufacturer directories are mostly traders and repackagers, because the listing category is self declared. For supplier outreach or market sizing the count is wrong before you use it, and the plants you most want are the small ones no directory carries.

Plan:
- Agree the definition first: own plant, contract manufacturer, or brand owner, and which products count.
- Build from sources that state manufacturing status rather than claim it: registry filings, factory and licence listings, association members, company sites.
- Capture company level fields only: legal name, plant location, brands, product categories, approximate scale, website, listed office number. No named individuals or personal contact details.
- Deduplicate on registration number rather than name, since one plant appears under several spellings.
- Hand check a random sample and report the measured accuracy rate instead of claiming completeness.

You receive the database as Excel and CSV, a source column on every row, the dedupe log and the sample check.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: all of India or specific states, and do you want only makers of finished consumer product, or also bulk and private label suppliers?
