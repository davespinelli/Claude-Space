## Proposal

Moving text fields between spreadsheets sounds like typing, but the failures are always structural. Two source files use different column orders. One has names as "Last, First" and another as "First Last". Trailing spaces and mixed capitalisation make the same value look like two values once it lands in your central sheet, and nobody notices until a lookup misses.

Plan:
- Look at every source spreadsheet and map each column to its destination field.
- Normalise as we go: trim spaces, fix casing, split or join name and address fields to one agreed shape.
- Load into your central file with a source column on each row so anything odd is traceable back.
- Run duplicate and blank checks, and hand you a short exceptions list rather than silently deciding.
- Repeat the transfer with a script so the next batch is minutes rather than another job.

You receive the populated central file, the exceptions list and the transfer script.
