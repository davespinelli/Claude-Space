## Proposal

Round the clock replies are worth having only if the wrong answer at 3am costs less than no answer. That is a content problem before it is a bot problem: the assistant has to answer from your own pages and prices, say plainly when it does not know, and hand the visitor to a human path rather than inventing something you then have to correct.

Plan:
- Index your site content and any FAQ or policy documents, so answers are grounded in your material and cite the page they came from.
- Set explicit refusal rules for pricing, availability and anything account specific, with a handoff to email or a form instead.
- Build the widget as a small script tag, so it drops into your existing site without a rebuild.
- Log every conversation with its answer source, and give you a weekly list of questions the bot could not answer, which is what improves it.
- Test against a question set we agree up front, including the ones it should refuse.

You receive the widget, the indexing pipeline, the conversation log and admin view, and a short guide for updating the content.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
