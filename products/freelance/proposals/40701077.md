## Proposal

Mixed text and numeric columns are where cleaning usually does quiet damage. Numbers stored as text get coerced, a value like "0012" loses its zeros, and "N/A", blank and "-" all become the same thing when they may mean different things in your data. So before changing anything I write down the rule for each column and show you the rows it will touch.

Plan:
- Profile every column: type, blanks, duplicates, outliers and inconsistent formats.
- Agree a written rule per column with you, including what counts as missing.
- Apply the rules in a script rather than by hand, so the same file can be cleaned again next month.
- Keep a change log listing every cell altered, with its old and new value.
- Deliver the cleaned workbook with the original preserved on a separate tab.

You receive the cleaned file, the change log, the profiling summary and the script.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
