## Proposal

Budget workbooks that grew over time almost always drift apart. The same expense is called two things in two files, one workbook runs January to December across columns while another keeps a row per transaction, and a few amounts sit in cells formatted as text so they never enter a total. Typed into one sheet without checking that first, the combined figures no longer match the originals.

Plan:
- Read every workbook first and send you a short list of the category names in use, including the near duplicates.
- Agree one line shape: date, category, description, income, expense, source file.
- Enter each line with the file and sheet it came from, so any figure can be traced back.
- Reconcile each workbook's own totals against the lines I transferred from it, which catches a skipped block at once.
- List anything ambiguous for you to decide rather than filing it in the nearest category.

You get the combined workbook, a category summary by month, per file reconciliation and a queries list.

Work I run publicly: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many workbooks and roughly how many lines, and do you want the original files left untouched?

David

## Delivery plan
1. Day 1: read the workbooks, send the category list and the proposed line shape.
2. Day 2: enter the largest workbook, confirm the shape works before doing the rest.
3. Day 3: enter the remaining workbooks and build the monthly summary.
4. Day 4: reconcile every source total, list the queries, hand over.
5. After handover: one round of corrections on whatever the queries resolve.
