## Proposal

You describe raw information that has to be typed and organised into a workbook. The part that usually goes wrong is not the typing, it is consistency: the same customer name entered three ways, dates in mixed formats, and a column that ends up half text and half number. That makes the workbook hard to sort or filter later, which is normally the reason it was built.

Plan:
- Agree the column layout and the format rules for dates, names and numbers before entry starts.
- Enter the records into that layout, keeping one row per record and no merged cells.
- Run a check pass for duplicates, blank required fields and values that do not match their column type.
- Send you a short list of anything ambiguous rather than guessing at it.
- Deliver the workbook plus a one page note on the rules used.

You receive the completed Excel file and the exceptions list.

Public example of my data work: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space

Two questions: what form is the raw information in, and roughly how many records are there?
