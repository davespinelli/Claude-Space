## Proposal

If the logic already works, the bridge is where the money is lost. IB drops the connection overnight, TWS restarts on its own schedule, and an order that was acknowledged but never filled leaves your logic believing it holds a position it does not. Reconnection and reconciliation matter more than order routing here.

Plan:
- Wrap IB in one interface your logic calls, so the strategy never sees a socket, a request id or a client id.
- Handle the connection properly: reconnect, resubscribe, and refuse to trade until positions have been read back from IB.
- Reconcile on every start and on a timer, comparing IB positions and open orders against what your logic thinks it has.
- Enforce exposure and order size caps in the bridge, so a logic bug cannot send an outsized order.
- Log every request and fill to CSV, and run it paper first until a full session is clean.

You get the bridge module, the reconciliation and limits, the logs and a runbook.

Proof: my public trading pipeline and bot code at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: is your logic in Python already, and are you on TWS or IB Gateway with paper access available?
