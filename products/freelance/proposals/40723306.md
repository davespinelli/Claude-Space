## Proposal

The failure that ruins a TradingView indicator is repainting. If the logic is evaluated on the bar that is still forming, the signal that appeared at 10:03 can be gone by the close, and the history you scroll back through shows entries that were never actually available. It looks excellent on the chart and does nothing in real trading.

Plan:
- Decide every signal on closed bars, and state in the code exactly where intrabar updating is allowed and where it is not.
- Verify the plotted history against what the indicator would have shown live, bar by bar.
- Wire the alerts to the same condition that draws the signal, so an alert and a plotted arrow cannot disagree.
- Expose the thresholds as inputs with sane bounds, so you can change the rules without me.
- Comment the source, since an indicator you cannot read is one you cannot trust.

Deliverables: the Pine Script source, the alert conditions, and a short note on what it does and does not repaint.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
