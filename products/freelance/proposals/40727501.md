## Proposal

A forex strategy that tests well in PineScript and then disappoints live usually did not fail on the rules. It failed on what the strategy tester leaves at zero. Spread and overnight swap default to nothing, which on a pair held across sessions is most of the edge, and a signal read while the bar is still forming can change before the close, so the history shows entries that were never available.

Plan:
- Write your rules out as a checklist first, entry, exit, stop, size, session filter, and confirm it before any code.
- Evaluate on closed bars only, with no lookahead in higher timeframe requests.
- Set spread, commission and swap per pair to your broker's real numbers rather than leaving them blank.
- Report the full period and each half separately, plus the worst months, so the result is not one lucky stretch.

Deliverables: the Pine strategy file, the settings used, and a short written report.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which pairs and timeframe, and do you already have the strategy in Pine or is it a written description?
