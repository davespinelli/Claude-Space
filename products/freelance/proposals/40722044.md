## Proposal

Loading financial data into EPM from an ERP rarely fails at the load step. It fails at the mapping layer: an account is renamed, a new cost centre appears, and the load either rejects the rows or posts them to the wrong member.

Plan:
- Reproduce one successful manual load end to end before changing anything.
- Build the extract and transform in Python: ERP export to a validated, EPM ready file, with every member mapping held in one table you can edit yourself.
- Drive the load through EPM Automate so it runs unattended, with import and validate checked as separate steps.
- Fail loudly. Unmapped members, out of balance totals and rejected rows go into a report before anything posts.
- Schedule it and write the runbook so your team can rerun a period without me.

You get the script, the mapping table, the exception report and the runbook.

Proof of my scheduled pipeline work: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which EPM module is this, and is EPM Automate available to a service account? And can your ERP produce a scheduled flat file export, or do I read it through an API?

David
