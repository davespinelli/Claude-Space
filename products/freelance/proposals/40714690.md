## Proposal

Images of plain text look like the easy case, but the errors they produce are the hard kind to find. O against 0, 1 against l, rn against m. A hand-typed sheet gives you no way to know which cells went wrong, so the checking ends up costing more than the typing did.

Plan:
- Run every image through text recognition, then a second independent pass, and compare the two.
- Flag any cell where the two passes disagree, or where confidence is low, for manual review.
- Apply your column rules and formats so the output loads straight into your sheet.
- Hand-check every flagged cell plus a random sample of the clean ones.
- Report the disagreement rate per batch so you can see the accuracy rather than take my word for it.

You receive the completed Excel workbook, a flagged-cells sheet showing exactly what was reviewed and changed, and the script if you want to run later batches yourself.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many images are there in total, and are they clean screenshots or photographs of printed pages?
