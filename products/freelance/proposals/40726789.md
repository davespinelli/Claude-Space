## Proposal

Most backlogs are not uniform. Some of the records arrive in a form a script can read straight through, and the rest genuinely need a person looking at them. Quoting the whole pile as typing means paying human rates for the part a script clears in an hour, and it means the same backlog builds again next quarter. I would rather split it on day one and tell you honestly which share is which.

Plan:
- Take a sample of the backlog and sort it into what can be read automatically and what cannot.
- Clear the readable share with a script, then check a sample of its output against the source.
- Key the remainder by hand, with the validation rules agreed up front.
- Normalise the whole set: real dates, real numbers, one spelling per name.
- Hand back a short note on what caused the backlog and what would stop it recurring.

Deliverables: the completed sheet, a flagged list of anything ambiguous, and the note.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many records are waiting, and what form do they arrive in?
