## Proposal

Customer analysis usually stalls on definitions rather than on SQL. Whether a customer counts as active at 30 or 90 days, whether refunds are netted out of revenue, and whether repeat purchases within one day count once or twice will each move the headline numbers more than any modelling choice. So I agree those first and write them down.

Plan:
- Agree the definitions: active customer, cohort start, revenue, and the reporting period.
- Write the SQL to pull a clean customer level table, one row per customer per period.
- Do the analysis in Python: cohorts and retention, repeat rate, revenue concentration, segment breakdown.
- Sanity check totals against your own reported figures before drawing any conclusion.
- Deliver a short written summary with the charts, plus the SQL and notebook.

You receive the summary, the charts, and the code so it can be re-run next quarter.

Public example of my analysis work: https://github.com/davespinelli/Claude-Space
