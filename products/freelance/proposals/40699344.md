## Proposal

Specifying local storage for the patient phone intake prototype is the right call, and it is worth being deliberate about it. Once intake notes contain patient identifiers, anything that quietly syncs to a cloud service turns a prototype into a compliance problem. Building it local first keeps that decision yours rather than a default.

Plan:
- Intake form that captures the fields your reception staff actually read out, with required fields enforced at entry.
- Local storage in an encrypted SQLite file on the machine, with no outbound network calls in the prototype.
- Structured record per call: timestamp, caller, reason, actions, follow-up flag.
- Search and export to CSV or Excel, so records reach your existing systems without retyping.
- A written note on what would need to change before this holds real patient data in production.

You get the application, the schema, an install and backup guide, and the export tooling.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
