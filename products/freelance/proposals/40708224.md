## Proposal

Across three to five sheets of text entries, the duplicates that matter are almost never exact. Remove Duplicates catches byte-identical rows only, so it leaves the trailing space, the different capitalisation, the extra middle initial and the same entry typed with and without a suffix. Delete on exact match and the file looks cleaner while the real repeats survive.

Plan:
- Normalise before comparing: trim whitespace, collapse internal spaces, unify case and punctuation, strip invisible characters from pasted text.
- Remove exact duplicates first and report how many, per sheet.
- Find near-duplicates by similarity and group them into candidate sets rather than deleting on a guess.
- Send you the candidate sets to approve, then apply the approved merges and keep the survivor you choose.
- Merge the sheets into one master if that is the goal, with a source column so any row traces back.

You get the cleaned sheets, the master file, a log of every removal, and the script so the next batch runs the same way.

Work I run in public: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many rows across the sheets, and should near-duplicates be merged automatically above a confidence level or always reviewed by you?
