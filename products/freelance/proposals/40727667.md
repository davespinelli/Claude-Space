## Proposal

The source is already in Excel, so this is a transfer rather than typing, and the failure that survives a transfer is the type. Mixed text and numbers is exactly where it bites. A reference with a leading zero turns into a shorter number, an amount with a trailing space or a comma decimal stays text, and the cell looks correct while every total, sort and filter quietly leaves it out. Nobody sees it until a figure does not add up.

Plan:
- Agree the target layout and the intended type for each column before anything is moved.
- Move the records by script, so the same run repeats identically on the next file.
- Keep references and codes as text with leading zeros intact, and force amounts and dates to real numbers and dates.
- Reconcile row counts in and out, and column totals against the source.
- List anything that will not convert cleanly rather than entering a guess.

Deliverables: the completed workbook, the exception list, and the script.
