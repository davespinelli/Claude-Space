## Proposal

Self-contained is the right instinct, because it keeps you off a monthly platform for a widget that should be one file. What decides whether visitors trust it is not the model, it is what the widget does when the question is not covered by your content. A bot that guesses once gets ignored after that.

Plan:
- Index your existing pages, FAQ or docs so every answer is grounded in your own wording.
- Build the widget as a single script tag you paste into the site, with no account and no subscription.
- Route anything outside the indexed content to a fallback, a contact form or a handover line, never a guess.
- Log every question asked so you can see what visitors actually want and fill the gaps.
- Match the widget to your colours and check it on mobile.

You receive the widget, the indexing script, the question log and a short guide for updating answers yourself.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which pages or documents should the bot answer from, and do you already have a model API key, since that usage bills to you directly?
