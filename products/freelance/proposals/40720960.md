## Proposal

Paragraphs do not have columns. That is the whole decision here: before anything gets transcribed, somebody has to say what one row is. A row per paragraph, a row per document, or a row per fact pulled out of the text are three different sheets, and only one of them is the one you can sort and filter later. Getting that wrong means the work is accurate and still unusable.

Plan:
- Agree the row definition and the column list against two sample documents first.
- Check whether the PDFs carry a real text layer, in which case the text is extracted rather than retyped and nothing is mistyped.
- Transcribe the batch with a file and page reference on every row.
- Keep the original wording intact, with any correction noted separately rather than applied silently.
- Check for dropped or duplicated sections by counting documents in against rows out.

You get the workbook, the reference columns and a short note on the row rules.

Proof: https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
