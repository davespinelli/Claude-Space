## Proposal

What decides whether an operations dashboard survives past the first month is not the charts, it is what sits underneath them. If the visuals point at a fixed cell range, next month's export lands outside it and the dashboard keeps showing old numbers with no sign it has stopped updating.

Plan:
- Agree the four or five measures the dashboard exists to answer, before building anything.
- Put the raw export on its own sheet as a structured table, so added rows are picked up automatically.
- Build the measures in a calculation layer, each one traceable back to the raw rows.
- Lay out the dashboard: current period, trend, and the breakdown that explains a bad number.
- Add a refresh check that shows the date of the latest row, so stale data is visible on the page.

You receive the workbook with raw, calculation and dashboard sheets separated, and a one-page note on how to drop in next month's export.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: what does the raw operational data look like and how often does it arrive, and which decisions do you want the dashboard to support?
