## Proposal

When rows are gathered from the web by hand, the sheet ends up with no record of where any single value came from, so nothing can be rechecked later without doing the whole job again. Adding a source column costs nothing during the work and makes the file worth keeping.

Plan:
- Confirm the template columns and the list of sources, then do ten rows first for you to approve the reading.
- Collect only publicly accessible pages, nothing behind a login, a paywall or a captcha.
- Fill each row with the source URL and the date collected alongside the values.
- Keep text fields as text so codes and reference numbers do not lose leading zeros.
- Check the finished sheet for blanks, duplicate rows and values outside plausible ranges.

You receive the completed template, the source column, and a list of any row I could not find a reliable public source for, rather than a guess in its place. If the sources are structured enough to pull automatically, I will script it and hand you the script.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many rows, and which sites are the data coming from?
