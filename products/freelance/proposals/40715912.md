## Proposal

Scan data programs pay on files that validate, not on effort. One UPC in your POS that is not on the manufacturer's item list, or a week-ending date that does not match their calendar, and the whole weekly submission is rejected rather than partially accepted. So what decides whether this earns anything is the item mapping and the validation, not the screen it launches from.

Plan:
- Map your POS items to the Altria, RJR and MSAi item lists once, and keep the unmatched SKUs visible as a working list.
- Build the weekly extract for each programme in its required layout and week-ending convention.
- Validate before sending: required fields present, every UPC known, totals reconciling to your sales.
- Expose it as a small API your React Native app calls, so the app side stays a thin screen.
- Log each submission and its response so a rejection is traceable.

You receive the mapping, the three extracts, the validation report and the API.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: which POS database do the sales sit in, and who maintains the React Native codebase, you or a developer I would coordinate with?
