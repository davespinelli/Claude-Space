## Proposal

Transcribing a batch of emails by hand gives you a spreadsheet that is right once. The problem shows up later, when the same sender writes their address two ways, dates land as text in three formats, and the body field holds a whole message where you wanted one value. Emails already carry structure, so most of this can be read out rather than retyped.

Plan:
- Take a sample of ten messages first and agree the exact columns, including what counts as one row.
- Pull the reliable fields straight from the messages: sender, recipient, date, subject and thread id.
- Extract the body fields you need against agreed rules, and mark anything ambiguous for review rather than guessing.
- Normalise as I go: one date format, consistent spelling, duplicates flagged rather than deleted.
- Check a random sample against the source messages and report the error rate.

You receive the clean spreadsheet, a flagged-items tab, and a note on the extraction rules used.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many messages are in the batch, and can you export them as files or do I work from forwarded copies?
