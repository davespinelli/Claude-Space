## Proposal

A retrieval bot is judged on the questions it should refuse. Answering from your documents is the easy half; the half that costs you is a confident answer to something your content never covered. So the retrieval step needs a floor, and below it the bot says it does not know and offers the contact route.

Plan:
- Load your sources once, split them so a chunk is a whole idea rather than a fixed number of characters, and keep the page reference with each chunk.
- Retrieve, then answer only from what came back, with the source shown under the reply.
- Set a similarity floor so a weak match returns a handoff, not an invention.
- Log every question with what was retrieved, so you can see what people ask and what your content is missing.
- Ship it as a web widget plus a simple API endpoint your app can call.

You get the ingestion script, the bot, the widget, the logs and a note on adding content.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
