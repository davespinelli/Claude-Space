## Proposal

Billing records carry two fields that decide whether the workbook is usable later: the patient identifier and the date of service. Both are the ones most often mangled, because identifiers with leading zeros lose them in Excel and dates written one way in the source get read another way by the sheet. Those two quietly break the match back to the record.

Plan:
- Confirm the exact column layout and which fields must stay text rather than become numbers or dates.
- Transcribe each record with its source file and page recorded in a reference column.
- Parse dates explicitly against the source format instead of letting Excel guess.
- Check the result: duplicate identifiers, missing required fields, dates outside a plausible range.
- Return a list of anything unreadable in the source rather than filling in a best guess.

You receive the workbook, the exceptions list, and a short note on how each field was handled.

I keep the files only for the length of the job and delete them on delivery.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
