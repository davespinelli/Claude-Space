## Proposal

On long-term entry work the cost is not the first batch, it is week six, when two people have keyed the same field three different ways and nobody notices until a report looks wrong. That is worth fixing on day one, while the sheet is still small.

Plan:
- Set up the workbook with typed columns, dropdown lists for anything repeated, and date and number formats fixed rather than free text.
- Add validation that rejects out of range values at the point of entry, so errors surface as refusals instead of plausible numbers.
- Key the current batch against that structure and flag anything the source does not resolve.
- Return a short queries list rather than guessing at unclear entries.
- Leave a one page note on how to add the next batch without breaking the validation.

You receive the completed workbook, the validation setup, the queries list, and that note.

Proof: my public repo and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: roughly how many rows are in the first batch, and what is the source, PDFs, images, or another sheet?
