## Proposal

Turning a raw sales export into a decision ready workbook usually stalls on the cleanup, not the charts. Product names spelled three ways, dates stored as text, and returns sitting as positive rows all move the totals. Fixed by hand, the workbook is right once and wrong after the next export.

Plan:
- Profile the raw file first and send you a short list of what is actually wrong, with row counts, so you can see the scale before I change anything.
- Clean it with rules rather than edits: consistent date and number types, normalised product and region labels, deduplication, and returns handled the way you specify.
- Build the summary sheet: revenue and units by period, by product and by region, with the totals reconciling back to the raw file.
- Add the charts that answer a question, trend over time, top products, and regional split, sized to print.
- Keep the raw sheet untouched so every number is traceable to source.

You receive the workbook with raw, clean, summary and chart sheets, plus a note on the cleaning rules applied.

Proof: my public repo and live dashboard at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/

Two questions: how many rows and what period does the file cover, and which decisions should the charts support?
