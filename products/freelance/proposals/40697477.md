## Proposal

The identify step is where most futures strategy work quietly goes wrong. Test enough rule variants against the same contract history and several will look profitable, because you have fitted the sample rather than found an edge. The forward test is the part that means something, and only if the rule was frozen in writing before the forward window started.

Plan:
- Agree the instrument, session hours, bar size and the contract roll method, since the roll alone changes the result.
- Build a vectorized backtest with commission and slippage, signals decided at bar close and applied on the next bar, so there is no look ahead.
- Test the candidate rules in sample, then keep only what survives on the held out half and a walk forward.
- Freeze the surviving rule, then forward test it on data it has never seen and report that separately.
- Report honestly, including the versions that failed.

You receive the engine, the frozen rule config, the data cache and a written report.

Proof: my backtester and live pipeline at https://davespinelli.github.io/Claude-Space/
