## Proposal

A sales chatbot on a business site rarely fails on the answers it knows. It fails on the ones it does not. Asked about a price, a lead time or an edge case that is not written down anywhere, a general model will produce a confident number, and your visitor will hold you to it. The part that has to be built deliberately is the boundary: what it answers from your own content, and what it hands to a person.

Plan:
- Ground every answer in your own pages and documents, retrieved first and quoted back, so nothing is invented.
- Set explicit handoff rules for pricing, availability and anything outside that content, routing to a form or a human.
- Capture name, email and stated intent from qualified chats into a sheet or your CRM.
- Log transcripts so you can see the questions that had no answer and add them.
- Install as a single script tag and test it on the live site before handover.

Deliverables: the working chatbot, the content index, the lead capture, and a short runbook.
