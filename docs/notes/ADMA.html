1. What the business actually does

ADMA fractionates human plasma at one FDA-licensed plant in Boca Raton, Florida, peak capacity 600,000 litres a year (10-K, Item 1). ASCENIV, a patented IVIG blended from donor plasma pre-screened for high RSV-neutralising titres (found in under 10% of samples tested) and carrying a permanent J-code, is later-line therapy for primary immunodeficiency patients failing standard IG. BIVIGAM is a commodity IVIG; Nabi-HB a small hepatitis B hyperimmune. A plasma-collection segment ran ten centres at end-2025; three were sold for $12.0M with long-term supply agreements attached.

2. Why it is mispriced — the edge case

A mix shift makes the income statement read worse than the business is. Q2 revenue grew 2.0% while gross margin went from 55.1% to 69.4% and operating income rose 22.5% to $52.4M (10-Q, Item 2). Screens keyed on revenue see a stalled compounder; screens keyed on FY2025 cash flow see a 1.4% FCF yield that no longer exists. The stock is down 45.6% in six months and 57.8% from its high (universe_v2.csv). The sellers are growth holders who owned the narrative of 50%-plus ASCENIV growth and ">$775 million" of 2027 revenue, repriced when management withdrew that frame in May. They are not wrong to sell a broken growth story; they may be indifferent to what remains, a 31% normalised-ROIC business at 11.9x buying back stock worth about 10% of its market value this year.

3. Unit economics and growth

ASCENIV was $362.5M of FY2025 revenue (+51%) and $200.4M in H1 2026 (+25.5%); BIVIGAM fell from $142.4M in 2024 to $122.0M in 2025 to $34.8M in H1 2026 (10-K Item 7, 10-Q Item 2). Gross margin is 69.4% on ASCENIV mix plus the April 2025 yield-enhancement approval, which lifts bulk yield "by 20% or more from the same starting source plasma volume" (10-K, Item 1). Normalised ROIC is 30.9% (universe_v2.csv). H1 2026 operating cash flow was $87.8M with investing a net $0.1M inflow, so essentially all of it was free (10-Q, Cash Flows), though inventory absorbed $32.8M, structural on a seven-to-twelve-month production cycle. BioCare and CuraScript were 73% of FY2025 revenue and 87% of year-end receivables (10-K, Item 1A). DSOs were 101 days, from 107 (Q2 call, CFO).

The arithmetic that matters: the $530M low end needs H2 revenue near $291M against H1's $238.9M, a 22% half-on-half step. Management's building blocks are ASCENIV "upper 20s to low 30%" growth and BIVIGAM "down roughly 40% to 50% from 2025" (Q2 call, CEO). ASCENIV at +28% implies about $464M for the year, so roughly $264M in H2, up 32% on H1; compounding the 2% to 4% monthly growth management guides to off Q2's roughly $34M a month gets to about $240M-$250M. The guide is reachable but needs the top of both ranges, and needs ASCENIV's year-over-year growth to accelerate from 25.5% in H1 to about 30% in H2.

4. Balance sheet and capital allocation

$300M of JPM facilities: a $75M term loan drawn August 2025 and $125M drawn on the revolver on 2 March 2026 to fund an accelerated repurchase, at about 6.12%, maturing August 2028, secured on substantially all assets, roughly $100M undrawn (10-Q). Buybacks are the main use of cash: 10.76M ASR shares plus 3.06M open-market in H1 2026, 5.3% of the company, against a stated "$200 million or more" 2026 target (10-Q); share count is down 6.1% year over year. Insiders own 3.9% as a group, CEO Adam Grossman 1.9%, BlackRock 15.5% (DEF 14A). Form 4s show net selling of $7,866 over twelve months, effectively neutral; director Steven Elms bought 7,000 shares at $15.67 on 2026-03-05, now 45% underwater. Long-term equity is time-based, not performance-vesting (DEF 14A, CD&A); 2025 CEO pay was $10.2M.

5. Management: what they said versus what they did

The 10-K filed 2026-02-25 stated the plant supported "projected annual revenues greater than $635 million in 2026 and $775 million in 2027" and 2026 adjusted EBITDA "exceeding $360 million" (Item 1). On 2026-05-06 the company guided FY2026 to $530M-$560M and adjusted EBITDA to $265M-$300M and withdrew long-term guidance, citing an IG market "reset" and "aggressive discounting and rebating in standard IG" (8-K): a 17% revenue and 22% EBITDA cut ten weeks after publication. A March 2026 short report drew a Reg FD rebuttal arguing ASCENIV is late-line and "in a different category" (8-K, 2026-03-27); the cut came two months later on the standard-IG side. Credit where due: the reduced guide was reiterated in August with H1 tracking to it, and the pediatric label expansion was approved on schedule (8-K, 2026-05-04).

6. Valuation

Base (50%): FY2026 near $540M; FY2027 ASCENIV +20% to about $565M with BIVIGAM flat near $65M, giving $637M at a 45% normalised operating margin, about $218M after tax at 24%; at 13x with net debt retired, roughly $13.50 a share. Bear (30%): competitors discount into the later-line niche, ASCENIV decelerates to 10%, FY2027 revenue $560M at a 38% margin, $162M after tax at 8x, roughly $5.70. Bull (20%): ASCENIV holds 30% and BIVIGAM stabilises, FY2027 revenue $700M at a 47% margin, $250M after tax at 15x, roughly $17.50. Probability-weighted $11.95 against $8.61, about 39% upside. Reverse DCF: at $8.61 the $1.99B EV against $166.9M of normalised after-tax operating profit implies roughly 1% perpetual growth at a 9% cost of capital, so the price already assumes ASCENIV's growth is permanently and fully offset by standard-IG erosion.

7. Catalysts and timeline

Q3 2026 results in early November, the first clean test of the H2 ramp. The ACAAI presentation in November of the 127-patient real-world outcomes analysis. Completion of the $200M buyback by year end. The SG-001 pre-IND package to FDA by year end, a claimed $300M-$500M opportunity absent from all three cases above.

8. Risks and pre-registered kill criteria

Two customers are 73% of revenue and one plant makes everything, in a hurricane-exposed state with no alternative manufacturing arrangement (10-K, Item 1A). Kill criteria: 1. Q3 2026 ASCENIV revenue below $120M, putting the reiterated guide out of reach and marking June's acceleration as inventory rather than demand. 2. Consolidated gross margin below 62% for two consecutive quarters, indicating ASCENIV pricing is being competed down rather than only BIVIGAM. 3. FY2026 revenue guidance cut a second time, or the $200M buyback target abandoned. 4. DSOs above 110 days for two consecutive quarters, or a receivable write-off at either large distributor.

9. Verdict and summary

WATCH, conviction 3. ADMA is a 31% return-on-capital plasma business whose headline revenue went flat because a commodity product collapsed inside it while its patented, differentiated product kept compounding at 24%, and the market prices the blend at 11.9x normalised after-tax operating profit with a quarter turn of leverage and a buyback worth about 10% of the market cap running this year. The reason this is a WATCH and not an IDEA is not the valuation, it is the forecast: management published 2027 targets in February and withdrew them in May, and my arithmetic on their own building blocks puts the reiterated FY2026 guide at the top of both ranges rather than comfortably inside them, requiring ASCENIV's growth to accelerate in the second half. The Q3 print in early November resolves that cheaply.
