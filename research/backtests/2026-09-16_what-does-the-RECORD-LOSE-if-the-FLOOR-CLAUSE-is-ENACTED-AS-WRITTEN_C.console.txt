## D1 — THE PREMISE'S 4.88 IS NOT THE CLAUSE'S TIE SET (a CORRECTION to 1102)
  1102's committed tie_set column is `mass_set(counts, rungs, 0.90)` — the smallest
  CONTIGUOUS set of rungs carrying 0.90 of the BOOTSTRAP ARGMAX MASS — computed ONCE
  at a fixed 0.90 and written into every q row.  It is NOT the set the clause as
  written publishes (the rungs within the ladder's FLOOR of the argmax), and it
  never consults q.  Two consequences, both measured here:
    (i)  1102's committed tie_set_width does NOT move with q: 0 of 32 cells differ between q=0.80 and q=0.95, while its own capped FLOOR moves in 15 of 32.  THIS run's floor tie set moves in 17 of 32, mean set size 3.78 / 4.69 / 5.06 at q = 0.80 / 0.90 / 0.95.
    (ii) the premise's headline reproduces EXACTLY as a mass-set figure over the 26 TIE cells only:
         mean width 4.885 rungs, 0.851 of its ladder, 12 of 32 WHOLE-LADDER — the queue's 4.88 / 0.851 / 12.
         The CLAUSE's own set, over all 32 cells: mean 4.688 of 6.75 rungs (0.787 of its ladder), 18 of 32 CONTENTLESS.
  THE CORRECTION, and it runs AGAINST the clause: enacted as written the clause
  costs MORE than 1102 published — 18 of 32 committed argmaxes lose all content, not 12.
