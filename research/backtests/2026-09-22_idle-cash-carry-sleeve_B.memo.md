# KEEP-candidate memo — idea 2294, IDLE-CASH CARRY SLEEVE (lane B, 2026-09-22, PATH 4a)

1. **Defect.** `engine.backtest` drifts the un-invested residual at a flat `(1 - cur.sum())` with **no return attached**, and RULES v2 runs at gross 0.75 and de-grosses further on every gated-out name: measured mean gross is **0.5327 (U56) / 0.5322 (B136)**, so the live book leaves **46.7% / 46.8% of NAV earning exactly 0%** in a backtest, which no real account does.
2. **Rule priced.** Route phi of that idle weight into a short-duration Treasury sleeve, charged the same turnover cost as any other leg. Two dials, both reported at every point: instrument {SHY, IEF, TLT} x phi {0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}, against the phi=0 control, on 2 panels x 4 cost rungs = **104 cells, all published**.

7. **It is duration-specific, which is the mechanistically right answer.** SHY passes 4a at every phi; IEF only at phi=0.25; TLT never (phi=1.00 costs -0.32 of Sharpe and 20.3 pp of drawdown). The sleeve must be a CASH sweep, not a bond position — this is the finding that makes the rule safe to write.
8. **What it costs when rates move against it.** SHY's own carry is **0.81%/yr in 2009-2016 and 1.73%/yr in 2017-2026**, so most of the +0.50 pp is earned after 2021 and a return to ZIRP shrinks the gain to roughly +0.2 pp. In **2022** the sleeve was a net **-2.64 pp** (book -7.20% vs control -4.56%): SHY is a 1-3yr Treasury ETF with ~1.9y duration, not cash. The MaxDD leg of 4a is decided by **2020-03-12** on both the sleeve and the control, i.e. an episode where SHY rose; a simultaneous equity-and-rates shock would hit both legs. **The true instrument (an MMF or BIL, zero duration) is not in the cached panels** — SHY is the shortest available and is therefore a conservative proxy for 2022 and an optimistic one for duration risk.
9. **Exact RULES wording proposed (a new clause; it changes NO equity decision the book makes).** *"Clause 7 — CASH SWEEP. Weight not allocated by clause 2 (`1 - sum(w)`, comprising the permanent `1 - gross` residual and every gated-out name's released weight) is held in a money-market fund or 1-3 month T-bill vehicle, 100% of it, rebalanced on the same weekly schedule as the book. The sweep vehicle is chosen for zero duration, never for yield: instruments with duration beyond one year (e.g. IEF, TLT) are excluded, and the sweep is never a discretionary bond position."*
