IDEA 1214 (lane C, 2026-09-17) — is a COUNT-MATCHING BAR BELOW ONE a CORRECTION
or a HANDICAP?
============================================================================================================

ARM 0 — THE ARITHMETIC, PRINTED BEFORE ANY PRICE IS READ.
  d2(k) Monte Carlo at 1,500,000 draws reproduces the published Hartley constants for the
  record's own rung counts (2/4/6/10) to 9.663e-04.
  R_k/d2(k) is UNBIASED for sigma at every rung count: max |E[R_k/d2(k)] - 1| = 8.564e-04.
  So 1155's point correction is correctly CENTRED in expectation.  It is not biased,
  and a bar below 1 is not a penalty on anyone.

  WHY A BAR BELOW ONE IS A NO-OP AND NOT A HANDICAP, WITH NO DATA IN IT.
    A pair is written in its realised direction, so M = R_wide/R_narrow >= 1 always.
    I = d2(k_wide)/d2(k_narrow) < 1 exactly when the wider ladder is the SHORTER one.
    M > 1 > I then holds for EVERY such pair: the bar cannot bind, ever.  The queue's
    'penalises the shorter ladder' is the wrong sign — the shorter ladder gets a FREE
    PASS, and the exposure is FALSE CONFIRMATION, not a handicap.
    On 1207's committed pairs table: 134 of 252 pairs carry a bar
    below 1 (0.5317) and 134 of 134 of them SURVIVE it (1.0000).

  THE CORRECT TWO-SIDED BAR, DERIVED.  The habit ORIENTS the pair on the data, so the
