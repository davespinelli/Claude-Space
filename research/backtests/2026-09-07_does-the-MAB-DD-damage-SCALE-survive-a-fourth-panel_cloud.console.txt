[4e] THE SPECIFICATION DEFECT, and the correctly-specified depth statistic
    Within a panel the anchor is a CONSTANT, so dMaxDD = MaxDD - anchor is an exact
    AFFINE function of MaxDD and corr(MaxDD, dMaxDD) is mechanically +1.000 — which
    is what [4b]/[4c] print for all 10 panels and BOTH arms.  Idea 387's statistic
    is therefore not a depth-scaling measurement: it is a between-panel statistic
    with a mechanical within-panel identity mixed into it, and its pooled value is
    driven by how the panels' anchors are spread, not by the instrument.
    The claim 'the damage SCALES with the book's own depth' is properly a statement
    about the PANEL's depth, so the specified statistic is corr(anchor_MaxDD, dMaxDD)
    — the anchor varies across panels and is fixed within one, so it carries no
    mechanical component.  Reported both pooled over cells and over panel means.
