# Idea 615 — why does a 0.83x/yr book still pay to SMOOTH?  (lane C, 2026-09-10) — **KILL**

**Answer: it doesn't pay. It ties.** Not a KEEP candidate; no memo, no RULES change.
`RULES.md` / `PROTOCOL.md` / `scan.py` / `bot.py` / `baseline.py` untouched.

## Design
2 tuned params — **lambda** (1.00, 0.70, 0.50, 0.35, 0.25, 0.15, 0.10, 0.06; idea 403's grid
verbatim) and **book** (EWall, TOP20). Reported axes: 3 panels (u56, broad/B136, SMALL439),
6 cost rungs (0/5/10/15/25/50 bps). 48 simulations read at 6 rungs = **288 arm-rows**, plus
30 weekday-phase yardstick runs. Weekly, t+1, gross 0.75. All grid points reported.

**The decomposition** (exact, residual 1.110e-16): an arm is a PATH (its zero-cost return series)
and a DRAG SCHEDULE (its daily turnover series), and `r[p,d] = r_0(lam=p) − to(lam=d)·c/1e4`.
COST = M(r[1,lam]) − M(r[1,1]); PATH = M(r[lam,1]) − M(r[1,1]); TOTAL = COST + PATH + INTER.
Gate G2 asserts the rung identity against a live 10 bps run at **0.000e+00** on all 6 (panel,
book) pairs, so the split is exact rather than approximate.

## Prescription
A lambda pick on a book trading under ~1x/yr should be published as a **TIE with the dial fixed
at 1.00**, not as a selection. Idea 403's "the chooser buys the turnover instrument … 12 of 24
EWall cells" should read "the argmax was indifferent on EWall". The weekday-phase spread of the
same book is a cheap, pre-registrable floor for any "the selector picked X" sentence in the record.

## Caveats
Survivorship (idea 54) on all three panels, worst on SMALL439. lambda can only lower turnover, so
the falsification is one-sided (idea 403's A3). MaxDD is one number off one path (idea 321). The
phase yardstick is a noise floor, not a null distribution — five phases give a spread, not a
p-value. u56/broad keep 403's panel convention (SPY a constituent); SMALL439 keeps the record's
small-panel convention (SPY benchmark only, 44 max_1d_move ≥ 1.0 names dropped).
