# Idea 89 — leave-one-year-out audit of every standing candidate (cloud, 2026-09-04)

1. **Harness.** LOYO deletes one calendar year of daily returns, chain-links the rest, and recomputes every statistic **and every 4b bar** on the retained days (SPY included), so a year that flattered book and benchmark alike scores as neutral. Zero tuned parameters. Reproduces idea 2 (12.7%/1.093/-18.3%) and idea 72 (10.7%/1.027/-17.7%) to the decimal.
2. **Only 2 of 7 published 4b passes are LOYO-robust:** idea 2's `top20` (18/18) and idea 46's `frac085` (18/18), both on universe.json. `ew-band3` is 17/18 on both lists, `frac085` 17/18 on broad, and idea 72's `B136/EWall` is **10/18** — the weakest standing candidate by a wide margin.
3. **But 12 of the 14 verdict flips are the BAR moving, not the book weakening.** Deleting 2020 raises SPY's MaxDD cap by 5.53pp (the COVID crash is most of SPY's -33.7%) and deleting 2022 raises the CAGR floor by 1.68pp, while the books barely move. 4b's DD and CAGR bars are themselves one-year-levered.
4. **No equity candidate is unusually year-dependent.** Worst-drop Sharpe deltas: SPY -0.059, v1 -0.065, `ew-band3` -0.071, `frac085` -0.074, `top20` -0.077 (u56); ratios to SPY 1.10-1.30 on u56 and 1.22-1.56 on broad. Nothing resembling a one-year result.
5. **Every candidate's single best year is 2022** (excess over SPY +7.3 to +14.0pp), except `top20`/u56 (2018, +10.5pp). The 4b edge over SPY is, in the main, one bear market avoided — that is the exposure the Sunday review should price, not per-parameter fragility.
6. **Validation on idea 15's PARKed crypto arm.** LOYO applied to the *paired difference* (`EWall+c10` minus its own control) gives t +2.04 full-sample, falling below 2.0 when any of 2016, 2017, 2020 or 2024 is deleted (worst: 2020, t +1.48). The harness flags it, and finds the dependence broader than the single year idea 15 named. LOYO on a book's own Sharpe would NOT have flagged it (-0.076, 1.30x SPY): **the audit must be run on the statistic the idea is argued on.**
7. **Rule 8 test — LOYO-robustness is a disqualifier, not a selector.** On broad, max-IS-Sharpe picks `ew-band3` (OOS 11.2%/1.074/-16.8%, PASS) while max-IS-worst-case-LOYO picks `top20` (OOS 12.5%/**0.894**/-20.1%). Robustness-aware selection did strictly worse out of sample. Do not adopt it as a selection rule.
8. **Proposed PROTOCOL rule 10 (for the Sunday review, exact wording):** *"**10. Leave-one-year-out (required for KEEP-candidates):** re-run the candidate deleting each calendar year in turn, recomputing the benchmark's bars on the same retained days. Report the number of LOYO samples that still pass, the worst-case Sharpe delta relative to SPY's own, and — where the idea is argued on a difference (sleeve vs control, arm vs arm) — the LOYO path of that difference's t-statistic. A candidate whose claim does not survive the deletion of any single year is PARK, not KEEP. LOYO is a disqualifier only; it must not be used to select parameters or books."*
9. **Consequences if rule 10 is adopted as written:** idea 2's `top20` and idea 46's `frac085` stand on universe.json; idea 57's `ew-band3` and idea 72's `B136/EWall` become PARK pending a bar-movement exemption; idea 15 stays PARK for a second, independent reason.
10. **Caveats.** A spliced equity curve's MaxDD is not a realisable path (drawdowns straddling the deleted year are shortened), so DD-based LOYO verdicts are optimistic — which makes the DD flips in §3 a floor on the problem, not a ceiling. Survivorship (current-constituent lists) inflates every level but does not create time-concentration. 18 calendar years, of which 2026 is a partial year.
