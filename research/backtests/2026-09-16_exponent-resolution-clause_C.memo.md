# PROPOSED (rule 6: not applied) — PROTOCOL rule 5 EXPONENT RESOLUTION clause

1. **Exact wording.** *A committed claim stating a fitted EXPONENT, SLOPE or SCALING law publishes, beside the point and in the same sentence, a two-sided 90% interval for that exponent and the BASIS that produced it, named from: `OLS` (the fit's own residual interval), `REDRAW` (the same construction re-run on the same tape with fresh draws), `TAPE` (the whole construction re-run on a resampled tape). A claim that an exponent IS a value, DIFFERS from another exponent, or DIFFERS from a theory value must quote the `TAPE` basis; the other two may be quoted beside it, never instead of it. The point carries no more decimals than half the interval width supports. Two exponents are reported as DIFFERENT only where their intervals are disjoint on the same basis, and where they are not the claim says so. An exponent published with no interval may not be cited by a later run as evidence that a scaling law differs from any other value.*
2. **Why:** on this tape 0 of 43 valued exponent claims in LEADERBOARD.md + CHANGELOG.md state a width on the exponent's own value (1 of 104 corpus-wide, and it is a proposed clause, not a result). 10 of 43 state a SPREAD across cells, which is a dispersion and not a resolution.
3. **Why the basis must be named:** on the identical object (U56, BLOCK21, 90%) the three bases read **OLS 0.0291 < REDRAW 0.0404 < TAPE 0.0429**. The interval the fit hands the author for free understates the tape width by 32%, because a 6-rung log-log ladder reads all six rungs off one tape.
4. **Why the decimals clause:** 1012's headline exponent is published to four decimals; its own 90% width of 0.0429 supports **one**.
5. **What it re-labels:** 103 of the 104 valued exponent claims in the corpus become "published without a resolution". None is asserted WRONG — 1012's ladder reproduces here over 522 SE cells at max |d| 8.33e-17 and its three exponents to 4.65e-05.
6. **What it forbids that the record does:** reading −0.5110, −0.5003 and −0.4943 as three measurements (pairwise |d| 0.0060 / 0.0107 / 0.0167 against a width of 0.0429, 0 of 3 separable), and reading any of them as distinguishable from the sqrt law (−0.5 is inside the interval in 2 of 2 panels).
7. **What it does NOT do:** it does not overturn 1012's `H_SCALE PASS`. The sqrt law is confirmed in expectation (zero-truth control median b −0.5020 against a truth of exactly −0.5); what the clause removes is the claim of separation, not the claim of the law.
8. **Cost:** free where the construction is already resampled; otherwise 100 reps of the published construction — 300 s for the 1012 ladder here. A claim whose construction is too costly to resample quotes `REDRAW` or `OLS` and says which.
9. **The resolution is bought with REPLICATIONS, not with tape:** at fixed tape the 90% width runs 0.0418 / 0.0887 / 0.1715 / 0.3317 / 0.8969 / 2.2498 at 1000 / 250 / 60 / 15 / 4 / 2 draws per rung, so a run that wants a resolvable exponent adds draws, and 1044's 0.67–0.73 is what about five replications per length buys.
