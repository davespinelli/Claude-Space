# Idea 146 — proposed PROTOCOL rule 8 clause: PIN m (for the Sunday review)

1. **Finding.** Rule 8's FAMILY screen never selected a book. In 7 of 7 entering cells it kept the
   arm the pinned screen picked and moved only the gross multiplier m, so its exchange rate is the
   gross ladder's own local slope **by identity**, not a selection result.
2. It buys no risk-adjusted return: mean OOS Sharpe change **−0.003**, improved in **0 of 7** cells.
3. It spends the exact resource 4b rations: +1.7 pp of OOS drawdown for +1.1 pp of CAGR, turning
   **2 of the 3** pinned 4b passes into failures (broad/EWall@10 and u56/EWall@10).
4. Its m is set by an artifact, not by the book: the IS-admissible m exceeds the OOS-admissible m
   in **4 of 5** comparable cells (mean +0.10 of gross), and in 2 further cells the IS window
   admits an m at which the OOS window admits **none**.
5. Ex ante the trade was priced at a median **1.08** pp CAGR per pp drawdown on the IS window and
   delivered **0.67** — it beats the IS slope in **0 of 7** cells.
6. **Proposed clause, appended to PROTOCOL rule 8** (verbatim wording):
   > "A walk-forward screen selects the CONSTRUCTION only. The static gross multiplier m is PINNED
   > at the published gross for every candidate the screen compares, and is never chosen by the
   > screen. Gross remains a separately reported construction dial (idea 66/84's ladder): a record
   > may quote a book at another gross, but the walk-forward pick must be read at the pin."
7. Rationale for the wording: idea 144's family convention stays valid for *reporting* (a rescaled
   book is the same book), and is only barred from *selection*.
8. **Not proposed:** deleting the family convention, changing phi/delta, or any RULES.md change.
   No new book and no KEEP candidate comes out of this run.
9. **Cost of the clause:** none measured. Pinning reproduces idea 144's S1 numbers exactly
   (OOS 12.7% / 1.022 / −21.1% paired over 7 cells) and passes 4b in 3 of 7 against 1 of 7.
10. **Caveat that limits it:** only 7 of 18 cells enter any 4b screen at all, so the clause rests
    on 7 paired cells; the small panel contributes none. Survivorship flatters high gross, i.e. it
    flatters the arm this clause rules out, so the finding is if anything understated.
