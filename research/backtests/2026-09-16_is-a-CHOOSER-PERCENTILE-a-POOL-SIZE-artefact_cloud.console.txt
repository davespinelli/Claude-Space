   THE QUEUE'S QUESTION, ANSWERED IN ITS OWN TERMS.  RESOLUTION is sufficient at N >= 10

   binding constraint, because it never was: 0.800 fails to separate from a coin flip
   because 1 draw in 5 beats it, and that is a RANK, readable at any N.
