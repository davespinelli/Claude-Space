Q2b  THE SAMPLING-FREE LIMIT.  A K-draw's verdict depends on the draw only through
     X = how many of the K sampled |dSharpe| reach the real |dSharpe|.  With an
     ORDER-STATISTIC band at level q the overlay clears iff X <= c(K) = K - ceil(q*K),
     and X ~ Hypergeom(N, M, K) where M is the POPULATION exceedance count.  So the
     ladder has a closed form with NO sampling error at all:
       P(clear at K)  = P(X <= c)
       P(error at K)  = P(clear) or 1-P(clear), whichever the population verdict denies
       P(flip, 2 disjoint K-blocks) = 2 P(A clears) P(B does not clear | A clears)
     (q=1 -> c=0 is the MAX band and is EXACT; q=0.95 uses the order-statistic band,
     which differs from the run's linear-interpolation band only by that convention --
     the gap is reported below, not hidden.)
