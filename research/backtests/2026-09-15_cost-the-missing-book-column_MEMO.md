9. **Exact RULES wording, if a Sunday review ever adopts it** (this is the wording, not a recommendation to adopt):
   > *"Hold every instrument in the universe whose price is above its 200-day moving average by more than 3%, at equal weight of 1.00/N of NAV where N is the number of instruments priced that day; exit a holding when its price falls more than 3% below that average, and hold the previous state in between. Weight released by the band is held in cash and is never re-spread across the remaining holdings. Rebalance weekly. No ranking, no volatility filter, no shorting, no leverage."*
10. **Do not fund it on this evidence.** A 1.21x lift over a blind pick is weak selection; the panel is a current-constituent list so both the CAGR and the −14.8% drawdown are upper bounds; 2009-2026 holds two real stress tests in a QQQ-favourable regime; and 4a fails.
