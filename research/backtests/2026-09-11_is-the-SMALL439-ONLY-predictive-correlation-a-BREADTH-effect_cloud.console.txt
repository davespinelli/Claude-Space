A2.  THE ATTENUATION MODEL - what pure BREADTH predicts, quantitatively
====================================================================================================================================================================================

draws of a given size the same (family, th) cell gives a spread: that is the NOISE.  The
spread of the draw-MEAN advantage across cells is the SIGNAL.  Classical attenuation then
predicts r(size) = r_true * sqrt(var_signal / (var_signal + var_noise)).
