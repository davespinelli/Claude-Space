  THE FINER PARTITION THE DATA ITSELF DRAWS (a POST-HOC reading, labelled as one and
  not used to move any gate).  What predicts invariance is not the OBSERVED / BAR
  side but WHICH FUNCTIONAL OF THE DRAW LAW the output is:
    * OBSERVED (5 outputs): invariant to every knob, exactly, because no draw is read.
    * LOCATION-FREE draw functionals (B_RECRANGE, B_SD): exactly invariant to
      RECENTRING (ratio 0.0000) — a range and an SD cannot see a location shift —
      while being knob-dependent under the draw law.
    * THRESHOLD CROSSINGS far from their threshold (B_RESOLVED, B_GAPEXCEEDS):
      exactly invariant to SEED and to B, and B_RESOLVED still moves with the draw
      LAW.  B_GAPEXCEEDS is additionally degenerate at L = 63 (1252's G5b: it fires
      zero times), so its invariance is a pinned constant and is worth nothing.
    * everything else: noise-bound under SEED and B, knob-dependent under DRAW KIND
      and RECENTRING.
