6. **Why it works, stated before the grid was cut and confirmed by it:** on a de-grossing book at a zero cash rate, gross is a pure scale on both return and vol, so Sharpe is invariant to it (spread **0.0002** over g 0.50→1.00 on U56) while CAGR is proportional (5.73%→11.55%). The CAGR floor is therefore the **only** 4b bar gross can move, and the live 0.75 misses it by 1.95 pp.
