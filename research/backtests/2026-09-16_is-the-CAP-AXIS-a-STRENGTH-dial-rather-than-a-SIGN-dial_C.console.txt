LIMITS, STATED BEFORE THE VERDICT
============================================================================================================
  1. The CROSSING arm's large-cap halves hold only 45 names each, so its k=40 panels overlap heavily and its 8 draws are far from independent.  Its rho is a firmer POINT than an INTERVAL; the MATCH arm, drawn from wider windows, is the arm to weigh.
  2. Vol matching can only be done where the two pools OVERLAP.  At V*=0.25 that is the bottom of the small pool and the top of the large one, so the matched arm speaks for NEITHER pool's typical name: the matched small panels run at vol 0.260 against the pool's 0.415.  Nothing here extends to a high-vol small-cap book.
  3. rho is measured on 8 draws x 3 widths x 4 rungs = 96 rows per cell.  No interval is published for it, so a 0.28 and a 0.40 in this table are not shown to differ; only the cell ORDERING and the SIGN are claimed (idea 1044's rule).
  4. Realised name vol is one proxy for 'small caps are noisier'.  A liquidity, spread or factor-loading match would be a different control and is not run here.
  5. SURVIVORSHIP again: the vol used to match is measured on names that survived to be screened today, so the matched window is a window on survivors in BOTH pools.
