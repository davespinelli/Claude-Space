### PART A — THE PREMISE: is the SPY column in data/prices.csv PRICE-ONLY?
   The queue filed this idea PARK for want of a dividend series. It does not need one:
   data/prices.csv carries six fixed-income ETFs whose PRICE return is structurally
   pinned and whose TOTAL return is not. They decide the convention of the file, and
   SPY is a column of the SAME file, written by the SAME engine.load_prices call.

   >>> PART A ANSWER: the premise is FALSE (3 of 3 legs say ADJUSTED).
   data/prices.csv is a DIVIDEND-REINVESTED (total-return) panel. SPY is a column of
   that same file. `engine.load_prices` fetches with auto_adjust=True and falls back to
   this cache, so the benchmark and the traded panel are on ONE convention by
   construction. THE MISMATCH THE IDEA POSTULATES IS ZERO BY CONSTRUCTION, not small.
   G5 adds the corollary: the sandbox holds no SECOND convention — across the 48 never-under-$5 tickers both cache files carry, the ratio between them drifts by 1.680e-03 over the whole window, where a price-only/total-return gap would open 0.2-0.3. So there is nothing to mis-match against even if one wanted to, and a genuine price-only comparand would still have to be fetched from outside.
