THE QUEUE'S OWN PREMISE, CHECKED
====================================================================================================
  The queue says: 'Every committed cadence comparison differences two books at one gross,
  so the residual does not cancel.'  Half of that is right and half is not, and the
  distinction is the whole result:
    (i)  AT the gross it was measured at, a cadence gain is EXACT.  It is the difference
         of two numbers the engine actually produced; no linearity is assumed and there is
         no residual to cancel or fail to cancel.
    (ii) The residual enters the moment that gain is read at ANOTHER gross -- which is
         what an unstamped figure invites, and 1076 measured that 0.924 of committed
         turnover/drag figures are unstamped.  THAT is the quantity priced above.
  And the queue's second clause -- that the residual does not cancel on a difference --
  is confirmed in direction and priced: 2.259x the level residual on the record's
  own ladder.
