10. **Exact RULES wording if the Sunday review adopts it** (replaces v2 clauses 2 and 4 verbatim; everything else in RULES v2 stands):
    > **2. Membership gate:** a 200-day moving-average band with hysteresis, per name. A name becomes **IN** on the first close above `ma200 * 1.10`; it stays IN until a close below `ma200 * 0.90`, which makes it **OUT**; it stays OUT until the next close above the upper band. Between the two bands the previous state persists. A name with fewer than 200 closes is OUT.
    > **4. Sizing:** let `N` be the number of instruments priced on the decision day and `k` the number of them that are IN. Each IN name is held at `(0.75 / N) * (1 + 0.75 * (N - k) / k)` of current NAV, rounded down to whole shares; if `k = 0` the book is fully in cash. **Three quarters of the weight released by the OUT names is re-spread pro rata over the IN names and one quarter stays in cash**, so the book's gross runs between `0.75 * k/N` and `0.75` and **never exceeds 0.75** — this is a re-spread, not leverage, and it supersedes v2's "de-gross, never re-spread" (idea 81) on the strength of PROTOCOL 4b, not 4a.
