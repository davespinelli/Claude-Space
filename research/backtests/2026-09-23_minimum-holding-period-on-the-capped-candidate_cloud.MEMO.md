# PARK memo — idea 2351, hold floors on the capped 4b candidate (lane cloud, run 37, 2026-09-23)

1. **Question.** Idea 2328 killed the weight-drift no-trade band as a turnover device for the standing CAP2 candidate (3.51 -> 3.07 turns/yr at 100% held-set fidelity). 2351 asks whether attacking the *set* rather than the *weights* — a hold floor `m`, under which a name admitted at decision day `t` cannot be dropped before `t + m` trading days even when the 200d band gates it OUT — does better.
2. **Answer: NO on the adoption question.** At matched fidelity the floor is strictly worse: m=10 (99.85% fidelity) removes 0.5% of turnover where the drift band removed 12.3% at 100%. The floor's deepest legal cut, m=63 (a full quarter, 97.05% fidelity), reaches 3.03 turns/yr against the drift band's 3.07 at perfect fidelity — still **1.71x the live book's 1.77**. Turnover is not the leg this family can fix.
3. **But the idea's own fallback KILL does not land either.** The floor does not cut return one-for-one: CAGR RISES at 18 of 32 (panel, book, gross, m>0) cells at 10 bps and at 24 of 32 at 50 bps, because the saving is a pure cost rebate. Hold floors are not killed as a class.
4. **What it costs instead is Sharpe, and only on the narrow panel.** Full-sample Sharpe rises at 12 of 32 cells at 10 bps and OOS Sharpe at only 8 of 32 — and the split is entirely by panel.
5. **THE PARK: on B136 the floor is a straight improvement at m in {42, 63}, at 8 of 8 (book, gross, m) cells, on BOTH full-sample and OOS Sharpe.** B136 / CAP2 / g=0.75 / 10 bps: CAGR 11.82% -> 12.70%, Sharpe 1.1180 -> 1.1429, H2 0.990 -> 1.029, OOS 11.65% / 1.0936 -> 12.80% / 1.1225, turnover 4.68 -> 3.70/yr. 4b passes at every m and the pass count rises 3/8 -> 4/8.
6. **On U56 it is the reverse, at 0 of 8.** U56 / CAP2 / g=0.75 / 10 bps: CAGR 11.62% -> 11.78% but Sharpe 1.2687 -> 1.2386, MaxDD -14.81% -> -15.80%, OOS Sharpe 1.3318 -> 1.2303. The candidate's own panel does not want the floor.
7. **Rule 8 is unanimous in the wrong direction.** 0 of 32 IS-only picks land on m=0 — every legal chooser buys a floor (m21 12, m42 2, m63 18) — yet only **4 of 32** beat the gross-matched m=0 book's OOS Sharpe. An in-sample chooser cannot be trusted with this dial; the 4 that work are the B136 cells of point 5.
8. **Mechanism.** The floor is the only device in this family that is not a de-grosser: mean names held RISES (38.45 -> 40.07 on U56, 93.19 -> 97.79 on B136) because gated-out names are carried, so gross is maintained and the binding 4b leg moves from `L_CAGR` (every other device) to `L_DD` (15/15/16/16 by rung, `L_CAGR` 0/0/0/15). The deepening drawdown is the price of holding names the band has already condemned.
9. **Why PARK and not KEEP.** 4a is 0 of 160. 4b passes 83 of 160, but the m=0 incumbent already passes — the floor adds nothing on U56 and the B136 gain rests on one panel, two rungs of a five-rung ladder, and a chooser that is wrong 28 times out of 32. Survivorship: both panels are current constituents only.
10. **Next.** The B136 result is a *panel-width* claim, not a hold-floor claim: it should be re-priced against a width-matched control before anyone spends capital on it. No RULES change is proposed and RULES.md is untouched.
