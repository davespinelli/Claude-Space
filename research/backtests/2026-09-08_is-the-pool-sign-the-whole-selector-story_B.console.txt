  A FIXED comparand is common to every pool in a panel, so its own cross-pool variance is zero and
  `pool` inherits all of it: R2 is then a measure of how much the COMPARAND moves, not of how much
  the pool explains the selector.  Differencing against the pool's OWN control removes that shared
  term and the same regression collapses.  Idea 204's proposed statistic is therefore not identified
  without also naming the comparand — which is idea 398's open question, reached from a new direction.
