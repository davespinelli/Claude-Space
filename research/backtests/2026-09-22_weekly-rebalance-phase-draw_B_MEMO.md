# KEEP-4b candidate memo — idea 2274 (lane B, 2026-09-22): the 5-tranche phase-averaged band book

1. **What it is.** The live RULES v2 band book on u56, gross 1.00 instead of 0.75, run as five equal sleeves that rebalance on five different weekdays. Nothing else changes: same 200d +/-3% band, same weekly cadence per sleeve, same t+1 execution, long-only, unlevered.
2. **Why it exists.** Idea 2264's candidate is the same book on ONE phase (Friday). This run priced all five and found the phase spread real but unforecastable (spearman(IS, OOS) median -0.15; IS argmax = OOS argmax at 0 of 32 families), so the phase is an exposure to be spent, not chosen.
3. **Numbers @10 bps, u56.** FULL 11.42% / 1.1916 / -14.99% (halves 1.2077 / 1.1800); **OOS 12.61% / 1.2768 / -14.99%**; turnover 2.3677x/yr. Live RULES v2: 8.62% / 1.2010 / -12.05% (OOS 9.46% / 1.2767 / -12.05%, 1.77x/yr). SPY: 15.14% / 0.8851 / -33.72% (OOS 15.29% / 0.8751 / -33.72%).
4. **Which path.** **PATH 4b ONLY.** 4b clears FULL and OOS at 0 / 10 / 25 bps; at 50 bps OOS still clears and FULL misses the CAGR floor by 0.23 pp. **PATH 4a is 0 of 192 cells and is unreachable here** — gross is Sharpe-neutral and phase-averaging moves Sharpe by +0.0035, so the book cannot beat the live one in both halves and is strictly deeper in drawdown (-14.99% vs -12.05%).
5. **What it buys over idea 2264's standing candidate.** Identical OOS CAGR to within 0.06 pp and identical OOS Sharpe to within 0.0008, at **0.92 pp less OOS drawdown** (-14.99% vs -15.91%) and **0.016x/yr more turnover**. It also beats its own sleeve mean (OOS Sharpe 1.2768 vs 1.2733; OOS MaxDD -14.99% vs -15.25%), so the gain is diversification, not arithmetic.
6. **Rule 8.** Choosing on 2009-2016 only and reading 2017-2026 once, 4b-OOS reach over 8 (panel x cost) cells: C_LIVE 0, C_SHARPE 2, C_DDB 4, **C_DDB_PHAVG 5**, C_ORACLE (peeks) 4. The phase axis is not chosen at all, so the candidate costs **zero** tuned parameters beyond gross.
7. **Exact RULES wording it would need** (clause 3, replacing the single weekly rebalance): *"Rebalance weekly. Divide the account into five equal sleeves and assign each sleeve one weekday, Monday through Friday. Each sleeve is rebalanced to the clause-2 target weights at the close of its own weekday and is held unchanged otherwise; sleeves are never rebalanced against one another. Gross exposure is 100% of sleeve NAV, allocated equally across every instrument inside the 200d band on that sleeve's rebalance day; gated-out weight is held in cash."*
8. **Why it is NOT recommended.** It is 2264's sizing decision (0.75 -> 1.00) with a free drawdown rebate bolted on. The sizing half buys +2.80 pp of CAGR with +2.94 pp of MaxDD and no Sharpe, and PROTOCOL rule 6 gives sizing to the Sunday review, not to a run. Adopt the tranching alone (at gross 0.75) and it is a pure operational improvement with no 4b pass to argue about.
9. **Panel dependence and cost.** b136 clears 4b FULL and OOS at only 6 of 96 cells, all at gross 1.00 and 0-10 bps; the PHAVG arm is one of them (0 bps) and misses at 10 bps by 0.09 pp of OOS CAGR. The pass is a u56 fact.
10. **Caveats.** Survivorship (PROTOCOL rule 9): u56/b136 are 2026 constituents held from 2008, so every CAGR level is optimistic and both 4b level legs are easier than on a point-in-time panel. Costs are flat per unit turnover with no spread, impact or borrow; one band (3%), one delay (t+1), one cadence (weekly per sleeve). Five sleeves means five times as many rebalance events at one fifth the size — modelled here as identical per-unit cost, which is optimistic if there is a fixed per-ticket charge.
