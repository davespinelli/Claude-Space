# Idea 464 — is the 0.2125 vol threshold a PLATEAU or a CLIFF?  (cloud, 2026-09-09)

**Verdict: SPLIT — and the split has a direction.** The 4b region in θ is WIDE (0.48 vol
points, 86 of 128 grid points on U56), so the queue's worry that idea 247's candidate sits on
a knife-edge is **wrong on the upside and right on the downside**: θ = 0.2125 sits **0.0125
vol points above the region's lower edge at 10 bps and exactly ON it at 25 bps**, with 0.4675
of headroom above. The adopted constant is not in the middle of its plateau; it is on the
plateau's cliff. A better constant exists inside the same 2-parameter budget, it is what
rule 8 picks, and it dominates the incumbent on every 4b bar at both cost rungs.

## 2. What the wide end of the interval actually is (the honest caveat)

A width of 0.48 vol points overstates the finding. **At θ = 0.680 the book arms 21 of 4,440
days (0.47%)** and is otherwise EW_ALL. EW_ALL itself fails 4b at every panel and rung, and
on U56/B136 it fails on **exactly one bar, the drawdown cap** (U56 −22.53% vs the −20.23%
cap, margin −0.0230; CAGR margin is +0.0262, comfortably clear). So the whole 4b region is
the set of θ that buy ≥ 2.3 pp of drawdown relief off EW_ALL — and 21 crash days do that.
The economically load-bearing interval is the narrower **4b ∧ beats-its-matched-gross-control**
window: **0.135 vol points at 10 bps, 0.0725 at 25 bps**, and the incumbent sits at its lower
edge in both.

Local slope of the binding bar at the incumbent: **−0.085 pp of CAGR per 0.01 vol point**
(U56, both rungs); B136 −0.016, SMALL439 −0.076 to −0.083.
