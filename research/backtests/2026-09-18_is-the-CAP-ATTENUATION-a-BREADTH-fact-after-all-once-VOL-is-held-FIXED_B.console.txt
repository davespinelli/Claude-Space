LIMITS, STATED BEFORE THE VERDICT
============================================================================================================
  1. At the tightest CROSS beta the decisive cells hold only 32 names, so 12 k=20 draws overlap heavily and are far from independent.  Every rho here is a firmer POINT than an INTERVAL; no interval is published (idea 1044's rule), so only the cell ORDERING and the SIGN are claimed.
  2. BREADTH and VOL are not orthogonal on this tape: within SMALL the LO-vol half carries IS breadth 0.6892 against the HI-vol half's 0.4621.  A breadth window therefore moves the vol mix too, which is why |d vol| is printed beside every shift above.
  3. The matched window is where the two pools OVERLAP in breadth, so it speaks for NEITHER pool's typical name.  Nothing here extends to a low-breadth small-cap book.
  4. IS breadth is measured over 2010-2016 only, a period with one real drawdown (2011) and no 2020/2022.  A breadth measured on a different window could rank names differently.
  5. SURVIVORSHIP again: a realised gate-pass rate is measured on names that survived to be screened today, so a high-breadth window is doubly a survivor window.
