ARM 0 — THE ARITHMETIC, PRINTED BEFORE ANY TEXT OR ANY PRICE IS READ.
  A range is not an estimator of dispersion: for k iid N(0,1) draws E[max-min] = d2(k),
  tabulated for k = 2..12.  The longest ladder this record has ever published is 10.
  d2 Monte Carlo at 2e5 draws reproduces every published constant to 2.383e-03.

  THE SUBSTITUTION CONVENTION IS NOT A DIAL, AND HERE IS WHY, WITH NO DATA IN IT.
  A verdict resting on a multiple M rests on the comparative M/I > 1, I being the
  inflation d2(k_max)/d2(k_min) the two counts alone buy.  Setting M to no effect:
    M' = 1      -> M'/I = 1/I  < 1 for every unit with k_max > k_min  -> flips ALL
    M' = I      -> M'/I = 1                                           -> flips ALL
    M' = M/I    -> (M/I) > 1 iff M > I                                -> flips {M <= I}
  Every convention is THE SAME THRESHOLD AT I.  It is gated (G3) and never walked.

  PRE-DECLARED OUTCOMES (before the trace): (A) MANY — 10 or more committed verdicts
  change; (B) FEW — 1 to 9; (C) NONE — the failing multiples are not what the verdicts
  rest on; (D) UNTRACEABLE — the failing units carry no recoverable verdict at all.
