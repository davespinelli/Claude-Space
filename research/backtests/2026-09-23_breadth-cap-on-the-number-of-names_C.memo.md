# KEEP memo (4a only — FILED, NOT RECOMMENDED) — the breadth cap's one honest pass (idea 2387, lane C, 2026-09-23)

1. **What this memo is, and what it is not.** Idea 2387 asked whether a cap on the NUMBER of names can do what idea 2322's 2% WEIGHT cap does. **The answer is NO and that is this run's headline (KILL).** But the grid threw off the first **4a** passes this capped family has ever produced — 18 of 160 rows — and PROTOCOL rule 4 makes 4a a KEEP path, so the book is filed here in full. It is **NOT recommended for adoption**: it fails 4b's CAGR floor by 4.92 pp and earns **less than the live book**.
2. **Exact RULES wording (v2 clauses 3 and 4 replaced; 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 8 unchanged).** Clause 3 becomes: *"hold the **10** IN names with the largest `close / ma200 - 1` on the decision day; if fewer than 10 names are IN, hold every IN name. Ties are broken by universe order in research/universe.json."* Clause 4 becomes: *"each held name is sized at `min(0.75 / min(N_in, 10), 0.02)` of current NAV — which is **2.00% exactly** at every N_in, since `0.75 / 10 = 7.5% > 2%`; every dollar not held in equities is held in **SHY**, reset on the weekly rebalance."* In words: **at most ten names, 2% each, at least 80% of NAV in SHY.**
3. **KEEP path: 4a, on BOTH panels, full sample and in both halves.** U56 / 10 bps: **5.74% / 1.3412 / −7.52%**, halves **1.2990 / 1.3964** against live RULES v2's 1.2052 (1.2262 / 1.1897) at −12.06% — Sharpe higher in both halves, drawdown 4.54 pp shallower. B136 / 10 bps: 6.37% / 1.3113 / −8.35%, halves 1.5323 / 1.1675 against 1.0972 at −12.24%. Turnover 3.30x (U56) / 4.12x (B136) per year against the live book's measured 2.79x / 3.03x (idea 2391's correction). Mean names held 9.94 / 9.99; max per-name weight **2.00%**, the lowest concentration in this family's record.
4. **It is rule-8 reachable, which is rare.** With (N_max, gross) fitted on ≤ 2016-12-31 only and 2017–2026 read ONCE, **both** IS choosers take **N_max = 10 at all 4 rungs on U56 and all 4 on B136** under the 2% weight cap — 16 of 16 picks — and those picks beat the live book's OOS Sharpe at 10 bps (U56 **1.3947** vs 1.2839; B136 **1.2239** vs 1.1017) and the CAP2 anchor's (1.3318 / 1.0936). An honest chooser finds this book; it does not have to be discovered in hindsight.
5. **Why it is filed and not recommended: it loses money to earn its Sharpe.** CAGR 5.74% (U56) / 6.37% (B136) against the live book's 8.65% / 7.96% and SPY's 15.23%. **4b fails on `L_CAGR` alone at every rung** (margin −4.92 pp at 10 bps, −4.57 at 0, −5.45 at 25); all four other 4b legs pass. 4a does not test return at all, so a book at **19.9% mean equity gross** can clear it by shrinking. This is a defect of 4a's construction, stated plainly, not a result to spend capital on.
6. **Where the 4a pass stops.** 18 of 160 rows, all at `N_max ∈ {10, 20}` with the 2% weight cap on: U56 at 0 and 10 bps (25 bps fails on H1 by 0.008), B136 at 0, 10 and 25 bps. 4a is **0 of 80** with the weight cap OFF and **0 of 64** at `N_max ≥ 30`. At 50 bps it is 0 of 40.
7. **The gross dial dies here, and that is a cost.** With the 2% cap on and `N_max ≤ 30`, `gross / N_held ≥ 0.02` at both settings, so the g = 0.75 and g = 1.00 rows are **bit-identical** (published in section A). The book cannot be re-sized by the one dial the live rules have; the only size control left is `N_max` itself.
8. **The structural finding behind all of this (gate G9, asserted not assumed).** At `wcap = INF` the breadth cap holds book gross **exactly** at `gross` on every day with `N_in > 0`, at every `N_max` (max deviation 2.9e-15 over 20 cells). The breadth cap **re-concentrates at constant gross**; the 2% weight cap **de-grosses at constant breadth** (idea 2381's 38.45 / 93.22 mean names held, reproduced here exactly). The two devices are opposites, they fail on opposite legs — `L_DD` for the breadth cap alone, `L_CAGR` once composed with the weight cap — and **no `N_max` substitutes for the weight cap.**
9. **SURVIVORSHIP (rule 9).** U56 and B136 are current-constituent lists held from 2008, so absolute levels are optimistic; `L_CAGR` is the most contaminated leg and it is the one this book fails, so the failure is if anything **understated**. A ten-name book selected from a survivorship-biased list is the most exposed construction in this record — 20% of NAV in the ten names that a current-constituent screen kept.
10. **Caveats and scope.** One tape, 17.7 years, one QQQ-favourable regime; SMALL is not priced (ideas 2318 / 2322 / 2326 / 2343 published it at 0 of 40–120 on both paths); B136 carries an all-NaN `MMC` column so it is a 135-name panel (idea 2332's defect, reproduced here). **No RULES.md, PROTOCOL.md, scan.py, bot.py or baseline.py change is proposed by this run**; adoption, if ever, is the Sunday review's call under rule 6 and PLAN Tier 3.
