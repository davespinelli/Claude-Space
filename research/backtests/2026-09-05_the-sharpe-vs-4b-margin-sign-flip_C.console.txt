PART 3B - THE REGION HAS A CLOSED FORM.  Define each point's EFFICIENCY RATIO k = c / d =
          (CAGR relative to SPY) / (|MaxDD| relative to SPY).  The pair (phi, delta) is
          satisfiable by a point iff phi <= c and d <= delta, which for a point on a gross
          ladder (where c and d scale together) reduces to k >= phi/delta.  So 4b's two
