# Idea 683 — is the 0.59% 4a FLIP RATE a PER-DAY constant?
lane C, 2026-09-11 · script `2026-09-11_is-the-0.59-percent-4a-FLIP-RATE-a-PER-DAY-constant_C.py`

## Verdict: ANSWERED / KILL — **no, on three separate counts, and the horizon the queue asks for is not reachable from price drift at all**

1. **It is not a rate.** Against the LIVE book (RULES v2), **0 of 848 book-rows pass 4a** on
   every equity-indexed vintage `data/prices.csv` has ever had. The denominator of
   "0.59% of 4a passes" is **empty at source**. The only vintages carrying 4a passes are the
   two **pre-fix calendar-day panels** (57 of 848 each) — i.e. the record's entire 4a-pass
   population on this grid was an artefact of the crypto-calendar index bug that commit
   `c006b43` fixed on 2026-09-04. (This corroborates open idea 679 from a different angle.)
2. **It is not per-day.** Decay is **not monotone in horizon**: channel E's 4b decay runs
   0.0000 (d ≤ 30) → 0.0064 (61) → 0.0311 (91) → **0.0819 (182)** → 0.0192 (365). A
   half-year-old verdict has moved *more* often than a year-old one. Horizon length is the
   wrong variable; what the added window *contains* is the right one.
3. **It is not price drift.** The single largest verdict mover in the record's own git
   history is a **code commit**, not the panel: the index-convention fix flips **9.43–9.55%**
   of 4b verdicts and 6.7–7.0% of 4a(v1) verdicts in one step — **30–80× a week of drift**.

## Why restatement cannot get there
Restatement touches **9–11% of cells** but with **median relative move 1.3e-06**. In
binding-margin units (each leg's slack ÷ that leg's cross-sectional sd), it moves a passing
row by a median **1e-06 to 1.2e-03** against a median 4b binding margin of **0.1345** — two to
three orders of magnitude short. And it has no direction: for symmetric drift,
P(flip | pass) = P(drift < −margin) → 0.50 only asymptotically and **never exceeds it**. Only
the extension channel, which changes the sample the verdict is *about*, can carry decay past
one half.

**The horizon:** decay never reaches 0.50 anywhere on the ladder. The log-log fit on channel
E's four non-zero rungs, `rate ≈ 1.303e-03 · d^0.588`, puts 0.50 at **d ≈ 24,867 calendar days
(68 years)** — an **extrapolation from 4 points, 68× beyond the ladder**, quoted as an order
of magnitude and nothing more. **The honest answer to the queue is that the horizon does not
exist inside any research timescale**, and that the question as posed points at the wrong
hazard.

**Which leg decays:** of the 157 4b passes on the newest panel, **83 bind on b5 (the CAGR
floor) and 63 on b4 (the DD cap)**; only 11 bind on any of the three Sharpe legs. Vintage
distance moves the floor and the cap, not the risk-adjusted comparison.

## Prescription for Sunday review (no RULES change proposed)
1. **Stop quoting a decay rate for 4a against the live book.** It has no denominator: RULES
   v2 is unbeaten by 848 of 848 books on this panel. Quote 4a-vs-v1, or quote counts.
2. **Vintage-stamp is necessary but not sufficient** (ideas 514/516's prescription): the
   commit that moved the most verdicts changed the **index convention**, which a price-vintage
   stamp does not capture. Stamp the **panel row count and last date** too — both would have
   caught `c006b43` instantly.
3. **A published 4b pass does not rot from price drift.** It rots from re-drawing the sample,
   and non-monotonically. Re-checking a committed claim on a schedule is the wrong ritual;
   re-checking it when the *code* changes is the right one.

## Caveats

  rests on 5 distance rungs and 8 vintages; every R number is a small-sample number.
- Channel E is **pure extension by construction** — it carries no restatement. R measures how
  much that omits (≈0 within a week); it is not measured beyond 7 days and is not claimed.
- The horizon figure is an **extrapolation from 4 non-zero rungs**, stated as such, not a
  measurement. The measured statement is "never within 365 days on this grid".
- `data/prices_broad.csv` and the small panel are weekly/static and carry **no vintage
  ladder**, so B136 and SMALL439 are unmeasured here. The claim is about U56.
- SURVIVORSHIP (idea 54): U56 is current constituents only; every LEVEL here is biased upward.
  The claims are about **differences between vintages of the same panel**, which this biases
  far less.
