# PARK memo — the 4b verdict's DD clause is uncertifiable (idea 2042, lane cloud, 2026-09-20)

1. This is a PARK, not a KEEP and not a KILL. It changes how a 4b pass must be WORDED, not which cells pass. No book is added or withdrawn; RULES.md, PROTOCOL.md, scan.py, bot.py and baseline.py are untouched.
2. Object: rule 4b's five legs against SPY, given a PAIRED moving-block bootstrap (book and SPY resampled on the SAME day blocks, segments FULL / H1 / H2 / OOS resampled independently). 420 cells, B=500 (2,000 deep), seed 20260924, blocks {21,63}, confidence {90,95,99}%. Gates 10/10.
3. Four legs are real: over the 187 point-passing 4b-FULL cells the mean t is +2.56 (H1 Sharpe), +2.38 (H2 Sharpe), +2.83 (OOS Sharpe), +2.85 (CAGR floor), and **88 of 187 resolve all four at 95%** (126 at 90%; 100 at block 63 / 95%).
4. One leg never is: the **MaxDD cap** has mean t **+1.14** and its CI covers zero at **187 of 187** cells, at every block length and every confidence level.
5. And a block bootstrap cannot honestly certify it: MaxDD is a path statistic, and the resample shifts its distribution **-80.8%** of the point margin off centre, against -1.7% to -3.0% on the four non-path legs (gate G9, a 27x separation). The claim is "uncertifiable", NOT "zero".
6. Rule 8: 48 legal IS-only picks at 10 bps, **10 clear 4b FULL+OOS on points, 0 at 95%** — 8 of the 10 blocked by the DD cap, 2 by the CAGR floor. Path 4a is 5 of 48 and is likewise point-estimate-only.
7. The standing KEEP cell (U56, T=M, t=0.16, h=0.12) reads L1 t +2.61, L2 +3.04, L3 +3.36, L5 +4.29 — and L4_DD t **+0.64**, P(>0) 0.352. Its return/Sharpe edge over SPY is resolvable; its drawdown claim is not.
8. **RULES wording — no change to the rule itself; the change is to what a memo may assert.** Any memo quoting a 4b pass should read: "clears 4b FULL and OOS on point estimates; paired-bootstrap t on the narrowest leg = <t> (<leg>)." Where that narrowest leg is the DD cap, the pass must be written **"4b (DD leg uncertified)"** and must not be sized on the drawdown claim.
9. **Proposed PROTOCOL clause, drafted and NOT applied** (rule 6 — Sunday review only): *every published 4b pass quotes the paired-bootstrap t of its narrowest leg beside the verdict; a pass whose narrowest leg is the DD cap is labelled "4b (DD leg uncertified)".* The missing tool is a path-aware test — a stationary bootstrap with a fitted dependence length, or a parametric drawdown model.
10. Survivorship: U56 / B136 current-constituent lists, SMALL665 a current sub-$2B screen (54 tickers with max_1d_move >= 1.0 dropped). A bootstrap resamples the tape it is given and **cannot** put a confidence interval on survivorship, so a resolvable margin here is still measured on a favourable panel.
