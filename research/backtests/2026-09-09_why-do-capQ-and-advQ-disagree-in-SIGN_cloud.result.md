# Idea 313 — why do capQ and advQ disagree in SIGN? (cloud, 2026-09-09)

**Answer: (ii) the look-ahead in the 2026 cap stamp — and once it is removed the two keys do not
disagree at all, they agree in sign. Two further findings undercut the premise itself: the gap is
a one-decile artefact, and `capQ` is not a reproducible column.**

## The causal rebuild

`research/deepvalue/universe_under2b.csv` satisfies `mktcap == price * shares` to **4.4e-16** on
all 716 rows (gate G2, leg 1). So the record's capQ is one number per name — the 2026 price times
the 2026 share count — and its look-ahead is *exactly the 2026 price*, removable while holding
the share count fixed:

| key | definition | membership |
|---|---|---|
| `capQ26` | `price_2026 * shares` — the record's column | STATIC |
| `capC` | `px_t * shares` — causal, time-varying | STATIC and **DYNAMIC** |
| `capC0` | `px_first * shares` — purely ex-ante stamp | STATIC |
| `advQ` | `(px*vol).rolling(60).median()` — idea 51's column | STATIC and DYNAMIC |

`capC-DYNAMIC` is the cell idea 424 recorded as non-existent ("capQ is constant in time, so
capQ-D does not exist"). The causal rebuild creates it.

## The three channels (each measured twice, MA-RS, FULL sample)

`G = rho(advQ,D) − rho(capQ26,S) = +0.4600`

| channel | measurement 1 | measurement 2 | mean abs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LOOKAHEAD** | −0.7815 (vs capC0) | −0.3681 (vs capC) | **0.5748** |
| MEMBERSHIP | +0.3848 (capC) | +0.2363 (advQ) | 0.3106 |
| CONTENT | −0.1445 (at S) | −0.2930 (at D) | 0.2187 |

**LOOKAHEAD is the largest channel in 5 of 6 (arm, window) cells, including both OOS readings.
H_LOOK holds; H_MEMB fails.** Its magnitude *exceeds G itself* — the other two channels partly
offset it.

The decisive cell is the sign flip: **`capC0`-STATIC MA-RS rho = +0.657 (t +2.46)** against
`capQ26`'s −0.125. Take the look-ahead out of the cap key and its slope points the same way advQ's
does. There is no cap-vs-liquidity disagreement to explain.

*(Housekeeping: the script prints a `pathA − pathB` "interaction" of exactly 0. That is an
algebraic identity — both paths telescope to `rho(advQ,D) − rho(capC,S)` — not a measured null.)*

## But the premise does not survive either

**Channel (iii) also holds.** Leave-one-decile-out: dropping **D1** cuts |G| by **80.8%** (MA-RS,
+0.4600 → +0.0882); dropping **D10** cuts it **92.0%** *with a sign flip* (MA-DG, +0.3114 →
−0.0249). Decile 1 is exactly where survivorship bites: capC-D D1 EWall Sharpe 2.30, advQ-D D1
**2.028** — the 2.03 the queue flagged, reproduced.

**n = 10 honesty.** SE(rho) ≈ 0.354. Of 14 bootstrapped statistics (1,000 paired calendar-year
block draws) only **3** have a 90% band excluding zero — and **G is not one of them** (MA-RS p05
−0.016, MA-DG p05 −0.181). The disagreement was never distinguishable from sampling noise.

## `capQ` is not a reproducible column (addendum)

G1: advQ reproduces idea 424 **exactly** (9.9e-17 over 60 rows × 6 columns). capQ does **not**
(max |dSharpe| 0.233, 12/30 rows with a different decile size). The main script's console guesses
this is "qcut edge placement on ties" — **that guess is wrong**, and
`..._addendum.py` replaces it:

- The rebuild is faithful: idea 424's `mktcap` construction and this script's `price*shares` give
  the **same 430 names, the same decile edges, and 0 names in a different decile** (max relative
  diff 3.3e-16).
- The cause is the file. `universe_under2b.csv` is regenerated by the scheduled Filings job and
  was rewritten on 2026-09-09 (717 → 716 rows, *every row restamped*); coverage 434 → 430.
- **The published capQ slope drifts −0.3265 → −0.1249 (MA-RS) and −0.3350 → −0.1988 (MA-DG) in
  two days with no change of method, panel, cadence, gross or cost.** Idea 51's headline
  −0.336/−0.335 has moved ~0.21 on its own.

Any claim resting on a capQ decile slope carries an undeclared **vintage** parameter. `capC` does
not: it is reproducible from the price panel plus a share count.

## KEEP paths

4a **4/180**, 4b **1/180**, BOTH **0/180**.

The lone 4b passer — `capC`-DYNAMIC decile 1 MA-DG, CAGR 14.51%, Sharpe 1.859, MaxDD −16.5%,
halves 1.259/2.371, OOS Sharpe 2.218 — is **declined, not promoted**. It is the smallest-cap
decile of a current-constituents-only sub-$2B panel, so it is the maximally survivorship-inflated
cell in the grid; and it **loses to its own decile-1 EWall control out of sample** (2.2179 vs
2.3291). The MA gate subtracts. Rule 8 W2 picks beat SPY OOS 14/18 and RULES v2 16/18, but 16 of
the 18 picks are decile 1 — the same artefact, counted eighteen times.

**SURVIVORSHIP:** the small panel is current constituents of the sub-$2B screen only — no delisted
names — so every level (CAGR, Sharpe) is biased up, the thin deciles most of all. The channel
claims are key-vs-key differences on one panel, which the bias affects far less than levels, but
no level here is capital-worthy.

## Verdict

**KILL** of "capQ and advQ disagree in sign" as a fact about size keys. The gap is (a) mostly a
look-ahead artefact that reverses the cap slope when removed, (b) carried by one decile out of
ten, (c) inside its own bootstrap band, and (d) computed from a column that changes week to week
without anyone touching the method.
