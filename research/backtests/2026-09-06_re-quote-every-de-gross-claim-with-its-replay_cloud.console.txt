IDEA 296 - re-quote-every-de-gross-claim-with-its-constant-leverage-replay (lane cloud, 2026-09-06)
===============================================================================================================================================================================
A de-grossing gate holds less on average, and holding less buys drawdown and costs CAGR by
itself.  Every published de-gross claim therefore carries a free rider.  This run attaches
the zero-parameter CONSTANT-LEVERAGE REPLAY - the comparand run at the de-grossed book's
own realised mean gross - to every cell of the published de-gross construction space and
counts how many gate claims survive.
Costs 10 bps (plus a 0-bps rung), gross 0.75, next-day execution, no shorting, no leverage.
