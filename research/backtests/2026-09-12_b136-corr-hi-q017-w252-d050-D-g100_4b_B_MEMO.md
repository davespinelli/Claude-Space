# 4b memo — B136 CORRELATION-HI de-gross gate, gross 1.00, daily (lane B, 2026-09-12, idea 606) — **NOT PROPOSED FOR PROMOTION**

1. **What it is.** RULES wording: *"Each day, compute the 20-day average pairwise correlation of the
   B136 panel from the equal-weight index-vs-name variance identity `rho = (s_idx^2 - s2bar/N) /
   (sbar^2 - s2bar/N)`, where `s_idx` is the 20-day standard deviation of the equal-weight mean daily
   return, `sbar` and `s2bar` the cross-sectional mean of the names' 20-day standard deviations and of
   their squares, and N the number of names priced. Hold every eligible name (above its 200d average,
   `vol20 < 0.60`) equal-weight at `gross / n = 1.00 / n` of NAV, rebalanced weekly (W). Whenever
   today's `rho` is above its own trailing 252-day 83rd percentile, halve every weight tomorrow and
   leave the released half idle at 0% (DE-GROSS — weight is never re-spread); restore full weight the
   day after `rho` falls back inside. 10 bps per unit turnover, next-day execution, no shorting, no
   leverage."* Two dials only: the quantile (0.17 tail) and its window (252d).
2. **How it got here.** Idea 606 is a measurement run about whether idea 602's placebo-excess rate
   slope is a property of gates that fire or of the breadth signal. This book is a **post-hoc** cell of
   that run's 3,456-cell head-rung grid. It is reported because it is one of only **2 of 3,456** arms
   that survive all four filters at once: 4b full sample, 4b read inside the OOS window, the rule-8
   `(level, w)` chooser fitted on 2009–2016 alone, and a **gross-matched twin that 4b itself rejects**.
3. **Full sample (2009-01-13 →, W book, D gate):** CAGR **14.02%**, Sharpe **1.1538**, MaxDD
   **−15.11%**, halves **1.2097 / 1.0973**. IS Sharpe 1.0936.
4. **OOS (2017-01-01 →, read once):** **14.29% / 1.2080 / −15.11%**.
5. **vs SPY (the 4b comparand):** SPY 15.16% / 0.8861 / −33.72%, halves 0.9596/0.8259, OOS 0.8767.
   All five 4b legs pass: H1 1.2097 > 0.9596 ✓, H2 1.0973 > 0.8259 ✓, OOS 1.2080 > 0.8767 ✓, MaxDD
   −15.11% ≥ −20.23% cap ✓, CAGR 14.02% ≥ 10.61% floor ✓. **4b PASS. 4a FAIL** — H1 1.2097 < RULES v2's
   1.2348 and MaxDD −15.11% is worse than the live book's −12.24%; 4a passes on 11 of this run's 10,368
   cells and on none of these two.
6. **The control that matters.** Removing the gate and holding the same book ungated at gross 1.00 gives
   14.20% / **1.0211** / **−23.09%** — it **fails 4b on the drawdown cap**. The arm's own gross-matched
   twin (the ungated book at the arm's realised mean gross of 0.9172) gives Sharpe 1.0211 / MaxDD
   −21.33% and **also fails 4b**. So on this cell 4b is seeing the *gate*, not the exposure it leaves —
   the distinction idea 596 found 4b structurally blind to, and which killed all 141 of its arms.
7. **Cost and execution ladders.** 4b passes at **0 / 5 / 10 / 25 bps** (15.46%/1.258, 14.74%/1.206,
   14.02%/1.154, 11.90%/0.997) and fails at 50 bps. 4b passes at execution lag **1, 2 and 3 days**
   (Sharpe 1.154 / 1.083 / 1.053). The gate fires on **16.57%** of days; mean gross 0.9172.
8. **Why it is NOT proposed.** (a) **Post-hoc**: the cell was read off a grid built to answer a different
   question, so its selection has not been priced. (b) The rule-8 chooser only ever picked `(level, w)`
   — depth, cadence and gross were reported axes, and choosing among them is selection this run did not
   pay for. (c) **164 of 3,456** head-rung arms are "clean" 4b+OOS passers and 81 of those are CORR-HI;
   singling out this one is a choice, even though only 2 survive the 25-bps leg as well. (d) The run's
   own claim leg fails rule 8 — the placebo excess measured on 2009–2016 does not predict the same arm's
   2017+ excess (Spearman +0.263 for CORR-HI, negative for two other families), so the *mechanism* this
   book is supposed to embody is not itself out-of-sample stable.
9. **Survivorship.** B136 is the **current** constituent list of `universe_broad.json`; dead names are
   absent, so the 14.02% CAGR is biased upward and the CAGR-floor leg is easier than on a point-in-time
   panel. The drawdown leg — which is what the gate actually buys here — is less affected. Correlation
   computed on a survivor panel is also an optimistic state variable: names that died are exactly the
   ones that would have co-moved hardest in the 2020 and 2022 episodes.
