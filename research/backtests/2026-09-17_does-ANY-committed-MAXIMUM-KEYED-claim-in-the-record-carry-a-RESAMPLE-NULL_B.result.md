**AND THE VINTAGE ARM SAYS SOMETHING ABOUT MAXIMA SPECIFICALLY, opposite to the naive reading.** The extra bar moves the same books' CAGR and Sharpe at 1e-03 and moves **ALL TWELVE maximum-keyed objects by EXACTLY 0.0000**.
