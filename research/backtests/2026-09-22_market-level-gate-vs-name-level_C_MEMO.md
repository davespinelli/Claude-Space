# Memo — idea 2311 (lane C, 2026-09-22): ONE market-level gate vs 56 name-level gates

1. **Question / answer.** Is RULES v2's per-name 200d band gate worth its cross-sectional dispersion, or is one market-level gate the whole effect? **Answer: the dispersion IS the effect.** Replacing the 56 name gates with one gate on an equal-weight index of the same panel loses Sharpe at **16 of 16 exposure-matched cells** (median **-0.1268**), and loses CAGR too (median **-0.7934 pp**) and drawdown (median **-4.3277 pp**). The market gate clears 4b at **0 of 80** cells; the name gate clears it at 28 of 80.

Two tuned dials only (level, band); gross, cost, panel, cadence reported.
