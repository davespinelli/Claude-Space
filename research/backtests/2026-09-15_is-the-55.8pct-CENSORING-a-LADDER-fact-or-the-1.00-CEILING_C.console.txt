RULE 8 WALK-FORWARD -- g chosen on 2009-2016 ONLY at each ceiling, OOS 2017-2026 read once
================================================================================================================
CHOOSERS (both IS-only): IS_MID = midpoint of the IS band at that ceiling; IS_SHARPE = the
  0.01 rung inside the IS band with the best IS Sharpe.  Stated, not worked around: the
  STRICT IS 4b set is often EMPTY because a half-sample Sharpe leg read inside an 8-year
  window fails at every gross (675's finding), so the IS band is the intersection of the
  two IS LEVEL legs and the choosers are IS-LEVEL choosers.  Every pick above g=1.00 is
