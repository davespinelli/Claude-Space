### (6) MECHANISM — is the correlation ordering a fact about DELIVERED Sharpe, or about
###     the benchmark idea 100's statistic subtracts?
  conv_per_pp subtracts the LINEAR BLEND (1-f)*Sharpe(0) + f*Sharpe(1).  Sharpe(1) is the
  sleeve's own standalone Sharpe, and adding equity ETFs raises it.  So a sleeve can look
  less 'convex' purely because the yardstick it is measured against got longer, with no
  change in the Sharpe the blend actually delivers.  raw_per_pp has no such term.
