## 1. The causal test: what does a clause with ZERO content do to 4a?

Idea 275's clause, with the trigger replaced by a coin: on `k` seeded-random days the book
sells one genuinely-held name in full, sell-only, proceeds to cash until the next scheduled
rebalance. Identical mechanics, identical accounting, **no information whatsoever**.
2 panels × 2 books × k ∈ {1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,256,512} × 40 seeds × {0,10,25} bps =
**4,800 arm-rungs, all reported**. Every arm fires exactly `k` events.

**PROTOCOL 4a admits pure noise.** Against its own control:

| | 0 bps | 10 bps | 25 bps | all rungs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| whole family (1,600 arms per rung) | 180 (**11.25%**) | 72 (**4.50%**) | 33 (**2.06%**) | **285 / 4,800 = 5.94%** |
| activity-matched (\|dTurn\| < 0.02 x/yr, the bucket idea 275's arm lives in) | **4.71%** | **5.49%** | **3.92%** | — |
| median \|dSharpe\| in that bucket | 0.0002 | 0.0002 | 0.0003 | — |

4a vs the **live** RULES v2 book: 167 / 4,800 (v1-book noise never clears it; v2-book noise
does, because there the control *is* the live book). **4b: 0 / 4,800 at every rung.**

**But the artefact is variance, not vacuity.** If the queue's story were right, the 4a rate
would be highest where the clause does least. Gross of costs it is the opposite — the rate
*rises* monotonically with activity:

| \|dTurn\| bucket (x/yr) | <0.02 | 0.02–0.05 | 0.05–0.10 | 0.10–0.25 | 0.25–0.50 | >0.50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4a rate, 0 bps | 0.047 | 0.108 | 0.136 | **0.163** | 0.149 | 0.153 |
| 4a rate, 10 bps | 0.055 | 0.062 | 0.060 | 0.058 | 0.037 | 0.011 |
| 4a rate, 25 bps | 0.039 | 0.021 | 0.044 | 0.005 | 0.000 | 0.000 |

More activity buys more chances to beat the control on both halves; **cost is the only thing
that takes them away.** That reproduces idea 275's own "it is a cost verdict, not a signal
verdict" from the opposite direction.

**The conjunction is what saves the parent's arm.** Idea 275's passer cleared 4a at 0 *and*
10 *and* 25 bps. A content-free arm does that **6 / 1,600 times (0.37%)**, and **3 / 510
(0.59%)** among activity-matched arms. On a 60-arm grid that is ~0.35 expected passers, so
observing one is unremarkable — the arm is **not demonstrably an artefact**, and it is also
not evidence of content. (For the record, the six noise arms that clear all three rungs are
listed in the console; the smallest carries \|dTurn\| = 0.0000 x/yr.)

Note on matching: idea 275's clause is near-costless (dTurn +0.000231 x/yr on 32 events)
because it front-runs a sale the weekly band was going to make anyway; a random sell at the
same `k` costs ~200× more (U56/v2 k=32 median dTurn **0.0486 x/yr**). That is why the null is
matched on **dTurn**, not on `k`.

## 4. The proposed PROTOCOL minimum-activity clause — priced, and NOT recommended

Clause under test: *delete a 4a pass whose turnover differs from its comparand by less than
tau x/yr AND whose worse-half Sharpe margin is under delta.* Share of the record's 10,019
published 4a passes (those carrying H1/H2 and a plausible turnover unit) it would delete:

| tau \ delta | 0.000 | 0.005 | 0.010 | 0.020 | 0.050 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.005 | 0.50% | 0.85% | 0.87% | 0.89% | 0.89% |
| 0.020 | 0.73% | 1.10% | 1.17% | 1.19% | 1.19% |
| **0.050** | 1.10% | 1.47% | 1.56% | 1.59% | 1.59% |
| 0.100 | 1.61% | 2.00% | 2.12% | 2.16% | 2.16% |
| 1.000 | 15.60% | 16.34% | 16.58% | 16.85% | 17.71% |

Against the demonstrable noise passes of Part 1 (n = 72 at 10 bps) the same clause catches
0% at delta = 0 (every noise pass has a positive margin by construction), 55.6% at
tau 0.05 / delta 0.005 and 70.8% at tau 0.10 / delta 0.010 — and only reaches 98.6% at
tau 1.00, where it is deleting 17.7% of the record.

**Recommendation (not adopted here; PROTOCOL is unedited):** the minimum-activity clause is a
blunt instrument aimed at the wrong failure. Two cheaper lines do the job the queue wanted:

1. **4a must clear at every cost rung the file reports.** On this evidence that alone cuts the
   content-free pass rate from **5.49% to 0.59%** — a 9× tightening for zero extra compute.
2. **Publish the matched null beside any 4a claim**: the 4a pass rate of a content-free arm of
   the same book matched on \|dTurn\|. A 4a pass with a rounding-scale margin is only
   interpretable against its own null, and the null is not zero.
