# Memo — the EWELIG 5-sleeve offset ensemble (idea 925, lane C, 2026-09-15). PARK, not promoted.

1. **What it is.** The 2026-09-03 memo's Finding-2 book (`EWELIG`), run as five weekly sleeves on five different rebalance dates instead of one, so that no calendar has to be chosen. It exists because this run found the book's published verdicts — the `ISH1_HALF` Sharpe leg AND the FULL-sample 4b CAGR floor — are both smaller than the book's own rebalance-offset spread.
2. **Exact RULES wording if it were ever promoted (rule 6: Sunday review only; NOT applied here).** *"Each week, a name is ELIGIBLE if it is priced that day, trades above its own 200-day moving average, and its 20-day realised annualised volatility is below 0.60. Hold every eligible name at 0.75/k of NAV, k = the number of eligible names that day; hold the remainder in cash (de-gross, never re-spread). Run FIVE sleeves of this book in parallel, each holding one fifth of NAV and each rebalancing weekly on its own offset — the last trading day of the ISO week plus 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 trading days. Weights are decided at the close and executed at the next close. No ranking, no vol scaler, no shorting, no leverage."*
3. **Full sample, U56, 10 bps, next-day execution (2009-01-13 … 2026-09-14).** CAGR 10.73%, Sharpe 1.087, MaxDD −15.72%, H1/H2 Sharpe 1.106 / 1.072, turnover ≈ 8.0×/yr per sleeve.
4. **Against the comparands.** SPY 15.13% / 0.885 / −33.72% (H1 0.959, H2 0.824); RULES v2 live 8.62% / 1.201 / −12.05%. Path **4b clears on all four legs**: H1 +0.1470, H2 +0.2487, MaxDD +4.52 pp inside the −20.23% cap, CAGR +0.14 pp above the 10.59% floor. Path **4a fails** — the live book's Sharpe is higher in both halves.
5. **Rule 8 (out of sample, 2017-2026, read once).** 11.70% / 1.155 / −15.72% against SPY 15.27% / 0.874 / −33.72% and RULES v2 9.46% / 1.277 / −12.05%; every 4b leg clears (+0.3458 / +0.2257 / +4.52 pp / +1.01 pp). The book is carried at its published gross, not chosen, so nothing about it was fitted out of sample.
6. **Why it is PARKED and not a KEEP.** The binding leg's margin is 0.14 pp of CAGR against a 0.91 pp spread across the five single-date sleeves — by the very clause this run applies to idea 922, the pass is inside its own convention noise. The ensemble removes the *choice* of date, not the fragility of the level.
7. **Second reason.** Its IS window (2009-2016) fails 4b on the same CAGR floor by −0.90 pp, so the pass is carried by the out-of-sample half; and **no IS-only chooser reaches this book** — all 48 rule-8 choosers pick `CAND20_NG` or `EWALL` instead (4a 0 of 48).
8. **Third reason.** On the published single calendar the same book at the same gross **misses** FULL 4b (10.36% CAGR against the 10.59% floor); 2 of the 5 sleeves miss it alone. A rule whose verdict depends on which weekday you trade is not capital-worthy as a single-sleeve book, and the five-sleeve form is 5× the operational surface for 0.14 pp of headroom.
9. **Survivorship.** U56 is a current-constituent list, so the CAGR and MaxDD levels above are optimistic for the book and for RULES v2 alike; SPY is not inflated, so every margin measured against SPY — including the 0.14 pp CAGR floor margin that decides this — is an **upper bound**. A survivorship-free panel would most likely take it below the floor.
10. **What would change the verdict.** A version of this book whose CAGR-floor margin exceeds its own convention spread (e.g. on a panel built without survivorship, or with the floor margin re-measured across rebalance *cadence* as well as offset), plus an IS-only chooser that actually selects it. Until then: PARK. Nothing in RULES.md, PROTOCOL.md, scan.py, bot.py or baseline.py was modified by this run.
