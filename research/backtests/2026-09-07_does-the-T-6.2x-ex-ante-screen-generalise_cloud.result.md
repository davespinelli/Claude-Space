## Mechanism

Low turnover on this menu is **not** a gross story (corr(T_IS, gross) = +0.236). It is a
**cadence and count** story: corr(T_IS, cadence rank W<M<Q) = **−0.446**, corr(T_IS, book
concentration rank EWALL<B03R<B12R<V2DG<N40<N20<N10) = **+0.563**. A turnover budget is therefore a
disguised n/cadence dial, and its cut point is a property of the menu's turnover distribution, not
of portfolios — which is exactly why 6.2 does not travel.
