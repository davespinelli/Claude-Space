## Caveats

1. **SURVIVORSHIP (idea 54).** Both pools are current constituents with no delistings; the bias
   is *larger* for the small pool, so B_SMALL — and B_MATCH at high q — is the more inflated bar.
   That makes every "the books fail the matched bar" finding here **conservative**.
2. **The benchmarks are index conventions, not tradable books**, priced at 0 bps while the books
   pay 10 bps — again conservative for a KILL. The 10-bps variant is reported above and moves
   nothing (10 → 10 survivors).
3. **B_OWN peeks at the panel's own draw** and is the strictest bar available, not a deployable
   one.
4. Six readings of one corpus are not six independent pieces of evidence; the fresh rule-8 block
   is the only genuinely out-of-sample leg and it is the one that reads 0/60.
