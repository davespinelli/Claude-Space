ARM 0 — WHAT THE EXCLUSION IS, BEFORE ANY PRICE IS READ
============================================================================================================

  A ladder of k rungs is EXACTLY monotone under a random ordering with probability 2/k!.
  The four ladders the record owns have four completely different null rates, so ONE
  exclusion rule is four different rules:

     ladder      k     P(|rho|=1)   P(|rho|>=0.95)  P(>=0.90)  P(>=0.80)  P(>=0.60)
     N           6     2.778e-03         0.0028     0.0167     0.0583     0.2417
     H           4     8.333e-02         0.0833     0.0833     0.3333     0.4167
     GROSS      10     5.511e-07         0.0001     0.0008     0.0070     0.0720
     CADENCE     2     1.000e+00         1.0000     1.0000     1.0000     1.0000

  READ IT BEFORE THE DATA: CADENCE is monotone with probability 1.000 — a two-rung
  ladder cannot be anything else — so ANY monotone exclusion deletes it unconditionally,
  on any tape, for a reason that has nothing to do with the tape.  GROSS's null is
  5.5e-07, so GROSS being monotone at 6 of 6 cells (1154) is either a 1-in-a-million
  coincidence six times over or a STRUCTURAL fact (1189: Sharpe is invariant to gross at
  a 0% cash rate; what survives is a monotone cost drag).  N and H are where the rule
  does actual work: 0.00278 and 0.0833.
