## VERDICT
   gates 10/10 PASS; hypotheses 0/5 PASS (H_COLLAPSE, H_MONO, H_RHO, H_NULL, H_LEN FAIL).

   ANSWER to the queue's question: NO — the composition band is not a rho artefact, and
   it does not collapse when the windows stop overlapping.  It does the OPPOSITE:
     - at L = 550, K = 6, the band runs 0.2382 -> 0.1353 -> 0.1266 -> 0.0809 -> 0.0682 over f = 0.00/0.25/0.50/0.75/0.90,
       i.e. the DISJOINT grid's band is 3.49x the f=0.90 grid's, not half of it;
     - measured cross-window rho moves -0.073 -> +0.019 -> -0.009 -> +0.142 -> +0.705 (worst |rho| at f=0 over the three lengths 0.073, against 0.9046 on the
       record's Q16), so the grid separates what its label says it separates;
     - AT MATCHED WINDOW COUNT the gap is an order of magnitude: the record's own Q16
       grid read at K = 6 (every stride-2 placement) bands 0.0193, against 0.2382 for
       the DISJOINT L = 550 grid — 12.3x;
     - and the reason is arithmetic, not economics: a band is max - min over K draws, and
       K nearly-identical (overlapping) draws cannot spread.  Overlap SUPPRESSES a band.

   THE TWO SHAPE FAILS, reported as loudly as the answer.  H_MONO and H_RHO both FAIL,
   and neither failure touches the answer above:
     - H_MONO: 2 violations of a 0.005 tolerance, L400 at f=0.90; L700 at f=0.25.  The large one is
       L=700 f=0.25 (0.2813 against 0.1837 at f=0.00) — and it is the SAME point that
       breaches H_NULL below, i.e. one grid, not a trend, and it sits in the direction of
       MORE band at LOW overlap, which the answer already claims.
     - H_RHO: its LEVEL leg passes (|rho| <= 0.073 at f=0.00 on all three lengths,
       rising to +0.705..+0.790 at f=0.90); only its MONOTONICITY leg fails,
       on wiggles of 0.026-0.028 at L=400 and L=550 where rho is statistically zero and a
       6-window correlation read over 18 books cannot resolve 0.02.  The bar was too tight
       for the object; it is scored FAIL as written.

   WHAT SURVIVES, and it is the reusable half.  Almost every band in this run — the
   record's own Q16 band included — is INSIDE the band its own geometry-matched null
   produces, but NOT all of them, and H_NULL is recorded FAIL for it:
     14 of 15 points sit at or below the 0.95 bar; the exception is L = 700, f = 0.25 at 0.970
     (worst single cell 0.978).  Over 15 points a correct sampler is EXPECTED to breach 0.95 about 0.75 times, so one
     breach is not evidence of a composition effect — but the bar was pre-registered at
     ALL points and it is scored as it was written, not as it would be convenient.
     Median percentile over the 15 points 0.335, median obs/null ratio 0.804;
     Q16_TAIL itself sits at 0.409 of its own null (observed 0.0335 against a null median of 0.0361), and
     Q16_FIXLEN at 0.280.  So 1036's 0.0191 is a SAMPLING-GEOMETRY fact
     and not a statement about which episodes a window holds: it should never have been
     read as END COMPOSITION.

   THE LENGTH CHANNEL — H_LEN FAIL.  Length-matching the record's own 16 windows moves the band
     from 0.0335 to 0.0469 (|d| 0.0134) and 1036's OWN convention from 0.0191 to 0.0348,
     i.e. 1.82x.  So a large part of the published 0.0191 is WINDOW LENGTH, not content — and
     equalising the lengths makes the band BIGGER, because equal-length staggered windows
     are less nested than tail windows.  Both directions are geometry; neither is content.

   WHAT THE RECORD MAY NO LONGER SAY: that the 0.0191 band shows END COMPOSITION moves
   the K-free null sd, or that overlap between OOS windows INFLATES a published band.  On
   this tape overlap deflates it, by roughly an order of magnitude at matched K.
   WHAT IT MAY SAY: that a band over K windows is only interpretable beside (i) K, (ii)
   the windows' lengths, (iii) their overlap, and (iv) the band the same books' own
   resampled paths give on that same geometry.

   KEEP / KILL under PROTOCOL rule 4.  This run carries NO new book: its object is the
   record's own null construction, and the rule-8 arm re-runs GRID ladder books the
   record already holds.  Neither KEEP path is claimable and none is claimed.

     rule 8 WINDOW GEOMETRY clause — a published band over a grid of windows states the
     window COUNT, the window LENGTHS and the OVERLAP, and is read against the band the
     SAME books' own resampled paths produce on that SAME geometry.  A band is max - min
     over K correlated draws: on this tape, holding K = 6 and the length fixed, it runs
     0.2382 at zero overlap against 0.0682 at 90% overlap, and the record's own Q16 grid bands 0.0193
     at the same K.  Geometry, not composition, is what an unqualified band measures.
