THE GUARD THIS RUN PROPOSES (PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED -- PROTOCOL rule 6)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PROTOCOL 4b currently reads THREE Sharpe legs: H1, H2 and rule-8 OOS. On this tree the
  H2 and OOS windows overlap by the percentages printed above, so the third leg is close
  to a restatement of the second rather than an independent test. Proposed amendment:
  (i) rule 8's OOS window must be DISJOINT from the second half used by 4b -- either the
      halves are taken INSIDE the IS window (2009-2016 split in two) or the 4b halves are
      dropped in favour of IS/OOS; and
  (ii) any published 4b PASS carries, beside it, the per-leg base rate of its OWN cell's
      gross-matched null under BOTH null kinds, and any leg whose base rate exceeds 0.90
      is reported as NOT CERTIFYING rather than as a pass.
  Scored on this run's grid, clause (ii) would strip the OOS leg from every cell where it
  reads >= 0.90 above, and clause (i) would make the H1/H2 split independent of rule 8.
