**Pre-registered bar — the constant scores 3/4, so it DOES NOT SURVIVE as written:**

- **B1 PASS** — M0's 95% CI [-0.02056, -0.00269] contains -0.014.
- **B2 PASS** — family homogeneity holds in both models, and carrying n_arms makes it *more*
  homogeneous, not less: Wald **23.05 -> 11.46**, p **0.1021 -> 0.4860**.
- **B3 PASS** — log2(arms) is not significant file-clustered (t -1.17, p 0.2759).
- **B4 FAIL** — the `n` family's own file-clustered CI is **[-0.04365, -0.01957]**, which
  **excludes -0.014**. cadence [-0.01706, +0.00212], gross [-0.01490, +0.00374],
  other [-0.01892, +0.00606] and trim [-0.10191, -0.00553] all contain it.

So: the record may quote -0.014 **pooled**; it may not quote it **for the n dial**, which is
where the record does most of its dial-tuning.

## What this costs the record

1. Quote **-0.014 pooled** if you must, with its CI [-0.0206, -0.0027] and its 73-file cluster
   count. Do **not** quote it for the `n` dial: -0.0316 [-0.0437, -0.0196] is a different number,
   and `n` is the dial the record tunes most.
2. Idea 270's homogeneity finding is **strengthened**, not weakened, by carrying n_arms
   (Wald halves, p 0.10 -> 0.49). The queue's suspicion that family is a proxy for menu size is
   **directionally right but statistically unproven** at file level (t -1.17): what tracks menu
   size cleanly is the DISAGREEMENT RATE (0.159 -> 0.713), not the size of the cost.
3. The number does **not transfer**. On a controlled live grid built for this question the
   same statistic is **+0.0140** and -0.014 is rejected at p 0.0001 — while zero is not
   rejected. The honest live reading is that choosing on IS CAGR instead of IS Sharpe costs
   **nothing measurable**; the record's negative constant is a property of which menus the
   record happened to publish, not of the choice.

## Caveats

- Leg A is observational: record menus differ in construction, cost rung, sample and split, and
  73 (93 today) file clusters with one file carrying 1,028 menus is why every p here is a wild
  cluster bootstrap rather than an analytic t.
- The live sign flip rests on 31 disagreeing menus out of 306; its magnitude (+0.1378
  conditional) is not a precise estimate, and it is dominated by `volgate` and `n`.
- `gross` disagrees on **zero** of its 63 designed menus — both selectors always take gross 1.00
  on these panels — so the gross family contributes no information to the live test.
- SURVIVORSHIP: B136 is current constituents of a current screen; SMALL439 is the 483-name
  sub-$2B panel less the 44 tickers with max_1d_move >= 1.0. 2009-2026 is a QQQ-favourable
  regime and only 2020 and 2022 are real stress tests.
- No network was used; all prices come from committed caches (`data/prices.csv` ends 2026-09-09,
  `prices_broad.csv` and `prices_small.csv.gz` end 2026-09-04).

## Reconciliation with the same-day cloud sibling

A cloud run of idea 493 landed on `main` while this one was executing; the two designs were
written independently and neither was on `main` when the other ran.

**They agree to the digit** on: file-clustered dS **-0.0162** over idea 270's committed 6,956
menus; **-0.0161** over a fresh corpus that both runs independently sized at **8,931 menus**;
the pooled arms slope **-0.00735** per doubling; M0's CI containing -0.014; and **BOTH KEEP
paths 0** on the live leg (this run 0/918 books, the sibling 0/360 arms).

**They split on the null, not on the data.** The sibling BLOCKS within file and arm count and
rejects family exchangeability at **p <= 0.0015 at 11 of 12 (FAMSET x ARMSCTL) points**; this
run makes the FILE the unit of inference (CR1 with a wild cluster bootstrap) and does not
(**Wald p 0.1021 -> 0.4860**). Both are right about their own null: the sibling is testing
within-file dial contrasts, this run is testing between-file variation, and idea 270's own
p 0.28 / 0.31 is the second of the two.

The consequence is the same under either null, and it is the answer to the queue's question:
**the per-family numbers are not all -0.014** — here the `n` family's own file-clustered CI
[-0.04365, -0.01957] excludes it — so the record may quote the constant **pooled only, with
its cluster unit stated**, and never for a single dial.
