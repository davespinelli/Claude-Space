(0) THE ARITHMETIC, PRINTED BEFORE ANY DATA IS TOUCHED
    A percentile read as mean(null < obs) over K draws lives on the lattice {0, 1/K,
    ..., 1}: it takes at most K+1 values, and a reading of 1.000 means ONLY 'obs
    exceeded all K draws'. Every convention below prints a DIFFERENT sentence for
    that same event, and only B_CP's number is a coverage probability.
        K    B_POINT      B_ONE  (1-1/(K+1))    B_CP (0.05**(1/K))
       10      1.000                0.909091              0.741134
       25      1.000                0.961538              0.887072
       50      1.000                0.980392              0.941845
      100      1.000                0.990099              0.970487
      200      1.000                0.995025              0.985133
      400      1.000                0.997506              0.992539
    NOTE THE SIGN OF THE DISAGREEMENT: B_ONE RISES with K (a finer lattice puts the
    ceiling closer to 1) while B_CP RISES TOO but from far below — at K=10 the honest
    95% statement is 0.741, i.e. 'the book beat a coin flip at least 74% of the time',
    NOT 1.000. The point form OVERSTATES the K=10 evidence by 0.259 IN THE STATISTIC'S
    OWN UNITS, and the record has 0.963-1.000 of its U56/B136 cells sitting there.
