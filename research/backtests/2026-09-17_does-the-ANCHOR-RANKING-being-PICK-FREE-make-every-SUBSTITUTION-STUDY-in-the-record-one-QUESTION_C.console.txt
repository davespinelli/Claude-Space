IDEA 1240 (lane C, 2026-09-17) — does the ANCHOR RANKING being PICK-FREE make every
SUBSTITUTION STUDY in the record ONE QUESTION?
==============================================================================================================

  DELTA(A,P) = SUM_c w_P(c) S(A;c) - K(P).  K(P) is PICK-ONLY and shifts every
  alternative equally.  EXACT CRITERION, declared before the tape is read:
      RANK-DEGENERATE  <=>  contrast is MEAN-FORM  AND  w_P == w_P'.
  Two escapes: (E1) a pick set that weights CELLS differently; (E2) a contrast that is
  not mean-form.  Whether either MOVES THE RANK on this tape is measured, not assumed.
  PRE-DECLARED: (A) ONE QUESTION — degenerate share > 0.50 AND escapes move the
  incumbent's rank < 1 place on median.  (B) NOT ONE QUESTION — share <= 0.50 or
  escapes move >= 2 places.  (C) MIXED — share > 0.50 but escapes move >= 2 places.
