# PARK memo — the B136 monthly book at gross 0.50 (idea 1346, lane cloud, 2026-09-19)

**Status: PARK, and this memo recommends AGAINST enactment.** De-grossing does exactly what idea
1346 predicted — it buys back the 4b drawdown leg on the one panel whose cadence argmax is monthly,
producing a cell that clears 4b on every leg full-sample and out-of-sample and beats the standing
2026-09-04 incumbent's Sharpe. It is filed so a Sunday review can see it, not because the record
should take it: rule 8's in-sample chooser rejects it on the panel it lives on.

**Exact RULES wording, if it were ever enacted.** *"Rebalance on the last trading day of each
calendar month only, not each week. On that day hold the 15 highest-composite eligible names at

**Why it is a PARK and not a KEEP.** (i) Rule 8 — GROSS and CADENCE chosen on warm-up..2016-12-31
by IS Sharpe — picks (0.75, W) on B136, not this cell, because **B136's monthly advantage is wholly
post-2017**: M − W is **−0.0307** of IS Sharpe and **+0.0808** of OOS Sharpe. A chooser standing in
2016 rejects monthly outright. (ii) The 4b window is **one gross rung wide** (0.40 fails the CAGR
floor by 1.04 pp, 0.60 fails the DD cap by 2.53 pp), so the pass is a knife-edge in a dial the
record tunes freely. (iii) 4a fails, as it does at all 24 cells. (iv) It is a BROAD-panel book, and
B136 carries the same current-constituent survivorship bias as U56 with none of U56's certification
history — an edge that appears only after 2017 is exactly what that bias would manufacture.

**What is real in it.** The DD/CAGR exchange is genuine and cheap: 0.60 → 0.50 buys +3.54 pp of
MaxDD for −2.45 pp of CAGR and costs 0.33/yr LESS turnover, and Sharpe moves by 0.0016 over the
whole four-rung ladder (de-grossing is a pure exposure dial, not a timing one). And monthly's
turnover advantage is real at every gross (1.34–2.48/yr against weekly's 1.80–3.28), worth
5–8 bp/yr of drag at 10 bps.
