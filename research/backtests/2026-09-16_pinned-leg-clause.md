6. **A per-leg vector cannot be multiplied.** On the 13 CANON cells with a non-zero independence product, the median ratio `base4b / Π p_leg` is **3.867** (range 0.991–20.000, 11 of 13 above 1.0). H_DEP **PASS**. Reading a published leg census as five independent probabilities understates the joint rate by roughly **4×**.

10. **PROPOSED PROTOCOL rule 4 reporting clause (exact wording):** *"A 4b base rate of 0.000 is published with the per-leg pass-rate vector that produced it, with the legs whose own rate is 0.000 named, and with the rule-of-three bound `1 − 0.05^(1/D)` for its draw count D. A cell with exactly one such leg is reported as unpassable on THAT leg, not as a 4b result; a per-leg vector is never multiplied into a joint rate, and a claim that a leg is pinned across a family is stated with the count of books it holds for."* **What it is NOT:** it does not reject 4b, add a leg, move a bar, or license a capital decision — a pin is a fact about a coin flip's base rate, and rule 8 shows it chooses nothing.
11. **Survivorship (rule 9).** U56 and B136 are current-constituent lists, so every CAGR and drawdown LEVEL here is optimistic and a coin flip drawn from a survivor panel is a better book than one drawn in real time.
