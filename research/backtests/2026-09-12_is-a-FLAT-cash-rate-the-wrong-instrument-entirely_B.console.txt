PART F — is GROSS even IDENTIFIABLE to an IS Sharpe selector? (why the picks flip)
====================================================================================================
Sharpe is scale-invariant, so with cash at ZERO the gross dial moves IS Sharpe only
through costs — it is very nearly a no-op. A cash credit breaks that invariance and
makes lower gross genuinely raise IS Sharpe. That is the mechanism behind PART D.

ZERO's gross dial is worth 0.0010 of IS Sharpe — the selector is choosing on the 4th
decimal — while it costs 6.19% of OOS CAGR. Every credited arm makes the
dial 84x to 309x more visible in sample, and points it at LOW gross.

So the ZERO arm's rule-8 4b PASS is a TIE-BREAK, not a selection: at band 0.08 its five
gross rungs span IS Sharpe 1.121735..1.122612 and OOS CAGR 5.98%..12.04%. Not a KEEP.
