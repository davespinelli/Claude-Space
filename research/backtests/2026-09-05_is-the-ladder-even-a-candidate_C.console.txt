Q4 — THE COLLAPSED BAR.  If neither the floor nor the cap is individually load-bearing but the pair is, they are ONE bar.
    Closed form: along the family CAGR rises and |MaxDD| rises with m, so the binding point is m* = the largest admissible m under the DD cap; the pair reduces to
       CAGR(m*) >= phi x CAGR_SPY   with   |MaxDD(m*)| <= delta x |MaxDD_SPY|
    and, if the rescale were exactly linear, to the single ratio bar
       CAGR / |MaxDD|  >=  (phi x CAGR_SPY) / (delta x |MaxDD_SPY|)   [a Calmar bar]
