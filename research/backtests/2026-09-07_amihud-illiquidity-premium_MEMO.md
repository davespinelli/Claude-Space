# Idea 33 — Amihud illiquidity premium: PARK memo (NOT a KEEP recommendation)

1. **What passes.** Q5 (least liquid quintile of SMALL439 by causal 60d Amihud), equal weight,
   weekly, next-day, 10 bps: CAGR 26.88%, Sharpe 1.688, MaxDD -33.81%, halves 1.783/1.739,
   OOS 1.724. De-grossed to 40% gross it clears **all five 4b bars** (13.88%/1.693/-19.29%).
2. **Why it is not being promoted.** One Q5 position is 78.4% of a median Q5 name's *daily*
   dollar volume at $10M AUM (median name trades $138,832/day). The 10 bps cost rung that the
   pass depends on is not attainable in that quintile at any size the project would deploy.
3. **Second blocker.** The panel is current constituents only. Q5 is exactly where delistings,
   bankruptcies and takeunders concentrate, so the premium is biased up by an unknown amount.
   The IS->OOS spread doubles (+13.0% -> +33.6%/yr), the signature of a survivor-back-loaded tape.
4. **What the probe could and could not do.** Restricting Q5 to the 239 names priced from day one
   moves Sharpe -0.076 (NONE) / +0.017 (MOM): late entrants are not the channel. Survival to 2026
   cannot be tested with this data — it needs a delisted-inclusive tape.
5. **Cost is not the binding constraint** (the queue's stated question is answered): Q5 keeps
   Sharpe 1.469 at 100 bps. Its c* is nonetheless -1, because the DRAWDOWN cap binds at 0 bps.
6. **The 4b pass at g<=0.40 is a dial placement, not an edge** (ideas 311/351: Sharpe is flat in
   gross to 3 decimals, 1.688 -> 1.693 over a 1.9x range). It buys the DD bar with exposure.
7. **Momentum filters do not earn their keep**: they halve the book, take turnover 3.5x -> 12-15x
   per year, and lower Sharpe on 3 of 5 quintiles. The queue's "with a momentum filter" is a no.
8. **The one durable finding**: the IS quintile ordering is preserved OOS exactly (rank corr
   +1.000, Q1 -0.250 -> Q5 1.724). If any key in this record deserves a delisted-inclusive
   re-test, it is this one.
9. **RULES wording: none is proposed.** Nothing here should reach RULES.md. If a future run wants
   to use the finding at all, the only defensible form is an *exclusion*: RULES may not hold a
   name whose 60d median dollar volume is below 20x the intended position size.
10. **To un-PARK this**, one thing is needed and it is not another backtest: a small-cap tape that
    includes delisted names. Until then the number above is not evidence about the future.
