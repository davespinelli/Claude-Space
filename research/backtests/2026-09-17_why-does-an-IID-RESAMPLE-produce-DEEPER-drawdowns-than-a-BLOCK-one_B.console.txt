    WHY IT FAILS, AND IT IS THIS RUN'S OWN BAR THAT IS WRONG, NOT THE MEASUREMENT.
    The bar demanded BLOCK == IID on a shuffled series.  The interpolation identity
    FORBIDS that: a shuffled path still HAS an observed drawdown, which differs from
    the iid median by sampling noise, and the block null still interpolates toward
    it.  The verdict stands as declared and is NOT re-cut; the corrected reading is
    the multi-permutation sub-arm below, which asks the question the bar meant to.

  C_SHUFFLE SUB-ARM — 5 independent permutations per panel (the same control, with
  its own noise published; not a new dial, nothing selected on it):
    U56    BLOCK/IID mean 0.9804 (range 0.9427..1.0291), sign agrees with OBS/IID at 0/5
    B136   BLOCK/IID mean 0.9789 (range 0.9231..1.0376), sign agrees with OBS/IID at 4/5
    SMALL  BLOCK/IID mean 0.9495 (range 0.8893..1.0253), sign agrees with OBS/IID at 4/5
    POOLED over 15 permutations: mean BLOCK/IID 0.9696, sign agreement 8/15, spearman(BLK/IID-1, OBS/IID-1) 0.2821
    AND THE SUB-ARM DOES NOT SAY WHAT THE RUN EXPECTED IT TO SAY.  With order
    DESTROYED the block null is NOT centred on the iid null: it sits systematically
    BELOW it.  That is a MACHINERY effect and it has a mechanism — a moving block is
    a contiguous slice of a fixed array, so it cannot contain the same bar twice,
    while an iid draw can stack the tape's worst days on top of each other.  Block
    sampling therefore shallows drawdowns even on a series with no order at all.

  THE DECOMPOSITION THIS FORCES — how much of 1159's effect is the BOOK and how much
  is the BOOTSTRAP.  The shuffled series has C_BOOK's exact marginal distribution, so
  its mean BLOCK/IID over 5 permutations IS the machinery baseline for that panel.
    U56    total -0.1193  =  machinery -0.0196  +  ORDER -0.0996   (machinery is 16.5% of it)
    B136   total -0.1286  =  machinery -0.0211  +  ORDER -0.1076   (machinery is 16.4% of it)
    SMALL  total -0.0600  =  machinery -0.0505  +  ORDER -0.0095   (machinery is 84.1% of it)
    So 1159's ordering is REAL on U56 and B136 and mostly MACHINERY on SMALL — a
    correction to the parent idea that no amount of re-running its own grid finds,
    because its grid never contained a series with the order taken out.
