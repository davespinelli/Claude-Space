## Caveats

1. **SURVIVORSHIP (idea 54).** U56, B136, SMALL439 and SMALL484 are current-constituent panels
   with no delistings; every CAGR level above is inflated and both KEEP columns inherit that whole.
   SMALL439 is the sub-$2B screen minus the 44 names with `max_1d_move >= 1.0` per PROTOCOL.
2. **Identification by reproduction is necessary, not sufficient.** A block whose published column
   happens to agree with a candidate on every row is called identified even if its script computed
   something else that agrees everywhere. **The 138 is an UPPER bound**; the non-degenerate 127 is
   the honest reading, and 73 of the 138 are matched by exactly one candidate.
3. Two of the five conventions differ only by a 1.9% SPY sleeve (idea 740), so SAMEv2 and
   SAMEv2_noSPY agreeing on a block resolves nothing between them.
4. One corpus read five ways is not five independent pieces of evidence.
