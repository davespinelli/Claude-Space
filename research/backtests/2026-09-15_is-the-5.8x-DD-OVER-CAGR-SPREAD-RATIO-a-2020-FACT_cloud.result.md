## Answer

**MOSTLY, AND THE CARRIER IS 24 TRADING DAYS — BUT THE RATIO DOES NOT COLLAPSE TO 1, AND THE
VERDICT STRADDLES THE PRE-REGISTERED BAR.**

Idea 879's published pair reproduces exactly from this run's own arms (G3): over its 71 books,
the median 21-calendar spread is **CAGR 1.413 pp, DD 8.201 pp, ratio 5.802**. Stripping 2020:

| episode removed | days | med CAGR spread | med DD spread | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NONE (control) | 0 | 1.413 | 8.201 | **5.802** |
| CRASH (SPY 2020-02-19→03-23, resolved from data) | 24 | 1.295 | 3.768 | **2.909** |
| HALF1 (2020-01-01→06-30) | 125 | 1.137 | 3.768 | **3.313** |
| YEAR (2020-01-01→12-31) — *declared headline* | 253 | 1.139 | 3.768 | **3.308** |
| PLACEBO 2018 calendar year | 251 | 1.454 | **8.201** | 5.641 |
| PLACEBO 2022 calendar year | 251 | 1.441 | **8.201** | 5.689 |

The DD spread falls 8.201 → 3.768 pp (−54.1%) and **the whole of that fall is bought by the
24-day crash window**: HALF1 and YEAR remove 5× and 10× more days and move the DD spread not at
all. The CAGR spread falls only −19.4%. A same-shape removal away from 2020 moves the DD spread
by **exactly zero** (8.201 at both placebos, to the printed precision), so this is not "remove a
year and dispersion shrinks" — it is 2020 specifically.

The mechanism is not subtle and is now measured: **71 of 71 books have their maximum-drawdown
trough inside 2020** (U56 38/38, B136 33/33). The DD leg of every book in 879's population *is*
the 2020 episode; the calendar walk moves how much of the crash each offset's rebalance sits
through, and that is the 8.2 pp.

**Pre-registered scoring.** H_EPISODE required the headline ratio < 3.00: at the declared
headline cell (YEAR × ALL71) the ratio is **3.308 — FAIL**; at CRASH × ALL71 it is **2.909 —
PASS**. The verdict flips on a level of the same tuned dial, so neither pre-registered reading
wins outright and the honest statement is the decomposition, not a label. H_BOTH FAIL (CAGR
−19.4% vs DD −54.1%: not a common dispersion source). H_FUNCTIONAL survives in the weak sense
that a 2.9–3.3× residual remains after the episode is gone — MaxDD stays the more
calendar-fragile leg by roughly 3×, not 5.8×.

**All 12 tuned cells + 6 placebo controls reported** (`.grid.csv`), and the direction is
unanimous: every (episode, book-set) pair moves the ratio down, on both panels, at both rungs,
on both cadence families. Book-set matters less than episode (NONE: ALL71 5.802, CAGRCENTRED
6.405, FAR 4.362). The WEEKLY family starts much lower (NONE 3.141) — a 5-offset walk cannot
straddle the crash the way a 21-offset walk can, which is itself consistent with the mechanism.
At 25 bps the MONTHLY ratios are 5.932 → 3.401, i.e. rung-inert.

measurement is already episode-free by construction. IS-only median ratio **2.290** (CAGR 1.480,
DD 3.388); OOS-only **3.752** (CAGR 2.186, DD 8.201). That the episode-free IS window reads 2.29
