CAVEAT, QUANTIFIED — what open idea 406's cash-credit convention would do to this frontier
====================================================================================================
  INC holds mean cash 1 - 0.750 = 0.250 of NAV; LEV borrows mean 0.250.  Crediting cash at k bps/yr therefore adds k*0.250/1e4 to INC's return and 0 to LEV's, i.e. it moves the frontier AGAINST LEV by roughly (0.250/borrowed) bps of borrow per bp of credit.
  With borrowed = 0.250, the exchange rate is ~1.00 bps of phi* per bp of cash credit: a 150-bps credit (idea 406's first rung) would cut phi* by ~150 bps.  Every phi* below is therefore an UPPER bound under the record's cash-at-zero convention.
