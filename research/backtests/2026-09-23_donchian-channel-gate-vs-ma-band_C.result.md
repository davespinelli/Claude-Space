# idea 2347 (lane C, 2026-09-23) — DOES A 52-WEEK CHANNEL GATE REACH THE 4b PASS THE 200d MA BAND REACHES, AT MATCHED MEAN EXPOSURE?

**ANSWERED = ON ONE PANEL YES, ON TWO NO. KILL of the Donchian channel as a replacement for clause 2's MA band. NO NEW CANDIDATE, NO RULES CHANGE.**

408 published rows (6 gates x 2 books x 2 gross x 3 panels x 4 rungs, plus 30 exposure-matched books).

(read **12.5950% / 1.1934 / -17.3923%, OOS 13.8525% / 1.2397**, max|d| 3.04e-06).

## 2. But the incumbent's pass is a TWO-PANEL pass, and the channel's is not
**B136: MA 3 of 8 cells, every Donchian 0 of 8**, and every failure is on **`L_DD` alone** — MA -17.97%
against the 0.60 x SPY cap of **-20.23%**, Donchian -20.51% / -20.95% / -21.29% / -22.17% / -23.33%
(h = 63 / 126 / 189 / 252 / 378). The channel buys its cheapness with drawdown, and on the wider panel
the drawdown is what binds. SMALL: **0 of 8 for every gate, MA included** (idea 2318's reading, reproduced).

## 3. At MATCHED MEAN EXPOSURE the MA gate strictly dominates on Sharpe
Two separate readings, both published:

**(i) The CAND comparison was already exposure-matched by construction** (section C). `RG100` re-grosses
to `gross` on any day some name is IN, so mean equity share is **0.7305 (MA) vs 0.7307 / 0.7314 / 0.7309 /
0.7322 (DON63..252) — a ratio of 1.0000 to 1.0022** on U56, and the same on B136. Only h = 378 differs
(0.9721) and it differs because of warm-in, not the gate. **What differs between these gates is WHEN
they are on, not how much they hold** — exactly the comparison the idea asked for, available with no
rescaling at all. Mean breadth differs by at most 7%.

**(ii) Where exposure genuinely varies (the DEGROSS/live-accounting book), matching it changes nothing**
(section E). The scaler is computed from exposure ALONE, in both directions, and lands **exactly**
(residual 0.0000 on all 30 books; **0 clips**, no book needed gross > 1.00; the IS-only scaler is
published beside it and agrees to ~0.01):

| panel | MA | DON63 | DON126 | DON189 | DON252 | DON378 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U56 Sharpe at matched exposure | **1.2052** | 1.0425 | 1.1647 | 1.0814 | 1.0371 | 0.9894 |
| B136 Sharpe at matched exposure | **1.0972** | 0.9891 | 1.0770 | 1.0486 | 0.9952 | 0.9825 |

**The MA band beats every channel length on both panels at matched mean exposure.** Where the channel wins
4b cells it wins them by being CHEAPER (2.13 vs 4.39 turns/yr, so it survives 50 bps), not by being a
better gate.

## 4. RULE 8 IS DECISIVE: THE HONEST CHOOSER NEVER FINDS THE CELL THAT PASSES
(h, gross) fitted on <= 2016-12-31 only, 2017-2026 read ONCE, two pre-stated IS choosers, run over the
DONCHIAN-ONLY pool and over the pool INCLUDING the incumbent MA gate. 96 picks.

* **64 of 96 beat SPY's OOS Sharpe**, but only **17 of 96 beat the live book's** and only **16 of 96 beat
  their own MA-GATED TWIN's**. **26 of 96** carry a full-sample 4b pass.
* **On U56 / CAND, 0 of 16 picks land on DON126** — the one channel length whose full-sample CAGR beats
  the MA's. The choosers take **DON63** at 0 and 10 bps (OOS Sharpe **0.9812** against the MA twin's
  **1.2397**) and **DON378** at 25 and 50 bps. The passing cell is not reachable.
* **On B136 / CAND every one of 16 picks is DON378**, which carries **no** full-sample 4b pass.
* Pool ALL: the chooser takes the incumbent MA gate only **4 of 48** times — it buys the channel 44 times
  and is paid for it 16 of 96.
* Pick distribution over h is bimodal at the ENDS of the ladder (**DON126 21, DON378 19, DON63 5,
  DON252 3, DON189 0**): the choosers do not converge on the 52-week channel of the idea's title.

## 5. A HARD DEFECT THE MA BAND DOES NOT HAVE: THE CHANNEL COLLAPSES TO ONE NAME
Max per-name weight, U56 / CAND / gross 0.75: MA **15.00%**, DON126 12.50%, DON189 15.00%,
**DON252 75.00%**, DON378 37.50% — and at gross 1.00, **DON252 reaches 100.00% of NAV in a single name.**
DON378 additionally spends **121 days fully out** (100% SHY). A gate that puts the whole book into one
name on its worst day is not a rule constant, whatever its pass count. Idea 2332 left concentration as
the standing candidate's one open risk at 15.0%; the 52-week channel makes it five times worse.
