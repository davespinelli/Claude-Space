  GRID FACT.
  No finite shrink threshold lifts the pooled median OOS advantage above zero under
  both keyings and at >= 2 of 3 choosers.  The best a shrunk chooser achieves is to
  stop choosing: the supremum of the curve is reached where every cell is back on
