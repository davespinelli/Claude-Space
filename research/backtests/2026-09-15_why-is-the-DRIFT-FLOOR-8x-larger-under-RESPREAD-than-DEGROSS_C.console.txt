  --- H_BILINEAR, identified AFTER the two pre-registered readings were scored -------
  Neither pre-registered reading fits both curves, and the algebra says why.  For a
  shared name held at the same target w by both arms, one bar after a rebalance
      h_A - h_B = w (1 + r_j) (1/tot_A - 1/tot_B),   tot_X = 1 + E_X * rp_X
  where E is the book's exposure and rp its own return, so tot_A - tot_B is FIRST ORDER
  IN E.  The floor is therefore BILINEAR:  |DRIFT| ~ w * E.  That is a parameter-free
  consequence of the engine's renormalisation, not a fitted form; it is scored below on
  the SAME unchanged grid, with no new tuning and no new axis.
     RESPREAD: w = g/k ~ 1/q, E = g       -> slope -1
     DEGROSS : w = g/n (flat), E = g*q    -> slope +1
     ratio at fixed q = (w ratio)(E ratio) = q * q  -> beta = 2 exactly
  total |median slope - prediction| :  H_DENOM 1.4463   H_EXPO 0.7681   H_BILINEAR 0.4463
  -> H_BILINEAR fits both curves best; its beta prediction of 2 is checked in section 3.
