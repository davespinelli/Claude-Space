THE ANSWER TO THE QUEUE'S QUESTION
========================================================================================================================
  Does a rank-based non-argmax chooser have expectancy?  NO, on the only comparand that
  can pay for one.  K_MEDIAN's lift REPLICATES on a disjoint corpus (+0.0232, t +2.20, 26/36) — the queue's premise survives — but the lift is
  measured against the POOL MEAN, and the pool mean is not an alternative anyone can
  hold.  Against the do-nothing arm, which is, K_MEDIAN loses -0.0443 (t -2.32), the do-nothing arm's
  OWN lift is +0.0675 (t +4.13), and the q-dial's positive
  region is the whole top half (q=0.75 +0.0262 >= q=0.50 +0.0232), not a peak at the median.
  Idea 241's minimum-margin rule cannot fix this: 100% of the 36 pools have a top-2 IS gap
  under 0.15, so every m >= 0.10 IS do-nothing by construction (delta exactly 0.0000) and
  the only m that ever trades, 0.05, still loses. The margin rule is damage control on the
  argmax (-0.0512 -> -0.0084), never expectancy.
