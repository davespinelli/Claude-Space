**THE CENSUS (lower bound; mechanical regex over committed bytes, gate G9).** 522 committed

**THE RE-CUT.** 45 device books per panel (STOP / MAGATE / VOLTGT; DIAL 1 STRENGTH x DIAL 2
THRESHOLD) on U56 / B136 / SMALL — **135 books, every cell published** — each matched to an
anchor on two ladders x two statistics and cut **twice**: against the record's COARSE NEAREST
RUNG and against the **EXACT** rung. **1,080 contrasts published, 897 bracketed.** Exact means:
on L_G (gross, continuous) the real number g\* solving X(g\*) = X(device), residual < 7.9e-07;
on L_H (min-hold, integer) the best of **all 504 integer rungs**, because X_CAGR is not monotone
in H and a bisection would be unsound.

**(1) THE BAR WAS MET, AND NOT NARROWLY.** Pre-registered before any number was read: material
only if >= 10% of bracketed contrasts on U56 **and** B136 change a 4b LEG verdict or flip the
sign of dCAGR / dMaxDD. **Observed: U56 40.3% of 313, B136 38.7% of 315** (SMALL 25.3% of 269;
35.2% pooled). 153 of 897 change an anchor 4b leg, 159 flip dCAGR's sign, 56 flip dMaxDD's.

**(2) 1509's CARVE-OUT REPRODUCES AND SHARPENS.** Rounding is free on SHARPE **only on the SCALE
ladder**: max |dSharpe| **0.0005 on L_G** against **0.2869 on L_H**, and |t| > 2 decision changes
0 of 504 on L_G against 11 of 393 on L_H. On LEVEL it is free nowhere: max |dCAGR| **5.89 pp/yr**
(L_H/X_TURN) and **1.12 pp/yr** (L_G), max |dMaxDD| **9.71 pp**, median |dCAGR| 0.25 pp/yr. The
worst single cell is SMALL / MAGATE / X_TURN OOS, where the nearest rung H = 126 and the exact
rung H = 107 differ by 5.89 pp/yr of CAGR — the two rungs are one ladder step apart.

**(3) THE MECHANISM IS THE 1509 TAXONOMY, MEASURED AGAIN.** Anchor 4b leg changes run at
**0.6% / 6.6% / 6.0%** on L_G (U56 / B136 / SMALL) against **34.5% / 42.2% / 18.8%** on L_H. A
scale ladder's rungs hold the same names, so rounding moves only the cash split; a composition
ladder's rungs hold different names, so rounding moves the book.

**(4) WHAT CHANGES PRACTICE.** A LEVEL claim cut against a nearest rung on a COMPOSITION ladder
carries a rounding error of ordinary size 0.25 pp/yr and worst case 5.89 pp/yr, against 4b
CAGR-floor margins this record publishes at +0.07 pp and +1.35 pp. **Every committed LEVEL claim
on a composition ladder should be re-cut against the exact rung, or state its ladder spacing.**
On a SCALE ladder the exact rung is free to compute and should simply be used: there is no
defence for rounding a continuous dial.

both — U56 38/39, B136 17/23, SMALL 0/0 — every one inherited from the frozen incumbent, none
earned by a device. Rule 8 ((FRAC, TH) by argmax IS Sharpe on warm-up..2016-12-31, 2017-2026 read
