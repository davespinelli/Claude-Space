ARM 0 — DATA-FREE.  WHAT AN SE BAR CAN AND CANNOT BE ASKED, PRINTED BEFORE ANY TAPE.

  (i) THE DEGENERATE ANSWER, RULED OUT IN ADVANCE.  R_SE(k) is monotone in k: the move
      set shrinks as k grows, and at k large enough R_SE IS R_ANCHOR.  1154 committed
      R_ANCHOR at 24 of 72 4b books against R_RAW's 6 — a gap of 18.  So 'R_SE recovers the 18 books'
      IS TRUE BY CONSTRUCTION at large k and means nothing: R_ANCHOR already recovers all
      18, costs nothing, needs no bootstrap and is in the record.  A new rule earns its
      place only by beating the alternatives AT A MATCHED MOVE COUNT.

  (ii) THE TWO COMPARANDS THAT MAKE THAT TEST MEAN SOMETHING:
      R_BAR      the rank bar R_SE is proposed to replace (1154's, P_pick >= 0.90)
      R_RANDOM   move on a uniformly random subset of the SAME SIZE (500 seeded
                 draws).  This is what 'move less often' is worth with NO information.

  (iii) WHAT THE RECORD ALREADY IMPLIES ABOUT THE BAR'S POWER.  1154 committed the median
      IS margin at 9.739e-03 among P_boot-resolved decisions against
      9.369e-03 among unresolved — a ratio of 1.0395, and Spearman 0.1551.
      An SE bar is a MARGIN bar with a scale attached, so it can only separate the two
      populations if the SE varies across decisions by MORE than the margin does.  If SE
      is near-constant across the 72, R_SE is a re-labelled margin bar and must rank the
      decisions the same way a raw margin bar does.  That is measured in Arm C (G8).

  PRE-DECLARED OUTCOMES (written before any tape is read):
    (A) THE SE BAR IS INFORMATIVE — at a matched move count R_SE books MORE 4b passers
        than R_BAR and sits ABOVE the 90th percentile of the count-matched R_RANDOM null.
    (B) IT IS ONLY CONSERVATISM — R_SE's 4b count tracks its move count along the same
        curve R_RANDOM traces (inside the null's 10-90 band), i.e. every book it recovers
        is bought by not moving, which R_ANCHOR does for free.
    (C) IT IS WORSE THAN THE RANK BAR — fewer 4b passers than R_BAR at a matched count.
    (D) DEGENERATE — R_SE's move count is 72 or 0 at every k >= 0.25, so there is no
        interior ladder to compare at all.

  R_SE would be a margin bar wearing a bootstrap.  Spearman(delta, delta/SE) at L=63:
    SE_JOINT     Spearman(delta, delta/SE) = +0.5560   Spearman(P_pick, delta/SE) = +0.7778   (n=59 movers)

    SE_IID       Spearman(delta, delta/SE) = +0.6341   Spearman(P_pick, delta/SE) = +0.7383   (n=59 movers)
  [PASS] G8    the SE bar is NOT the identity on the margin bar (Spearman < 0.999)  ->  5.560e-01

  WHY THE JOINT SE IS THE RIGHT ONE: correlation between the pick's and the anchor's
  bootstrap statistic across draws, over the 72 at L=63 (a difference of two highly

    median 0.9247   min 0.6777   max 1.0000

    overstates the error bar by this factor and therefore moves far less often.
