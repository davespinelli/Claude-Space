=== 4b. DECOMPOSITION — is H1 the CONCENTRATION the queue named, or something else? ===
Write f for the armed fraction, d_on / d_off for the ALWAYS-ON arm's annualised delta
on regime-ON / regime-OFF days, and c_on / c_off for the CONDITIONAL arm's own delta on
the same two day sets.  Because ann() is additive over day sets,
    L(always) = f*d_on + (1-f)*d_off      L(cond) = c_on + ((1-f)/f)*c_off
so the queue's quantity splits EXACTLY three ways:
    L(cond) - L(always) = CONC + ACT + LEAK, where
    CONC = (1-f)*(d_on - d_off)   the instrument being dearer in its own regime
                                   -- this, and only this, is idea 75's mechanism
    ACT  = c_on - d_on             the conditional arm acting differently ON armed days
                                   (re-entry turnover, different entry state)
    LEAK = ((1-f)/f)*c_off         the conditional arm's delta on days it is SUPPOSED
                                   to be inert, blown up by the 1/f normalisation

identity check  max|total - CONC - ACT - LEAK| = 7.283e-14 -> EXACT
