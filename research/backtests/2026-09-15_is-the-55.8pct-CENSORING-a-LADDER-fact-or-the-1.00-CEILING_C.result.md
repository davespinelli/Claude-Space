## Limits, stated

The calibration population is 30 books from **one** committed file (804's grid), not a sample of
the 96 band-carrying files, so the 100% ceiling-bound rate is a calibration of the record's ladder
practice and the extrapolation to the 630-band class is a point estimate, not a census.
U56 / B136 / SMALL are current-constituent lists (SMALL additionally drops the 52 tickers with
`max_1d_move` >= 1.0), so every CAGR and drawdown level is optimistic; a CEILING-BOUND label says a
book's drawdown never reaches 0.60 × SPY's at any admissible gross, which survivorship makes
*easier* to earn, so the ceiling-bound share here is an **upper** bound on a point-in-time panel.
The census and the ladder-vs-ceiling contrasts are same-tape comparisons and unaffected.
