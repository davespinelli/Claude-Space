# CAVEAT on the standing 2026-09-20 KEEP-4b candidate (idea 1799's drift-threshold cell) — idea 2022, lane cloud

1. This is NOT a new KEEP and NOT a withdrawal. It qualifies one published claim attached to 1799's KEEP-4b candidate: its "DRIFT beats a turnover-matched CALENDAR at 263 of 263 cells, mean +0.0596 OOS Sharpe".
2. Replication is exact: rerunning 1799's estimator on the same corpus gives 263 of 263, mean +0.0596 (gate G5, 10/10 gates pass).
3. Excise the 2020 crash (24 trading days, 2020-02-19 -> 2020-03-23; 1.0% of the OOS sample) and the mean falls to **+0.0066**, win share to 188/263. A 51-day window gives +0.0126 and 210/263. **89% of the published edge lives in 24 days.**
4. It is not a cost fact: at 0 bps the edge is +0.0603 and at 50 bps +0.0569 — already fully present before costs, and shrinking with them.
5. The credit is drawdown, not return: outside the crash the OOS MaxDD gap is +0.66 pp (from +4.07) and the OOS CAGR gap turns **negative** (-0.26 pp).
6. Paired moving-block bootstrap (B=1000, seed 20260922, blocks 21 and 63, whole arm resampled on the same blocks): on the FULL tape only **61 of 263** cells have a 95% CI excluding zero, mean |t| 1.49. The sweep was never resolvable.
