ARM 0 — WHAT IS DECLARED BEFORE THE TAPE IS TOUCHED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A bar has to INFORM to be worth anything: mean d GIVEN FIRE must exceed mean d GIVEN
  REFUSAL, where d = OOS Sharpe(pick) - OOS Sharpe(anchor).  1252 measured the incumbent at
  -0.0061 vs +0.0133 (t -1.28) — NEGATIVE.  Pre-declared outcomes:
    (A) OBS-SIDE INFORMS  — some observed-side (score, quantile) cell has a POSITIVE
        licensed-minus-refused delta that survives its own t and the inner-split rule-8 arm.
    (B) THE LINE IS THE STORY — observed-side cells are systematically better than bar-side
        ones, even if none is individually significant.
    (C) NEITHER SIDE INFORMS — no bar in either class separates, i.e. decisiveness on this
        tape is not a property either side can measure and the record's bar is not merely
        on the wrong side of the line, it is on the wrong axis.
    (D) OBS-SIDE IS WORSE — the draw side, for all its knobs, is the better of two bad bars.
  H_SIGN: at least one observed-side cell has delta > 0.  H_CLASS: mean delta over the 12
  observed-side cells > mean over the 12 bar-side cells.  H_LFREE: the observed-side scores
  are bit-identical across L (they touch no draw).  H_CAPITAL: some selector's realised book
  set beats the do-nothing anchor on mean OOS Sharpe AND does not cost 4b passes.
