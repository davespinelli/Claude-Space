#   COST LEG?  Walk gross FINE at several cost rungs; find the hand-over; ask if it moves with cost.

#   B_SPY is the PROTOCOL floor and the headline; B_SPYCOST (SPY charged the same cost rate on
#   its OWN turnover) and B_SPYGROSS (SPY held at the SAME gross, rest in cash) are CONTROLS
#   computed at every cell and never selected on.
# FROZEN: CAND20 legs [(21, 252), (0, 126), (0, 63)], cap INF, max_vol 0.6, min hold 126, N 20, cadence W,
#   LAG 1, warm-up 260, IS end 2016-12-31, zero cash, block L=63, 1000 draws, crc32 seeds, q=0.9.
#
# THE BINDING-LEG RULE, DECLARED BEFORE ANY NUMBER: every 4b leg is re-expressed as the
#   SIGNED FRACTION OF ITS OWN BAR still in hand (Sharpe legs (book-SPY)/|SPY|; L_DD
#   (0.60|SPY_dd|-|dd|)/(0.60|SPY_dd|); L_CAGR (CAGR-0.70*SPY_CAGR)/|0.70*SPY_CAGR|).
#   BINDING LEG = argmin of the five.  Negative == that leg FAILS.  Never re-chosen.
#   g* (THE HAND-OVER) = the HIGHEST gross rung whose binding leg is L_CAGR.  MONOTONE means
#   L_CAGR binds at every rung at or below g* and at none above it.
#
# DECLARED BEFORE ANY NUMBER:
#   (a) H_PREMISE       the record's committed gross-ladder 4b legs reproduce exactly (G8),
#                       i.e. at 10 bps L_CAGR is the ONLY failing leg on the low-gross rungs.
#   (b) H_HANDOVER      a hand-over EXISTS and is MONOTONE on both panels at every cost rung:
#                       L_CAGR binds below g*, a Sharpe-family leg above it.
#   (c) H_NOT_COST      g* is INVARIANT to the cost rung across 0..50 bps (moves <= 1 rung of
#                       0.025 on both panels).  This is the idea's question, stated as a bar.
#   (d) H_SHARPE_FLAT   across the WHOLE 0.20..1.00 ladder the full-sample Sharpe spread is
#                       < 0.05 while the CAGR spread is > 0.10 (absolute), at every cost rung.
#   (e) H_GROSSMATCHED  under B_SPYGROSS (floor de-grossed with the book) L_CAGR binds at NO
#                       rung on either panel at any cost rung.
# DECISION RULE, declared before any number: the CAGR floor is a DE-GROSSING DETECTOR and
#   NOT a cost leg iff H_NOT_COST holds AND H_GROSSMATCHED holds.  If g* moves materially
#   with cost it is a cost object and the idea's premise dies.  If the hand-over does not
#   exist or is not monotone (H_HANDOVER fails) the question is mis-posed and that is the answer.
