# Memo — idea 395, the macro sleeve priced against its own de-grossed control (lane B, 2026-09-10)

1. **The queue's premise is REFUTED.** De-grossing the equity book is almost Sharpe-neutral (the λ ladder's own Sharpe spread over λ ∈ [0.02, 1.30] is **0.0003–0.0075** on all 24 panel×base×cadence×cost cells, MaxDD and mean gross monotone 24/24), so a matched-gross or matched-DD control carries essentially the base book's Sharpe and the sleeve cannot win on exposure. It wins anyway: against its **matched mean gross** control the sleeve takes Sharpe in **209/288 = 0.726** (median **+0.0532**), OOS in **247/288 = 0.858** (median **+0.0947**); against its **matched MaxDD** control, Sharpe 0.726 (median +0.0540) and Calmar **0.875** (median +0.1007). Same picture off the arm's own gated base (0.729 / +0.0443).
2. **H395 as pre-registered is KILLED.** "Beats both controls in both halves AND out of sample" holds on **140 of 288** arms (48.6%). The failing leg is **H1** (win 0.531, mean **−0.0676**) — the edge is an H2/OOS phenomenon (H2 win 0.885) — and **f = 0.75 reverses everywhere** (median d_Sharpe −0.191).
3. **It is a drawdown instrument, not a return instrument.** At matched gross the sleeve gives up **1.7–8.4 pp of CAGR** and buys **6–13 pp of MaxDD**; at matched drawdown it earns **+1.1 to +1.9 pp more CAGR** than de-grossed equity. That asymmetry is the whole finding.
