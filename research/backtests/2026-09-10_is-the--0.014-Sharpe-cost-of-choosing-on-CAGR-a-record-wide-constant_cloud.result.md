Raw, longer menus look worse — but **file-clustered, the slope of dS on log2(arms) is −0.00041 per
doubling (t −0.06, 59 files)** against a menu-pooled −0.00735. The arms effect is *entirely

"−0.014 is dial-specific once n_arms is controlled" — comes out **dial-specific, yes; because of
n_arms, no.**

## Verdict

**KILL for capital, ANSWERED for the record.** −0.014 is a **quotable pooled level** (−0.0162,
CI [−0.0275, −0.0072] on the committed census; −0.0161 on a fresh corpus a third larger) and **not
a constant**: family exchangeability is rejected at p ≤ 0.0015 at 11 of 12 grid points once the
file and the arm count are blocked, with per-family means spanning −0.0537 (trim) to −0.0056
(gross). The queue's suspected confounder is cleared — the arm-count slope is −0.00041 per
doubling (t −0.06) file-clustered, and controlling for it does not shrink the family spread. Quote
the level if you must; never quote it *for a dial*. Nothing promoted: the only 4b passers on 360
live arms are the live book at gross ≈ 1.00, and no arm and no rule-8 pick clears both paths.
