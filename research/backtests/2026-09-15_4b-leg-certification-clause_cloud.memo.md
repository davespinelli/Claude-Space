# Memo — the 4b leg-certification clause (PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED; PROTOCOL rule 6)

2. **It is not dead because of holding length.** `H_CADENCE` FAILS: the leg's base rate is W 0.048 → M 1.000 → Q 0.940 on U56 and W 0.000 → M 0.720 → Q 0.624 on B136. It peaks at MONTHLY on both panels, so the leg is a cost/turnover optimum, not a cadence monotone.
3. **It is also redundant.** 4b's H2 window is **100.0% contained inside** the rule-8 OOS window (OOS is 91.2% H2): on this tree the halves split in Nov 2017 and OOS starts Jan 2017. Dropping L_OOS changes the joint 4b verdict on **0 of 1,500** RANDROT draws — the ratio P(all 5)/P(other 4) is exactly **1.0000** in all four cells where it is defined. `H_REDUNDANT` is scored **FAIL** because the pre-registered bar demanded 5 of 6 cells and two are undefined (no draw clears the other four); the bar is not moved after the fact.
4. **No leg of 4b is safe.** `H_LIVE` FAILS: worst-cell RANDROT base rates are L_H1 1.000, L_H2 0.996, L_OOS 1.000, L_DD 1.000, L_CAGR 0.884. Every leg is cleared by a coin flip almost always in at least one cell; L_CAGR is merely the least dead.
5. **What this costs the standing candidate.** U56/CAND20 monthly is a 4b PASS (OOS 17.53% / 1.305 / −19.51% against SPY 15.27% / 0.874 / −33.72%) — and **76.0% of that cell's gross-matched coin flips also pass 4b.** The pass is not evidence. 4a PASS is 0 of 12 across the whole grid.
6. **PROPOSED CLAUSE (i), exact wording for PROTOCOL rule 8:** *"The out-of-sample window must be disjoint from the second half used by rule 4b. Either the 4b halves are taken inside the in-sample window (2009–2016 split in two), or the halves are dropped and 4b reads IS and OOS. A 4b verdict whose H2 and OOS windows overlap is reported as a two-leg verdict."*
7. **PROPOSED CLAUSE (ii), exact wording for PROTOCOL rule 4b:** *"Any published 4b PASS carries, beside it, the per-leg base rate of its own cell's gross-matched null under BOTH null kinds (rotating and fixed). Any leg whose base rate exceeds 0.90 is reported as NOT CERTIFYING rather than as a pass, and a 4b PASS in which every leg is non-certifying is reported as UNADJUDICATED, not as a KEEP."*
8. **Scored on this grid**, clause (ii) strips the OOS leg from 8 of 18 (panel, cadence, kind) cells and strips L_DD from 5 more; clause (i) makes the H1/H2 split independent of rule 8 at the cost of shortening each half to about four years.
9. **Null-kind caveat, carried deliberately.** The record has already committed that the null kind can move a joint base rate from 74.8% to 0.2%. All three kinds are printed here and none is the headline; the L_OOS = 1.000 finding on U56/M holds under RANDROT **and** RANDFIX, and weakens to 0.892 under the ungated RANDROT_FREE.
10. **SURVIVORSHIP.** U56 and B136 are current-constituent lists, so a coin flip drawn here is a better book than one drawn in real time and every base rate is an **upper** bound. That cuts *against* this memo's own headline — a leg measured live could be less dead than printed — so the dead-leg claims should be treated as the upper bounds they are, while the cadence and leg-overlap contrasts are same-tape comparisons and survive it.
