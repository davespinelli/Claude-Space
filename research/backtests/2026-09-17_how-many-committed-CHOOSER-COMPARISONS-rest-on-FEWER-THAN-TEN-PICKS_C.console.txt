ARM 0 — THE ARITHMETIC, PRINTED BEFORE THE TAPE IS READ.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  K_req(X) = (z * sigma_d / X)^2 is EXACTLY quadratic in 1/X: halve the quoted gap and
  the claim needs FOUR times the picks.  Equivalently the smallest gap K picks can
  resolve is  X_min = z * sigma_d / sqrt(K).  At z = 2:
         K   X_min / sigma_d
         1   2.0000
         3   1.1547
         5   0.8944
        10   0.6325
        20   0.4472
        50   0.2828
        72   0.2357
       100   0.2000
       240   0.1291
       472   0.0921

  1206 measured this object at 472 picks and read a CLUSTERED SE of 0.0526
  on a difference of +0.0016; 1155 published a five-way span of
  0.0736 on THREE draws.  If sigma_d is of order 0.3, then at K = 3 the
  smallest 2-SE-resolvable gap is 0.3464 and at K = 10 it is
  0.1897 — both an order of magnitude above the gaps the record
  habitually publishes.  THE EXPECTED FINDING IS THEREFORE 'MOST ARE UNRESOLVED'; the
  number worth reporting is how many are not, and whether any ADJUDICATED one is.

  UPPER BOUND, NOT ESTIMATE: sigma_d/sqrt(K) assumes K independent picks.  The record's
  picks share panels, anchors and ONE tape, so the true SE is larger and every
  'resolved' count below is the most generous reading available.
