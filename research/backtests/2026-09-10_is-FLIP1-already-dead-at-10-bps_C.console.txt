On the record's OWN committed corpus (15194 sweeps / 86859 points over 149 files) the neighbour-flip rate at the PROTOCOL rung is 12.65%, ABOVE the 7.98% at 0 bps and above the 7.41% at 25 bps.         LIKE-FOR-LIKE (the 42 files publishing the same dial family at both rungs) the rate does fall, 7.98% -> 5.87%, but it does not die: 36 of 42 of those files still carry flips at 10 bps, and per file it falls in 22, rises in 7, is unchanged in 13.

        WHERE IT DOES FALL, THE MECHANISM IS DEPOPULATION, NOT SMOOTHING.  flip = mixed_weight x flip|mixed exactly; at 10 bps the MIXED leg carries -0.0152 of the -0.0212 like-for-like fall and the WITHIN leg -0.0060.  On the fresh grid flip|mixed is FLAT at 13.3% across all nine rungs while the mixed share falls 22.2% -> 22.2% -> 11.1% -- the within leg is identically zero.  At FILE level the same thing: the mean flip rate CONDITIONAL on a boundary is 0.456 at 0 bps and 0.459 at 10 bps, a change of +0.003.
