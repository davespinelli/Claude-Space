5. **EXACT CLAUSE WORDING** (to sit beside 1031's proposed rule 8 CHOOSER PERCENTILE clause): *"A claim that a rule-8 chooser beats, or loses to, a random draw from its own pool names its CONTROL BASIS — per-end resample, one book held across the window, or a single end — beside the pool size and the draw count. The same gap earns percentiles up to 0.274 apart across the three bases and the choice is not implied by the words 'a random draw from its own pool'. A gap larger than twice the null's standard deviation is published as 'outside the null's realised support', never as a magnitude, because at that distance all gaps earn the same 0.000 or 1.000. A claim missing its basis, its pool size or its draw count is UNADJUDICABLE and may not be cited as evidence for or against a chooser."*
