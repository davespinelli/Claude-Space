The mechanism is measured, not asserted: **the IS window's own 4b band is EMPTY** — no width clears 4b on

n=20 from n=40 is drawdown (−22.5% vs −17.9%), and IS Sharpe is blind to it: IS Calmar, which is not
blind to it, picks n=10 and is also wrong.

## Verdict: KILL for capital. 3 of 8 pre-registered hypotheses pass.

PASS: H_REPRO, H_40, H_INTERIOR. FAIL: H_PICK, H_PICK40, H_COST, H_DELAY, H_NEIGH (all five failures are
the same fact — the pre-registered selector picks n=20, not a width in the band).

This is a **cleaner** kill than 589's park. The book is robust to everything a trader controls (cost
through 25 bps, a day of fill slippage, ±8 widths). It fails on the one thing a trader cannot control:
**you would have had to know, in 2016, to hold 40 names rather than 20, and nothing in the 2009–2016 data
says so.** Under PROTOCOL rule 8 that is not a candidate, it is an artefact of reading the whole sample.

## Caveats carried

- **SURVIVORSHIP.** `research/universe.json` is a current-constituent list, so every level here is
  optimistic, and **widening flatters most** — the names a wide book adds are exactly the survivors that
  a point-in-time panel would partly replace with delistings. The bias runs in the same direction as the
  finding under test, which is an independent reason to distrust a wide-book 4b pass.
- `data/prices.csv` is re-downloaded daily, so U56 rows reproduce to ~3e-3, not bit-exact (idea 406).
- Only 2020 and 2022 are real stress tests in this sample; 2009–2026 is a QQQ-favourable regime.
- The IS/OOS split is the record's standing one (2016/2017) and is itself a convention; this run did not
  vary it, so "the IS band is empty" is a statement about that split.

## Lead for the queue (filed, not acted on)

The object this run actually found is a **CONTIGUOUS, COST- AND DELAY-ROBUST 8-rung width band with an
empty in-sample counterpart**. That pattern — a band that exists on the full sample and not on its own
first half — is testable across the record's other committed width/gross bands, and would say whether
"the band is only visible ex post" is this book's problem or the record's. Filed as idea 809.
