# KEEP-4b candidate memo — idea 1505 (lane C, 2026-09-22): the RANK-HYSTERESIS top-20 book at K_out = 50 (U56)

1. **What it is.** The frozen 2026-09-04 incumbent top-20 book (live 3-leg composite, MAXVOL 0.60 eligibility, equal weight, gross 0.75, weekly, t+1) with ONE change: a name ENTERS when its rank among the eligible is <= 20 and is then held until its rank exceeds **K_out = 50**, instead of being dropped the moment it leaves the top 20. No min-hold, no turnover cap, no leverage, no shorting.
2. **Why it exists.** It is an INCIDENTAL by-product of a mechanism run, not the run's question. Idea 1505 asked whether slowing rotation costs drawdown; the answer is yes at 100 of 120 braked rungs — and the rank-hysteresis family is the ONE exception, which is why this cell surfaced.
3. **Numbers @10 bps, U56.** FULL **11.69% / 1.0778 / -15.27%** (halves 1.1489 / 1.0240); **OOS 12.94% / 1.1303 / -15.27%**; turnover **4.804x/yr**, mean position age 142.6 trading days, 19.18 names held. Live RULES v2: 8.62% / 1.2012 / -12.05% (OOS 9.46% / 1.2770 / -12.05%, 1.77x/yr). SPY: 15.14% / 0.8852 / -33.72% (OOS 15.29% / 0.8753 / -33.72%). 4b bars: CAGR floor 10.60% FULL / 10.70% OOS, DD cap -20.23%.
4. **Which path.** **PATH 4b ONLY.** All four legs clear FULL and all three clear OOS at **0 / 5 / 10 / 25 bps**; at 50 bps the FULL half-Sharpe legs fail (0.9736 / 0.8396 vs SPY's) while OOS still clears. **PATH 4a is 0 of 660 published grid points** in this run and is unreachable here: the book is strictly deeper than the live band book (-15.27% vs -12.05%) and loses both halves to it.
5. **What it buys over the standing incumbent (H = 126, same base book).** **3.86 pp less drawdown** (-15.27% vs -19.13%) — and that is all. It gives up **4.09 pp of CAGR** (11.69% vs 15.78%), **0.075 of Sharpe** and **0.054 of OOS Sharpe**, at **1.77x/yr more turnover**. Every one of those is the wrong direction.
6. **Rule 8 (2017-2026 read ONCE).** Choosing K_out on 2009-2016 alone, **three of four choosers reach it**: argmax IS Sharpe (the record's habitual), the AGE-regression chooser and the TURNOVER-regression chooser all pick K_out = 50; only C_LIVE (K_out = 20, the no-information control) does not. IS Sharpe 1.0102, IS MaxDD -11.21%. It is therefore a REACHABLE cell, not a hindsight one — the rarest property in the record.
7. **Exact RULES wording it would need** (a replacement for clauses 2-3 of a top-20 rule set, NOT for live RULES v2's band): *"Rank every instrument that is priced on the decision day, is above its 200-day moving average and has 20-day annualised volatility below 0.60, by the composite score (the equal-weight mean of the cross-sectional percentile ranks of the 12-1 month, 6-month and 3-month returns, halved for a name below its 200-day moving average). A name that is not held is bought only if its rank is 20 or better. A name that is held is sold only when its rank is worse than 50, when it is no longer priced, or when it fails the eligibility test above. If fewer than 20 names are held after those two steps, fill the remaining places from the best-ranked eligible names not already held. Hold each name at 0.75/20 of NAV; rebalance on the last trading day of each week only; unfilled weight stays in cash."*
8. **Why it is NOT recommended.** It is dominated by the book it modifies. The incumbent already clears the 4b DD cap with 1.10 pp to spare (-19.13% vs -20.23%), so spending 4.09 pp of CAGR to buy 3.86 pp more DD margin buys a leg that was never binding. PROTOCOL rule 6 gives that trade to the Sunday review; this run proposes **no rules change**.
9. **Panel dependence and cost.** The pass is a **U56 fact**: 85 of the 95 both-FULL-and-OOS 4b grid points in this run are U56, 10 are B136 (and none of those is K_out = 50 — B136's only 4b hysteresis rung is K_out = 25), and **SMALL contributes zero**. On B136 the same K_out = 50 cell fails the DD leg outright (FULL MaxDD -21.32% against the -20.23% cap), and the only B136 hysteresis rung that clears 4b FULL and OOS is K_out = 25 (13.74% / 0.9888 / -20.03%).
10. **Caveats.** Survivorship (PROTOCOL rule 9): U56 is a 2026 constituent list carried back to 2008, so every CAGR level is optimistic and both 4b level legs are easier than on a point-in-time panel. Costs are flat per unit turnover with no spread, impact or borrow. MaxDD is a single-path extremum: under this run's own paired block bootstrap the cross-book MaxDD contrasts do NOT resolve at |t| > 2, so the 3.86 pp DD edge over the incumbent should be read as a point estimate, not a resolved one. One cadence (W), one delay (t+1), one K_in (20).
