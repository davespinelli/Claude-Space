   "Universe U56. Score = mean percentile rank of 12-1, 6m and 3m returns, halved for names at or
   below their own 200d MA; no volatility scaler. Eligible = above own 200d MA and 20d realised vol
   < 0.60. Hold the top 20 eligible names, equal weight 0.75/20 of NAV, gated weight to CASH.
   Rebalance on the last trading day of each week, executing at the next close. A name entering the
   book must be held at least H trading days (H = 5 / H = 10) before it can be displaced."
3. The only change from the standing 2026-09-04 KEEP 4b book is H: 126 -> 5 or 10. Everything else is
   the incumbent's, unchanged.
4. Rule 8: the IS-only choosers do NOT pick either rung (they pick 189 on C_ISSHARPE/C_ISCAGR and 10

6. So the pass is a floor test clearing, not an improvement: 4b has no return term above 70% of SPY's

   incumbent, and nothing on the ladder is choosable out of sample (mean IS/OOS rank corr -0.1825).
