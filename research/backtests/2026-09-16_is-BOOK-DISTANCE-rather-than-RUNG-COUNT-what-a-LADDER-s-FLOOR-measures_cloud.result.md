# Idea 1108 (cloud lane, 2026-09-16) — is BOOK DISTANCE rather than RUNG COUNT what a LADDER's FLOOR measures?

**ANSWERED = NO on the premise, YES on the question.** Book distance is **not** what a ladder's
floor measures, and the sign runs **backwards** to 1108's own reading: over the 8 ladders,
Spearman(rel_floor, adjacent-pair distance) is **POSITIVE in 12 of 12** (distance statistic,
statistic) combinations, median **+0.7214** — the *closer* a ladder's books, the *better* it
resolves. **H_DIST FAILS 0 of 12. H_BEATS_COUNT FAILS 1 of 12** (median |rho(dist)| 0.7214
against |rho(count)| 0.8460, and rung count carries the *negative* sign 12 of 12). But **one
predictor does replace the bootstrap** — not a ladder-level distance scalar, the **pairwise
analytic SE** built from the two rungs' own return difference: **H_REPLACE_CAGR and
H_REPLACE_SHARPE both PASS all three pre-registered legs**, reproducing **24 of 24 cell tie-set
verdicts** at a **median floor ratio of 1.0000**. And a **DEFECT**: 1102's per-ladder bootstrap
seed is `SEED + hash(...) % 10000`, which Python randomises per process, so **1102's published
floor numbers are not reproducible** — its *verdicts* are (32 of 32 agree), its *levels* are not
(2 of 32 move, one by 0.220 of its ladder's spread). No RULES change, no book promoted, no

## THE KILL, IN ONE LINE
The **GROSS ladder's ten rungs are literally the same book scaled against a cash sleeve**:
RETCORR distance **0.0000**, OVERLAP distance **0.0000**, the smallest distance any ladder can
carry. It is also the **only** ladder that resolves anything — median rel_floor **0.109** against
**0.961 / 1.000 / 1.000** for N / H / CADENCE, and **2 of 8 tie sets** against 8 of 8 for each of
the other three. A ladder whose rungs are the *same book* resolves a **0.0002 Sharpe gap**; a
ladder whose rungs are *different books* cannot resolve a 0.0071 one. Distance is therefore not
what the floor measures — it is (most of) what the **SE** is made of, and the floor is a
**gap-against-SE** quantity in which distance enters **both** terms.

**H_RHO PASSES and is the mechanism**: Spearman(SE_analytic, sqrt(2(1-rho_ij))) = **+0.8710**
over **558 rung pairs**. Distance sets the denominator exactly as 1108 supposed; 1108's error was
to forget the numerator.

## THE REPLACEMENT — PRE-REGISTERED, THEN MEASURED
Declared before any number: an analytic `A_ij = Phi(|gap_ij| / SE_ij)`, with SE built **only**
from the two rungs' own series, replaces the bootstrap for a statistic iff it reproduces (i)
>= 0.95 of pair RESOLVED verdicts, (ii) >= 0.90 of cell TIE-SET verdicts, and (iii) a floor within
1.5x in median. **All three legs, or it does not replace it.** The SE is **exact in sign** for
CAGR (CAGR is a monotone map of the resampled log-sum, so the sign of the CAGR gap *is* the sign
of the log-sum gap) and a delta-method influence function for Sharpe.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAGR   | **0.9839** of 186 (bar 0.95) | **1.0000** of 8 (bar 0.90) | **1.0000** (bar 1.5) | **REPLACES** |
| S_FULL | **0.9624** of 186 (bar 0.95) | **1.0000** of 8 (bar 0.90) | **1.0000** (bar 1.5) | **REPLACES** |

Spearman(SE_bootstrap, SE_analytic) reads **0.9982 / 0.9995 / 0.9993** on CAGR / S_FULL / S_OOS —
the two SEs are the same quantity; what fails on S_OOS is the **normal** approximation at the
pair level over the shorter OOS window, not the SE. **H_NO_DD, declared and not measured:** MaxDD
is a path functional, the resampled ORDER decides it, and no pairwise SE built from marginal
moments can carry it. **MaxDD keeps the bootstrap** — and it is where the bootstrap still earns
its cost (6 of 8 DD cells are tie sets under it).

## D1 — WHAT THE 8-LADDER CORRELATION IS ACTUALLY DRIVEN BY (POST-HOC, LABELLED)
