----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  P_boot >= 0.90 asks: do 90% of draws put the SAME rung on top?  It is a COUNT over
  draw argmaxes and ignores how big the observed margin is.
  B_GAPEXCEEDS asks: is the observed top-minus-second gap larger than 95% of the gaps
  the draws produce?  It is a COMPARISON OF ONE SCALAR against a draw distribution and
  ignores WHICH rung the draws put on top.  A ladder can be decisive on either reading
  and not the other, so the pair is logically independent, not nested.

  WHY IT IS NOT: the bar compares the OBSERVED top-minus-second margin of a k-rung ladder
  against the 95th percentile of the DRAWS' OWN top-minus-second gap.  A resample of k
  correlated books produces a maximum-minus-second spread that is dominated by sampling
  noise and is SYSTEMATICALLY WIDER than the real ladder's separation, so the bar asks a
  real ladder to be more separated than 95 of 100 pure-noise ones.  Nothing about this
  tape is involved; it is a property of the statistic.
