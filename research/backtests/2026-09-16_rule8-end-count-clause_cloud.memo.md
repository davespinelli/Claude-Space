## EXACT WORDING PROPOSED (not applied — PROTOCOL rule 6 reserves changes to the Sunday review)

Append to PROTOCOL rule 8:

> **END COUNT.** Any published percentile, z-score or "beats a coin flip" claim must state the NUMBER OF ENDS its null averages over AND the end list itself, beside the pool size, the draw count and the control basis. A basis label (per-end resample / one book held / a single end) does not identify the yardstick: at a matched end count the three bases agree to 0.004, and at sixteen ends they disagree by 0.276 — entirely through the count. The end list is required because the same count-free null sd moves across a 0.019-wide band on the choice of which end.

7. **Relation to 1035's clause.** This SUBSUMES it: naming the count and the ends pins the yardstick; naming the basis alone does not. If both are adopted, the basis clause is retained as a convenience label, not as the identifying field.
8. **What the record may still say.** That a larger published gap earns a higher percentile (1035, ρ 0.986), and that IS_CAGR beats a uniform per-end draw in 5 of 6 cells (1023/1031) — that claim names its count implicitly by citing Q16.
9. **What it may no longer say.** That any percentile quoted without an end count has been adjudicated; a percentile of 0.004 and one of 0.278 are the same evidence read at sixteen ends and at one.
