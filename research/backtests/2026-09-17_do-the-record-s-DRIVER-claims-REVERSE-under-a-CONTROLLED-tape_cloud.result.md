**THE THREE THINGS WORTH READING:**

1. **1164's reversal does not generalise.** Five of six price-native drivers keep their sign on every tape construction, including the most adversarial (T_FACTOR, where one common factor removes most of the diversification channel). A cross-book rho is not automatically a fiction.
2. **But the MAGNITUDE is not a mechanism, and that is where the record over-reads.** For **D_N_SH — the EDGE-ladder premise — the real panel shows only 0.202 of the controlled tape's relation (+0.1659 against +0.9134)**, and D_N only 0.582 (−0.4980 against −0.9377). On an exchangeable tape, book size orders Sharpe and drawdown almost perfectly; on the real panel SELECTION cancels four-fifths of it, because the names a small book picks are the ones the composite says are best. **So the sign of an n-ladder claim is a mechanism and its rho magnitude is a net of mechanism against selection.** The record quotes those magnitudes as strength.
3. **The queue's OWN cheapest driver cannot be tested this way at all.** 1094's turnover → c\* is DEGENERATE at 9 of 9 synthetic cells, and on 2 of 3 real panels, **for a structural reason: c\* is defined only on books that pass 4b, no synthetic book ever does, and a column of tied zeros is not a measurement.** On U56 the real panel reproduces the claim cleanly (**−0.6154** against 1094's −0.65 and 1151's −0.6484 over its own 13 live books), so the claim is not refuted — it is **unfalsifiable by a controlled tape**, which is a different and more useful verdict than either pass or fail. **Any 4b-conditional outcome inherits this.** Filed as a follow-up.

**Tape construction matters and is the dial:** T_FACTOR carries 8 of the 14 degenerate cells (a one-factor tape produces books too alike to rank) and its max cross-seed spread is 0.9874 against T_IID's 0.1538 and T_BLOCK's 0.2130. **A one-factor tape is the weakest of the three as an instrument, and the queue named it.**
