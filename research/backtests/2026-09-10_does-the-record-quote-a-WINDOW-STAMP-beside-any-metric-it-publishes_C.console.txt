A3  RE-PRICING THE RECORD'S PUBLISHED COMPARISONS AT THE STAMP'S ABSENCE
====================================================================================================
  A published COMPARISON is only decidable if both numbers share a window.  Three
  populations of comparisons are re-priced against the PURE-SPAN bar of A2, weakest
  evidence first, and all three are reported:
    A3a ADJACENT   the within-file adjacent-value gaps of a metric column — the finest
                   distinction a file draws.  DECLARED WEAK: adjacent gaps shrink with row
                   count by construction, so the pooled share is dominated by the largest
                   grids.  Reported with a per-file median beside it for that reason.
    A3b COMPARAND  within-ROW gaps between an arm metric and its own comparand column in
                   the same row (spy_/v1_/v2_/base_/twin_/ctrl_...).  These are comparisons
                   the record makes explicitly, and both sides share a window BY
                   CONSTRUCTION — so this population is the one the stamp does NOT
                   endanger.  It is the internal control.
    A3c DELTA      the record's explicit d<metric> columns (dSharpe, dCAGR, dMaxDD ...).
                   A delta column IS a published comparison, in the metric's own units.
                   Its own window is stamped only if its file is.  THIS IS THE HEADLINE.
  Exact zeros are EXCLUDED from every population: a published delta of exactly 0.0 is not
  a distinction being drawn, and counting it would inflate every share below.
  exact zeros excluded: 53,161 delta values, 9,957 comparand gaps.
