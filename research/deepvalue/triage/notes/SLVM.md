# SLVM — Sylvamo Corp · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 7/10

Sylvamo makes uncoated freesheet paper and market pulp at mills in Europe, Latin America and North America, selling copy, offset and converting grades under brands including Hammermill, Chamex and REY to distributors, converters and end users. The trough is documented rather than assumed: 2025 adjusted EBITDA fell to $448 million from $632 million, Europe swung to a $112 million segment operating loss from $10 million, and Q2 2026 produced a $11 million net loss with negative $23 million of free cash flow. Management names the causes as temporary: the Riverdale offtake exit, tariffs and an extended Eastover outage, with a paper machine project adding 60,000 tons in Q4 and capex normalizing after $224 million in 2025. Against a $1.4 billion market cap and 8.5x EV/EBIT, the stated potential of over $300 million of annual free cash flow is the quantified gap, though that is management's aspiration, not a result. The biggest risk is that the demand decline is secular, not cyclical: the MD&A itself lists adoption of electronic mediums and hybrid work as drivers of UFS demand. No transcript in the pack.
