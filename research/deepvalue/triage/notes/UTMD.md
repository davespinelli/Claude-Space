# UTMD — UTAH MEDICAL PRODUCTS INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 4/10

Utah Medical makes niche medical devices for neonatal intensive care, labor and delivery and women's health, sold direct to hospitals in the US, UK, Ireland, France, Canada, Australia and New Zealand and through roughly 200 distributors elsewhere, with the Filshie clip sterilization system and OEM contract manufacturing as the other legs. The only claim available is balance sheet: $87.5M of cash and investments and no debt against a $225.1M market cap, so 39% of the price is net cash and the operating EV is $137.8M on 26.0% ROIC and 30%-plus operating margins. That is not enough, because the revenue base is eroding and the pack says so plainly. First half 2026 sales fell 12.3%, driven by two former customers, PendoTECH and a China distributor, going to zero, and the replacement biopharma OEM sales management planned reached only $211K. Management now projects a full-year 2026 sales decline of 10% to 13%, abandoning its flat-revenue plan. Meanwhile the stock sits 4.4% off its 52-week high, so the market is not discounting the decline. Other flags: rising Filshie clip litigation costs, a $395K written-off China receivable, and a $195K embezzlement by the Australia subsidiary manager. No insider buying.
