# NGS — NATURAL GAS SERVICES GROUP INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

NGS rents, designs, installs and maintains natural gas engine and electric motor drive compressors for E&P customers, mostly for gas-lift artificial lift on unconventional oil wells; 95.4% of 2025 revenue was rental and 78% of rental revenue came from the Permian. Operations look genuinely good: rented horsepower rose 14.4% to 562,676, utilization to 84.9%, rental gross margin held at 60.6%, and the 8-K exhibit (which is a transcript of the Q2 2026 call, filed as the results exhibit) claims record Q2 results, DSO cut from 108 to about 33 days, and the $120M Flatrock acquisition at roughly 6.2x annualized adjusted EBITDA. The problem is price, not quality: the screen row (universe_under2b.csv, CY2025 revenue/EBIT, CY2026Q2 balance sheet) shows 21.1x EV/EBIT, net debt of $327.9M at 8.8x EBIT, FCF of negative $58.6M on $121.5M capex, 4.7% ROIC and 2.8% share growth, with the stock up 41.2% on 12-1 momentum and only 18.8% off its high. Biggest risk is that growth capex and leverage keep FCF negative through an oil-price downturn the MD&A itself calls cyclical. No transcript beyond the filed exhibit; I could not check pro forma post-Flatrock leverage against a full quarter.
