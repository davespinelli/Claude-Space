# OIS — OIL STATES INTERNATIONAL, INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 7/10

Oil States makes engineered capital equipment and consumables for energy, military and industrial customers through three segments: Offshore Manufactured Products (subsea and production infrastructure, 91% project-driven), Completion and Production Services (US land, call-out), and Downhole Technologies (perforating and completion products). The consolidated numbers are misleading. FY25 shows a $98.0M operating loss, negative 13.4% ROIC and an unusable EV/EBIT, but the segment table attributes all of it to Downhole Technologies, which took $112.7M of non-cash long-lived and inventory impairments inside a $124.3M segment loss, while Offshore Manufactured Products earned $69.2M of operating income on $431.1M of revenue. That segment's backlog reached $451M at June 30, 2026, the highest since March 2015, on 1.2x quarterly book-to-bill, and Downhole itself returned to a $2.7M operating profit in Q2 as revenue hit its best level since Q2 2023. The balance sheet supports the look: $532.4M EV against essentially zero net debt after retiring the $52.7M convertibles, $581.4M of book equity above the $534.5M market cap, and $5M of buybacks. Risk is US land, where impairments and restructurings have recurred in 2024 and 2025 and could again. Q2 operating cash flow was negative $6.3M on working capital, and the CEO retired in 2026.
