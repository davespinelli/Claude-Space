# CVEO — Civeo Corp · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

Civeo owns and operates 26 workforce lodges and villages with about 26,300 rooms in the Australian natural resource regions and the Canadian oil sands, and provides catering, housekeeping, facilities and integrated services at another 22 customer-owned sites. The edge type is cash-cow-narrative. The company is converting a commodity-linked lodging business into an aggressive share count reduction: shares outstanding fell 17.6% year over year to 10.3M, the 20% repurchase authorization was completed and a further 10% authorization is about half done, funded partly by $115M of 4.50% convertible notes issued in July 2026 with an initial conversion price near $40.51. Q2 2026 revenue rose 11% to $180.0M, adjusted EBITDA was $23.8M, and full year guidance of $675M-$700M revenue and $85M-$90M adjusted EBITDA was maintained against an EV of $537.0M and capex guidance of only $25M-$30M. The biggest risk is that little of that EBITDA reaches earnings: FY25 EBIT was $4.1M, net loss $20.1M, and income tax expense of $6.7M was charged on $0.3M of first-half pretax income.
