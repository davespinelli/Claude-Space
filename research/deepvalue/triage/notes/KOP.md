# KOP — Koppers Holdings Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

Koppers runs three vertically integrated businesses: RUPS, the largest supplier of railroad crossties to North American Class I railroads and the second largest US utility pole producer; Performance Chemicals, wood preservation chemicals including CCA and DCOI; and Carbon Materials and Chemicals, which distills coal tar into creosote, carbon pitch and carbon black feedstock. The claim is cash, not earnings: 2026 guidance holds free cash flow at $110M to $130M against a $886.2M market cap, versus $68M actual in 2025, with record year-to-date free cash flow of $72.6M against $1.4M a year earlier, funding debt reduction, a 4.1% share count reduction and dividends at 10.4x EV/EBIT. The risk is that two of three segments are eroding. Q2 adjusted EBITDA fell 7.9% to $71.0M, RUPS fell 18.7% on crosstie price decreases and CMC fell 54.8% to $7.6M, and the 10-K describes the coal tar supply that feeds CMC as in long-term decline with non-Asian blast furnace steel. Management took a $215.8M impairment and restructuring charge, is closing Stickney, Illinois, and cut the top of adjusted EBITDA and EPS guidance. Net debt is $852.0M. Insiders sold 105,361 shares for $5.2M with no buys, after an 83.1% twelve-month move.
