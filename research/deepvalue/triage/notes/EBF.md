# EBF — ENNIS, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 5/10

Ennis is a trade printer, manufacturing custom business forms, pressure-seal forms, labels, tags, envelopes and presentation folders at roughly 50 US plants and selling almost entirely through independent distributors; about 95% of output is custom or semi-custom. The screen flags missing debt data, but the Q1 balance sheet resolves it: total liabilities of $52.9 million consist of payables, accruals and lease obligations with no borrowings, and management states the company operates with no debt, so the $49.1 million of cash is genuine net cash. That supports a cash-cow-narrative: 30.7% gross margin and 13.4% operating margin held flat for three years in an industry the market treats as terminal, $41.0 million of FCF (7.6% yield), a $0.25 quarterly dividend and a 1.9% share count reduction, at 9.3x EV/EBIT. Biggest risk is the melting core: fiscal 2026 sales fell 0.6% only because roughly $22.8 million of acquired revenue offset about $25.0 million of organic volume decline, and fiscal 2025 fell 6.1% before that, so acquisitions are masking a mid-single-digit annual erosion. The sole domestic carbonless-paper mill has also closed. At $21.30 the stock is 5.5% off its high, so little of this is discounted.
