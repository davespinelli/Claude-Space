# PGNY — Progyny, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

It has 604 fertility clients and 7.2M covered lives. The pack does not support an edge. The balance sheet is genuinely clean: $236.9M cash and marketable securities, no debt and an undrawn $200M revolver, with 10.8M shares repurchased across two programs. Biggest risk is client concentration, evidenced by one non-renewal costing $48.5M of 2025 revenue. Insiders sold $621K net and bought nothing.
