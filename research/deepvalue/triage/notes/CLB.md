# CLB — Core Laboratories Inc. /DE/ · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

Core Laboratories runs an asset-light global lab network of over 70 offices in more than 50 countries in two segments: Reservoir Description, which analyses reservoir rock, fluids and crude assays with roughly 80% of revenue originating outside the US, and Production Enhancement, which sells perforating systems and completion diagnostics tied to US onshore completions. The trough has a named cause rather than a general slump: second-quarter revenue fell 4% year over year and operating income 36%, because Middle East client operations were suspended, crude assay work tied to cargo shipments through the Strait of Hormuz was disrupted, and expanded Russia-Ukraine sanctions hit demand, while management deliberately kept the Middle East cost structure in place expecting trade routes to normalize. One director bought 5,000 shares at $10.78, the only open-market trade in twelve months. What holds the score down is price: 11.8x EV/EBIT on 2025 earnings, and the quarter's $9.2M of operating income annualizes well above that, so the trough is not on offer at a trough multiple, with quarterly free cash flow of $3.1M against $91.1M of net debt.
