# XPER — Xperi Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

Xperi licenses and operates four things: the TiVo One media platform and its advertising inventory, DTS AutoStage and HD Radio in connected cars, TiVo IPTV software for pay TV operators, and DTS audio licensing to consumer electronics brands. Consolidated revenue hides the mix shift. FY26 revenue is guided to $440-470 million against $448.1 million in FY25, essentially flat, while Media Platform revenue grew 44% year over year, TiVo One monthly active users reached 6.3 million on 70% growth with trailing ARPU of $6.70, and AutoStage reached 17 million vehicles across 14 brands after BYD joined. The screen row (2026-09-05 pack, $5.83) puts EV at $233.7 million with $90.6 million of cash against $40.0 million of debt, and guidance implies $75-89 million of adjusted EBITDA at a 17-19% margin, with Q2 GAAP operating income turning positive at $2.9 million. The reason to stop at 6 is cash conversion: operating cash flow is guided to only $15-25 million against capex raised to about $25 million, so free cash flow is near zero once roughly $29 million of stock compensation and $20 million of non-GAAP tax are accounted for. Biggest risk is that legacy licensing declines faster than advertising scales, and the pack gives no segment profitability to check.
