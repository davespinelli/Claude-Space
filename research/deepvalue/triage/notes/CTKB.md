# CTKB — Cytek Biosciences, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 5/10

Cytek sells full spectrum flow cytometry instruments (Aurora, Northern Lights, the new 7-laser Borealis) plus reagents, software and service to research and clinical labs, with an installed base of 3,933 instruments after adding 142 in the quarter. The balance sheet is the asset: cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $262.0 million at June 30 against a $610.9 million market cap, so roughly 43% of the market value is liquid, and the pack's DEBT DATA MISSING warning cuts the other way here since no borrowings appear in the release. That leaves about $349 million of enterprise value against guided FY26 revenue of $207-212 million. The operating business does not yet support a re-rating. Q2 revenue grew only 6% to $48.1 million, the full-year guide is barely 3% above FY25's $201.5 million, adjusted EBITDA was negative $1.5 million versus positive $1.3 million a year earlier, and the reported 59% gross margin falls to 53% excluding a one-time tariff refund. Recurring revenue is improving, at 35% of trailing revenue versus 32%. Biggest risk is that the cash erodes rather than returns: G&A rose 24% on litigation, severance and personnel costs, and both the chief legal officer and the head of global sales left in mid-2026.
