# MEI — METHODE ELECTRONICS INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 4/10

Methode is a custom-engineered supplier of power distribution, user interface, lighting and sensor products, historically weighted to automotive and appliance customers and now growing through an Industrial segment led by data centres. The misunderstood-segment case is that consolidated numbers hide two opposite trends: fiscal Q1 2027 net sales rose 10.4% to $265.4M driven by Industrial data centre volumes, while the dataMate divestiture and consumer appliance programme roll-offs pulled the other way, and management booked new non-data-centre awards worth $75M of peak annual revenue, about $400M of lifetime revenue. The pack gives no segment profit split, so the claim cannot be sized. The biggest risk is that profitability is moving backwards while revenue grows: adjusted EBITDA fell to $13.7M, or 5.2% of sales, from $15.7M and 6.5%, the company posted a $3.9M operating loss and an $11.4M net loss, free cash flow was an outflow of $10.9M against an inflow of $18.0M a year earlier, and net debt is $194.1M. The shares are already up 131% over twelve months.
