# CERT — Certara, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 5/10

Certara sells biosimulation software and technology-enabled services for model-informed drug development: mathematical models of how drugs behave in the body, licensed to more than 160,000 users and 20 regulators, plus scientist-delivered consulting on customer drug programs. Edge is post-restructuring. In May 2026 it divested the Regulatory and Medical Writing business, reorganized into two units, and cut about 5% of headcount for roughly $13M of run-rate savings, so trailing 2025 figures ($418.8M revenue, 9% growth) overstate the continuing company, which guides to $367M to $382M. The buyback completed $100M and the board added $50M, with shares down 5.0%. Biggest risk is that the remaining business is not growing: Q2 revenue rose 1%, services fell 3%, services bookings fell 6%, adjusted EBITDA fell 3%, and full-year growth is guided at 0% to 4%. Against a $1.2B market cap (screen price $8.13, date not shown because price data was skipped), guided adjusted EPS of $0.31 to $0.36 is not obviously cheap, and GAAP results are loss-making with $56.6M of annual amortization.
