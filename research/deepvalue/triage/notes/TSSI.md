# TSSI — TSS, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

TSS integrates AI and high-performance computing racks for data centres: it builds, configures and deploys server racks whose components are consigned by one large US OEM customer, and it also runs facilities management and an agency-style IT procurement business. Edge is misunderstood-segment. Consolidated revenue is therefore a misleading proxy for the earning power here. Biggest risk is concentration the pack states plainly: a material decline in volume from, or loss of, the single OEM customer would materially affect results.
