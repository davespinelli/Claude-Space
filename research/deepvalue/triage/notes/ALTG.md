# ALTG — ALTA EQUIPMENT GROUP INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

Alta runs one of North America's larger integrated equipment dealerships, over 80 branches selling, renting and servicing lift trucks, earthmoving, crushing and screening and environmental processing equipment as exclusive territory distributor for Hyster-Yale, Volvo, JCB, CNH, Takeuchi and others; parts and service are the profitable, recurring end. The cycle case has numbers behind it: FY25 revenue fell 2.2% to $1,835.9M entirely on rental revenue down 10.2% organic and rental equipment sales down 21.0% as the fleet was deliberately shrunk, while Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA held flat year over year at $48.6M on lower revenue, rose $20.5M sequentially, and management tightened FY26 guidance to $167.5M to $177.5M against a $652.6M EV. Material Handling bookings are up 12.3% year to date, service gross margin up 160bp to 61.4%, interest expense down $2.8M, and three insiders including Mill Road Capital bought $722k of stock. The trap is the capital structure: net debt of $465.4M is 20.1x EBIT, book equity is negative $36.2M, FY25 interest expense of $88.4M swamped $23.2M of operating income, and the company has lost money for two straight years. I could not check covenant headroom or the maturity schedule.
