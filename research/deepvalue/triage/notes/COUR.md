# COUR — Coursera, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Coursera runs a global online learning platform with two segments, Consumer subscriptions led by Coursera Plus and Enterprise sales to employers, universities and governments, monetising content built by more than 375 university and industry partners. It closed an all-stock combination with Udemy on 2026-05-11, which is why the share count is up 61.5% and why trailing consolidated numbers are stale. The edge is that reset cost base: management now expects at least $85 million of annual run-rate net synergies by end-2026 and guides FY26 revenue to $1.220-1.245 billion at roughly a 14% adjusted EBITDA margin, about $170 million, against a screen EV of $717.3 million ($6.01 price, $871.7 million cash, 2026-09-05 pack). The board authorised a $500 million buyback and had repurchased roughly $140 million by July 28 at an average $5.45. Two caveats: the pack carries the DEBT DATA MISSING warning so I could not verify borrowings, though FY25 shows net interest income of $32.0 million and no interest expense line; and adjusted EBITDA excludes stock compensation, which was $95.1 million in FY25 standalone. Biggest risk is demand erosion, with enterprise net retention at 91% versus 95% and enterprise customers down 2%.
