# SBH — Sally Beauty Holdings, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 5/10

Sally Beauty Holdings is a specialty retailer and distributor of professional beauty supplies running two segments: Sally, an omni-channel retailer selling professional-quality hair color, care and nail products to DIY consumers and stylists from 3,096 stores, and Beauty Systems Group, a distributor selling third-party professional brands exclusively to salons and licensed professionals from 1,326 Cosmo Prof stores plus salon consultants. The cash-cow-narrative case is that the market treats flat sales as decline while the cash keeps arriving: fiscal 2025 revenue of $3.70 billion fell 0.4%, yet consolidated operating earnings rose 15.9% to $327.8 million and net earnings rose 27.7% to $195.9 million on Fuel for Growth savings. Fiscal 2026 guidance holds free cash flow near $200 million with roughly half directed to buybacks, the share count is already down 5.4%, and net debt leverage is 1.4x. The risk is that nothing is actually growing: consolidated comparable sales were 0.3% in fiscal 2025 and are guided to about 0.5% for 2026, while BSG net sales fell 2.4% in Q3 with comps down 2.1%. The CFO resigned in March 2026.
