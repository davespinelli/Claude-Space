# TDAY — USA TODAY Co., Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 5/10

USA TODAY Co. is a diversified media company running the USA TODAY NETWORK of national and local US titles, Newsquest in the UK, and LocaliQ, a digital marketing services business selling to small and medium businesses. The cash-cow-narrative case is that a business the market treats as terminal keeps producing cash: Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA was $56.9M and free cash flow $19.6M, up 11% year over year, operating expenses fell about 8%, net income was positive for a second consecutive quarter, first lien net leverage fell 14% to 2.3x, and full-year guidance is for operating cash flow and free cash flow to grow double digits. Digital is now 47.4% of revenue, with digital-only subscription revenue growing for a second consecutive quarter and record ARPU. The biggest risk is that the melt continues faster than the deleveraging: total revenue still fell 8.3%, or 6.1% same-store, to $536.3M, and $970.5M of debt principal sits against a $949.9M market cap, so the equity is a levered stub whose free cash flow is only about 4% of the $1.7B enterprise value.
