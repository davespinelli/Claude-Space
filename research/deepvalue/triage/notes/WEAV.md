# WEAV — Weave Communications, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Weave sells a vertical SaaS platform for patient engagement and payments to healthcare practices, bundling phones, messaging, scheduling, an AI receptionist and payment processing, integrated with EHR systems such as athenaOne and Elation Health. No edge type applies, because the pack shows the equity is already spoken for: an 8-K dated 2026-08-19 discloses that on August 18, 2026 Weave entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Willow Parent, LLC and a merger sub. The pack does not give the consideration, the price per share, or the expected closing, so I could not check the spread or the terms; a March 2026 Cooperation Agreement with Engine Capital and 2717 Partners preceded it. The operating business is sound: Q2 revenue was $67.5 million, up 15.5%, GAAP gross margin 72.0%, free cash flow $8.7 million, cash $47.6 million, and FY26 guidance is $273-275 million of revenue with $12-14 million of non-GAAP operating income. The biggest risk to any value thesis is simply that a signed merger caps the outcome.
