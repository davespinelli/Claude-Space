# CYH — COMMUNITY HEALTH SYSTEMS INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Community Health Systems operates hospital networks in 36 markets across 14 states, with 69 affiliated hospitals holding more than 10,000 beds plus over 1,000 outpatient sites including physician practices, urgent care, freestanding emergency departments and surgery centres, paid by government programs, private insurers and patients. It is a levered equity stub: $9.4 billion of net debt and negative $1.4 billion of equity beneath a $410.3 million market cap. The deleveraging is real and quantified. The company used roughly $600 million of divestiture proceeds to retire $368 million of its 4.750% notes due 2031, about 35% of that issue, and $231 million of its 10.875% notes due 2032, and has divested nine hospitals in 2026. The problem is that the treadmill runs the wrong way. Each sale removes earnings: Q2 adjusted EBITDA fell to $330 million from $380 million and first-half adjusted EBITDA fell to $638 million from $756 million, and adjusted diluted loss per share widened to $(0.67). Same-store revenue grew 2.5%, not enough against roughly 7x net leverage.
