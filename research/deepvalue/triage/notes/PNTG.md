# PNTG — Pennant Group, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Pennant, spun off from Ensign in 2019, is a holding company for 175 home health, hospice and home care agencies and 69 senior living communities across the western US and, since a Q4 2025 acquisition, Alabama, Georgia and Tennessee. It is paid by Medicare, Medicaid, managed care and private-pay residents, and it grows by acquiring and turning around local operations under a decentralized cluster model. Revenue rose 36.3% to $947.7M in 2025 and 35.8% in Q2 2026, with same-store home health admissions up 9.7% and same-store hospice census up 10.8%, so the growth is not purely bought. There is no mispricing to name. At $38.54 the EV of $1.5B is about 15x the midpoint of 2026 guided Adjusted EBITDA of $94.4M to $98.0M and the price is roughly 27x guided adjusted EPS of $1.34 to $1.41, on a GAAP net margin of 3.1%; the stock is up 57.2% over twelve months and 8.3% off its high, and insiders sold 13,396 shares with no open-market buys. Biggest risk: this is a debt-funded roll-up, with net debt of $177.2M and Q2 interest expense of $3.3M against $1.2M a year earlier, integrating its largest acquisition ever.
