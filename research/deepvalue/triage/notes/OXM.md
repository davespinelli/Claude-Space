# OXM — OXFORD INDUSTRIES INC · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

Oxford owns Tommy Bahama, Lilly Pulitzer, Johnny Was, Southern Tide and other lifestyle apparel brands, selling 82% of FY25 revenue direct to consumer through its own stores, e-commerce and Tommy Bahama restaurants. The edge is cash-cow-narrative built on a capex step-down the trailing screen hides: FY25 free cash flow was only $11.3M because capital expenditure was $108.3M for new stores and a new Lyons, Georgia distribution centre, and management now guides FY26 capex to about $60M while first-half operating cash flow was $97M. That cash cut borrowings from $143M at Q1 to $73M. The company has paid a dividend every quarter since 1960 and the CEO bought 2,500 shares at $36.90 in June. The biggest risk is that earnings are shrinking into that payout: FY26 guidance was just lowered to $1.60-2.00 of adjusted EPS versus $2.11 in FY25 and a $2.80 annualised dividend, Lilly Pulitzer fell 5.6% and Johnny Was 8.8% in Q2, and management is adding promotions at Lilly to clear slow-moving inventory. GAAP EPS guidance of $3.07-3.47 includes $2.07 of one-off tariff refunds. Screen price $36.63.
