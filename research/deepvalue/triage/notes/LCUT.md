# LCUT — LIFETIME BRANDS, INC · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

Lifetime Brands designs and sources branded kitchenware, tableware and home solutions (Farberware, KitchenAid, Mikasa, Taylor, Pfaltzgraff, S'well) and sells them to mass merchants, warehouse clubs, department stores and Amazon, in two segments, U.S. and International. Almost all product is sourced outside the U.S., primarily China, which is why tariffs run the P&L. The apparent edge is the raised FY26 guidance: income from operations guided up from $12-14.5M to $48-50.5M and adjusted EPS from $0.73-0.80 to $2.06-2.13 against an $8.81 screen price (screen row dated in the 2026-09-05 pack). But the entire uplift is a $40.1M one-time tariff refund recognised in Q2; underlying adjusted EBITDA guidance of $53.5-56M was left unchanged and net sales guidance was reaffirmed. The screen's -$5.5M net debt is not credible next to $20.0M of 2025 interest expense and $40M of term debt repaid in Q2, so EV is understated. Biggest risk is structural demand decline: 2025 sales fell 5.1% with a $33.2M U.S. goodwill impairment. The stock is already up 181% in six months and insiders sold 36,683 shares with zero open-market buys.
