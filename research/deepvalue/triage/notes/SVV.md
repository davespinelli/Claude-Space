# SVV — Savers Value Village, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

Savers is the largest for-profit thrift operator in the US and Canada, running 375 stores that buy secondhand clothing and household goods from non-profit partners, process and price them, sell them at roughly a $5 average unit retail, and move unsold items to wholesale buyers. The edge type is none. The trading is genuinely improving, with Q2 net sales up 7.4% to $448.2M, US comparable store sales up 6.6%, a third consecutive quarter of adjusted EBITDA growth at $74.5M, guidance nudged up and 1.2 million shares bought back at $8.10, but that is not a mispricing at 17.7x EV/EBIT and a 3.1% free cash flow yield. The biggest risk is the combination of leverage and a selling holder: net debt is $614.6M or 5.0x EBIT against $444.3M of equity, and the Form 4 summary shows 46,639,742 shares sold for $478,962,709 in twelve months with zero open-market purchases. FY2025 operating income also fell to $124.1M from $130.2M despite 9.2% sales growth. Score 4: decent operations, full price, heavy supply.
