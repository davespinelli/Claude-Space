# IDN — Intellicheck, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: fallen-growth · Score 5/10

Intellicheck sells SaaS identity validation, reading and authenticating driver's licences and other government IDs for KYC, fraud prevention and age verification, priced per scan or per device, to banks, fintechs, retailers and government; banking and lending was 48% of Q2 2026 revenue and retail 29%. The operating record has turned: FY25 revenue rose 13% to $22.7M at a 90.4% gross margin, and Q2 2026 revenue rose 16% to a record $5.9M with $0.6M of operating income, $0.7M of net income and $1.1M of adjusted EBITDA, the fifth consecutive positive quarter. The balance sheet is clean despite the screen's missing-debt flag: the June 30, 2026 balance sheet shows $11.8M of cash and total liabilities of just $3.1M with no debt line at all. What broke the stock, down 68% from its high and 42.3% in six months, is disclosed in the same release: a customer at roughly 29% of first-half revenue is moving from sole-source to multi-source and testing an alternative, with the main transition phase beginning in late July. That is the risk and it is not small; at a 91% gross margin, losing that revenue would erase more than the current adjusted EBITDA. Management says the remaining base grew about 19% in the first half. Valuation is not yet compensating at 42.8x EV/EBIT on a $46.5M EV, and insiders sold $344k with no buys.
