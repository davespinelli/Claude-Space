# KFRC - KFORCE INC - triage 2026-09-04 - Edge: none - Score 4/10

Kforce is a staffing firm: 93% of revenue is Technology and 98% is Flex, placing hourly consultants at roughly a $90 average technology bill rate with Fortune 500 clients, plus a small Direct Hire business and a growing solutions-oriented offering delivered partly from a new Pune development centre. The cyclical turn is real: Q2-26 revenue was $349.3 million, up 4.5% year over year and 5.7% sequentially, gross margin rose 140 basis points to 28.5%, EPS of $0.73 was up 23.7%, and Q3 is guided to $349-357 million and $0.71-0.79. But no edge type applies, because the turn is already in the price: the shares are up 99.1% over twelve months, sit 10.8% off the high at $53.95, and trade near 18x the annualised Q3 guide on 21.4x trailing EV/EBIT and a 4.8% free cash flow yield. The biggest risk is that capital returns are funded by the revolver: 2025 returns of $76.0 million exceeded $61.6 million of operating cash flow, credit facility debt rose to $107.1 million from $66.4 million in six months against $123.6 million of equity, and SIA projects only 1% temporary technology staffing growth in 2026.
