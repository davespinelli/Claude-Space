# TRIP — TripAdvisor, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 7/10

Tripadvisor now reports two continuing segments, Experiences (tours and activities bookings, $278.6M of second-quarter revenue on about 6.5 million bookings) and Hotels and Other (the legacy hotel meta-search and media business, $163.3M), after reclassifying TheFork as discontinued. The hidden asset is contractual and near-term: on 14 June 2026 the company signed a put option agreement with American Express Travel for TheFork at $700.0M in cash under an irrevocable commitment, exercised the put on 1 August, signed the equity purchase agreement on 2 August, and expects to close by the end of 2026. That $700.0M is about 64% of the $1.1B market cap and sits on top of $843.2M of continuing-operations cash against $815.9M of long-term debt, after the company repaid $345.4M of 2026 notes on 1 April. The risk is what remains: continuing revenue fell 7%, Hotels and Other fell 21% with segment adjusted EBITDA down 23%, Experiences segment EBITDA fell 19% despite 5% booking growth, and marketing rose to 48.7% of revenue. The pack gives no after-tax proceeds figure and no stated use of proceeds.
