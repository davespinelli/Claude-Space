# PLTK — Playtika Holding Corp. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

Playtika operates a portfolio of free-to-play mobile games (Bingo Blitz, Disney Solitaire, June's Journey, Slotomania), monetised through in-game virtual item sales, increasingly through its own direct-to-consumer store where payment fees are 3% to 4% rather than the 30% charged by Apple and Google. Edge is cash-cow-narrative. FY26 adjusted EBITDA is guided to $750M to $790M, toward the low end, against an $854.4M market cap (screen price $2.24, date not shown because price data was skipped), and DTC revenue reached $286.9M in Q2, up 63% year over year, which structurally lifts margin. Biggest risk is that the equity is a thin stub on a shrinking audience: net debt is $1.9B against a $399.9M stockholders' deficit, $370M of contingent consideration remains, and average MAUs fell from 30.0M to 24.8M and paying users 2.9% year over year while Bingo Blitz revenue fell 9.5%. The screen's 62.2% FCF yield is not current: H1 2026 free cash flow was $15.0M against $119.6M a year earlier, and cash fell from $684.2M to $438.5M.
