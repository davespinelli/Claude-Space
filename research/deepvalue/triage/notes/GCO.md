# GCO — GENESCO INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 7/10

Genesco is a footwear retailer and wholesaler running 1,186 stores plus e-commerce across Journeys and Journeys Kidz, Schuh in the UK, Johnston & Murphy, and a licensed branded wholesale arm, on $2.4B of revenue. The mispricing is scale of cash flow against a tiny equity: 11.1M shares at $34.44 make a $382.5M market cap against $552.4M of book equity, cash of $57.1M versus total debt of $15.8M, and a screen FCF yield of 21.9%. Fiscal 2027 guidance was raised to the high end of $2.00 to $2.40 adjusted EPS and $34M to $40M of operating income, and a new cost program targets $40M to $50M of savings by Fiscal 2029, which is larger than the operating income the company earns today. Journeys posted its eighth consecutive positive comparable quarter at +2% and Johnston & Murphy +4%, the company repurchased 317,503 shares in August, and an officer bought $99,980 in July. Biggest risk: Schuh comparable sales fell 9% with sales down 10%, e-commerce comps fell 6%, and operating margin of 0.7% leaves no cushion if the UK deteriorates further. Share count is still up 3.0% year over year.
