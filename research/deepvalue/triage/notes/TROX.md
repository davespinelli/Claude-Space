# TROX — Tronox Holdings plc · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 7/10

Tronox is the world's largest vertically integrated producer of titanium dioxide pigment, mining titanium-bearing mineral sands in Australia and South Africa and processing them at seven pigment plants for roughly 1,200 customers, with zircon, pig iron and rare-earth-bearing monazite as co-products. The edge is a cyclical trough with a visible turn: Q2 2026 revenue rose 19% to $868M on TiO2 volumes at their highest since Q2 2022, TiO2 and zircon prices each rose 5% sequentially, free cash flow was positive $60M, and management guides Q3 adjusted EBITDA of $95-115M against $73M in Q2. The cost programme is tracking to the upper end of $125-175M of annual run-rate savings, and 2025 already absorbed $232M of Botlek and Fuzhou closure charges. The biggest risk is the capital structure, which makes this an equity stub: $3.2B of total debt, $3.0B net, 11.4x trailing net leverage and $189M of 2025 interest expense against $336M of 2025 adjusted EBITDA. The offset in the pack is that there are no financial covenants on the term loans or bonds and no significant maturity until 2029. Score 7: a quantified cyclical inflection against a $779.3M market cap on a $3.7B enterprise value, with the leverage cutting both ways.
