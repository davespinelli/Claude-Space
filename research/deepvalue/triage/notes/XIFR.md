# XIFR — XPLR Infrastructure, LP · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

XPLR is a limited partnership holding interests in about 10 gigawatts of contracted wind, solar and battery storage across 28 states, selling output under long-term fixed-price PPAs, with PG&E and Southern California Edison each about 14 to 15% of revenue. In January 2025 it announced a strategic repositioning and suspended the common distribution, which is the event that would orphan a former yield vehicle. Since then it sold Meade, repaid the $500 million of convertible notes with cash, completed the first minimum buyout of CEPF 5 for about $150 million, and reaffirmed 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $1.75 to $1.95 billion and free cash flow before growth of $600 to $700 million against a $1.1 billion market cap and a $6.5 billion enterprise value. The reason not to score this higher is that those figures are consolidated and the units are a thin residual: XPLR owned only a 48.8% limited partner interest in XPLR OpCo at year end, noncontrolling interests carry $7.373 billion of the $10.610 billion of equity, and third-party Class B and differential membership investors sit on most of the capacity, with buyouts that consumed $931 million in 2025 and $290 million in the first half of 2026. The pack does not let me attribute EBITDA or FCFBG to the common units, which is the whole question.
