# NSP — INSPERITY, INC. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 5/10

Insperity is a professional employer organization that co-employs about 305,000 worksite employees for small and medium US businesses, charging a fee on top of payroll for HR administration, benefits and workers' compensation, with a newer Workday-based HR Scale offering. The edge type is post-restructuring: FY25 was broken, with adjusted EBITDA down 51% to $131M and a $7M GAAP net loss as benefits costs per participant rose 9% against 3% pricing, and management's margin recovery plan of repricing, benefit plan changes and cost control now guides FY26 to $185-225M of adjusted EBITDA, up 41% to 72%. Q2 delivered on that, with operating expenses down 8% and adjusted EBITDA up 13%. Chairman and CEO Paul Sarvadi bought 233,000 shares for $7.9M at $34.05 in June. The problem is that the recovery is already in the price: the screen price of $51.82 is 52% above that insider purchase, sits 3.0% off the 52-week high after a 150.8% six-month move, and is 21 to 28 times guided adjusted EPS of $1.88-2.43. The biggest risk is the volume side of the model, since paid worksite employees fell 1% and FY26 guidance assumes a further 1.0% to 1.6% decline.
