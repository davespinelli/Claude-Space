# EVTC — EVERTEC, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

Evertec is a transaction processor and financial technology provider across Puerto Rico, the Caribbean and 26 Latin American countries, running merchant acquiring, the ATH debit network and ATH Movil, card and payment processing, and core banking outsourcing for banks, merchants and governments. The cash-cow-narrative case is the gap between cash economics and the reported line. FCF was $203.7 million, an 11.2% yield on the $1.8 billion market cap; adjusted EBITDA margin runs near 40%; the share count fell 6.6% with $47.1 million repurchased in Q2 at $24.68 and the authorization raised to $150 million; and 2026 guidance was raised to $1,085-1,095 million of revenue and $3.94-4.04 of adjusted EPS, about 7.7x the screen's $30.57 price. One insider bought 16,202 shares at $26.42 in June. Biggest risk is that the adjustments are hiding real costs: Q2 GAAP net income fell to $5.4 million ($0.09) from $40.5 million ($0.62) on an equity-method impairment, cybersecurity incident response, acquisition integration, higher acquired-intangible amortization and higher interest, while net debt stands at $961.2 million, 5.2x EBIT. Business Solutions revenue is also contracting on the 10% discount granted to Popular.
