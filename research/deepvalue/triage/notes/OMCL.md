# OMCL — OMNICELL, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

Omnicell sells medication management automation to hospitals and health systems: connected dispensing devices (XT Series, Titan XT), central pharmacy robotics and IV compounding, plus technical services and a growing SaaS and Expert Services stream including specialty pharmacy and 340B. About 90% of revenue is United States. The half-year looks good: Q2 revenue rose 7% to $312 million, non-GAAP EBITDA rose to $67 million from $38 million, and full-year non-GAAP EBITDA guidance was raised to $175-185 million with non-GAAP EPS of $2.15-2.30, on $292 million of cash against $168 million of debt. The edge type is none. First, the quarter contained a $15 million IEEPA tariff refund that flowed through cost of revenues into GAAP net income, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP EBITDA and cash flow. Second, the same release cut the forward-looking metrics: product bookings guidance is $425-560 million against $535 million actually booked in 2025 and $558 million in 2024, and ARR guidance was updated because certain growth opportunities are now expected to take longer to develop. Third, GAAP earnings barely exist: CY2025 EBIT was $5.2 million on $1.2 billion of revenue, hence 283.8x EV/EBIT and 0.3% ROIC. The biggest risk is the hardware cycle: the MD&A says XT Series volumes fell because the company is largely through the replacement cycle. Price 34.82, 32.2% off the high. Insiders sold 13,083 shares and bought none. Score capped at 5 for edge type none.
