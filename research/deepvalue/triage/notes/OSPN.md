# OSPN — OneSpan Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 5/10

Biggest risk: revenue was exactly flat in 2025 and Cybersecurity revenue fell 7% year over year in Q2 as hardware declines offset software, so the segment margin may be harvesting a shrinking base.
