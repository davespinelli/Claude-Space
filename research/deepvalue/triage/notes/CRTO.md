# CRTO — Criteo S.A. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

Criteo sells digital advertising through two segments: Performance Media, the legacy retargeting and AdTech supply business, $1,681.0 million of 2025 revenue and flat to declining for three years, and Retail Media, $263.9 million, where brands buy inventory on retailer sites. The consolidated line hides the split. Criteo also became OpenAI's first advertising technology partner in March 2026, with over 2,000 brands on ChatGPT.
