# GWRS — Global Water Resources, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Global Water Resources owns and operates thirty-nine regulated water, wastewater and recycled water utility systems around metropolitan Phoenix and Tucson, serving 69,429 active connections under Arizona Corporation Commission rate regulation, with 87.3% of connections in the GW-Santa Cruz and GW-Palo Verde service area. Growth is organic connection additions of about 2.6% annualized plus the July 2025 purchase of seven Tucson Water systems for $8.1 million. The apparent edge is insider buying: Form 4 shows Jonathan Levine buying 651,618 shares for $5.77 million on 2026-08-20. That is not open-market conviction I can rely on, because an 8-K dated 2026-08-21 discloses a securities purchase agreement for an issuance made on August 20, so the purchase most plausibly reflects a negotiated placement, and share count is already up 4.8% year over year. The valuation does not support a look regardless: the screen row (2026-09-05 pack, $8.92) shows 53.6x EV/EBIT, 17.7x net debt to EBIT and negative $47.2 million free cash flow on $67.3 million of capex. Biggest risk is that rate relief lags the capital program, and the pending settlement is worth only about $1.9 million net.
