# OEC — Orion S.A. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

Orion makes carbon black in two segments: Specialty Carbon Black for coatings, polymers and printing, and the larger Rubber Carbon Black sold to tire manufacturers, with formulaic oil-price pass-through in its contracts. Q2 2026 net sales were $500.9M, up 7%, and adjusted EBITDA was $58.2M, up 26% sequentially. The cyclical-trough case is that the depressed leg is identifiable and policy-supported: Rubber segment adjusted EBITDA fell 61% year over year on softer tire build rates, elevated imports and surplus channel inventories, while management states the segment has seen no meaningful benefit yet from recently implemented EU duties and US Section 232 tariffs that it expects to support local tire manufacturing. Specialty adjusted EBITDA rose 96% at the same time, and cost savings are on track for a $20M full-year benefit. The biggest risk is the balance sheet against a $337.4M market cap at the $5.97 screen price, date not stated in the pack: the release reports net debt of $961M at 4.4x trailing adjusted EBITDA with only $178M of liquidity, and the CFO calls debt reduction the most important financial priority. Treat the screen's 16.2% FCF yield as unusable, since management guides FY2026 free cash flow to a range of negative $10M to positive $20M, with adjusted EBITDA of $170M to $210M and $90M of capex. Q2 free cash flow was $2M. No insider open-market activity in twelve months.
