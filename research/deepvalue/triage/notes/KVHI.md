# KVHI — KVH INDUSTRIES INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 6/10

The risk is that this growth earns nothing: Q2 adjusted EBITDA rose only $0.3M to $3.0M on that 27% revenue gain, FY25 EBIT was negative $11.2M, and KVH resells the LEO network that is destroying its own VSAT base.
