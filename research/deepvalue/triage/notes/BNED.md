# BNED — Barnes & Noble Education, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Barnes & Noble Education runs 1,116 physical and virtual campus bookstores for colleges and K-12 institutions and is one of the largest textbook wholesalers. Its BNC First Day programs bill required course materials to the school rather than the student: First Day revenue rose 28.0% to $760.1M in fiscal 2026, with First Day Complete at 232 stores covering about 1.25 million students, up 31%. The edge is that trailing figures still carry the old capital structure. Against a $485.2M enterprise value the reiterated fiscal 2027 guide of $85-92M Adjusted EBITDA is about 5.5x, with fall 2026 First Day Complete enrollment expected up 23%. Risk: 2.1% GAAP operating margin on $1.7B of revenue, First Day cash collection lagging revenue, and $1.03M of insider selling with no buys.
