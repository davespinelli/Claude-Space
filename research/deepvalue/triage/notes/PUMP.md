# PUMP — ProPetro Holding Corp. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 5/10

ProPetro is a Permian Basin completions company, roughly 73% hydraulic fracturing with wireline alongside, that in December 2024 launched PROPWR, a mobile natural gas power generation business selling behind-the-meter capacity to oil and gas, industrial and data center customers. PROPWR is the segment the consolidated numbers obscure: about 350 megawatts is now committed under contract, over 100 megawatts more is in advanced negotiation, and assets are running at a Midwest hyperscaler site. The completions side is at a cyclical low, with FY25 adjusted EBITDA down 26% to $208.4 million and Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $45 million on $306 million of revenue. I am not scoring this higher because the market has already priced the story and insiders have used it: the stock is up 130.1% on 12-1 momentum, and Form 4s show 16,706,527 shares sold for $277.7 million over twelve months with zero open-market purchases. Share count rose 18.1% and the company issued $690 million of 0.00% convertible notes in May 2026 to fund the build. Biggest risk is that PROPWR capex outruns contracted revenue while frac pricing stays soft, leaving $764.9 million of debt against a business earning $6.3 million of FY25 EBIT.
