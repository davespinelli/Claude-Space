# RAIL — FreightCar America, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 7/10

The edge type is cyclical-trough: FY2025 backlog had fallen to 1,926 units worth $137 million, but the Q2 2026 release reports backlog of 3,972 units worth $344 million, up 121% sequentially, on roughly 45% of industry new-railcar orders, with Aftermarket revenue up 13% and $12 million of annualised savings from the Castaños realignment beginning in Q3. Meanwhile the screen row shows the stock down 46.7% over six months and 52.6% off its high, at a $229.9 million cap and $264.7 million EV against guided FY26 Adjusted EBITDA of $36-44 million. The biggest risk is that the guide requires a large second-half ramp: Q2 gross margin was 5.5% versus 15.0% and Adjusted EBITDA was $1.2 million versus $9.3 million, with deliveries slipping into 2027. No insider open-market buying at all.
