# LZB — LA-Z-BOY INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

La-Z-Boy is a vertically integrated residential furniture maker and retailer: a Wholesale segment manufacturing recliners, sofas and motion furniture largely in four US plants and three Mexican facilities, and a Retail segment of 230 company-owned stores inside a 378-store network, plus the direct-to-consumer Joybird brand. Earnings are depressed by a downturn management calls a continued challenging backdrop for the industry: fiscal 2026 EBIT was $129.2 million on $2.1 billion of revenue, Q1 fiscal 2027 sales fell 3% to $476 million, adjusted operating margin fell to 3.9% from 4.8% on wholesale volume deleverage, and GAAP operating income was negative on plant-closure charges. The trough signal is Retail: written sales rose 16%, written same-store sales turned positive at 3%, a 500 basis point sequential improvement, and retail adjusted operating margin expanded to 6.5%. The balance sheet carries $267 million of cash and no external debt. The risk is that the turn does not come: Joybird written sales fell 17% and Q1 free cash flow was an outflow of $7.6 million.
