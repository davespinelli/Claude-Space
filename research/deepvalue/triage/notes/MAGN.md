# MAGN — Magnera Corp · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: spin-off · Score 7/10

Magnera makes nonwoven and specialty materials for consumer end markets: components for diapers, adult incontinence and feminine care, wipes, protective apparel, tea bags and coffee filters, air filtration and building products, from 45 plants across the Americas and Europe for more than 1,000 customers. It exists in this form only since November 2024, when Berry's health, hygiene and specialties business merged with Glatfelter and Berry holders took 90%, which is the edge. The screen shows EV/EBIT of 413.0x and ROIC of 0.1% because GAAP operating income was $5M on $3.2B of revenue after $186M of LTM depreciation and $82M of integration charges. The release gives LTM Adjusted EBITDA of $372M and a pro forma $405M including $35M of Project CORE and merger synergies, against a $2.1B EV, and the 10-K guides fiscal 2026 free cash flow of $90M to $110M against a $443.9M market cap. Biggest risk is the leverage that creates the asymmetry: $1.9B of debt at roughly 4.3x LTM Adjusted EBITDA, $141M of annual interest, and organic volumes that fell 2% to 3% in fiscal 2025 and rose only 1% in the June quarter.
