# RPD — Rapid7, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Rapid7 sells integrated security operations software, the Command Platform spanning vulnerability management, SIEM, cloud security and managed detection and response, to over 11,500 customers in 150 countries including 36% of the Fortune 100. Recurring revenue was 96% of the $859.8M FY2025 total, with no customer above 1%. The post-restructuring case is specific and dated: a Nomination and Support Agreement with JANA Partners on 2026-03-26, Wael Mohamed appointed CEO effective 2026-06-01, four directors resigning on 2026-08-27, and a Q2 2026 plan cutting roughly 12% of the workforce for a $10M to $11M charge. Management guides FY2026 to $129M to $133M of non-GAAP operating income and roughly $130M of free cash flow against a $773.1M market cap at the $11.47 screen price, date not stated in the pack. The biggest risk is that the top line is still eroding: Q2 revenue fell 1.5%, ARR fell 2.0% to $824.0M and Q3 ARR is guided to about $812M, so the cost reset is being applied to a shrinking base. Heed two data gaps. The screen shows DEBT DATA MISSING and the release confirms convertible senior notes exist, so the negative $425.6M net debt is not a verified figure; total cash and government securities were $702.6M. And FY2025 stock-based compensation of $104.3M dwarfs GAAP operating income of $11.6M, so free cash flow overstates economic earnings.
