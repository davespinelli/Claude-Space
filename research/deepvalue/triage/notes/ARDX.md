# ARDX — ARDELYX, INC. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: fallen-growth · Score 5/10

Ardelyx is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company selling two branded forms of tenapanor, a first-in-class NHE3 inhibitor it discovered: IBSRELA for irritable bowel syndrome with constipation in adults, and XPHOZAH to reduce serum phosphorus in adults with chronic kidney disease on dialysis, with a Phase 3 CIC trial (ACCEL) reading out in the second half of 2027 and RDX10531 in IND-enabling studies. The edge candidate is fallen-growth: Q2 2026 product revenue was $118.1M, up 31% year over year and the largest quarter in company history, with IBSRELA up 33% to $86.2M and XPHOZAH up 27% to $31.9M, while the shares are 53.6% off the 52-week high and down 40.8% over six months. The biggest risk is that the reset in expectations looks warranted rather than mistaken: management cut full-year IBSRELA guidance to $350M to $370M, pulled XPHOZAH long-term guidance outright, cited significantly increased payor utilization management, lost its CMS reimbursement appeal on June 26, 2026 after XPHOZAH was moved into the ESRD Prospective Payment System (which already cut 2025 XPHOZAH sales 36% to $103.6M), and still guides opex below $500M against that revenue. Long-term debt rose to $251.8M after a further $50M SLR draw in June, insiders sold 249,625 shares over 12 months with no open-market buys, and profitability is a 2027 claim, not a result.
