# MNRO — MONRO, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 4/10

Monro operates 1,115 company-run automotive service and tire stores plus 47 franchised locations, selling tires, brakes, batteries, alignments and scheduled maintenance to retail customers. The edge type is post-restructuring: the company closed 145 underperforming stores in fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2027 Q1 operating expenses fell to 33.7% of sales from 37.5%, helped by $17.8M of lower store closing costs and $3.7M less consultant spend on the operational improvement plan. The problem is that the reset is not showing up in earnings. Q1 fiscal 2027 sales fell 4.6% to $287.1M, comparable store sales fell 1.7% with tires and brakes down 1% and maintenance down 5%, gross margin fell 50 basis points on higher occupancy, and adjusted operating income collapsed to $2.2M, or 0.8% of sales, from $14.0M, or 4.7%. The company reported a net loss and declined to give fiscal 2027 guidance. The biggest risk is the dividend: $0.28 per quarter on 31.3M shares consumes most of the $38.8M of trailing free cash flow while the share count grew 4.3% and net debt sits at 4.9x EBIT.
