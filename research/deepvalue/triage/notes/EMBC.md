# EMBC — Embecta Corp. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: insider-buying · Score 5/10

Embecta is the diabetes injection business separated from Becton Dickinson in 2022: pen needles, insulin syringes and safety injection devices that the company estimates are used by more than 30 million people in over 100 countries, sold through wholesalers and distributors into retail and institutional channels. The one clean signal in the pack is insider buying. Four distinct insiders bought 115,000 shares for $557,945 over the trailing twelve months with zero open-market sales, the largest being Robert Hombach at 45,000 shares at $4.97 on August 12, 2026, against a $5.69 share price. The company also repaid roughly $53 million of debt and repurchased $9 million of stock in the June quarter and raised full-year adjusted operating margin and EPS guidance. The risk is that this is a levered stub over a shrinking asset: Q3 US revenue fell 24.6%, pen needles fell 18.2%, adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 44.3% to 31.5%, equity is negative $612.7 million, and $1.1 billion of net debt sits against a $322.4 million market cap. The 10-K names GLP-1s, weekly insulin and pumps as reducing demand.
