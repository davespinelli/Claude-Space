# DAKT — DAKTRONICS INC /SD/ · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

Daktronics designs and manufactures LED video displays, scoreboards and message displays for stadiums, commercial venues and transportation, selling through five segments and running a vertically integrated model from engineering through installation and service. FY2026 was a record year: sales up 10.9% to $838.7 million, orders up 10.2% to $860.8 million, operating margin up 290bp to 7.3%, EPS of $0.92 against a prior-year loss, backlog of $356.2 million, and the Live Events unit winning five of five MLB stadium installations since Q3 FY2025. The balance sheet holds $154.6 million of cash against $9.4 million of long-term debt. The trouble is price: the screen row (universe_under2b.csv, 2026-09-04) shows $19.48 for a $940.6 million cap at 13.1x EV/EBIT and only a 3.6% FCF yield, because $49.2 million of operating cash flow was consumed by a $25.8 million receivables build and $14.9 million of capex. The biggest risk is tariffs, which the MD&A says have already hurt gross margins and federally funded project demand; potential IEEPA refunds exist but the company says eligibility, timing and amount cannot be estimated, so no value can be claimed for them here.
