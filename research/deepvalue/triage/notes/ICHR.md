# ICHR — ICHOR HOLDINGS, LTD. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

Ichor designs and manufactures gas and chemical fluid delivery subsystems and precision components that semiconductor OEMs outsource, with Lam Research, Applied Materials and ASML its largest customers and over 90% of FY25 sales from semiconductor capital equipment. The edge type is cyclical-trough, and the pack quantifies the turn: Q2 2026 revenue was $294.8M, up 15% sequentially and 24% year over year, gross margin rose from 11.3% to 13.9%, GAAP EPS turned positive at $0.03, and Q3 is guided to $315-345M with non-GAAP EPS of $0.40-0.50 against management's claim of customer visibility into 2027. The biggest risk is that this is a thin-margin assembler whose recovery is being funded by shareholders rather than by cash: FY25 was a $52.8M GAAP net loss at a 9.3% gross margin, operating cash flow was negative $18.8M in the first half on a $58.9M inventory build, and the share count is up 9.1% after a $200M at-the-market offering priced at an average $80.70, versus the $52.82 screen price. Insiders sold $5.6M with no buys, and the $2.0B cap is roughly 29x an annualized Q3 non-GAAP EPS.
