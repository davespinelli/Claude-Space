# WWW — WOLVERINE WORLD WIDE INC /DE/ · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: fallen-growth · Score 6/10

Wolverine Worldwide designs and markets footwear and apparel through an Active Group led by Merrell and Saucony, a Work Group led by the Wolverine boot brand, and Sweaty Betty in activewear, selling wholesale, internationally and direct-to-consumer. The fallen-growth case is that the shares are 37.6% off their 52-week high while the business is accelerating: second-quarter revenue rose 6.8% to $506.4M with Merrell up 11.1% and Saucony up 9.9%, adjusted operating margin improved 80bp to 10.0%, adjusted diluted EPS rose 14.3%, net debt fell 22% to $443M, inventory fell 17%, and management raised full-year guidance to $1.980-$2.000B of revenue and $1.55-$1.65 of adjusted EPS, roughly twelve times the current $19.55 price. The single biggest risk named in the pack is tariffs compressing the gross line: gross margin fell 70bp to 46.5% in the quarter on higher US tariffs, only partly offset by price increases, and full-year gross margin is guided down 40bp. Two brands are also shrinking, with Sweaty Betty down 2.4% and the Work Group down 1.6%. Share count rose 1.0% and insiders sold $1,030,340 with no purchases.
