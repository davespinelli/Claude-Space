# TRS — TRIMAS CORP · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 7/10

What remains of TriMas after the March 2026 Aerospace sale is two businesses: TriMas Packaging, roughly 82% of sales, making caps, closures, dispensers and pumps for consumer, life sciences and industrial customers under Rieke and related brands, and Specialty Products, mainly Norris Cylinder steel gas cylinders. The balance sheet is the case. Aerospace went to Blackstone and Tinicum for about $1.5B, netting roughly $1.2B after tax, leaving $1,242.5M of cash against $396.9M of debt, so net cash of $845.6M is about 60% of the $1.4B market cap and the operating business carries a $548.1M EV. Management is converting that into share count: more than five million shares repurchased since November 2025, a 11.7% year-over-year reduction, with $76.5M still authorized. Operations are improving off a low base, with Q2 adjusted operating profit up 29.1% to $14.9M and $16.0M of annualized cost-out on track, and full-year adjusted EPS guidance raised at the low end to $1.60 to $1.70. Two risks: that guidance assumes $9M to $10M of interest income per quarter, so a large share of adjusted EPS is the cash pile rather than the business, and the redeployment of $845M into acquisitions is undecided. Q2 free cash flow was negative $12.9M. Insiders were net sellers.
