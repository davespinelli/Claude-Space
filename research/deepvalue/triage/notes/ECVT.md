# ECVT — Ecovyst Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Ecovyst provides regenerated sulfuric acid to refiners making alkylate, plus virgin sulfuric acid and, since June 30, 2026, sulfur dioxide and derivatives for mining, water treatment and food processing through the Calabrian business bought from INEOS. The edge is post-restructuring: the company sold its Advanced Materials and Catalysts business, including the Zeolyst joint ventures, on December 31, 2025, so the screen's CY2025 figures, a $71.1M net loss, $64.9M of EBIT, 23.5x EV/EBIT and 5.2% ROIC, describe a company that no longer exists. On the continuing base, Q2 2026 sales rose 42% to $250.0M, adjusted EBITDA rose 27% to $53.1M, and management raised full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance to $195-207M, against an enterprise value of roughly $1.5B. The stock is 31.8% below its 52-week high. Two things to check in a deep dive: about $55M of the sales increase is pass-through of higher sulfur costs rather than real growth, and leverage is understated here, since the pack's $405.2M net debt predates both the June 30, 2026 credit agreement and the Calabrian purchase. A new CFO took over August 24, 2026. Insiders sold 8,450 shares with no purchases.
