# AIRS — Airsculpt Technologies, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 4/10

AirSculpt performs premium minimally invasive body contouring (fat removal, fat transfer, skin tightening, cellulite treatment) through 31 centers across 20 US states and Canada, operating as a holding company that provides management services to surgeon-owned professional associations. The edge candidate is post-restructuring: the company closed its London facility, cut 2025 SG&A by $16.7M, reduced gross debt by roughly $30M to $44.2M since the start of 2025 while lifting cash to $18.8M, and same-center case volume grew 1.0% in Q2 and 1.1% year to date, a second consecutive quarter of stabilisation after 2025 revenue fell 15.8%. One insider bought 260,000 shares for $729k across eight open-market purchases with no sales. The biggest risk is that the stabilisation is not translating into cash: the company reduced its 2026 adjusted EBITDA outlook to $12M to $14M and put revenue at the low end of $151M to $157M, Q2 operating income was negative, operating cash flow was negative $1.2M in the quarter, and enterprise value of $197.4M against that guided EBITDA is roughly 15x, which is not a deep-value price. Financing is tight: only $5.0M of revolver capacity, an August 2026 term loan amendment that extends maturity only to November 2027 while requiring $2.5M payments at signing and by September 30 plus a sweep of 50% of future equity proceeds, and an at-the-market program that raised $5.0M in the quarter and helped push share count up 15.5%. Revenue per case is also falling, down 2.0% on a same-center basis.
