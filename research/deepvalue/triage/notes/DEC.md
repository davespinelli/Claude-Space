# DEC — Diversified Energy Co · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 4/10

Diversified Energy buys and operates mature, shallow-decline natural gas, NGL and oil wells, over 69,000 net productive wells across Appalachia and the Central region, and runs its own well-plugging business. Rather than drill, it acquires producing assets and funds them with long-dated, fully amortising, non-recourse asset-backed notes. The cash-cow story is what the screen sees: Q2 adjusted EBITDA of $240 million, adjusted free cash flow of $115 million, $136 million returned year to date including $93 million of buybacks, a 25.2% FCF yield, and a market that dislikes conventional gas and well retirement. The biggest risk is the other side of that balance sheet. Total debt rose from $1.7 billion at end-2024 to $3.0 billion at end-2025, asset retirement obligations are $864 million discounted and $3.6 billion undiscounted, against a $1.1B market cap, and leverage is 2.45x with 76% of debt in ABS notes. Growth was bought with stock: 21.2 million shares for Maverick, 3.7 million for Canvas, 8.5 million sold in February. Insiders sold 2.1 million shares and bought none, and the Chairman resigned in August.
