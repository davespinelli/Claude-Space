# ANAB — AnaptysBio, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: spin-off · Score 5/10

After separating First Tracks Bio on 20 April 2026, AnaptysBio is no longer a clinical-stage biotech but a vehicle that manages two out-licensed royalty streams: Jemperli with GSK and imsidolimab with Vanda. Management describes the remaining company as requiring limited headcount and running above a 95% EBIT margin, and has authorised a $100M buyback. Edge is spin-off, and the trailing figures are meaningless for it: FY25 collaboration revenue of $234.6M included $125M of milestones, and the Q1 2026 net loss of $52.9M still consolidates the spun business. The pack's DEBT DATA MISSING warning is justified: the balance sheet carries a $263.7M liability related to the sale of future royalties that the screen tagged as zero, so the stated $248.5M net cash and $1.4B EV are wrong, and stockholders' equity is only $12.7M. Biggest risk is that near-term royalties are pledged: roughly $325M of non-recourse monetisation is not expected to be paid down until the end of Q2 2027, so the $1.7B market cap rests on GSK's peak-sales guidance, a company claim, after the stock rose 266% over twelve months.
