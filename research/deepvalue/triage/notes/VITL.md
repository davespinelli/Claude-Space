# VITL — Vital Farms, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

Vital Farms is a Certified B Corporation selling pasture-raised shell eggs, hard-boiled eggs and liquid whole eggs sourced from 625 small family farms into more than 23,500 stores, and is the leading US brand of pasture-raised eggs by retail dollar sales. The trough is visible and quantified: first-quarter 2026 gross margin fell to 28.3% from 38.5% and adjusted EBITDA to $5.0M from $27.5M, driven by an oversupply of egg inventory that pushed volume into breaker and wholesale channels at lower prices, cutting gross profit by an estimated $4.9M, while full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance of $0-$10M absorbs roughly $32M of oversupply costs. Volume itself still grew $34.7M. The balance sheet supports waiting: $51.4M of cash and marketable securities with no outstanding debt at quarter-end, capex cut to $70-75M from $140-150M, 3.9% of shares retired, and four insiders buying with no sales. The risk is that this is competitive, not cyclical: the pressure came from pricing and promotion by other outdoor-access eggs, which would mean permanent premium erosion. The pack's latest results are Q1; Q2 is not included.
