# NTGR — NETGEAR, INC. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 7/10

NETGEAR sells networking hardware, software and subscriptions through two segments: Enterprise, meaning ProAV managed switches, wireless LAN, cloud management and security for small and medium businesses, and Consumer, meaning WiFi mesh routers and mobile hotspots sold through retail and service providers. The consolidated loss hides the mix. In Q2 2026 Enterprise revenue grew 7.7% to $89.0 million at an all-time-high 54.1% non-GAAP gross margin and a 25.9% contribution margin, the best in seven years, and now represents more than half of revenue and about 69% of non-GAAP gross profit, while Consumer revenue fell 9.4% to $79.6 million at a negative 2.2% contribution margin. Enterprise alone annualises to roughly $356 million of revenue against an enterprise value of $398.9 million, with $174.3 million of cash inside a $573.2 million market cap, a 6.3% share count reduction, $116 million repurchased since 2024 and $75 million left authorised. The risk is that Consumer keeps consuming the profit before it reaches the shareholder: Q3 GAAP operating margin is guided to negative 12.0% to negative 9.0%, memory costs are a 200 basis point second-half gross margin headwind, and CY2025 free cash flow was negative $18.9 million. The DEBT DATA MISSING warning applies, so the net cash figure is unverified.
