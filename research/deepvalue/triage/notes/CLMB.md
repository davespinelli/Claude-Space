# CLMB — Climb Global Solutions, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

Climb is a value-added IT distributor. Its Distribution segment, 96% of net sales, sells software licenses, maintenance contracts and drop-shipped hardware from emerging vendors to resellers and integrators in North America and Europe, and a small Solutions segment resells directly to end users. The model is capital-light with 33.7% ROIC, $56.6M of cash and no debt drawn on its $50M revolver. Top-line growth is real, with Q2 gross billings up 17% to $587.3M and gross profit up 15% to $30.2M, helped by the February 2026 Interworks acquisition and by Darktrace reaching the top-20 vendor list within a year. What is missing is the earnings follow-through that would justify 16.1x EV/EBIT and a 3.2% FCF yield. Q2 net income fell to $5.5M from $6.0M and adjusted EBITDA was flat at $11.3M against $11.4M, as SG&A rose 26% and effective margin fell to 37.5% from 43.3%. The board also suspended the quarterly dividend from Q1 2026 to preserve flexibility, an odd move for a net-cash balance sheet. Operating cash flow of $16.6M against $21.3M of 2025 net income reflects a $295M receivable book. No insider open-market activity, no transcript.
