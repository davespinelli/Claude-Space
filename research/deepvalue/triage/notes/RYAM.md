# RYAM — RAYONIER ADVANCED MATERIALS INC. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

RYAM is a pulp producer in two segments: High Purity Cellulose, which makes cellulose specialties for demanding industrial applications plus commodity cellulose and biomaterials, and Paperboard and High Yield Pulp. The trough case is visible in the sequential numbers. Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations rose to $40 million in Q2 from $8 million in Q1 and was up 43% year over year, net sales rose 18% sequentially to $376 million, and cellulose specialties pricing was up 21% year over year on newly negotiated 2026 contracts with volumes up 19% sequentially. On top of that the board is running a strategic review under a new CEO, with a stated intention to conclude it and communicate a path forward in the fourth quarter, and management expects positive free cash flow in 2026. The risk is that the balance sheet does not leave much room to wait: consolidated net secured leverage is 4.2 times covenant EBITDA, global liquidity is only $145 million including $57 million of cash, and the Paperboard and High Yield Pulp segment lost $27 million in the quarter on a 10% price decline against new US capacity, taking a $13 million impairment. Enterprise value is $1.3 billion against a $579.0 million market cap.
