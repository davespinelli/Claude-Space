# EVH — Evolent Health, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

Evolent manages specialty care for health plans in oncology, cardiology and musculoskeletal, selling a capitated Performance Suite, a fee-based Specialty Technology and Services Suite and administrative services, priced per member per month. The post-restructuring case is that CY2025 (revenue down 26.6%, EBIT -$410.1M, net loss -$579.4M) predates a reset: Evolent Care Partners was sold for $100M in December 2025, the 2025 notes were refinanced into 2031 notes, $40M of stock was repurchased and share count is down 3.8%, and Performance Suite PMPM has been repriced from $13.76 to $24.05, up 75% year over year, with new contracts carrying what management calls enhanced contractual protections. Q2 revenue was $652.5M against $444.3M, 2026 guidance is $2.6B to $2.7B of revenue and $120M to $135M of adjusted EBITDA, and a $300M annualized oncology partnership is due to launch by December. The risk is that the repricing has not yet fixed unit economics: medical expense ratio was 95.3% against 80.0%, adjusted EBITDA fell year over year despite 47% revenue growth, and net debt of $850.8M is roughly 6.7x the guidance midpoint against a $481.6M market cap.
