# EBS — Emergent BioSolutions Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Emergent sells medical countermeasures for public health threats, mainly anthrax (BioThrax, CYFENDUS), smallpox and mpox (ACAM2000, TEMBEXA, VIGIV) and botulism antitoxin, almost all to the US government, plus NARCAN and KLOXXADO naloxone nasal sprays and a bioservices CDMO arm. The setup is a levered equity stub against a resetting cost base: $319.4M market cap on $761.5M EV, 7.6x EV/EBIT, and a 49.1% FCF yield on CY2025 free cash flow of $156.8M. Q2 2026 revenue rose 66% to $234.3M with adjusted EBITDA of $96.5M at a 41% margin, H1 adjusted EBITDA was $132.0M, and management is cutting about 90 roles for roughly $40M of annualized savings while shrinking the share count 3.9%. The risk is that both revenue legs are impaired or lumpy: naloxone fell 43% in 2025 and 22% in Q2, drawing a $191.3M non-cash NARCAN impairment, while management attributes the Q2 beat to accelerated USG contract modifications, which is timing. The April 2026 refinancing into a $150M OrbiMed facility is not a cheap-capital signal. Insiders sold 64,925 shares and bought none. No transcript in the pack.
