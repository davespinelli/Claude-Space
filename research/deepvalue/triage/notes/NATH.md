# NATH — NATHANS FAMOUS, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Nathan's licenses its hot dog brand, sells Nathan's products to foodservice operators through its Branded Product Program, and franchises and operates a small restaurant system, 221 franchised units plus a handful of company-owned locations. The economics are concentrated in licensing: product licensing produced $13.5 million of the $12.7 million total quarterly operating income (Branded Product contributed $1.3 million, restaurants $0.9 million, corporate cost -$3.1 million), with royalties from Smithfield Foods alone at $33.6 million in fiscal 2026. There is no edge to work with, because the price is set by a deal rather than by the business: on January 20, 2026 Nathan's agreed to be acquired by Smithfield Foods for $102.00 per share in cash, and the screen price of $98.15 sits 3.9% below that, so this is merger arbitrage on CFIUS clearance and a shareholder vote, not a value question. Biggest risk if the deal breaks is the operating trend underneath it: Branded Product operating income fell to $1.3 million from $2.276 million as beef and beef trimmings cost rose 22%, restaurant sales fell, and licensing royalties were flat because a 15% price increase was offset by a 13% retail volume decline.
