# DFIN — Donnelley Financial Solutions, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 7/10

DFIN sells SEC compliance and regulatory software and services to public companies, private issuers and investment companies: ActiveDisclosure and Arc Suite for recurring filings, Venue for deal data rooms, plus tech-enabled filing services and a shrinking print and distribution business. It was spun out of R.R. Donnelley in 2016. The edge is misunderstood-segment. Consolidated FY2025 revenue fell 1.9% to $767.0M, but that nets software solutions up 8.7% to $358.4M against print down 16.1% to $110.3M; in Q2 2026 software reached a record $99.4M, 44.3% of sales, with ActiveDisclosure up about 29%, and adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 170bp to 36.7%. GAAP FY2025 net earnings of $32.4M are depressed by an $82.8M non-cash pension settlement charge while income from operations rose to $141.1M, so trailing GAAP earnings understate the business. Net leverage is 0.7x and the share count is down 10.6% year over year at 9.8x EV/EBIT. Biggest risk: revenue is still shrinking overall and Q3 guidance implies $175-185M of sales at a 26-28% margin, so growth depends on transactional capital markets volumes the company does not control.
