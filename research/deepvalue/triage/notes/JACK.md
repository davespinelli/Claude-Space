# JACK — JACK IN THE BOX INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Jack in the Box operates and franchises 2,115 hamburger restaurants across 25 states, Mexico and Guam, earning company restaurant margins plus franchise royalties and rent. The edge type is post-restructuring. Del Taco was sold in December 2025 and is in discontinued operations; in Q3 fiscal 2026 the company prepaid $110.0M of its 2019-1 notes using excess COLI funding and refinanced $500M of new 2026-1 Class A-2 notes out to May 2031; and the "JACK on Track" plan is closing blocks of restaurants and selling real estate. Guidance for fiscal 2026 is $225M-$230M of adjusted EBITDA against capex of only $45M-$55M, and trailing free cash flow of $74.1M is a 25.5% yield on a $290.8M market cap. The equity is a small stub on a large asset base: 19.2M shares at $15.16 against $1.5B of net debt and negative $901.3M of book equity, so modest changes in EBITDA move it hard. The biggest risk is that same-store sales are guided to a low single digit decline for the year, and at roughly 6.5x net debt to guided EBITDA the dividend and buyback are already discontinued.
