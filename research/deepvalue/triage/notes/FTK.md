# FTK — FLOTEK INDUSTRIES INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 4/10

Flotek runs two segments: Chemistry Technologies, which formulates and sells oilfield specialty chemistry, largely to related party ProFrac Services under a long-term supply agreement, and Data Analytics, which rents and sells real-time gas measurement equipment and, since the April 2025 PWRtek asset acquisition, mobile power generation. The edge type is misunderstood-segment: Data Analytics revenue rose 223% to $19.2 million in Q2 with 63% from external customers, and it produced $12 million of gross profit, 51% of company total against 26% a year earlier, overtaking Chemistry for the first time. Full-year guidance was raised to $340-350 million revenue and $47-51 million adjusted EBITDA, excluding any contribution from the announced 10-year, $400 million Puerto Rico power services contract. The problem is price and quality. At $23.74 the enterprise value is $895.5 million, roughly 18x the midpoint of guided adjusted EBITDA, after a 213.7% twelve-month run. Related party revenue was $146.8 million of $237.3 million in CY2025 and related-party receivables are $70.7 million. Contract Shortfall Fees of $27.4 million in 2025 and $32.4 million in 2024 are liquidated damages, not product sales, and the 2025 amount was settled as $7.2 million cash plus a $12.5 million equipment credit. CY2025 net income of $30.5 million included a $10.9 million tax benefit against just $7.2 million of operating cash flow, and the share count rose 21.3%. Insiders sold 18,853 shares and bought none.
