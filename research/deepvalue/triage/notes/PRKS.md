# PRKS — United Parks & Resorts Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 5/10

United Parks operates SeaWorld, Busch Gardens, Discovery Cove and related theme parks, earning admission and in-park spending per guest across a seasonal event calendar. The edge type is cash-cow-narrative: the market dislikes an attendance story while the company converts cash and retires stock, with FY2025 free cash flow of $162.6M, an 8.8% FCF yield, and the share count down 17.6% year over year, including 5.9 million shares or 12.1% of the total repurchased in the first half of 2026 for $217.7M. Management calls the shares materially undervalued. The evidence against it is in the same release. First-half attendance fell 3.6% to 9.3 million, revenue fell 2.0%, adjusted EBITDA fell 7.4% and net income fell 54.4%, with the growth coming only from in-park per capita spending, up 5.1%, while admission per capita fell 1.4%. The biggest risk is the balance sheet behind those buybacks: net debt is $2.2B at 6.1x EBIT with shareholders' equity of negative $617.0M, so a fixed-cost park business with falling attendance is repurchasing stock from a levered position. Insiders sold 6,622 shares and bought none.
