# USPH — U S PHYSICAL THERAPY INC /NV · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

USPH runs 781 outpatient physical therapy clinics in 45 states through majority-owned clinic partnerships where the local therapist keeps a minority stake, plus an industrial injury prevention segment selling onsite injury prevention and testing directly to employers. It is a serial acquirer: fourteen partnership deals in three years plus tuck-ins, and three more in 2026 for $37.6M. The pack does not support a mispricing at 15.7x EV/EBIT. Revenue grew 8.5% in Q2 but adjusted EBITDA was flat at $27.0M versus $26.9M, and $47.2M versus $46.4M year to date, because physical therapy margin fell 170bp to 19.5% and mature revenue grew only 3.5%. Reaffirmed full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance of $102M to $106M therefore requires roughly $55M to $59M in the second half, resting on integrating 39 remaining hospital-affiliated clinics in Q3 and front-desk virtualization. Net debt is $190.6M with facility borrowings up to $221.0M from $161.8M in six months while the company bought back $19.2M of stock at $62.80. Medicare rates are set annually by CMS and fell 2.9% in 2025. One insider bought 492 shares.
