# MED — MEDIFAST INC · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 7/10

Medifast sells meal-replacement products and a coach-led weight and metabolic health system (rebranded Trilivy) through a network of independent earning coaches, roughly 90% of whom were clients first. The edge candidate is hidden-asset, and the screen's DEBT DATA MISSING warning resolves in its favour: the June 30 balance sheet carries $71.9M of cash plus $97.9M of investments, $169.8M in total, with total liabilities of $64.0M consisting only of payables and lease obligations and the release stating no debt, against a $140.2M market cap. Cash and investments were nonetheless roughly flat at $167.3M to $169.8M over the six months.
