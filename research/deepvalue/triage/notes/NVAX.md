# NVAX — NOVAVAX INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 4/10

Novavax has become a vaccine technology licensor rather than a commercial vaccine company, monetising its Matrix-M adjuvant and recombinant protein nanoparticle platform through Sanofi (which now leads Nuvaxovid commercialisation, paying high-teens to low-twenties royalties plus up to $350M of milestones), Pfizer, Takeda and Serum Institute, while running an early pipeline including a C. difficile candidate. That is where the case ends. The screen's 4.0x EV/EBIT is an artefact of 2025 one-time milestones, including $175M for the Nuvaxovid BLA approval; second-quarter 2026 revenue fell 76% to $57M and the company lost $53M, with a 2026 adjusted revenue framework of $235-275M against $310-340M of non-GAAP operating expense. Total stockholders' deficit is $190.7M. The biggest risk is that milestone timing, not operations, drives results, and shares are 7.6% off the high.
