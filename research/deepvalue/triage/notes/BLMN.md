# BLMN — Bloomin' Brands, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

Bloomin' Brands operates and franchises more than 1,440 casual and fine dining restaurants across 46 states and 12 countries under Outback Steakhouse, Carrabba's Italian Grill, Bonefish Grill and Fleming's, having sold 67% of its Brazil business in December 2024. The edge type is post-restructuring: a turnaround strategy centred on Outback was announced in November 2025, the dividend was suspended in October 2025 to fund debt paydown and asset refresh, new unit development was slowed, and the numbers have started to move, with Q2 2026 U.S. comparable sales +2.3% (Bonefish +8.1%), restaurant-level margin up 40bp to 12.4%, and full-year adjusted diluted EPS guidance raised to $0.90-$1.00 from $0.75-$0.90 against a $9.73 share price. Two insiders made small open-market purchases and none sold. The biggest risk is the balance sheet against thin margins: net debt of $636.2M is 17.1x the $37.2M of fiscal 2025 EBIT, capex of $179.9M consumes most of the $276.7M operating cash flow, and Q3 is guided to a loss of $0.23-$0.28 per share. Score 6: a genuine inflection with raised guidance, but the shares are already up 69.5% over twelve months and leverage leaves little room.
