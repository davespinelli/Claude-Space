# PACK — Ranpak Holdings Corp. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 5/10

Ranpak makes paper-based protective packaging: void-fill, cushioning and wrapping systems placed with e-commerce and industrial shippers, who then buy the paper consumables, plus a newer end-of-line automation line. The edge type is misunderstood-segment. Automation revenue grew 133.8% in Q2 2026 to $16.6M and management expects nearly $60M for the year, while the consolidated picture stays a net loss of $7.9M; Q2 revenue rose 14.0% to $105.2M and adjusted EBITDA rose 15.8% to $19.1M at an 18.2% margin. The biggest risk is the installed base underneath that growth: total PPS systems fell 2.3% year over year to 141.7 thousand, with cushioning down 2.6% and void-fill down 2.4%, so the consumable annuity is shrinking while automation scales. Leverage compounds it: $395.8M of term debt maturing December 2031 against roughly $76M of annualised adjusted EBITDA, and goodwill and intangibles of $729.6M against $519.9M of equity. The pack flags capex as missing, so the 6.1% FCF yield is operating cash flow, not free cash flow, in a business with $131.6M of net PP&E.
