# EAF — GRAFTECH INTERNATIONAL LTD · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

GrafTech manufactures ultra-high-power graphite electrodes, a consumable essential to electric arc furnace steelmaking, at plants in Calais, Pamplona and Monterrey, and is the only large-scale producer substantially vertically integrated into petroleum needle coke. The cycle is visibly at a trough: 2025 realised price fell 13%, capacity utilisation ran 74% in Q2 2026, and FY25 gross loss was $15.7 million after an $18.3 million lower-of-cost-or-market writedown. The turn is nameable, not hypothetical. Q2 volume rose 8% year over year, full-year volume is guided up 5-10%, and since announcing $600-1,200 per MT increases the company has secured commitments more than 15% above comparable Q1 prices. The balance sheet is why this is not scored higher. Gross debt is $1,225 million against $253 million of total liquidity and negative $345.6 million of equity; interest expense was $104.1 million in 2025 while H1 2026 adjusted EBITDA was negative $11.7 million and adjusted free cash flow negative $102.6 million. Note the screen's -89.9% share change is the 1-for-10 reverse split of 2025-08-29, not a buyback, and an Evercore equity distribution agreement was signed in May 2026. Biggest risk is that pricing recovers too slowly to outrun the burn.
