# SHEN — SHENANDOAH TELECOMMUNICATIONS CO/VA/ · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

Shentel sells broadband, video and voice over fiber and hybrid coax in eight eastern states, plus commercial fiber, dark fiber leasing and a small rural telephone business, serving about 262,000 revenue generating units across roughly 19,800 route miles. The case is a capex cliff the pack states outright: the Glo Fiber construction phase is expected substantially complete by the end of 2026, capital expenditure guidance is $220 to $250 million against $296 million in 2025, a February 2026 reduction in force of about 10% of employees is expected to save $12.3 million annually from 2027, and 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance of $131 to $136 million is up 12.1%. Glo Fiber revenue grew 32.8% in Q2 to $26.3 million, and fiber is now 51% of total revenue. The risk is that the incumbent half is melting while leverage sits on top: incumbent broadband revenue fell 6.0% on a 14.1% decline in video units and a 2.6% fall in data ARPU driven by competitors' rate cards, and net debt of $691.1 million is above five times guided EBITDA with interest expense running $9.7 million a quarter and rising. Trailing free cash flow is negative $257.9 million.
