# CCSI — Consensus Cloud Solutions, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 7/10

Consensus sells secure information delivery, principally digital cloud fax under eFax and related brands, plus interoperability and data extraction tools, to roughly 703 thousand accounts, with healthcare its largest vertical and FedRAMP and HITRUST authorisations behind its public sector business. Consolidated revenue was flat in 2025 at $349.7 million and that flatness is the point: Corporate revenue grew from $170 million in 2021 to $222.7 million in 2025, a 7% compound rate, while SoHo shrank from $162.9 million in 2023 to $127.0 million. The two offset each other in the consolidated line that a screen sees. Q2 2026 showed the crossover, with Corporate up 9.3%, SoHo down 4.7%, total revenue up 4.1%, adjusted EBITDA margin 52.9%, and the buyback authorisation raised to $200 million. The biggest risk is in the same table: Corporate monthly churn rose from 1.49% in 2023 to 2.36% in 2024 to 3.03% in 2025, so the growing segment is retaining worse each year, and adjusted EBITDA was flat year over year despite the revenue gain. No insider bought stock.
