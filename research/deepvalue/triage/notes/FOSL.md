# FOSL — Fossil Group, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 5/10

Fossil designs and distributes fashion watches, jewelry, leathers and sunglasses under owned brands (FOSSIL, SKAGEN, MICHELE, RELIC, ZODIAC) and licensed ones (ARMANI EXCHANGE, DIESEL, EMPORIO ARMANI, MICHAEL KORS, SKECHERS, TORY BURCH), through wholesale, 199 owned stores, e-commerce and third-party distributors across the Americas, Europe and Asia. The edge candidate is post-restructuring: under a CEO appointed in September 2024 the company cut about $100M of SG&A in fiscal 2025, closed 49 stores (19.8% of the base), exited smartwatches, moved smaller markets to distributors, refinanced its revolver and exchanged its 2026 senior notes, and Q2 2026 shows the margin effect, with gross margin up 490 basis points to 62.4% on full-price selling and sourcing changes, constant currency adjusted operating income doubling to $8.6M, and full-year guidance raised to a 4% to 6% adjusted operating margin with positive free cash flow. The biggest risk is that the top line has not turned and the balance sheet is carrying the wait: Q2 net sales still fell 4.9% with Europe down 18.2% and direct-to-consumer down 14.6%, the quarter produced a $10.6M net loss, interest expense rose to $8.3M against $203.0M of total debt and only $96.6M of total liquidity, 2025 operating cash flow was negative $57.9M, share count rose 10.0%, and most product is imported from China into a tariff regime the MD&A itself calls volatile. The shares are up 81.8% over 12 months and only 15.8% off the high, so the turnaround is at least partly priced. Insiders were net sellers of $165k, with two small open-market buys.
