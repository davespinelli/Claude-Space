# CMP — COMPASS MINERALS INTERNATIONAL INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 5/10

Compass Minerals mines and sells essential minerals: highway deicing and consumer and industrial salt from the world's largest rock salt mine at Goderich, Ontario, the UK's Winsford mine and several evaporation plants, plus sulfate of potash specialty fertilizer from Ogden, Utah. The transformation is real and documented: the back-to-basics strategy exited the Fortress fire retardant business and lithium development, cut SG&A by $24.5M, released $117M of inventory, refinanced into 2030 notes, retired $150M of 2027 notes, and took net leverage from 4.3x to 2.8x with an S&P upgrade to B+. Fiscal 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance is $218M to $242M against a $1.7B EV, and management describes the 2026-27 highway bid season as very constructive with substantial year-over-year price improvement. Biggest risk: the screen's 12.1% FCF yield is not repeatable, since it rests on a working capital release, while guided capex of $90M to $110M and interest of $62M to $67M consume most of the EBITDA, and Salt segment Adjusted EBITDA still fell 15% year over year because per-unit mining costs have not improved on plan. No insider buying; the stock is up 58.6% over twelve months.
