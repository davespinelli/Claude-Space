# STRA — Strategic Education, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

Strategic Education runs three segments: US Higher Education, which is Capella and Strayer universities serving 85,306 mostly working adult students, Australia and New Zealand, mainly Torrens University, and Education Technology Services, which sells employer education benefits through Workforce Edge and credit-bearing courses through Sophia Learning. ETS is the segment the consolidated 4.0% revenue growth hides. Its 2025 revenue grew 41.4% to $148.4 million and operating income grew 37.7% to $58.8 million; in Q2 2026 ETS revenue rose 15.4% to $42.4 million at a 46.2% operating margin, with Sophia subscribers up 32% and Workforce Edge holding 81 corporate agreements covering roughly 4,020,000 employees. That sits inside a $1.7 billion enterprise value with $123.8 million of net cash. The risk is the other 88% of revenue. USHE enrollment fell 0.5% and ANZ enrollment fell 5.2% on Australian international-student regulation, ANZ Q2 operating income fell from $12.8 million to $1.0 million after a $13.9 million Fair Work Ombudsman reserve, and Title IV rulemaking is named as an open risk.
