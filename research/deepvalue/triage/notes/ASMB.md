# ASMB — ASSEMBLY BIOSCIENCES, INC. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Assembly Bio is a clinical-stage biotech developing small-molecule antivirals: helicase-primase inhibitors for recurrent genital herpes (licensed to Gilead in December 2025), an oral HDV entry inhibitor ABI-6250 now expanding into cholestatic liver disease, an HBV capsid assembly modulator being shopped to partners, and a preclinical NNPI. All revenue is collaboration revenue from Gilead, a related party. There is no deep-value edge to name: the screen's 153.5% revenue growth is the one-time recognition of $35.0M for the HPI license, and the balance sheet is a burn-funding cash pile rather than a hidden asset. Cash and marketable securities were $320.4M at June 30 against total liabilities of $25.2M, none of it debt, on a $735.6M market cap, but H1 2026 loss from operations was $17.7M and 2025 CFO was -$41.1M. The biggest risk is dilution as the mechanism of funding: shares went from 7,672,261 to 20,360,596 in a year, including a $115M gross raise in May 2026, and runway "into 2029" assumes Gilead pays the $75M extension fee in Q4 2026.
