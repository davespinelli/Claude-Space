# ACVA — ACV Auctions Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

ACV runs a digital wholesale marketplace where dealers and commercial sellers such as captives, banks and fleet companies auction used vehicles, plus attached services: transportation, floorplan lending through ACV Capital, inspection and data products including True360, ClearCar and VIPER. FY2025 revenue rose 19.2% to $759.6M and Q2 2026 revenue was a record $214M, up 10%. The reason to call this `none` rather than an edge is in the same release: marketplace GMV of $2.7B and 211,472 units were both approximately flat year over year, so the growth is take-rate and services rather than volume, and management guides FY2026 to a GAAP net loss of $44M to $49M. The biggest risk is that the adjusted profitability is stock compensation in disguise: the FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guide of $73M to $77M sits against roughly $63M of stock-based compensation excluded from non-GAAP, so the $1.1B enterprise value at the $6.70 screen price, date not stated in the pack, is not supported by GAAP earnings. Trailing EBIT was negative $63.2M. The CFO announced his resignation on 2026-08-06. There were no open-market insider purchases or sales in twelve months, and no transcript, so the VIPER wallet-share claim is unchecked.
