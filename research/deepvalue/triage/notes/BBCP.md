# BBCP — Concrete Pumping Holdings, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 7/10

CPH rents out operated concrete pumps on a time-and-volume fee basis in the US (Brundage-Bone, about 95 branches, 17% share) and UK (Camfaud, about 30% share), plus Eco-Pan, which supplies pans for concrete washout containment. It owns roughly 1,520 units and takes no ownership of the concrete, so there is no fixed-bid or commodity risk. Edge is cyclical-trough. FY25 revenue fell 7.7% to $392.9M on weather and a residential and commercial slowdown, but Q3 FY26 revenue rose 12.6% to $116.8M on data centre and infrastructure work, adjusted EBITDA rose 13.3% to $30.4M, guidance was raised again to $425M to $435M revenue and $103M to $108M adjusted EBITDA, and a $0.52 annual dividend was initiated at a stated 5.6% yield. Management explicitly assumes light commercial and residential do not recover this year, so that part of the cycle is still absent. Against a $456.1M market cap (screen price $9.05, date not shown because price data was skipped), EV of $831.8M is about 8x guided EBITDA. Biggest risk is the $425.0M of fixed 7.5% second-lien notes: leverage is 3.6x, interest ran $31.6M in FY25, and insiders sold $4.25M with zero buys on $1.4M average daily volume.
