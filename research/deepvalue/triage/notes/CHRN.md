# CHRN — ChronoScale Holdings Corp · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 2/10

ChronoScale Holdings is what remains after Applied Digital contributed its cloud subsidiary into Ekso Bionics in a May 2026 business combination, followed by a July 2026 holding company reorganisation and a decision to divest the legacy Ekso exoskeleton business, which is now held for sale. The continuing business rents energised colocation space with company-provided equipment to AI and machine learning customers in Colorado, Minnesota and Utah at fixed rates. No edge is claimable. The operating business is shrinking, not troughing: fiscal 2026 revenue fell 15% to $71.6M on the loss of a single customer in December 2024, the operating loss was $37.5M, the net loss $50.3M, and the cost reductions cited in the MD&A are lease renegotiations and depreciation, not demand. Free cash flow was negative $12.0M and share count rose 36.4% to 3,563,381 after a reverse split undertaken to cure a Nasdaq minimum bid price deficiency. The biggest risk is that the price already embeds a story the filings do not support: the shares are up 452.5% over 12 months, and the only recent 8-K exhibit is a Reg FD Item 7.01 announcing "plans with Microsoft" for a 50 MW deployment, with no contract value, term, financing or start date disclosed, against a company with $9.7M of cash. Book equity of $180.0M against a $71.6M market cap is stated but the pack has no balance sheet to check what it consists of, and no Form 4 summary was fetched, so insider activity is unknown.
