# OSS — ONE STOP SYSTEMS, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

One Stop Systems builds ruggedised enterprise-class compute, data storage and liquid-cooled servers for AI, autonomy and sensor processing at the edge, sold mainly to defence primes, including P-8A storage and combat vehicle vision prototypes, and to a medical imaging OEM. It sold its Bressner distribution subsidiary in December 2025, which is the source of the pack's earnings quality flag: FY25 net income of $5.1M against EBIT of negative $3.4M is the disposal gain, not operations. The pack's DEBT DATA MISSING warning applies, so the apparent net cash is unverified; the release gives cash and short-term investments of $34.4M and working capital of $44.7M at March 31, 2026 but no total debt figure, so I could not confirm the balance sheet. No edge type fits, because the price is not low: a $243.4M market cap against 2026 guidance of 20% to 25% growth on a $32.2M continuing revenue base, roughly 40% gross margin and merely "positive" EBITDA is about 6x forward sales. Q1 adjusted EBITDA was $0.2M. The biggest risk is dilution and mix: shares rose 13.8% year over year, insiders sold $369,380 with no purchases, and revenue depends on a handful of named defence programmes.
