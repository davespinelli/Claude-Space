# NUTX — Nutex Health Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: insider-buying · Score 4/10

Nutex Health runs 27 micro-hospitals, specialty hospitals and hospital outpatient departments across 12 states, plus a smaller population health management division of IPAs. It bills almost entirely out of network, with over 99% of patient service revenue paid by insurers and no negotiated rates, so realised pricing depends on No Surprises Act arbitration: the company says it submits 50 to 60% of claims through IDR and prevails in over 85% of determinations. The one clean positive signal is Form 4 activity, five distinct insiders buying on the open market over twelve months and no open-market sales, though the total is only $148,237. The biggest risk is that the profit is not repeatable. Q2 revenue fell 13.6% to $210.8 million, and operating income tripled only because contract services expense dropped from $61.1 million to $1.1 million on a $52.3 million retroactive HaloMD amendment and a CMS fee cut from $115 to $15. Of 2025 net income, $109.6 million of $180.4 million went to noncontrolling interests in entities partly controlled by the CEO. The stock sits at its 52-week high.
