# XPEL — XPEL, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

XPEL supplies automotive paint protection film, window tint, windshield and architectural films and ceramic coatings to aftermarket installers, dealerships and OEMs, plus its own installation labor and the DAP pattern-cutting SaaS that installers pay monthly to access. It is a good business at a full price rather than a mispricing: 22.4x EV/EBIT, a 4.5% FCF yield, 43.0% 12-month momentum and 7.4% off the 52-week high. Operations are fine, not exceptional. Q2 2026 revenue grew 14.7% to $143.1M with gross margin up 120bp to 44.1%, but net income attributable to stockholders grew only 10.7% as sales and marketing rose 29.7%, and Q3 guidance of $137M to $139M is a sequential step down. The bigger change is capital intensity: investing outflows were $72.9M in Q2 against $1.3M a year earlier for the San Antonio and China plants, property and equipment jumped from $15.8M to $104.5M, and the balance sheet took on $43.5M of long-term debt from zero. That converts an asset-light model into one carrying fixed manufacturing cost. No insider open-market buying or selling, and no transcript in the pack.
