# MBC — MasterBrand, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

MasterBrand is the largest residential cabinet manufacturer in North America, selling into new construction and repair and remodel channels, and it closed an all-stock merger with American Woodmark on May 28 2026. The cyclical-trough case is that the market, not the company, is the problem: legacy net sales fell 5.6% in Q2 2026 on a mid to high single digit market decline, legacy gross margin fell 540 basis points to 27.4% on fixed cost deleverage, and legacy adjusted EBITDA fell from $105.4M to $58.2M. Against that, roughly $30M of annual synergy actions are already executed, the run-rate target was raised above $100M by year three, and a separate $30M legacy cost programme and the Monterrey closure are incremental. The stock is 40.9% off its high. The biggest risk is leverage into the trough: net debt is $1,148.7M at 3.9x using credit-agreement pro forma EBITDA, H1 free cash flow was negative $17.6M, H2 interest expense is guided at about $50M, and H2 adjusted EPS is guided at only $(0.05) to $0.03 with gross tariff costs of 5-6% of sales.
