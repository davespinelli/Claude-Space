# ULCC — Frontier Group Holdings, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Frontier is a Denver-based ultra low-cost carrier flying 165 leased Airbus single-aisle aircraft on US and near-international leisure routes, earning fares plus ancillary and loyalty revenue including a co-branded Barclays card. The post-restructuring case is quantified in the Q2 release: the trailing screen row is CY2025, when the airline lost $137M and RASM fell 1%, while Q2 2026 revenue was a record $1.279B, up 38%, RASM rose 28% to 11.52 cents on 8% more capacity, and the adjusted pre-tax margin improved from -7.5% to -1.9%. The reset is visible in the actions, not just the tone: 24 aircraft returned early under the Early Return Agreement, 11 A321neos being sold to Avolon, the Barclays program extended to 2037, and adjusted CASM excluding fuel of 7.42 cents. Guidance is Q3 adjusted EPS of -$0.10 to $0.10 and Q4 breakeven to $0.20, against a $1.3B market cap on roughly $3.7B of revenue. The risk is that this is a lease-financed airline whose true obligations sit outside the screen's $666.4M EV: aircraft rent was $748M in 2025, book equity is $136M, CY2025 CFO was -$525M, and insiders sold $87.1M of stock with zero buys.
