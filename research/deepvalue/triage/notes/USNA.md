# USNA — USANA HEALTH SCIENCES INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 6/10

USANA manufactures nutritional supplements, functional foods and personal care products sold mainly through a direct-selling Brand Partner and Preferred Customer network across 25 markets, with mainland China about 41.3% of net sales, alongside the 78.85%-owned Hiya children's vitamin subscription business and the Rise protein brand. The quantified edge is the balance sheet: the release confirms $169M of cash and zero debt against a $266.6M market cap on the 2026-09-04 screen row, so an enterprise value near $98.1M carries a full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guide of $87M. That guide is also the warning. Every business shrank in the June quarter: core nutritional sales down 4% with active customers down 8%, Hiya down 17% with subscribers down 17%, Rise's full-year outlook cut from $65-80M to $35M, and a $29.1M Hiya goodwill impairment driving a $21.4M net loss. Consolidated guidance fell to $910M of sales and $0.76 adjusted EPS from $1.95-2.29. Trailing free cash flow of $8.5M, a 3.2% yield, is nothing like what the Adjusted EBITDA figure implies.
