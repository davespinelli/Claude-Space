# LWAY — Lifeway Foods, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 4/10

Lifeway is the largest US producer of kefir, a cultured dairy drink, which was 85% of 2025 sales, plus farmer cheese, butter and drinkable yogurt sold through retail-direct, distributor and DSD channels from four owned plants. Sales have grown for 27 consecutive quarters and reached a record $66.9M in Q2 2026, up 24.1%. The problem is price: the screen shows EV of $402.5M at 24.9x EV/EBIT on 2025 EBIT of $16.2M, which is not a margin of safety for a commodity-input dairy processor. Biggest risk: milk and resin costs are outside management's control and there is no evidence in the pack that price increases have been taken to recover the 910 basis points lost.
