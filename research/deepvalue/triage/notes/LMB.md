# LMB — Limbach Holdings, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: fallen-growth · Score 6/10

Limbach designs, installs and maintains mechanical, electrical, plumbing and controls systems for owners of mission-critical buildings: hospitals, industrial plants, data centers, life sciences, universities. It has deliberately shifted mix toward Owner Direct Relationships, 74.0% of Q2 2026 revenue, which carries higher and more recurring margins than general contractor work. The multiple has collapsed while the top line grew: the stock is 56.6% off its 52-week high and down 54.2% over twelve months, yet FY2026 revenue guidance was raised to $760M to $790M, Q2 revenue rose 21.9% to $173.5M, bookings were $182.0M at 1.1x book-to-bill, and the screen shows 17.6% ROIC on modest net debt of $18.3M. The biggest risk is that the growth is bought, not earned, and the margin is going the wrong way: adjusted EBITDA guidance was cut in the same release from $90M to $94M down to $78M to $84M, gross margin guidance from 26-27% to 23-24%, Q2 adjusted EBITDA fell 22.3%, and the 21.9% revenue gain was almost entirely the Pioneer Power acquisition while organic ODR revenue fell 3.4%. Management says Pioneer margins converge to the company average over two to three years, which is a claim, not evidence. Insiders sold $2.7M and bought nothing.
