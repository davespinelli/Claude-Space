# INVA — Innoviva, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 8/10

The asset case is quantified in the release. The biggest risk is that $457.7M of those investments is a single stake, Armata Pharmaceuticals, whose markdown drove a $161.0M fair value hit and an $83.4M net loss in Q2; royalties also fell 2% on US pricing pressure. Share count rose 14.6% year over year despite the buyback, which the pack does not explain.
