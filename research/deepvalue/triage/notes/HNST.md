# HNST — Honest Company, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 4/10

The Honest Company sells cleanly-formulated personal care products, wipes, diapers and baby personal care, primarily through Amazon, Target and Walmart rather than its own site. The edge type is post-restructuring: Transformation 2.0, approved in October 2025, exits apparel, Honest.com fulfillment and Canada at a cost of $30-35M for $10-15M of annualized benefit, which makes headline revenue misleading. Q2 revenue fell 10.9% while organic revenue rose 6.7%, gross margin rose 800bps, and FY26 guidance was raised to $319-325M revenue and $23.0-25.0M adjusted EBITDA. The balance sheet is clean: management states no debt outstanding and $105.9M cash, and 5.6M shares were repurchased at an average $3.35. The problem is the price. At the screen price of $5.85 the stock is at its 52-week high after a 100.3% six-month run, and the $627.6M cap against $521.7M EV is roughly 21x the midpoint of guided adjusted EBITDA. The biggest risk is that the reported profit is flattered by tariff refunds: Q2 net income of $10.7M becomes $5.1M of underlying adjusted net income once refunds are excluded, and diapers keep declining on distribution losses. Insiders sold $1.6M with no buys.
