# HLIT — HARMONIC INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Harmonic sells broadband access equipment and software to cable and fibre operators, centred on its cOS virtualised broadband platform, deployed with 161 customers serving 48.2 million CPE devices; 89% of 2025 revenue came from the Americas. The edge type is post-restructuring, and the pack quantifies it. On June 16, 2026 Harmonic completed the sale of its Video business to MediaKind for $137.9 million at closing and is now a single-segment broadband company. The screen row is therefore wrong by construction: its 26.2% revenue decline compares continuing-operations CY2025 revenue of $360.5 million with an as-reported prior year that still contained Video, and its 81.1x EV/EBIT rests on $14.1 million of CY2025 EBIT. The continuing business guides 2026 GAAP operating profit of $74 to $86 million on $505 to $525 million of revenue, against a $1.1 billion enterprise value, with Q2 revenue up 54% to $133.5 million, backlog and deferred revenue up 71% to $587.6 million, $231.9 million of cash, $124.2 million net cash and a 4.1% share count reduction. The biggest risk is that this is an operator capex cycle: Q3 revenue is guided at $125 to $135 million, below Q2, and $10 million of stranded costs remain.
