# CMCO — COLUMBUS MCKINNON CORP · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

Columbus McKinnon makes material handling equipment, hoists, cranes and lifting and motion control products, sold worldwide to industrial customers, and has just absorbed the Kito Crosby acquisition. The edge type is post-restructuring. Trailing figures are meaningless: FY25 showed EBIT of negative $119.3M and CFO of negative $146.2M, and Q1 FY27 reported a net loss of $88.7M that included $70.3M of acquisition and integration expense. The forward numbers look different. Q1 FY27 net sales rose 125% to $531.5M with orders up 120% and a 1.1x book to bill, adjusted EBITDA was $111.5M at a 21.0% margin, up 720 basis points, and FY27 guidance was raised to $2.09B-$2.15B of sales, $405M-$420M of adjusted EBITDA and $1.90-$2.10 of adjusted EPS. One insider bought 2,750 shares at $17.14. The biggest risk is the capital structure: credit agreement net leverage is 4.9x, guidance assumes $185M-$190M of interest expense, nearly half of EBITDA, and the same guidance assumes 52M adjusted diluted shares against 28.8M common outstanding because preferred dividends are accruing rather than being paid.
