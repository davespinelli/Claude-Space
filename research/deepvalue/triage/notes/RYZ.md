# RYZ — Ryerson Holding Corp · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

Ryerson is a metals service center: it buys stainless, aluminum, carbon and alloy steel in bulk from mills and resells roughly 75,000 products in smaller lots to about 40,000 customers across 103 North American facilities, processing nearly 80% of what it sells. Earnings are at a visible cycle low rather than structurally broken. FY25 net sales fell only 0.6% to $4,571.3 million on tons up 0.5%, but average selling price fell 1.1% and gross margin compressed to 17.1% from 18.1%, producing a $30.8 million operating loss and a $56.4 million net loss. Management notes ISM PMI was below 50 in ten months of 2025 before reading 52.6 in January 2026, and that Ryerson's North American volumes fell 0.4% against an industry decline of 1.5%, implying share gain. The Olympic Steel merger closed 2026-02-13, making North America's second-largest service center and explaining the 61.2% higher share count. Biggest risk is that the pack contains no post-merger figures: no 8-K, no earnings release, no Form 4 data, so I could not check integration, current pricing or the $913.3 million net debt against combined earnings. Score capped at 6 for the missing release.
