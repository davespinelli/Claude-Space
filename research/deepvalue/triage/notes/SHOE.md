# SHOE — SHOE STATION GROUP INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: hidden-asset · Score 7/10

Shoe Carnival, renaming itself Shoe Station Group, is an omnichannel family footwear retailer running 426 stores in 35 states and Puerto Rico under two banners: Shoe Carnival for moderate and low-income families, and the higher-end Shoe Station, which carries 20-25% less inventory per store. The pack's DEBT DATA MISSING warning resolves favourably and is the edge: the Q1 release confirms the company ended the quarter debt-free with $129.3M of cash and marketable securities, up 39% year over year, on a $370.6M market cap, marking a 22nd consecutive debt-free year. Against that, fiscal 2026 guidance is adjusted EPS of $1.40-$1.60 on $1.125-$1.147B of sales, so the operating business is capitalised at roughly six times earnings ex-cash, the stock is 45.3% off its high, and the CFO bought $500,030 of stock at $16.13 with no insider sales. The risk is that the growth thesis has already broken: 101 stores were rebannered in fiscal 2025 with variable in-store results, the pace was cut to about 21 for the first half of fiscal 2026, $8.3M was written off, and gross margin is guided down about 260bp on tariffs.
