# APEI - AMERICAN PUBLIC EDUCATION INC - triage 2026-09-04 - Edge: misunderstood-segment - Score 7/10

American Public Education teaches roughly 108,600 students through American Public University System (online, military and veteran focused), Rasmussen University and Hondros College of Nursing, with tuition funded largely by DoD Tuition Assistance, VA benefits and Title IV. The edge type is misunderstood-segment. In Q2-26 the Military+ segment earned $23.7 million of operating income on $85.5 million of revenue, a 28% margin, while Health+ earned $0.3 million on $86.2 million; for the half, Health+ made $0.8 million on $171.6 million against a $3.2 million loss a year earlier. The balance sheet holds $146.5 million cash plus $76.3 million short-term investments against $87.3 million of debt. Full-year guidance is $690-698 million revenue and $2.48-2.79 diluted EPS versus $1.36. The biggest risk is payor concentration: the 2025 federal shutdown alone cut APUS TA registrations by about 20,600 in Q4, and Q3-26 adjusted EBITDA is guided to $14-17 million against $20.7 million last year.
