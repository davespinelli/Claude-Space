# HLLY - Holley Inc. - triage 2026-09-04 - Edge: post-restructuring - Score 7/10

Holley designs and sells performance automotive aftermarket parts to enthusiasts: fuel injection, carburetors, tuners, exhaust, superchargers and safety equipment under Holley, MSD, Flowmaster, Simpson and Accel, across four consumer verticals and both DTC and B2B channels. The edge type is post-restructuring. This is a deleveraging equity stub from a 2021 SPAC: full-year guidance is $127-137 million of adjusted EBITDA against $42-47 million of interest and $15-20 million of capital expenditure, leaving roughly $65-80 million of cash generation against a $375.8 million market capitalisation at $3.10, and guidance was reiterated at the half. Management reports $115.0 million of voluntary debt prepayments since September 2023, a target of finishing the year below 3.5x leverage, $40.9 million of second-quarter free cash flow, $8.3 million of cost savings from strategic initiatives, and a $2.0 million buyback. The biggest risk is that the leverage cuts both ways: long-term debt is $518.6 million against equity of $449.7 million that is entirely goodwill and intangibles, the Q2 GAAP loss reflected a $28.3 million loss on selling the Restoration brands, and adjusted EBITDA margin fell 230 basis points on discretionary enthusiast demand.
