# OLN — OLIN Corp · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

Olin is a vertically integrated chlor alkali producer and the owner of Winchester ammunition: Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls was 54% of 2025 sales, Epoxy 20%, Winchester 26%. The edge type is cyclical-trough and the pack shows the trough clearly. Net loss was $100.5 million in 2025 against $108.6 million of net income in 2024, Epoxy lost $103.5 million at segment level against subsidised Asian competition, Winchester segment income fell from $237.9 million to $67.7 million, and the shares are 42.6% off the 52-week high. The turn has begun on the company's own numbers: Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA was $191.3 million against $176.1 million a year earlier despite a $40 million Freeport VCM outage, Epoxy swung from a $23.7 million segment loss to $16.0 million of income, and Q3 is guided to $160 to $200 million. The biggest risk is the capital structure: net debt is $2.85 billion at 5.0x adjusted EBITDA against an $1.9 billion market capitalisation, with roughly $185 million of Shintech payments in 2026. The pending all-stock merger of equals with Huntsman, targeted for the first half of 2027, means any recovery is shared rather than captured.
