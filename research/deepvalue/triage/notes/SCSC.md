# SCSC — SCANSOURCE, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

ScanSource is a technology distributor serving about 25,000 channel partners in the US and Brazil across barcode and mobility, POS, payment terminals, physical security, networking and communications, with about 500 suppliers. It reports two segments. The interesting one is small and buried: Intelisys & Advisory did $101.1M of FY2026 revenue but $99.7M of gross profit, a 98.6% gross margin because recurring connectivity and cloud commissions are booked net, on roughly $2.88B of Intelisys net billings. That sits inside a consolidated business with a 13.6% gross margin and a 3.1% operating margin, so consolidated multiples price the whole company as a low-margin distributor. Capital allocation is real: share count fell 10.1% year over year, $71.4M repurchased in nine months, FY2026 FCF guided to at least $90M. The biggest risk is that the segment worth re-rating has stopped growing, at 3.1% for the year and down 1.5% in the March quarter, so the sum-of-parts case rests on a decelerating asset. Price is also not distressed: $57.86 on the screen row dated 2026-09-04, 1.7% off the 52-week high, 13.9x EV/EBIT. A MicroAge acquisition was signed August 19, 2026 with terms not in this pack.
