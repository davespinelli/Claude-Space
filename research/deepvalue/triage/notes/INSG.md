# INSG — INSEEGO CORP. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 2/10

Inseego designs cloud-managed 5G wireless WAN hardware and software: MiFi mobile hotspots, fixed wireless access routers, Skyus IoT devices and the Inseego Connect and Subscribe cloud platforms, sold largely through US carriers. The pack shows a liquidity problem, not an opportunity. Cash fell from $24.9M at December 2025 to $1.9M at June 2026 while first-half operating cash flow was negative $22.1M, against $50.3M of 2029 senior secured notes and $10M drawn on a working capital facility that was only just raised to $20M. Q2 adjusted EBITDA collapsed to $0.5M from $4.7M a year earlier, the GAAP loss was $8.4M, and Q3 adjusted EBITDA is guided to negative $2.0M to negative $1.0M on revenue of $28-35M, well below Q2's $44.0M. Stockholders' equity is negative $30.6M. The one genuinely good piece of capital allocation, retiring $42M of Mubadala preferred at a 38% discount in January, has already been spent. The biggest risk is that the company must fund the announced purchase of Nokia's fixed wireless access business from this position. Revenue fell 13.1% in FY25 on a 31% drop in mobile solutions. Screen price $4.03, 80.1% off the high.
