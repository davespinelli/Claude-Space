# ACCO — ACCO BRANDS Corp · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

ACCO Brands sells branded office, school and technology accessory products, including Five Star, Mead, At-A-Glance, Swingline, Leitz, Kensington and PowerA, through mass retailers, e-tailers, superstores, wholesalers and dealers in the US, Europe, Australia, Canada, Brazil and Mexico. Roughly 75% of 2025 sales came from brands ranked first or second in their category. The market treats it as a melting ice cube and prices it accordingly: at $4.34 the equity is $400.6M against 2026 guidance of $0.87 to $0.91 adjusted EPS and reiterated free cash flow of $75M to $85M, a 19% to 21% yield on the market cap, with a $0.30 annual dividend and a $100M multi-year cost program running. Biggest risk is the combination of leverage and shrinkage: consolidated leverage was 4.3x at June 30 against guidance of 3.7x to 3.9x by year end, comparable sales fell 2.3% in the quarter and 9.3% in 2025, and the International segment's adjusted operating income fell to $3.6M from $12.4M. Six-month net income was flattered by a $36.5M bargain purchase gain on EPOS. Share count rose 2.4% and insiders bought nothing.
