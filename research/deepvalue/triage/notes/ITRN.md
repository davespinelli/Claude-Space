# ITRN — Ituran Location & Control Ltd. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 5/10

Ituran sells telematics services, principally stolen vehicle recovery, fleet management, connected car and usage-based insurance, to 2,711,000 subscribers in Israel, Brazil and other Latin American markets, plus the hardware those services run on, split roughly 74% services and 26% products. The operating record is strong and improving. Q2 2026 revenue was a record $104.8 million, up 21%, subscription revenue rose 25% to $79.8 million, net subscriber additions were 41,000 in the quarter and 163,000 year over year, operating income rose 30% and EBITDA rose 24% to $28.5 million, and the company holds $103.7 million of net cash with no debt while paying a $10 million quarterly dividend. New OEM programs with Stellantis, Renault and BMW Motorrad are named as growth drivers. What is missing is a mispricing. At 11.8x EV/EBIT after 81% twelve-month momentum, the pack shows a good business at an unremarkable price rather than a market error. As a foreign private issuer it filed no Form 4s, so insider activity cannot be checked.
