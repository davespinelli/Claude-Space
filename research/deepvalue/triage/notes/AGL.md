# AGL — agilon health, inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 4/10

agilon partners with community primary care physician groups, forming risk-bearing entities that take global capitation from Medicare Advantage payors, and supplies the platform, capital and network behind roughly 2,300 PCPs across about 30 geographies; 549,000 members at June 30, 2026, of which 437,000 are MA. The turn is real on the P&L: after a 2025 gross loss of $160.0M, negative $56.6M medical margin and a $296.2M adjusted EBITDA loss, Q2 2026 delivered $197M of medical margin, $70M of adjusted EBITDA and $18M of net income, and full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance was raised to $75M to $95M from $10M to $40M on membership deliberately shrunk 10%. Two things stop this being cheap. The screen's headline signals are artefacts: the "-95.9% buyback" is the one-for-five to one-for-twenty-five reverse split done to cure a November 2025 NYSE sub-$1.00 listing notice, not repurchases, which also makes the 259.5% and 420.2% momentum figures unusable. And the recovery is front-loaded, with Q2 medical margin explicitly including favorable prior-period claims development, Q3 adjusted EBITDA guided to negative $5M to positive $5M, receivables up from $673.8M to $1,059.8M in six months, and FY25 CFO of negative $105.8M against a $1.4B EV. I could not check the reverse-split ratio or how much of Q2 margin was prior-year development.
