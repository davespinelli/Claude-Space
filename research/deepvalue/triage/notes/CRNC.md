# CRNC — Cerence Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 7/10

Cerence sells white-label conversational and agentic AI to nearly all major automakers and their tier-one suppliers: edge software licensed per vehicle head unit, cloud connected services prepaid per vehicle, and professional services. The market treats it as an AI casualty after FY25 revenue fell 24.0% to $251.8 million, but the cash statement says otherwise. Q3 FY26 revenue rose 12%, connected services more than 20%, GAAP net income turned positive at $1.5 million, adjusted EBITDA was $13.5 million up 50%, and free cash flow was $19.6 million, up more than 20%, none of it flattered by the $49.5 million Samsung IP licence that inflated Q1 FY26. That quarter alone annualises near the screen's 12.2% FCF yield on a $384.0 million cap ($8.49, 2026-09-05 pack). The board authorised a first-ever $30 million buyback, roughly 8% of the cap, and five-year backlog grew to $1,169.2 million from $952.7 million. Biggest risk is disintermediation: OEMs building their own assistants on general-purpose LLMs would erode the per-vehicle royalty, and $173.5 million of net debt limits the margin for error. The screen's $0.00 cash figure is not credible given management intends to fund buybacks from existing cash.
