# HE — HAWAIIAN ELECTRIC INDUSTRIES INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

HEI is a holding company whose one reportable segment is Hawaiian Electric, the regulated utility supplying about 95% of Hawaii's population across five island grids, with $5.8 billion of net utility plant; the bank was sold in December 2024 and the remaining Pacific Current assets are being sold. The edge type is post-restructuring: the open-ended Maui wildfire exposure has become a fixed contractual obligation, remeasured in Q2 2026 from $1.44 billion to $1.30 billion in present value after the April settlement was finalised, the PUC has fully approved Wildfire Mitigation Plan cost recovery which the company plans to securitise, and a second agency upgraded its credit rating. The stock sits 35.1% below its 52-week high after a 28.7% six-month decline. The biggest risk is that the fixed liability is roughly two thirds of the $1.9B market cap and must be funded from a holding company whose own commentary flags lower cash and rising interest, while Q2 core net income fell to $22M from $35M on higher O&M and interest in what management calls a transitional year before 2027 rate rebasing. Score 7: a specific, quantified reset with an identified 2027 catalyst, though current core earnings are depressed.
