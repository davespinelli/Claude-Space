# CRI — CARTERS INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

Carter's is the largest US apparel company for babies and young children, selling Carter's, OshKosh, Skip Hop and Little Planet through 1,068 owned stores, eCommerce and roughly 19,500 wholesale doors, plus exclusive brands made for Walmart, Target and Amazon. It holds about 9% of a $29B category, the number one position. Earnings are in a visible cost trough rather than a demand one: 2025 sales rose 1.9% to $2.90B while operating income fell 43.5% to $143.9M and margin dropped 400bp to 5.0%, with incremental tariffs alone adding about $60M to product cost and SG&A up 230bp. Volumes and comps kept growing, with Q2 2026 sales up 5.2% and US retail comps up 5.1%. At the screen price of $33.15 (row dated 2026-09-04) that is a $1.2B cap, 7.9x EV/EBIT with net cash of $85.8M. The biggest risk is how thin the cushion now is: adjusted operating margin was 2.9% in Q2 and 3.6% in the first half, and the company guides FY2026 adjusted EPS down high single to low double digits. Note that the $230M to $240M operating cash flow guide includes a one-time $132M duty recovery. A new CEO started May 2026.
