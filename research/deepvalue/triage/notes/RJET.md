# RJET — Republic Airways Holdings Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 8/10

Republic is the second largest independent US regional airline, flying 314 Embraer E170/175s under fixed-fee capacity purchase agreements for American, Delta and United, who set routes and fares and pay for fuel, so revenue is contracted block hours rather than ticket sales. Edge is post-restructuring. The Mesa merger closed 25 November 2025, so every trailing screen figure is stale: FY25 revenue of $1,676.5M excludes Mesa against a 2026 guide of about $2.1B and adjusted EBITDAR of $395M to $405M, raised from over $380M in April. GAAP carries $47.1M of merger and separation charges in 2025 and $23.1M more in H1 2026, and management says true operational synergies only arrive on consolidation of the operating certificates, 18 to 24 months out. Against an $806.2M market cap (screen price $17.22, date not shown because price data was skipped), book equity is $1,386.3M and H1 EPS was $1.26 GAAP with Q2 adjusted EPS of $0.89, at 2.4x leverage. Biggest risk is that revenue rests entirely on three partners whose contracts begin expiring in 2026 and 2027, and FY25 capex of $396.9M exceeded $322.0M of operating cash flow.
