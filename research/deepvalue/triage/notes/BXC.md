# BXC — BlueLinx Holdings Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

BlueLinx is a two-step wholesale distributor of building products, buying from manufacturers such as Louisiana-Pacific and Huber and warehousing them across 57 cities for pro dealers, home centers and industrial buyers. Specialty products (engineered wood, siding, millwork) are 70% of sales and 82% of gross profit; structural lumber and panels are the rest. Edge is cyclical-trough. Fiscal 2025 operating income fell to $32.5M from $87.6M as gross margin dropped from 16.6% to 15.3% on pricing alone, with volumes up, so trailing EV/EBIT of 18.1x is measured on depressed earnings. Q2 2026 shows the turn: sales up 4.4%, gross margin up 190 basis points, adjusted EBITDA up 33% to $35.6M, though $7.2M of that was an import-duty item and the underlying gain was 6%. The balance sheet is the other half: $318M cash against $300M of 2029 notes, net leverage 0.6x, $655M of liquidity against a $607.4M market cap (screen price $77.28, date not shown because price data was skipped), and $53.7M of buyback authorization on 7.86M shares. Biggest risk is that distributor gross margin is set by lumber and panel pricing, not by management, and insiders sold $2.87M with no buys.
