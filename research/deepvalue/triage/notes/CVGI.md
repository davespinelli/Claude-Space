# CVGI — Commercial Vehicle Group, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

CVG supplies seats, wire harnesses and plastic trim to commercial vehicle and off-highway OEMs through three segments (Global Seating, Global Electrical Systems, Trim Systems and Components), with plants in ten countries. The edge type is cyclical-trough: consolidated revenue fell from $835.5 million in 2023 to $649.0 million in 2025 as North American Class 8 build weakened, but Q2 2026 revenue rose 13.5% to $195.2 million with all three segments growing, gross margin up 140bp year over year and 120bp sequentially, and management raised full-year guidance to $725-755 million of revenue and $26-31 million of Adjusted EBITDA against ACT Research's forecast of Class 8 production up 9%. The biggest risk is that the recovery is being funded by shareholders: Adjusted EBITDA margin is still 2.8%, the company remains loss-making from continuing operations, and it is issuing stock through a June 2026 at-the-market programme (shares up 9.7%, $11.6 million of proceeds used to pay down term loan) while carrying $86.9 million of net debt. No insider open-market buying.
