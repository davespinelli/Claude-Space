# MDXG — MIMEDX GROUP, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

MiMedx processes donated placental tissue into allografts sold to hospitals, wound care centres and physician offices, split into a Wound portfolio (EPIFIX, EPICORD, EPIEFFECT) and a Surgical portfolio (AMNIOFIX, AMNIOEFFECT, AXIOFILL), increasingly supplemented by third-party distributed products. I can name no edge here, because the cheap screen numbers are stale. The 8.2x EV/EBIT, 52.6% ROIC and 20.0% revenue growth are CY2025, before CMS replaced ASP+6% with a flat $127.14 per square centimetre skin substitute rate on January 1, 2026, which the 10-K itself describes as cutting costs by nearly 90%. The Q2 2026 release shows the effect: net sales $64 million versus $99 million, Wound down 61%, gross margin 69% from 81%, a $15 million net loss, and full-year guidance of $260 to $290 million against $418.6 million in 2025. The biggest risk is that the reset is permanent and the balance sheet no longer cushions it: MiMedx is funding the $350 million Sanara MedTech acquisition partly with a $300 million Hayfin term loan, converting $119 million of net cash into leverage. No insider bought stock in twelve months.
