# DCH — Dauch Corp · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Dauch is the renamed American Axle, a driveline and metal forming supplier that on 2026-02-03 acquired Dowlais, parent of GKN Automotive and GKN Powder Metallurgy, for roughly $1.7 billion in cash and about 117 million new shares, doubling the share count and taking the company to 24 countries and more than 175 locations. The screen's 51.9x EV/EBIT is built on stale pre-merger CY2025 earnings and misses what the company now is: 2026 guidance is $10.6 to $10.8 billion of sales and $1.36 to $1.425 billion of adjusted EBITDA, against an enterprise value of $5.8 billion, with synergies of $60 to $75 million in year one and a stated run rate above $100 million, and adjusted free cash flow guided to $260 to $325 million on a $1.6 billion market cap. Q2 adjusted EBITDA margin held at 13.2% on nearly doubled sales. The risk is that leverage and cash costs eat that: net debt is $4.2 billion, interest expense ran $89.8 million in the quarter, GAAP loss attributable to Dauch was $99.3 million for the half, and the adjusted free cash flow figure excludes $115 to $150 million of restructuring and $95 to $110 million of synergy implementation cash payments. GM, Ford and Stellantis are 72% of sales, with USMCA and tariff exposure flagged.
