# TWI — TITAN INTERNATIONAL INC · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

The plausible edge is cyclical-trough: the Ag segment's Q2 sales fell 5.0% and its operating income fell 72.2% to $3.2 million on lower farm income and OEM destocking, while consumer grew 27.2% and full-year guidance is maintained at $1.85-1.95 billion of sales and $105-115 million of adjusted EBITDA, against a stock 33.0% off its 52-week high.
