# MTW — MANITOWOC CO INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

Manitowoc makes and services cranes under the Grove, Manitowoc, National Crane, Potain and Shuttlelift brands through three geographic segments, and is pushing its CRANES+50 strategy to grow less cyclical non-new-machine revenue (parts, service, used, rental) via MGX Equipment Services and 17 US branches. The segment split is where the numbers hide: in 2025 Americas earned $95.7M of operating income and MEAP $44.6M, while EURAF lost $42.9M on $667.2M of sales, leaving consolidated EBIT at just $53.8M and ROIC at 4.0%. EURAF has now lost $46.6M and $42.9M in consecutive years, so roughly two thirds of group operating income is being absorbed by one region. Q2 2026 was strong: orders up 56.1% to $708.7M, backlog $1,050.1M, adjusted EBITDA up 85.9% to $48.9M with 330bp of margin expansion, and FY26 guidance raised to $150M to $170M adjusted EBITDA and $50M to $70M adjusted free cash flow. Against that the risks are real: net debt of $364.8M at 6.8x FY25 EBIT, FY25 FCF of negative $15.3M, a $108.7M first-half inventory build, guidance that includes a $16M net tariff-refund benefit, and a share price 2.0% off its 52-week high after a 43.9% run.
