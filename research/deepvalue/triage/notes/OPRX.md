# OPRX — OptimizeRx Corp · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 5/10

Revenue is concentrated: the top five customers were 47% of 2025 sales and three exceeded 10% each. The screen row shows a business that no longer exists in that form. What is left is cash generative: adjusted EBITDA guided to $21M to $25M against a $149.7M market cap and a $143.4M EV at the screen price of $7.95 (row dated 2026-09-04), with $24.1M of cash, and $8.3M of term loan principal repaid since inception. The biggest risk is that the decline is customer attrition, not timing: net revenue retention fell from 121% to 90% over the trailing twelve months and average revenue per top-20 manufacturer fell from $3,095k to $2,659k. Insiders neither bought nor sold on the open market.
