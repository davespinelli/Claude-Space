# AKBA — Akebia Therapeutics, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 7/10

Akebia is a commercial-stage kidney disease pharma selling two products: Auryxia, a phosphate binder now facing generics, and Vafseo, an oral HIF-PH inhibitor for anemia in dialysis patients, approved in 37 countries, plus mid-stage pipeline assets ebribafusp and praliciguat. The edge is misunderstood-segment: consolidated Q2 2026 revenue fell to $49.1M from $62.5M, which hides two products moving in opposite directions. Vafseo net revenue grew to $21.3M, up 34% sequentially and from $13.3M a year earlier, with active patients up 41% to over 10,500 and prescribers up 17% to about 1,200, while Auryxia fell to $25.5M from $47.2M on generic entry. A positive VOICE interim analysis in 2,116 patients and 14 Orange Book patents running to 2036 support the Vafseo franchise. Cash of $155.5M against a $272.4M market cap and $24.8M of long-term debt is roughly half the capitalization. Biggest risk: the crossover may not happen fast enough. Q2 was a $8.9M net loss, cash fell $7.1M in the quarter, shares grew 4.5% in a year, and the company plans to refinance its senior secured term loan. Screen metrics are trailing CY2025 and stale.
