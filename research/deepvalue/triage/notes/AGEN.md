# AGEN — AGENUS INC · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 2/10

Agenus is a clinical-stage immuno-oncology company whose lead asset is botensilimab plus balstilimab, an Fc-enhanced anti-CTLA-4 combined with an anti-PD-1. It has no approved product; the $114.2M of FY25 revenue is mostly non-cash royalty revenue tied to royalties it already monetized, plus $6.4M of Q2 pre-commercial product revenue from paid access programs in France and elsewhere. There is no value edge here. Equity is negative $199.1M, trailing operating cash flow is -$77.2M, cash was $18.7M before a July private placement of $85M with warrants for up to $255M more, and the share count rose 41.3% in a year with more dilution embedded in those warrants. The stock is up 82.6% on 12-1 momentum and sits 1.4% off its 52-week high at the screen price of $8.24, so nothing is being ignored. The biggest risk is binary and self-evident: the planned Phase 3 ROBBIN trial in neoadjuvant MSS colon cancer has not dosed a patient and is not expected to until Q1 2027, funding runs to Q3 2027 on upfront proceeds alone, and the company just discontinued its funding of the BATTMAN Phase 3, which was then terminated. Zero open-market insider buys.
