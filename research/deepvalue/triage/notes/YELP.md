# YELP — YELP INC · triage 2026-09-06 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 7/10

Yelp sells performance-based local advertising, priced per click through an auction, against a platform of more than 300 million consumer ratings and reviews, plus a smaller non-advertising business of subscriptions (Guest Manager, Host, Receptionist) and data APIs. The edge type is cash-cow-narrative: FY25 revenue of $1,465.0M produced $184.5M of operating income and $323.7M of free cash flow, a 27.5% yield on the $1.2B market cap at $21.66 and 6.4x EV/EBIT, and the company has repurchased nearly $2.0B of stock to date, shrinking shares outstanding 14.0% year over year to 54.3M. Guided 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $315-325M against EV of $1.2B is under 4x. The biggest risk is that the cash is being harvested from a shrinking core: Q2 26 advertising revenue fell 3% year over year, Restaurants/Retail/Other fell 6% in 2025, adjusted EBITDA fell 9%, and the pack names reliance on internet search engines whose providers compete directly with Yelp. Insiders sold $1,428,294 with no open-market purchases, and $263.6M of cash plus a $100M revolver draw funded the Hatch acquisition.
