# KLC — KinderCare Learning Companies, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

The post-restructuring case rests on a footprint reset in progress: 49 centers were closed in Q2 2026 under an ongoing optimisation initiative, driving $22.9M of impairment losses in the quarter and a goodwill writedown that took goodwill from $964.8M to $692.4M in six months. Trailing GAAP is therefore unrepresentative, and management guides FY2026 to adjusted EBITDA of $200M to $220M. The prior-year comparison is distorted by Employee Retention Credits that offset FY2025 cost of services. No transcript, so the closure programme's endpoint is unchecked.
