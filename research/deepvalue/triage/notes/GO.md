# GO — Grocery Outlet Holding Corp. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

Grocery Outlet is an extreme-value grocer running 547 small-box stores in 16 states, buying name-brand closeout inventory opportunistically and selling it 40% to 70% below conventional retailers through independent operators who put up their own capital and split store gross profit roughly 50/50. The edge is post-restructuring. The Optimization Plan closed all 36 underperforming stores in the first half, a $158.0M non-cash goodwill impairment is behind it, and FY26 guidance was raised on both ends to $4.70-4.72B of sales and $225-235M of adjusted EBITDA against a $1.6B enterprise value, roughly 7x. Comparable sales improved sequentially with transactions up 1.8%, quarterly capex fell $21.5M year over year, and four open-market purchases by two insiders included 15,000 shares at $11.70 in late August. The biggest risk is that the value proposition itself is eroding rather than merely being mismanaged: comps are still negative on a 2.1% smaller basket, gross margin fell 40bps on promotions, and the company had to raise its provision for independent-operator notes and receivables, which is the model's own credit exposure. Net debt is $416.4M; screen price $12.30, about 23x guided adjusted EPS.
