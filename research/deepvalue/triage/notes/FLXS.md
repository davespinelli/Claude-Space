# FLXS — FLEXSTEEL INDUSTRIES INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 4/10

Flexsteel is one of the larger US residential furniture makers and importers, selling sofas, recliners, bedroom, dining and occasional furniture through retail partners and online under the Flexsteel brand and sub-brands including Zecliner and Zen. It manufactures in Juarez, Mexico and sources the rest from Vietnam. FY2026 was a good year: sales up 4.1% to $459.2M, an eleventh consecutive quarter of growth, adjusted operating margin 7.5%, record adjusted EPS of $4.94, more than $47.5M of free cash flow, roughly $64M of stock repurchased including a $62.6M buyout of a director's family holding, and a 25% dividend increase. The debt flag on the screen row is an artefact: the press release shows $16.7M of cash, $22k of interest expense and $54.1M available on the credit line, so leverage is not the issue. Price is. At $80.37 (screen row dated 2026-09-04) the stock is 2.2% off its 52-week high after a 70% twelve-month run, roughly 12x adjusted operating income, and the reported margin is flattered by IEEPA tariff refunds worth 780bp of Q4 gross margin that do not recur. The biggest risk is the tariff going the other way: Section 232 duties on most seating are scheduled to rise from 25% to 30% on January 1, 2027, and Q1 FY2027 guidance already steps margin down to 6.5% to 7%. Insiders sold 15,858 shares and bought none.
