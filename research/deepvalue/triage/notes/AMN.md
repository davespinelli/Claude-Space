# AMN — AMN HEALTHCARE SERVICES INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

AMN provides healthcare workforce solutions to US hospitals, health systems and schools across three segments: nurse and allied staffing (60% of 2025 revenue), physician and leadership solutions (26%) and technology and workforce solutions (14%). Revenue fell 8% in 2025 to $2,730.4M, driven by a 14% decline in travelers on assignment, and the company booked goodwill impairments equal to 4.0% of revenue in 2025 and 7.5% in 2024. The cyclical-trough case is that Q2 2026 shows the turn: travel nursing and allied volume and revenue grew year over year for a second consecutive quarter, nurse and allied revenue rose 11%, adjusted EBITDA rose 26% to $73M, and management guides Q3 nurse and allied revenue up 9-11%. Trailing FCF of $233.8M is an 18.1% yield on the $1.3B cap. The biggest risk is that this yield is not repeatable: Q3 guidance implies only 6.5-7.0% adjusted EBITDA margin and 0.2-0.8% operating margin, Q2 gross margin was flattered by reserve releases from labor disruption events, and $117M of client deposits still has to be returned.
