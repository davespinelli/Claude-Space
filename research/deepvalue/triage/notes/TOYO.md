# TOYO — TOYO Co., Ltd · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: fallen-growth · Score 5/10

The pack's own numbers show real growth with a collapsed multiple: revenue $427.4M versus $177.0M prior year (+141.5%), EBIT $59.0M, 1.8x EV/EBIT, 24.9% FCF yield, $58.9M net cash and a 19.0% lower share count, against a price 74.2% below the 52-week high.
