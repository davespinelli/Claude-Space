# OPK — OPKO HEALTH, INC. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 6/10

OPKO is a diversified healthcare company with three parts: a pharmaceutical business (Rayaldee for secondary hyperparathyroidism, plus a Pfizer profit share on NGENLA/somatrogon), international manufacturing platforms in Spain, Ireland, Chile and Mexico, and BioReference, a US lab now reduced to New York/New Jersey clinical testing plus the 4Kscore urology franchise. ModeX develops multispecific antibodies. The edge is post-restructuring: two Labcorp divestitures in 2024 and 2025 for up to $462.5M reset the cost base, and Q2 2026 operating loss was $7.0M against $60.0M a year earlier, with the diagnostics segment swinging to $21.3M of 2025 operating income from a $24.1M loss. Shares are down 6.0% year over year on a $105.3M buyback, and Form 4s show 13 open-market purchases by two insiders including Frost Gamma, with zero sales. The biggest risk is that the trailing numbers still burn cash: FY2025 free cash flow was negative $190.8M against $300.8M of cash, and the company's own FY2026 guidance of $560-585M revenue against $710-740M of costs implies another large operating loss. Q2 IP revenue also included $29.4M recognized from Nicoya equity shares, not cash. Score 6: the cost reset is real and quantified, but the burn and the lumpy IP line mean the pack does not yet evidence a valuation gap.
