# CSTL — CASTLE BIOSCIENCES INC · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

Castle Biosciences is a molecular diagnostics lab selling gene expression and spatialomics tests that guide treatment: DecisionDx-Melanoma and DecisionDx-SCC in dermatology, TissueCypher for Barrett's esophagus, DecisionDx-UM in ophthalmology, and the newly launched AdvanceAD-Tx for atopic dermatitis. The edge is misunderstood-segment. Consolidated FY25 revenue grew just 3.7%, which obscures a $52.8M increase in non-dermatologic revenue on an 86.2% jump in TissueCypher volumes, netted against a $40.6M dermatology decline caused almost entirely by DecisionDx-SCC losing Medicare coverage in April 2025. That reset is now lapped: Q2 core test reports rose 32%, TissueCypher volumes went from 9,170 to 14,988, and FY26 revenue guidance was raised to $365-375M from $345-355M with positive adjusted EBITDA guided for the second half and the full year. Cash and marketable securities are $266.8M against a $997.4M cap. The biggest risk is that the same reimbursement mechanism that cut dermatology can hit the growth engine, and profitability leans on adjustments: first-half adjusted EBITDA fell to $7.3M from $23.4M, operating cash flow was negative $6.9M, and stock compensation was $21.4M. Insiders sold $5.1M across 47 rows with no buys.
