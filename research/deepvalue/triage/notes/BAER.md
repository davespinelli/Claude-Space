# BAER — Bridger Aerospace Group Holdings, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Bridger operates CL-415EAF Super Scoopers for wildfire suppression, Air Attack surveillance aircraft, and a maintenance and modification business, contracting mostly with the U.S. Forest Service, the Department of the Interior and state agencies. Demand is genuinely improving: two 160-day Forest Service task orders carry at least $30 million of guaranteed standby revenue, a $58 million Texas A&M Forest Service contract for three King Air 360s was signed in July, two Super Scoopers were leased into Portugal, and FY26 guidance is reiterated at $135-145 million of revenue and $55-60 million of adjusted EBITDA. The equity is still not the way to own it. Below the $64.1 million market cap sit $233.1 million of long-term debt and, in mezzanine, $421.4 million of Series A Preferred that accreted $14.1 million in the first half alone, roughly half the guided full-year EBITDA, none of which the screen's $290.0 million EV captures. Stockholders' deficit is $387.1 million, cash fell to $7.2 million from $31.4 million, FY25 capex was $80.9 million against $16.7 million of operating cash flow, and the 10-K references a going concern assessment. Biggest risk is that the preferred compounds faster than the fleet earns. The CFO and chief legal officer both resigned in March 2026 and insiders sold 9.36 million shares.
