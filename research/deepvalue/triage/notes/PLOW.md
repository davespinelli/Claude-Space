# PLOW — DOUGLAS DYNAMICS, INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 6/10

Douglas Dynamics manufactures snow and ice control attachments for light trucks under the FISHER, SNOWEX and WESTERN brands, sold through roughly 3,000 distributor points of sale into an installed base of over 500,000 units, and upfits Class 3-8 work trucks under HENDERSON and DEJANA. The cyclical-trough case is documented in the MD&A: the snow season ended March 2025 was the seventh consecutive year below the ten-year average, and the season ended March 2024 ran 39% below it, depressing Attachments volumes to $256.0 million in 2024 before recovering to $295.7 million in 2025. The cycle appears to be turning. Q2 2026 Attachments sales rose 20% on strong pre-season orders, and 2026 guidance was raised again to $765-805 million of sales and $120-135 million of adjusted EBITDA, roughly 8.6x the $1.1 billion EV at the midpoint. Biggest risk is that the turn is being financed with working capital: operating activities used $25.2 million in the first half on inventory and receivable builds, versus $12.7 million used a year earlier, and net debt is already $142.1 million. Guidance also assumes average Q4 snowfall. Solutions segment adjusted EBITDA fell to $8.8 million on softer commercial demand. Price is the screen's $41.30, 23.8% off the high.
