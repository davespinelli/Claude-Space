# CLW — Clearwater Paper Corp · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 7/10

Clearwater Paper is an independent, non-integrated manufacturer of solid bleached sulfate paperboard sold to North American converters serving folding carton and food service markets. The cyclical-trough case is unusually well quantified in the Q2 2026 pack. Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations was negative $8.2M against positive $39.9M a year earlier, with a $32.0M price/mix hit as paperboard pricing fell 9% to $1,077 per ton and a $22M Lewiston maintenance outage. Against that, volumes rose 8% on share gains, industry operating rates are forecast to move from 81% in Q1 to 90%+ by year end, roughly 300K tons of SBS capacity has already come out with 200K more forecast, imports are down 11%, and RISI reported July increases of $40 per ton on folding carton and $60 on cup. At $20.70 the equity is roughly 0.42x the $794.1M book. The biggest risk is the balance sheet under a trough: net leverage is 3.5x on $266.8M of net debt with EBITDA currently negative, and capex of $88.8M against $12.3M of operating cash flow gave -$76.5M FCF.
