# GOGO — Gogo Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

Gogo sells in-flight broadband to business and military/government aviation, combining its legacy air-to-ground network in North America with LEO (Gogo Galileo, launched 2025) and GEO satellite service, and it absorbed Satcom Direct in December 2024. Consolidated numbers hide two opposite trends. Military and government service revenue rose 40% year over year and 20% sequentially to $39.9M in Q2 2026, a segment management calls a durable base, while business aviation service revenue fell 8% and total ATG aircraft online fell 15% to 5,731 as the old fleet churns faster than Galileo (184 aircraft online) and 5G replace it. Total revenue was down 1%. FY2026 guidance is $870M to $895M of revenue, $175M to $185M of adjusted EBITDA and $65M to $85M of free cash flow against a $359.1M market cap at the screen price of $2.65 (row dated 2026-09-04), so the equity FCF yield is very high. The biggest risk is that this is a thin stub: net debt of $750.9M is 6.6x EBIT and more than twice the market cap, so the transition has to finish before the legacy decline outruns it. Litigation expense in guidance was raised from $8M to $22M mid-year. No open-market insider buying.
