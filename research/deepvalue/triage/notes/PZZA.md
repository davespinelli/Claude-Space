Papa John's operates and franchises 6,083 pizza delivery and carryout restaurants, 475 company-owned, earning revenue from company restaurant sales, franchise royalties, North America commissary sales of food and paper to franchisees, and advertising fund contributions. No edge type applies. Q2-26 North America comparable sales fell 8.3%, company-owned fell 8.9%, total revenue fell 8.8% and global system-wide sales fell 4.8%; only International is positive, at 1.5% comps and a seventh consecutive positive quarter.
