# CRMD — CorMedix Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: fallen-growth · Score 8/10

CorMedix sells hospital and dialysis-clinic pharmaceuticals: DefenCath, the first FDA-approved antimicrobial catheter lock solution for hemodialysis patients, plus the six-product Melinta anti-infective portfolio and Toprol-XL acquired in August 2025. Growth is real and the multiple has collapsed. Total revenue went from $43.5 million in 2024 to $311.7 million in 2025, Q2 2026 consolidated revenue was $101.9 million with $58.7 million adjusted EBITDA, and the company maintained full-year revenue guidance of $325 to $345 million while raising adjusted EBITDA guidance to $125 to $140 million. Against that the shares trade 35.3% below the 52-week high at 2.7x EV/EBIT with $256.7 million of cash inside a $660.2 million market cap. The reason is dateable: DefenCath left TDAPA on July 1, 2026 for a post-TDAPA add-on of $2.37, which the 10-K says will significantly reduce reimbursement and net pricing in H2 2026. That is also the biggest risk, because the 2027 recovery to three to five times that add-on is the company's own estimate of a CMS methodology not yet published. Insiders sold $4.5 million net.
