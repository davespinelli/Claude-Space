# JELD — JELD-WEN Holding, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

JELD-WEN designs, manufactures and distributes interior and exterior doors (68% of 2025 revenue), windows (19%) and related building products from 76 facilities in 14 countries, selling to wholesale distributors, retailers and contractors across new construction and repair and remodel, in two segments, North America and Europe. The edge candidate is cyclical-trough: 2025 core revenue fell 12% on a 13% volume decline in a housing downturn the MD&A ties to interest rates and credit availability, and Q2 2026 shows the first inflection, with adjusted EBITDA up $3.3M to $42.3M, which the CEO says is the first year-over-year growth in ten quarters, margin up 50 basis points to 5.2%, North America segment adjusted EBITDA up 17.1%, and full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance raised to $120M to $150M from $100M to $150M. The biggest risk is that the equity is a thin stub on top of the cycle rather than a claim on the recovery: net debt is $1.2B against a $197.0M market cap, book equity is negative $23.9M, interest expense runs about $67M a year against a $135M guided EBITDA midpoint (roughly 9x leverage), first-half 2026 operating cash flow was negative $100.2M and free cash flow negative $144.8M, and full-year operating cash flow is guided to only about $10M. Goodwill impairments were $334.6M in 2025 after $94.8M in 2024. Volume/mix was still down 3% in the quarter, so the raised guidance rests on cost reduction, not demand. Insiders sold with no open-market buys.
