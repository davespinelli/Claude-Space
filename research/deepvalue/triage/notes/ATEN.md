# ATEN - A10 Networks, Inc. - triage 2026-09-04 - Edge: none - Score 4/10

A10 Networks sells secure application and network infrastructure: application delivery and traffic management, DDoS and API protection and centralised management, as hardware, software and cloud offerings to service providers (60% of 2025 revenue) and enterprises. Growth is real but the pack shows no valuation gap, so the edge type is none. Second-quarter revenue rose 15.5% to $80.1 million on 79.1% GAAP gross margin, full-year guidance was raised to 12-14% revenue and 14-16% EPS growth, and a multi-year Microsoft agreement came with a warrant over 800,000 shares. Against that, GAAP diluted EPS was $0.12 versus $0.25 non-GAAP, because stock-based compensation and related payroll tax of $9.3 million in the quarter almost equals the $9.0 million of GAAP operating income, and trailing EV/EBIT is 36.9x on a stock up 76.6% over twelve months. The biggest risk is customer concentration: the ten largest end-customers were 40% of 2025 revenue, purchases are large, irregular and hard to predict, and insiders sold $1.1 million with no buys.
