# HTLD — HEARTLAND EXPRESS INC · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

Heartland Express is an asset-based dry van truckload carrier running the Heartland, Millis Transfer, Smith Transport and CFI brands, average length of haul under 400 miles, earning revenue per mile on loads for major shippers. The freight cycle is clearly at a trough: FY25 revenue fell 23.1% to $805.7 million on soft rates and a 107.1% operating ratio, and management points to industry capacity reductions as the turn. Q2 2026 showed an operating ratio of 91.0% against 105.9%, net income of $10.6 million against a $10.9 million loss, and acquisition debt down to $135 million from $494 million in 2022. The catch is earnings quality, visible in the pack's own expense table: Q2 operating income of $16.5 million includes a $25.1 million gain on disposal of property and equipment, so operations excluding disposals still lost money, and the same is true of the $13.3 million H1 figure against $32.4 million of gains. Management guides a further $13-19 million of disposal gains for the rest of 2026 against $8-14 million of net capex. Biggest risk is that revenue keeps shrinking, down 12.5% year over year in Q2, while profit comes from selling trucks. It trades at roughly 1.26x the $756.3 million book.
