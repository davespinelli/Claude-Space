# TITN — Titan Machinery Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cyclical-trough · Score 5/10

Titan Machinery is a full-service dealer network for agricultural and construction equipment, operating in North America, Europe and Australia, earning on equipment sales plus higher-margin parts, service and rental. The cyclical-trough case is management's own: the CEO says fundamentals suggest calendar 2026 could be the bottom, Agriculture same-store sales fell 8.4% on grower profitability pressure, yet consolidated gross margin rose to 18.6% from 17.1% as aged inventory cleared, Agriculture pre-tax loss narrowed to $3.3M from $12.3M, Construction same-store sales grew 9.2% on data centre and infrastructure work, and the shares trade near the $557.8M book. The biggest risk is that the trough is not yet paid for: fiscal 2027 guidance is an adjusted net loss of $28M to $40M, or $(1.25) to $(1.75) per share, on adjusted EBITDA of only $17M to $29M, Europe was cut to down 30-40%, inventories rose $28.4M to $931.5M against $623.6M of floorplan payables, and H1 operating cash flow was negative $25.1M, so the screen's 25.8% FCF yield is a prior-year inventory liquidation, not earnings.
