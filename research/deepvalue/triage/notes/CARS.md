# CARS — Cars.com Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

Cars.com runs the Cars.com automotive marketplace plus an interconnected suite of dealer websites, trade and appraisal tools and media solutions, sold on subscription to about 19,500 franchise and independent dealers, with dealer revenue at 89% of the total and OEM advertising at 9%. The capital return is the case: the share count is down 12.9% year over year, 6.2 million shares were retired for $57M in the first half against a $90M full-year target on a $631.2M market cap, and adjusted EBITDA margin of 29.4% beat guidance while Q2 revenue grew 1%. Marketplace revenue grew over 7%, its best since 2021, with marketplace dealer count up 2% and ARPD up 3%. The risk is the audience underneath it. Average monthly unique visitors fell 14% year over year to 22.8 million and traffic fell 12%, both also down double digits sequentially, in a business whose own 10-K calls unique visitors and traffic fundamental. Management attributes this to a deliberate shift toward value delivery, which is a claim, not a finding. Two numbers to check: the screen's 23.3% FCF yield rests on CY2025 capex of only $4.3M, while first-half 2026 free cash flow was $43.5M, roughly a 14% annualized yield, and net debt is $413.8M. Insiders sold 152,580 shares.
