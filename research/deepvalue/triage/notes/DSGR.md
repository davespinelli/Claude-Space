# DSGR — Distribution Solutions Group, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

Distribution Solutions Group is a specialty distributor formed from Lawson Products, Gexpro Services and TestEquity, selling MRO C-parts, supply-chain services and electronic test and measurement equipment to roughly 220,000 customers, with a Canadian branch division added. Operationally the quarter was good: Q2 revenue rose 11.0% to $557.7 million on 10.2% organic growth, adjusted EBITDA rose 11.1% to $53.9 million at a 9.7% margin, and adjusted EPS went from $0.24 to $0.47 sequentially. None of that is the reason for the price. On July 15, 2026 the company signed a definitive merger agreement under which LKCM Headwater, already holder of approximately 79% of the shares and led by DSG's own chairman and CEO, will take the company private at $35.00 per share in cash, an 81% premium to the $19.31 close before the proposal was disclosed. The screen's $34.73 price is 0.8% below that, and the stock is 0.7% off its high. There is no mispricing left for a value desk, only a merger spread whose risks are the HSR waiting period and the majority-of-the-minority vote. Price 34.73 is the screen row's, undated.
