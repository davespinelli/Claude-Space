# OPTU — Optimum Communications, Inc. · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 2/10

Formerly Altice USA, Optimum is a cable operator delivering broadband, video, telephony and mobile to about 4.2 million customers across 21 states over roughly 10.1 million passings, plus the Lightpath enterprise fiber business. It is controlled by Patrick Drahi through Next Alt. There is no edge here; this is a balance sheet problem. Consolidated net debt was $25.3 billion at 8.0x leverage, the CSC Holdings Restricted Group alone sits at 22.8x, equity is negative $5.5 billion, and the weighted average life of debt is 2.8 years at a 6.8% cost. Operations are shrinking into that: Q2 revenue fell 5.8%, broadband lost 40k subscribers, total customer relationships fell 216.6k in FY25, and Q2 free cash flow was a deficit of $91.9M. Management raised $300M of preferred at 13% cash or 15% compounding to fund a tender offer, which is expensive money. The biggest risk is simply refinancing: a highly levered structure with under three years of average maturity and negative free cash flow. Milfam bought 3.0M shares at around $0.84, but that does not change the capital structure. The screen price is $1.04.
