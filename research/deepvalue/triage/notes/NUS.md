# NUS — NU SKIN ENTERPRISES, INC. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

The setup is cheap and hated: $228.8M market cap against $1.5B of 2025 revenue, 3.5x EV/EBIT, 20.1% FCF yield, essentially no net debt ($189.6M cash against $193.7M long-term debt), 62.1% off the 52-week high, and 2026 guidance of $0.70 to $0.90 adjusted EPS on a $4.70 share price. Q2 revenue fell 17.1% with every one of the seven Nu Skin geographies down, customers down 14%, paid affiliates down 8% and sales leaders down 9%, extending a 14% 2025 decline. Two signals argue against the bulls: a $186.6M Q2 tax provision on a pre-tax loss alongside a $78.9M impairment, and zero buyback in the quarter against $137.3M of remaining authorization.
