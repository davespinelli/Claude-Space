# GBX — GREENBRIER COMPANIES INC · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

Greenbrier builds and sells freight railcars in North America, Europe and Brazil, provides wheel services, parts and maintenance, and owns and leases a fleet of about 20,600 railcars. The consolidated numbers hide the shape of the earnings: in the May quarter, Leasing and Fleet Management produced $29.2M of the $31.9M of total earnings from operations on $47.4M of the $576.5M of revenue, at a 60.3% gross margin and 99.0% fleet utilization, while manufacturing earned a 5.7% operating margin. The fleet grew 23% sequentially, funded by a new $425M non-recourse term loan, and of the $1.8B of debt, $1.07B is non-recourse against that fleet, so the screen's $2.9B EV and 4.3x net-debt-to-EBIT overstate the recourse claim on manufacturing. Book equity of $1.6B stands against a $1.3B market cap, with a 49th consecutive quarterly dividend. Biggest risk: manufacturing is rolling over, with 2,200 units ordered against 3,600 delivered, backlog at 13,800 units and $2.0B, fiscal 2026 gross margin guidance cut to 13.8% to 14.2% and EPS to $3.00 to $3.15, which is still about 14x the current price. The CEO announced her retirement in August.
