# NPCE — NeuroPace Inc · triage 2026-09-05 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

NeuroPace sells the RNS System, an implanted brain-responsive neuromodulation device for adults with drug-resistant focal epilepsy, into US Level 4 comprehensive epilepsy centers and, since a 2023 label change, a wider prescriber base; over 8,000 patients have been implanted and the roughly 11-year battery life creates replacement revenue. Q2 2026 revenue was $22.8M with RNS revenue up 21.3%, non-GAAP gross margin 83.4%, and full-year guidance raised to $99.5M to $101.5M. There is no value edge in the pack: the $506.3M market cap is roughly 5x guided revenue for a company still guiding to a full-year adjusted EBITDA loss of $7.5M to $8.5M, with no insider purchases and no 8-K events of substance in six months. The biggest risk is that the business does not yet fund itself before the IGE label expansion lands: cash and investments fell to $51.9M against $59.0M of long-term borrowings, book equity is $11.9M, and the NAUTILUS study missed its primary effectiveness endpoint, so the PMA supplement rests on pre-specified secondary endpoints and an FDA meeting still being prepared.
