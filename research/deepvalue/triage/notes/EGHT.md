# EGHT — 8x8, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: cash-cow-narrative · Score 6/10

8x8 sells cloud communications to mid-market and small-enterprise customers: contact centre, unified communications and communications APIs on one platform, billed per seat plus usage for minutes, messaging and AI interactions. Edge is cash-cow-narrative. The pack shows $52.1M of FCF against a $277.3M market cap, a 18.8% yield (screen price $1.92, date not shown because price data was skipped), with debt cut from $323.9M to $309.4M in one quarter and FY27 operating cash flow guided at $45M to $52M against roughly $4M of capex, so the equity is a deleveraging stub on $179.5M of net debt. Biggest risk is what sits under the 5% headline growth: FY26 service revenue grew $22.3M only because usage revenue rose $51.4M while subscription revenue fell $29.1M on churn and down-sell, and usage carries far lower margin, so gross margin fell from 67.9% to 64.6% and Q1 FY27 GAAP gross margin was 61% versus 66%. The seat base is shrinking and each replacement dollar is worth less. Shares also rose 5.9% on dilution rather than falling.
