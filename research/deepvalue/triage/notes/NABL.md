# NABL — N-able, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: fallen-growth · Score 7/10

N-able sells a subscription cybersecurity and IT management platform covering unified endpoint management, security operations and data protection, sold through a channel of managed service providers and resellers to roughly 500,000 businesses; it was spun out of SolarWinds in July 2021. The edge type is fallen-growth: the shares are 53.1% below their 52-week high and down 36.1% over twelve months, while the company guides 2026 to $539-542M of revenue and $158-161M of adjusted EBITDA, a 29-30% margin, against a $753.7M market cap and $1.0B enterprise value, roughly 6.4x guided adjusted EBITDA, with $75.1M of trailing free cash flow, a 10.0% yield, and management stating an intent to be active with the buyback. The biggest risk is that growth is halving rather than pausing: ARR growth has fallen from 11.9% in 2025 (helped by the Adlumin acquisition) to 6.0% in Q2 and a 4-5% full-year guide, net revenue retention is 103%, the Chief Revenue Officer departed in July, and $392.3M of debt is generating about $12M of quarterly interest that turned 2025 GAAP net income negative.
