# INNV — InnovAge Holding Corp. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: none · Score 3/10

No edge is claimable because the price already reflects it: the screen row (2026-09-04, $10.77) shows a $1.5B market cap and $1.4B EV, about 16x guided adjusted EBITDA, after a 194.2% twelve-month move and 11.1% off the high. GAAP remains loss-making, with a $29.9M net loss in the quarter. The biggest risk is the size of the adjustment doing the work: $51.9M of litigation costs and settlement in one quarter, unexplained in the pack, exceeds the entire quarter's adjusted EBITDA, and management flags OBBBA-driven state Medicaid rate and enrolment pressure.
