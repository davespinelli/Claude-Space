# PRG — PROG Holdings, Inc. · triage 2026-09-04 · Edge: misunderstood-segment · Score 6/10

PROG Holdings is a fintech holding company for Progressive Leasing, the lease-to-own provider at retail point of sale, plus Four, a buy-now-pay-later product, MoneyApp, and Purchasing Power, acquired January 2, 2026. The edge is misunderstood-segment. Consolidated revenue grew 22.3% to $719.7M in Q2 2026 and GMV grew 60.1%, but the segments diverge sharply: Progressive Leasing, at $550.6M of revenue, saw revenue fall 3.4% and pre-tax earnings fall 11.9%, while Four grew GMV 110.6% and revenue 118.2% to $35.1M with pre-tax earnings up 139.9%, its eleventh straight quarter of triple-digit GMV growth, and Purchasing Power added $130.4M of revenue and $10.6M of adjusted EBITDA. Net leverage fell to 1.7x from 2.5x after the acquisition and buybacks resumed. The biggest risk is that the shrinking core is three quarters of the business and carries subprime credit exposure, with lease merchandise write-offs at 8.4% of leasing revenue, so consolidated growth is acquisition-supplied. The screen's 21.2% FCF yield is also flattered: capex is only $10.0M because lease merchandise purchases do not run through capex, so this is not free cash in the usual sense.
