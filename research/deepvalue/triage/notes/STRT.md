# STRT — STRATTEC SECURITY CORP · triage 2026-09-06 · Edge: post-restructuring · Score 7/10

Strattec designs and sells engineered vehicle access systems, spanning secure entry, powered access and latching, to automotive OEMs, with a majority of sales to the three largest North American manufacturers and significant manufacturing in Mexico. Item 1 is absent from the pack, so this description comes from the MD&A overview. The edge type is post-restructuring: FY26 closed a multi-year transformation with headcount down about 7%, gross margin up 150bp to 16.5%, adjusted EBITDA up 15.3% to $50.5M, all debt repaid, and $108.2M of cash against a $302.2M market cap, leaving EV of $194.0M or about 3.8x adjusted EBITDA at 7.3x EV/EBIT. Three distinct insiders bought 4,598 shares for $286,430 over twelve months with zero open-market sales, and a new $40M repurchase authorization equals roughly 13% of the market cap. The biggest risk is the customer base: management expects North American light vehicle production down 2-3% in FY27 and its own primary customers down 5-6%, with peso appreciation raising Mexican costs and new platform wins not reaching production until model year 2030.
