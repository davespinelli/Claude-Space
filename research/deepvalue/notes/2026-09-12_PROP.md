## 1. What the business actually does
Prairie is a single-basin operator in the Denver-Julesburg Basin in Colorado, drilling Niobrara and Codell wells on acreage assembled through the Nickel Road acquisition in October 2024 and the $482.5M cash Bayswater acquisition that closed on 26 March 2025 (10-K, Item 7). Proved reserves at 31 December 2025 were 121.1 MMBoe, 66.4 MMBoe of it proved developed producing and 52.6 MMBoe proved undeveloped, with PV-10 of $1,219.8M at an SEC oil price of $65.34 per barrel (10-K, Item 1). It runs one rig and one frac crew and drilled 27 wells in the first half of 2026 (10-Q).

## 2. Why it is mispriced — the edge case
There is an identifiable mechanical seller, and it is the reason to stay away rather than a reason to buy. The Series F preferred holder converts under an Alternative Conversion Rate priced off "the average of the two lowest daily volume-weighted average per share trading prices during any five consecutive trading-day period within the 35 trading-day period ending on the date of such calculation" (10-Q), subject to a 4.99% ownership cap (DEF 14A). That forces continuous conversion and sale into a stock trading $1.6M a day. In the six months to 30 June 2026, 29,323 preferred shares converted into 22,454,702 common shares and the count went from 62,499,375 to 105,828,010 (10-Q). The seller is neither wrong nor indifferent: the formula pays it more shares the lower the price goes, so the selling is self-reinforcing rather than a dislocation to harvest.

## 3. Unit economics and growth
Q2 2026 cash costs were $6.85 per Boe of lease operating expense, $1.22 transport, $4.01 taxes and $6.01 G&A (Q2 8-K). G&A at $12.0M was 12% of revenue, and 21% in FY2025, heavy for 22,000 Boe/d. The bigger fact is the lender-required hedge book: Q2 realised $94.21 per barrel of oil before derivatives and $59.79 after, with realised derivative losses of $32.7M in the quarter and $46.9M in the half. Swaps cover 4.66 MMBbl in 2027 at $62.51 and 2.86 MMBbl in 2028 at $62.17. Gas realisations were negative $1.30 per Mcf because gathering and processing fees exceeded gross sales on weak CIG pricing (Q2 8-K). Execution is genuinely improving: all 12 Q2 wells came in below AFE, the first three-mile lateral was drilled in a single run, and a 7-7/8 inch hole trial saved more than $40,000 per well (Q2 call). August production reached roughly 27,000 Boe/d, though the CEO expects "a small decline exiting the year."

## 4. Balance sheet and capital allocation
Cash at 30 June 2026 was under $0.1M against a $125.5M working capital deficit and $39.0M of availability on a $475M borrowing base with $436M drawn (10-Q). On 14 August the current ratio covenant was cut from 1.00x to 0.50x for Q2, 0.40x for Q3 and 0.60x for Q4, and a minimum production covenant was added, first tested 31 August 2026 on a rolling three-month average (8-K 2026-08-17); the Series F holder separately waived its own current ratio covenant only through 31 December 2026. The Anniversary Warrant issuance date has been pushed from 26 March to 7 April, 9 April, 8 July, 7 August, 14 August, 31 August and now 1 December 2026 (10-Q; 8-Ks of 10, 17, 31 August), with a Second Penny Warrant for 3,000,000 shares at $0.01 payable if it slips again. If issued it covers 65% of then-outstanding stated value divided by the prior ten-day VWAP, and 21,156,339 Incremental Share Rights sit alongside, convertible "at any time and at any price" while the preferred is outstanding. Nasdaq sent a minimum bid price notice on 2 July 2026, deadline 29 December (8-K 2026-07-09). Insider buying is trivial, $79,858 across two purchases in twelve months (Form 4s); the largest holder is the O'Neill Family Trust at 22.07% (DEF 14A).

## 5. Management: what they said vs what they did
The CEO and President both left on 2 March 2026 and the prior CFO in April 2025 (DEF 14A); Greg Patton became CEO in June and Michael Shelly CFO in July 2026 (Form 4s). Asked why the warrant date keeps slipping, Patton said the preferred is down from $148M to $121M to $78M of stated value and warrant coverage from 1.25 to 0.65 times, and that "there is also the beneficial need to redeem the preferred as soon as possible" (Q2 call). That progress is real but has been paid for in shares. Management's own full-year reconciliation adds back $96.0M of non-cash loss on financial instrument liabilities for 2026 against a $16.4M gain booked in H1, which is their own estimate that roughly $112M more of value transfers to the preferred holder in H2.

## 6. Valuation
Bear ($0.10, 40%): the 1 October redetermination cuts the borrowing base or the production covenant is missed, the Anniversary Warrants issue on 1 December at a sub-$0.40 VWAP, and the count passes 450M. Base ($0.45, 45%): guidance is met, the enterprise is worth 4.0x $185M of EBITDA or $740M, less roughly $470M of year-end net debt and $80M of preferred claims leaves about $190M of common value over roughly 420M shares. Bull ($1.40, 15%): the Series F is refinanced for cash, 2027 capex falls below EBITDA as the $62 swaps roll off, and the count stays near today's 185.6M diluted. Probability-weighted value $0.45, exactly the price. Reverse DCF: at $0.45 the market pays about $565M of enterprise value, or roughly $691M including the working capital deficit, for a reserve base whose PV-10 was $1,219.8M at 31 December 2025, so the price implies the proved base realises a little over half its PV-10 after 43% of it still has to be drilled. That is not obviously wrong.

## 9. Verdict and one-paragraph summary
PASS, conviction 4. Prairie's assets and drilling execution are better than a $0.45 share price suggests, with 121.1 MMBoe of proved reserves, $1,219.8M of PV-10, wells consistently below AFE and August production near 27,000 Boe/d, and on corrected LTM numbers the enterprise trades at 7.8x normalised after-tax operating profit rather than the screen's 9.9x. The reason to pass is that the common is not analysable as an equity while the Series F sits above it: the conversion rate is set off the two lowest VWAPs in a 35-day look-back under a 4.99% ownership cap, which turned 29,323 preferred shares into 22.5 million common shares and grew the share count 69% in six months; the Anniversary Warrant, sized at 65% of stated value divided by the prior ten-day VWAP, has been deferred seven times to 1 December 2026; and 21.2 million Incremental Share Rights convert at any price. Underneath, the company holds $21 thousand of cash against a $125.5M working capital deficit and $39M of revolver availability, had its current ratio covenant cut to 0.40x and a minimum production covenant added in August, is spending $185-195M of capex against $180-190M of EBITDA, and gives away roughly $16 per Boe to lender-mandated hedges. The screen's 302.5% free cash flow yield is an artefact of no capex being tagged; real first-half free cash flow was negative $49.6M. Revisit only if the Series F is retired for cash or refinanced into fixed-share instruments and 2027 capex is guided below EBITDA, at which point 3 to 4 times EBITDA for this reserve base would deserve a fresh look.
