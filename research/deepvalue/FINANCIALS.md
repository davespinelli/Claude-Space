# Financials & REIT Screen (v2) -- 2026-09-21

Banks, brokers, insurers and holding companies (SIC 6000-6799 except real estate) are scored on P/E, price to tangible book, return on tangible equity and tangible book-per-share growth, all on the common slice: preferred stock is deducted from equity and preferred dividends from earnings, because market cap only buys the common. Gates: growing (tangible BVPS or continuing net income up), cheap (P/E <= 12x or P/TBV <= 1.2x), equity at least 5% of assets. REITs and real-estate operators (SIC 6798, 6500-6599) are scored on P/FFO (FFO = net income + D&A + impairments), FFO growth and dividend yield; gates: FFO growing, P/FFO <= 15x, total liabilities <= 80% of assets.
