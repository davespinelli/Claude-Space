# INFO-5. Insider gifts: pre-registration (copy) and deviations

The spec below is copied unchanged from `research/infoedge/PREREG.md` (written 2026-09-26, before any data was collected). The common rules in that file apply. Everything after the spec was written on 2026-09-26 **before any return was computed**: it records how the spec was turned into code and what the data forced.

## Spec (copied, locked)

### Common rules (all five tests)

- **Point in time.** A signal is usable only from the first trading day after the date it became public. For a registry or database that publishes with a delay, that is the publication date, not the event date. If the publication date is unknown, use a conservative assumed lag, stated in Deviations before any return is computed.
- **Returns.**
  - Measure: the stock's return minus the return of a size-and-industry benchmark. If no benchmark can be built, use IWM for companies under $2B and SPY for the rest.
  - Source: daily prices from Yahoo (throttled; empty results can mean rate limiting). Record every company excluded for lack of prices, and run the -30% / +15% bounds for missing companies as in research/oplev.
- **Costs.** Report results after 0.5% a year, or 0.2% per event for event studies.
- **Verdict bar: t >= 2.5**, not 2.0. We are testing five ideas at once; at 2.0, one of them would pass by luck about a fifth of the time. A result must also have the pre-registered sign in both halves of the sample to be a Yes. Anything else is No; report it regardless.
- **Data forcing a change.** Record it in a "Deviations" section of that test's own PREREG before computing any return. If a test is infeasible (the data don't exist, or can't be dated), say so and stop. Do not swap in a different idea.
- **Shared rate limits:**
  - SEC EDGAR: at most 2 requests a second per test (five tests share one IP and SEC's 10-a-second limit), User-Agent "Claude Space research dspinjr@gmail.com".
  - Yahoo: throttle and retry.
- **Output.** research/infoedge/<test>/ holds scripts, data/ (small cleaned files), cache/ (gitignored) and RESULTS.md, with the plain-English answer first and the style of research/oplev/RESULTS.md.

### INFO-5. Insider gifts

- **Why the market might not see it:** officers and directors report stock gifts (Form 4 code G). Gifts to charity or family, timed at high prices, maximise the tax deduction and can reflect private knowledge. Gifts attract little attention compared with open-market sales.
- **Data:** SEC Form 3/4/5 bulk data sets, already cached at data/sec_cache/form345, from 2006 on.
- **Events:** gifts by an officer or director worth at least $1M, or at least 1% of the donor's holdings, dated by Form 4 filing date. Also report on the gift date, for information only; the verdict uses the filing date.
- **Primary:** abnormal return over trading days +1 to +60 after the filing date. Expected sign: negative. Same bar.
- **Secondary:** gifts in December; CEO/CFO gifts; companies under $2B; the pre-filing window (gift date to filing date), for information only.

("Same bar" refers to INFO-2's wording: t computed across events, clustered by filing month; t >= 2.5 in the expected direction and the expected sign in both halves.)

## Deviations (data forced)

1. **Coverage of the cached data sets.** `data/sec_cache/form345/` holds 2012q1 to 2026q1 only, not "from 2006 on". The missing quarters 2006q1 to 2011q4 and 2026q2 were downloaded from the SEC page (https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/insider-transactions-data-sets) into `cache/form345/` by `fetch_form345.py` (2026q2 sits under a different SEC folder, `/files/datastandardsinnovation/...`, found from the page link). The sample is therefore Form 4s filed 2006-01-01 to 2026-06-30, as specified. Events whose +60 trading-day window would run past the last Yahoo price (2026-09-25) are dropped; in practice none are, because day +60 for a 2026-06-30 filing is 2026-09-24.
2. **The 2023 gift-reporting change.** SEC Release 33-11138 (effective 2023-02-27) made gifts reportable on Form 4 within two business days. Before that, gifts were Form 5 items (due 45 days after fiscal year end) that insiders could, and often did, report voluntarily on Form 4, often weeks or months late. The data show it: the median Form 4 gift line was filed 4 days after the gift before 2023, but the 90th percentile was about 90 to 110 days; from 2024 the 90th percentile is 5 days. So the "filing date" signal means something different in the two regimes. Results are reported separately for gifts made before and on/after 2023-02-27 (by gift date). The verdict still uses the full sample, as pre-registered.
3. **Form 5 gifts are not events.** The spec dates events by the Form 4 filing date, so gifts disclosed only on Form 5 / 5/A (38,203 + 1,149 gift lines, overwhelmingly before 2023) are excluded. Gifts on Form 4/A are included (dated by the 4/A's filing date) when the gift does not already appear in an earlier filing.
4. **Missing prices dominate the early years.** Yahoo only has prices for symbols that still trade. Of the 6,754 issuers with an officer/director gift on a Form 4, only 2,913 are on the SEC's current ticker list; the share of gift lines from currently listed issuers rises from 44% in 2006 to 95% in 2025. Every qualifying event without a usable price is counted and listed (`data/missing_events.csv`), and the -30% / +15% bounds are run on the headline (below). For missing events the $1M test cannot use a Yahoo close, so it uses the gift line's own reported price if positive, otherwise the insiders' median open-market (code P/S) Form 4 price for that issuer within 30 days of the gift date; if neither exists, only the 1%-of-holdings test is applied.
5. **Benchmark universe is survivors only.** The size-and-industry benchmark is built from the same Yahoo panel (all currently listed NYSE / Nasdaq / CBOE issuers that appear in the Form 4/5 data, plus every event issuer with a validated ticker). It therefore contains only companies that still trade today. Event firms with prices are survivors too, so the comparison is like-for-like, but the benchmark is not the historical market.
6. **Shares outstanding before XBRL.** Market cap needs shares outstanding, taken from the SEC XBRL frames API (cover-page `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding`, falling back to balance-sheet `us-gaap:CommonStockSharesOutstanding`), which starts in 2009 and covers small companies only from 2011. For dates before a company's first XBRL value, its earliest value is carried back. This is used only to assign size groups and the under-$2B cut, never to select events. Companies with no XBRL share count at all have no market cap: they are benchmarked against IWM and left out of the under-$2B secondary. The XBRL share counts contain scale errors (Whirlpool reports 76,398,669,000,000 shares in one quarter, Clorox and Qualcomm similar; Citizens Inc reports 1 share), which put a few companies above $10 trillion. Before any return was computed, a cleaning rule was fixed: share counts under 10,000 are dropped; a value more than 5x above or below both neighbouring values is dropped as a one-off error (real splits persist, so they survive); an end value more than 50x from its only neighbour is dropped; any market cap above $6 trillion is treated as unknown.
7. **Line-level data errors.** Gift lines with zero or missing shares, a gift date after the filing date, or a gift date more than two years before the filing date (typo dates such as 2104 or 1914 appear) are dropped and counted.

## Definitions fixed before any return was computed (interpretation, not change)

- **Gift line:** non-derivative table, TRANS_CODE 'G', TRANS_ACQUIRED_DISP_CD 'D' (a disposal; receiving a gift is not a gift by the insider), on a Form 4 or 4/A.
- **Officer or director:** at least one reporting owner on the filing has "Director" or "Officer" in RPTOWNER_RELATIONSHIP (joint filings count once).
- **Deduplication:** (a) identical lines within one filing (same gift date, shares, ownership account and post-transaction balance) are kept once; (b) the same gift appearing in more than one filing (same issuer, gift date and share count, e.g. a 4/A, or spouses or co-trustees filing separately) is kept only in the earliest filing.
- **Gift:** all remaining gift lines in one filing with the same gift date, shares summed.
- **Gift value:** shares x Yahoo's unadjusted closing price on the gift date (last close on or before it if it is not a trading day). Unadjusted close = Yahoo's split-adjusted close x the split ratios that occur after that date.
- **Donor's holdings:** for each ownership account in the filing (security title x direct/indirect x nature of ownership), the last reported SHRS_OWND_FOLWNG_TRANS from the transaction and holdings tables, summed. Holdings before = that sum + all gift disposals in the filing. Share of holdings = gift shares / holdings before. No balance reported: only the $1M test applies.
- **Qualifying gift:** value >= $1,000,000 or share of holdings >= 1%.
- **Event:** an issuer and a filing date with at least one qualifying gift. Several qualifying gifts or donors at one issuer on one filing date are one event. The event's gift date is its earliest qualifying gift date.
- **Timing:** day 0 = the filing date (if it is not a trading day, the last trading day before it). Day +1 = the first trading day after the filing date. Primary window: days +1 to +60.
- **Abnormal return:** buy-and-hold abnormal return (BHAR) = compound return of the stock minus compound return of its benchmark over the window, using Yahoo adjusted closes (dividends included). If the stock's prices stop inside the window, both legs stop at its last price.
- **Size-and-industry benchmark:** industry = Fama-French 12 industries from the SEC SIC code; size = quintile of market cap across the panel at each 30 June (market cap = latest XBRL share count x close, both on today's split basis), cells re-formed each July. Benchmark daily return = equal-weighted average daily return of the other panel stocks in the same industry-size cell. A cell with fewer than 10 other stocks, or a company with no SIC, falls back to IWM (market cap under $2B or unknown) or SPY.
- **Ticker mapping:** issuer CIK on the SEC's current ticker list -> that ticker; otherwise the ISSUERTRADINGSYMBOL it used most in its last two filing years, unless that symbol now belongs to a different CIK (reused ticker: no price). Every mapping is checked against the insiders' own reported prices: for each event, the median ratio of Form 4 open-market (P/S) prices to Yahoo's unadjusted close over matching days within 365 days of the gift date must lie in [0.8, 1.25] when there are at least 3 such days; mappings from an old symbol (not on the current list) must pass this check with at least 3 days to be used at all.
- **Bad prints (as research/oplev, applied to daily data):** non-positive prices are dropped; a one-day round trip of 10x (a daily return <= -90% followed by >= +900%, or the reverse) removes both days; any remaining daily return above +1,000% is removed.
- **Statistics:** mean BHAR across events; t = mean / cluster-robust standard error, clusters = calendar month of the filing date (CR1 small-sample factor).
- **Halves:** events split at the median filing date (equal counts).
- **Costs:** 0.2% per event. The expected sign is negative (a short or an avoid), so the after-cost mean is mean BHAR + 0.2%.
- **Verdict (unchanged from the spec):** Yes only if the clustered t of the full-sample mean BHAR (+1,+60) is <= -2.5 and the mean is negative in both halves; otherwise No. The after-cost number is reported next to it.
- **Missing-company bounds:** every qualifying event without a usable price is added with a BHAR of -30%, then of +15%, over the window.
- **Secondaries:** December = the event's gift date is in December. CEO/CFO = a reporting owner on a qualifying-gift filing has a title containing CEO, CFO, Chief Executive, Chief Financial, Principal Executive or Principal Financial. Under $2B = market cap on day 0 below $2B. Pre-filing window = BHAR from the close on the gift date to the close on day 0 (events with the gift and filing on the same trading day are left out of this average). Gift-date version (information only) = BHAR over trading days +1 to +60 after the gift date.
- **Information-only checks:** 1%/99% winsorised BHAR; IWM/SPY benchmark instead of size-and-industry; only the first event per issuer in any 60-trading-day span (no overlapping windows); t clustered by issuer.
